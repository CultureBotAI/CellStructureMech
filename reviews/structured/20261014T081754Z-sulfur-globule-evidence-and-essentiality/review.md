major / open / confirmed; issue key: sulfur-globule-sgp-mutant-interpretation.

The nonforming double mutant was B+C, not A+B. Conditional necessity can be retained only with the relevant genetic background explicit; a smaller formed globule alone fits native DISPENSABLE semantics. This finding does not claim SgpC is dispensable in every background.
