## Findings

### F1: Correct the assay and preparation context attributed to Bresan 2016

major / open / confirmed; issue key: pha-bresan-experiment-attribution.

The core component note and resolved discussion say Bresan analyzed purified granules and found polymer plus protein with no detectable phospholipids; the core graph edge claims a measured dominant polymer chemical fraction. The inspected paper instead bases its principal conclusion on in-vivo fluorescent phospholipid probes. Its isolated-granule experiment uses fluorescence and reports occasional positive granules interpreted as contamination. The record thereby invents a chemical-fraction measurement and obscures the in-vivo versus isolation distinction central to the source. The supported protein-coated-core model can remain, but its evidence must describe the actual assay, organisms and preparation caveats.

### F2: Support or qualify the generic phasin conditional-essentiality annotation

major / open / confirmed; issue key: pha-phasin-conditional-essentiality.

The component encodes CONDITIONAL, which means required for formation in some conditions or lineages, but gives no such condition or lineage. Its cited H16 paper supports surface localization and altered granule architecture; PhaP6/PhaP7 deletion retains formation, and the discussion describes granules remaining after PhaP1 deletion. Neither variable abundance nor effects on size/number establish the required condition. Provide direct, scoped necessity evidence or use an appropriately qualified/unknown annotation. Do not extrapolate individual deletion results into universal dispensability of the whole phasin class.
