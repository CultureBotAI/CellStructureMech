The local identifier denotes the expanded cytoplasmic plaque formed after the satellite and before completed daughter SPB. The protein-containing-complex parent is appropriate. Mature GO0005816 and half-bridge GO0005825 denote different entities; a duplication process is not a structural identity.

### Budding-yeast model and transient stages

scope: supported. Targets: cellstructuremech:spindle_pole_body_duplication_plaque.

The species and qualitative COMMON assertion are supported as a normal characterized assembly stage. Adams experiments used K699 and isogenic K842 derivatives, including tagged, synchronized and mutant strains; these are not relabeled as S288C or generalized to every fungal cell at every time. Tetraploid satellite morphology and overexpression phenotypes are separate contexts.
