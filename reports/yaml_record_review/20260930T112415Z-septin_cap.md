## Findings

### 1. Remove the indirect Martin and Konopka DOI from the cap-colocalization edge

Severity: medium

Location: `data/structures/cytoskeleton/septin_cap.yaml:71`

The record cites `DOI:10.1128/EC.3.3.675-684.2004` globally and again on the
`septin_cap_topology` `colocalizes with` edge. Martin and Konopka 2004 support
sterol polarization at the *Candida albicans* hyphal leading edge, but the
specific edge states that the septin cap colocalizes with an ergosterol-rich
plasma-membrane region. That direct colocalization is already present in the
`GO:0032159` definition, so the literature DOI should not be cited as if it
independently observes the septin cap and ergosterol-rich region together.

Recommendation: remove `DOI:10.1128/EC.3.3.675-684.2004` from top-level
evidence and from the `septin_cap_topology` colocalization edge; leave the
`GO:0032159` evidence on the edge.
