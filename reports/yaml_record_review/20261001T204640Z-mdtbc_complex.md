## Authority Checks

- QuickGO reports `GO:1990203` as an active cellular-component term named
  `MdtBC Complex`, with exact synonym `multidrug efflux pump MdtBC`, a
  `PMID:20038594` definition cross-reference, and definition text specifying a
  complex with a MdtB dimer and one unit of MdtC.
- QuickGO reports `GO:1902495` as the active cellular-component term
  `transmembrane transporter complex`; the `GO:1990203` `is_a` ancestor set
  includes `GO:1902495`.
- QuickGO reports `GO:0042910` as the active molecular-function term
  `xenobiotic transmembrane transporter activity`.
- UniProt REST reports `P76398` as the reviewed E. coli K-12 entry named
  `Multidrug resistance protein MdtB`, with gene name `mdtB` and InterPro
  entries `IPR027463`, `IPR001036`, and `IPR022831`.
- UniProt REST reports `P76399` as the reviewed E. coli K-12 entry named
  `Multidrug resistance protein MdtC`, with gene name `mdtC` and InterPro
  entries `IPR027463`, `IPR001036`, and `IPR023931`.
- InterPro reports `IPR022831` against `UniProtKB:P76398` as `Multidrug
  resistance protein MdtB`; shared `IPR001036` and `IPR027463` are broader
  AcrB-family and domain entries and are therefore correctly unused for the
  MdtB component grounding.
- InterPro reports `IPR023931` against `UniProtKB:P76399` as `Multidrug
  resistance protein MdtC`; shared `IPR001036` and `IPR027463` are broader
  AcrB-family and domain entries and are therefore correctly unused for the
  MdtC component grounding.
- PubMed reports `PMID:20038594` as the Journal of Bacteriology article
  `Multidrug efflux pump MdtBC of Escherichia coli is active only as a B2C
  heterotrimer.`, with DOI `10.1128/JB.01448-09`.

## Review Findings

No concrete defects were found.

The review specifically checked:

- the new record identifier is the exact GO cellular-component term for the
  MdtBC complex, not only the broader transmembrane-transporter parent;
- `GO:1902495` parentage, GO exact synonymy, and GO definition text are
  represented without broadening the record to all RND efflux pumps;
- `GO:0042910` is molecular-function evidence for xenobiotic transport and is
  kept under `functions` rather than used as the structure identity;
- the MdtB and MdtC components use exact source-neutral InterPro groundings,
  while broader AcrB-family InterPro accessions exposed by the same reviewed
  UniProt entries are deliberately avoided;
- the reviewed E. coli K-12 UniProt examples have the expected `mdtB` and
  `mdtC` gene labels and match the MdtB and MdtC components;
- `PMID:20038594` supports the E. coli canonical example and the modeled
  `SUBUNIT_COUNT` value of 3 through the active B2C heterotrimer;
- the Bacteria-level taxonomic-distribution row is variable and conservative
  rather than claiming that MdtBC is universal in Bacteria;
- the history record targets the new YAML path and summarizes the GO, UniProt,
  DOI/PMID, taxonomy, function, component, stoichiometry, and InterPro additions;
- rendered `pages/`, README statistics, and text embedding artifacts are
  regenerated from the new corpus state.
