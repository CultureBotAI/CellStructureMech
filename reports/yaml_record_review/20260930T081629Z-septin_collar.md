evidence support, component grounding, graph claims, taxonomy scope, and artifact

## Findings

### 1. Saccharomyces example rows are under-cited

- Severity: medium
- Location: `taxonomic_distribution`, `canonical_examples`
- Issue: #1815

The new record asserted `NCBITaxon:4932` for `Saccharomyces cerevisiae` in both
the taxonomic distribution and canonical example rows but cited only
`GO:0032173`. The generic GO term defines the septin collar as forming in
"budding yeast cells" and has no Saccharomyces-specific taxon constraint in the
local GO metadata. The species-level rows should cite a Saccharomyces source,
or the exact Saccharomyces bud-neck child term plus its primary literature,
rather than asking the generic parent term to support a species-level example.

Recommended fix: keep the `NCBITaxon:4932` rows, but cite
`DOI:10.1091/mbc.E04-09-0764` as primary literature support for
Saccharomyces mother-bud-neck septin collar formation and add that DOI to the
record-level evidence.

## Non-findings

- `GO:0032173` is an exact GO cellular-component identifier for the generic
  septin collar and is not already present as a top-level record in the
  ignored-file-inclusive `data/structures` search.
- `septin hourglass` is a GO exact synonym.
- `GO:0032156` is an is-a parent, not a parthood target, for this record.
- The record correctly keeps `GO:0032174` and `GO:0062140` as narrower collar
  classes instead of merging them into the generic parent.
- The `septin_proteins` component is intentionally `REVIEWED_LABEL_ONLY`; no
  unverified InterPro, Pfam, NCBIfam, or UniProtKB accession was guessed.
- The ring-to-collar graph edges are scoped as nonmechanistic and grounded to
  the GO definition rather than overclaiming a full Saccharomyces septin
  recruitment pathway.
