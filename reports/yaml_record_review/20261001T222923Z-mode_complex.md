## Authority Checks

- QuickGO reports `GO:1990198` as an active cellular-component term named
  `ModE complex`, with exact synonym `ModE dimer`; the definition states that
  the complex contains two ModE subunits, binds directly to DNA to regulate
  transcription, and regulates molybdenum metabolism.
- QuickGO reports `GO:0005667` as the active cellular-component term
  `transcription regulator complex`; `GO:1990198` is a child of `GO:0005667`.
- QuickGO reports `GO:0030151` as the active molecular-function term
  `molybdenum ion binding`.
- QuickGO reports `GO:0000987` as the active molecular-function term
  `cis-regulatory region sequence-specific DNA binding`.
- UniProtKB reports `P0A9G8` as the reviewed E. coli K-12 entry named
  `DNA-binding transcriptional dual regulator ModE`, with gene names `modE`,
  `modR`, `b0761`, and `JW0744`, a `GO:1990198` cross-reference, and
  `GO:0030151` and `GO:0000987` molecular-function cross-references.
- InterPro reports `IPR016462` against `UniProtKB:P0A9G8` as the exact
  `Molybdate-dependent transcriptional regulator ModE` family entry; the other
  ModE InterPro hits are narrower domains or broader superfamilies and are
  correctly unused for the component grounding.
- RCSB reports `PDB:1O7L` as `Molybdate-activated form of ModE from
  Escherichia coli`, with homomeric dimer biological assemblies.
- RCSB reports `PMID:12581638` and Crossref reports
  `DOI:10.1016/S0022-2836(02)01358-X` for Schuttelkopf, Boxer and Hunter's
  2003 Journal of Molecular Biology structural paper on activated E. coli ModE.

## Review Findings

No concrete defects were found.

The review specifically checked:

- the new record identifier is the exact GO cellular-component term for the
  ModE homodimer, not only the broader transcription-regulator parent;
- `GO:0005667` parentage and GO exact synonymy are represented without
  broadening the record to all DNA-binding transcription-regulator complexes;
- `GO:0030151` and `GO:0000987` are molecular-function evidence for molybdate
  and cis-regulatory DNA binding and are kept under `functions`, not used as
  structure identifiers;
- the ModE component uses the exact source-neutral InterPro family grounding,
  while broader superfamily and domain InterPro accessions exposed by the same
  reviewed UniProt entry are deliberately avoided;
- the reviewed E. coli K-12 UniProt example has the expected `modE` gene label
  and matches the taxon-agnostic ModE family component;
- `PDB:1O7L` supports the E. coli canonical example and the `SUBUNIT_COUNT`
  value of 2 through a homomeric ModE dimer biological assembly;
- the record avoids a broad taxonomic-distribution row because the reviewed
  evidence establishes the E. coli example and source-neutral InterPro family,
  not a directly cited clade-wide distribution claim;
- no exact snippets were added from unreadable literature sources, so the
  citation-verification gate remains responsible only for pre-existing snippets;
- the history record targets the new YAML path and summarizes the GO, InterPro,
  UniProt, PDB, DOI/PMID, function, component, and stoichiometry additions;
- rendered `pages/`, README statistics, and text embedding artifacts are
  regenerated from the new corpus state.
