## Findings

### 1. Cvt complex claims a pathway that acts on it as its own function

- Issue: #1813
- Severity: Medium
- Location: `functions`

The `functions` row assigns `GO:0032258` (`cytoplasm to vacuole targeting by the
Cvt pathway`) to the Cvt complex. The cited GO term and Cvt-pathway evidence
support receptor-dependent recognition and vesicle enclosure of the Cvt complex,
but the Cvt complex is the preApe1p-containing cargo complex targeted by that
pathway. Modeling the whole Cvt pathway as a function carried out by the cargo
complex overstates the `GO:0034270` record boundary.

Fix: remove the passive `functions` row and keep `GO:0032258` as evidence for
the causal-graph enclosure edge that places the Cvt complex as delivered cargo.
