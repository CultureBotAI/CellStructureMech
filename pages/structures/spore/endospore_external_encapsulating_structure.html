endospore external encapsulating structure

GO:0043591 · SPORE · ENVELOPE_LAYER · PROPOSED

The layered envelope that surrounds a bacterial endospore and gives it its resistance: a thick modified peptidoglycan cortex, a proteinaceous coat of dozens of proteins outside it, and in some species an additional loose exosporium. It encases the dehydrated, mineralised core in which the chromosome is held. [GO:0043591]

Synonyms: spore coat and cortex

Functions

  • spore-mediated resistance to environmental stress — The cortex dehydrates the core and the coat excludes lytic enzymes and chemicals, giving decades of dormancy and resistance to heat, radiation and solvents.
    • DOI:10.1128/9781555819323.ch10 Spore Resistance Properties (2016) reviews the contributions of cortex and coat to resistance.
    • DOI:10.1038/nrmicro2921 McKenney et al. 2012 describe the coat as a barrier to lytic enzymes and toxic chemicals.
