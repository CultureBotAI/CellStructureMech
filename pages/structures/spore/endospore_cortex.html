endospore cortex

GO:0043595 · SPORE · ENVELOPE_LAYER · PROPOSED

The thick, loosely cross-linked peptidoglycan layer between the spore core and the coat of a mature bacterial endospore. [GO:0043595]

Synonyms: spore cortex

Part of: GO:0043591

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0364

endospore cortex peptidoglycanPEPTIDOGLYCANpart ofREVIEWED_LABEL_ONLYESSENTIALThick, modified peptidoglycan layer that holds the mature endospore core in a dehydrated, heat-resistant state and is cleaved during germination.
  • DOI:10.1128/9781555819323.ch10 Setlow 2016 reviews modified cortex peptidoglycan as a determinant of core dehydration and wet-heat resistance.

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:1239 Bacillota — COMMON: Endospore cortex is produced by endospore-forming Bacillota lineages and is lost from many non-sporulating members of the phylum. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro2921]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:1423 Bacillus subtilis — Classical model for endospore differentiation and the cortex layer that surrounds the dormant spore core. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro2921]

Functions

  • spore core dehydration — The endospore cortex helps maintain low water content in the spore core, a major physical basis for wet-heat resistance.
    • DOI:10.1128/9781555819323.ch10 Setlow 2016 reviews the cortex contribution to low core water content and endospore wet-heat resistance.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the endospore cortex separate from the broader endospore external encapsulating structure. GO:0043595 denotes only the thick cortex layer, while GO:0043591 denotes the multilayered external structure containing the cortex, coat and outer membrane.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Find an exact source-neutral grounding for endospore cortex peptidoglycan.
