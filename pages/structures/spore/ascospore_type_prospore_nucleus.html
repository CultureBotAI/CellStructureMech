ascospore-type prospore nucleus

GO:1905754 · Spore · Membrane bounded organelle · Proposed

Any nucleus that is part of a ascospore-type prospore. In sporulating Saccharomyces cerevisiae and Schizosaccharomyces pombe asci, each of the four haploid nuclear lobes generated by meiosis II is engulfed by an expanding prospore membrane nucleated at the meiotic outer plaque of the spindle pole body, partitioning one haploid ascospore-type prospore nucleus into each developing prospore. [GO:1905754]

Synonyms: prospore nucleus

Part of: ascospore-type prospore (GO:0042764 — ascospore-type prospore)
