spore inner membrane

GO:0140549 · SPORE · MEMBRANE · PROPOSED

The membrane surrounding the spore core (endospore core) that separates it from its external environment. [GO:0140549]

Synonyms: spore membrane

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:1239 Bacillota — COMMON: Spore inner membranes surround the dormant core in endospore-forming Bacillota lineages, while many non-sporulating Bacillota lack the endospore pathway. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro2921]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:1423 Bacillus subtilis — Classical bacterial endospore model in which the mature core is surrounded by the spore inner membrane and external cortex and coat layers. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro2921]

Mechanism graphs

Spore inner membrane bounds the endospore core (FUNCTION)

The spore inner membrane encloses the dormant endospore core. The endospore cortex lies outside that inner membrane and helps maintain low water content in the core.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the spore inner membrane separate from the generic UniProt Spore membrane location, the forespore membrane, the outer endospore membrane, and non-bacterial prospore membranes. GO:0140549 denotes the bacterial membrane surrounding the endospore core, GO:0043594 denotes the separate outer endospore membrane between cortex and coat, GO:0005628 denotes a fungal ascospore-type prospore membrane, and UniProt SL-0363 is a broader spore membrane location without a GO mapping.
