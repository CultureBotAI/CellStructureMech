microsporidian-type endospore

GO:0090641 · SPORE · ENVELOPE_LAYER · PROPOSED

The middle layer in a microsporidian spore wall that lies under the exospore and outside the plasma membrane, containing chitin and proteins. [GO:0090641]

Synonyms: chitin-rich inner spore wall layer

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:6029 Microsporidia — COMMON: Microsporidian spores have a thick wall made of an outer exospore and a chitin-containing inner endospore; this scope is tied to the spore stage. [DOI:10.1016/j.ijpara.2005.10.005]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:27978 Nosema bombycis — Silkworm-infecting microsporidian in which SWP25 was localized to the endospore. [DOI:10.1111/j.1550-7408.2008.00375.x]
  • NCBITaxon:6035 Encephalitozoon cuniculi — Human-pathogenic microsporidian model in which EnP1 and EnP2 were assigned to the chitin-rich endospore. [DOI:10.1016/j.ijpara.2005.10.005]

Functions

  • spore wall protection — The endospore contributes to the thick wall that protects extracellular microsporidian spores during transmission between host cells.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.ijpara.2005.10.005 Peuvel-Fanget et al. 2006 describe environmentally resistant microsporidian spores with a rigid wall composed of an exospore and chitin-rich endospore.
    • DOI:10.1128/IAI.01225-09 Polonais et al. 2010 describe the infective resistance stage as a microsporidian spore protected by a thick wall whose inner layer is the chitin- and protein-containing endospore.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Keep the microsporidian-type endospore separate from the bacterial endospore external encapsulating structure. Microsporidian biology uses endospore for a chitin-rich inner spore-wall layer, whereas GO:0043591 denotes a bacterial external encapsulating structure containing cortex, coat and sometimes exosporium layers.
