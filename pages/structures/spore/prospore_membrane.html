prospore membrane

GO:0005628 · SPORE · MEMBRANE · PROPOSED

An ascospore-forming fungal double-membrane structure that grows from meiotic outer plaques to encapsulate meiotic nuclei and provide the foundation for spore-wall assembly. [GO:0005628]
