prospore membrane

GO:0005628 · Spore · Membrane · Proposed

An ascospore-forming fungal double-membrane structure that grows from meiotic outer plaques to encapsulate meiotic nuclei and provide the foundation for spore-wall assembly. [GO:0005628]

Synonyms: ascospore-type prospore membrane; FSM; forespore membrane

Part of: ascospore-type prospore (GO:0042764 — ascospore-type prospore)

Has part: prospore membrane leading edge (GO:0070056); prospore membrane spindle pole body attachment site (GO:0070057)

Secretory traffic and the leading edge build the prospore membrane (ASSEMBLY)

During budding-yeast sporulation, post-Golgi secretory traffic supplies membrane at meiotic outer plaques to generate prospore membranes, while leading-edge coat proteins track the open rim of the growing membrane before closure.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral groundings and component boundaries for the prospore membrane leading-edge coat subunits. The initial record treats the leading-edge coat as one reviewed-label-only constituent and records Saccharomyces SSP1, ADY3 and DON1 as gene-symbol metadata. Exact family groundings for individual Ssp1, Ady3, Don1 and Irc10 components, and the boundary between stable coat proteins and Spo20/Sso1 fusion machinery, need a focused curation pass.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Should the meiotic spindle pole body or meiotic outer plaque be modeled as a prospore membrane component? The meiotic outer plaque nucleates and anchors prospore membrane formation, but it is a specialization of the spindle pole body rather than a molecular constituent of the membrane itself.
