Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve an exact source-neutral grounding for the mature dityrosine-containing spore-wall macromolecule. The record has enough biochemical support to name the dityrosine-containing wall material, but the exact chemical boundary of the assembled insoluble wall macromolecule has not been matched to a source-neutral CHEBI identifier.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Treat Dit1 and Dit2 as precursor biosynthetic enzymes rather than dityrosine-layer constituents. DIT1 and DIT2 encode sporulation-specific enzymes that synthesize soluble LL-dityrosine-containing precursors before incorporation into the wall, so they support the precursor-biosynthesis pathway rather than a mature-layer constituent call.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the Saccharomyces dityrosine layer separate from the Schizosaccharomyces Isp3 layer. GO:0005630 denotes the Saccharomyces dityrosine layer, whereas GO:1990916 denotes the Schizosaccharomyces pombe Isp3 spore-wall layer.
