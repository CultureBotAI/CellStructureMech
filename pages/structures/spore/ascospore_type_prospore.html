ascospore-type prospore

GO:0042764 — ascospore-type prospore · Spore · Subcellular region · Proposed

An immature spore undergoing development. The spore usually consists of nucleic acid, prospore membrane(s) that encase the nucleic acid, and ultimately a cell wall that covers the membrane(s). This type of spore is observed in ascospore-forming fungi. The nascent intracellular spore compartment formed during meiosis II in ascomycete yeasts such as Saccharomyces cerevisiae and Schizosaccharomyces pombe before spore wall maturation. Each ascospore-type prospore is delimited by the double-layered prospore membrane that grows from the spindle pole body to engulf a haploid nucleus and maternal cytoplasm. [GO:0042764 — ascospore-type prospore]

Has part: prospore membrane (GO:0005628); prospore contractile ring (GO:0032157); prospore septin ring (GO:0032169); ascospore-type prospore nucleus (GO:1905754)
