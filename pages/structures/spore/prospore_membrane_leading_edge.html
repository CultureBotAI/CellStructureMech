prospore membrane leading edge

GO:0070056 · SPORE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The region of the prospore membrane that extends to surround the spore nucleus; coated with specific proteins that are thought to play a role in prospore membrane organization. [GO:0070056]
