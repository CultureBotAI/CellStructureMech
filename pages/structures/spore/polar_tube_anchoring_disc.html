polar tube anchoring disc

GO:0160202 · SPORE · OTHER · PROPOSED

A structural component of the microsporidian spore's invasion apparatus that is located at the anterior end of the spore and serves to attach the polar tube to the inside of the spore wall. [GO:0160202]

Synonyms: polar tube anchoring disk

Functions

  • polar tube attachment — The anchoring disc attaches the intracellular polar tube to the inside of the anterior spore wall, positioning the tube for apical release during germination.
