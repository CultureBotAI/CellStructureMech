endospore coat

GO:0043593 · Spore · Envelope layer · Proposed

The layer in a bacterial endospore that lies under the exosporium, and is impermeable to many toxic molecules. The coat may also contain enzymes that are involved in endospore germination. [GO:0043593]

Part of: endospore external encapsulating structure (GO:0043591)
