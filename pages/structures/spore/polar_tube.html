polar tube

GO:0044099 · SPORE · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A microsporidian invasion tube that is coiled inside the spore as the polar filament and rapidly everts from the anterior pole during germination, forming a conduit for the sporoplasm toward a host cell. [GO:0044099]

Synonyms: polar filament; PT

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:6029 Microsporidia — COMMON: The polar tube is a defining invasion apparatus of microsporidian spores; this scope is tied to the spore stage, not every vegetative cell. [DOI:10.1186/s13071-023-05908-9]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:27978 Nosema bombycis — Silkworm-infecting microsporidian in which polaroplast-derived membranes were tracked through fired polar tubes during sporoplasm membrane formation. [DOI:10.1128/mbio.02749-23]

Functions

  • host cell invasion — The polar tube rapidly everts from the spore, penetrates or apposes the host-cell surface and establishes the route used to initiate infection.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.ijpara.2005.04.003 Xu and Weiss 2005 reviewed polar-tube discharge and host-cell invasion by microsporidian spores.
    • DOI:10.1186/s13071-023-05908-9 Chen et al. 2023 reviewed polar-tube origin, structure, composition and function in microsporidia.
  • sporoplasm delivery — After firing, the everted tube acts as a narrow conduit for microsporidian sporoplasm and associated cargo released from the spore.
    • DOI:10.1128/mbio.02749-23 Lv et al. 2024 visualized polaroplast-derived membranes moving through fired Nosema bombycis polar tubes during sporoplasm membrane formation.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.pbio.3002533 Sharma et al. 2024 imaged cargo-containing states of Vairimorpha necatrix polar tubes during germination by cryo-electron tomography.
