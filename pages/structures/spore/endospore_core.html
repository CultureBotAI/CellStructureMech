endospore core

GO:0070477 · SPORE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

An intracellular part that represents the innermost portion of an endospore; the endospore core is dehydrated, enriched in dipicolinic acid and divalent cations, and metabolically inactive. [GO:0070477]

Synonyms: spore core; spore matrix

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0253

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:1239 Bacillota — COMMON: Endospore cores are produced by endospore-forming Bacillota lineages as the dormant innermost compartment, while many non-sporulating members of the phylum have lost the pathway. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro2921]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:1423 Bacillus subtilis — Classical bacterial endospore model in which the forespore matures into a dehydrated endospore core inside cortex and coat layers. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro2921]

Mechanism graphs

Surrounding layers keep the endospore core dehydrated (FUNCTION)

The dormant endospore core is the innermost compartment. It is bounded by the spore inner membrane, while the surrounding cortex helps maintain the low core water content that supports wet-heat resistance.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the endospore core separate from the surrounding spore inner membrane, cortex, coat, and external encapsulating structure. GO:0070477 denotes the innermost dehydrated endospore core, whereas GO:0140549 denotes the membrane surrounding it, GO:0043595 denotes the cortex outside that membrane, and GO:0043591 denotes the broader cortex-coat external encapsulating structure.
