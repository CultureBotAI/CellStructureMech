Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Use CHEBI:16261 for the chitosan polymer constituent, not for the complete chitosan layer structure. CHEBI:16261 denotes the source-neutral chitosan polymer, whereas GO:0005631 denotes the full Saccharomyces spore-wall layer made from chitosan.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Treat Chs3, Cda1, and Cda2 as chitosan-layer biosynthetic machinery rather than mature-layer constituents. Chs3 synthesizes chitin and Cda1/Cda2 modify chitin into chitosan for wall assembly, but the curated constituent is the resulting chitosan polymer rather than its biosynthetic enzymes.
