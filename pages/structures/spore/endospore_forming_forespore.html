endospore-forming forespore

GO:0042601 · SPORE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

Portion of the cell formed during the process of bacterial sporulation that will ultimately become the core of the endospore. An endospore is a type of dormant cell that is resistant to adverse conditions. [GO:0042601]

Synonyms: forespore; prespore

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0119

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:1239 Bacillota — COMMON: Endospore-forming forespores occur as transient daughter compartments in endospore-forming Bacillota lineages during sporulation, with many non-sporulating members of the phylum lacking the structure. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro2921]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:1423 Bacillus subtilis — Classical endospore model in which an asymmetric polar septum creates a smaller forespore compartment and a larger mother-cell compartment. [DOI:10.1016/j.cub.2005.02.041]

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the bacterial forespore separate from the broader intracellular immature spore and from ascospore-type prospore records. GO:0042601 specifically denotes the bacterial sporulation compartment that becomes an endospore core, whereas GO:0042763 also parents fungal ascospore-type prospores and the existing GO:0005628 prospore membrane record is a yeast double-membrane structure.
