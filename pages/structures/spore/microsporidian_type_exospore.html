microsporidian-type exospore

GO:0090642 · SPORE · ENVELOPE_LAYER · PROPOSED

The dense, protein-rich outermost layer of a microsporidian spore wall that lies above the endospore. [GO:0090642]

Synonyms: glycoprotein-rich outer spore wall layer

Part of: GO:0031160

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:6035 Encephalitozoon cuniculi — Human-pathogenic microsporidian model in which SWP1 was identified as an outer spore-wall protein. [DOI:10.1128/IAI.68.4.2268-2275.2000]

Functions

  • spore wall protection — The exospore contributes to the thick wall that surrounds and protects extracellular microsporidian spores during host-to-host transmission.
    • DOI:10.1002/pmic.200700584 Wu et al. 2008 describe microsporidian resistant spores as protected by a thick wall made of a glycoprotein-rich exospore and a chitin-rich endospore.
    • DOI:10.1128/IAI.68.4.2268-2275.2000 Bohne et al. 2000 describe the rigid spore wall as surrounding the Encephalitozoon cuniculi spore and providing mechanical resistance.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Keep the microsporidian-type exospore separate from the bacterial exosporium. GO:0090642 denotes the outer layer of a microsporidian spore wall, whereas bacterial exosporia are optional outer layers of bacterial endospore coats that occur in a different spore architecture.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact family groundings for microsporidian exospore spore-wall proteins. This record models SWP1, SWP32 and NbSWP16 as an aggregate exospore protein cohort because known exospore-localized SWPs are lineage-specific and no exact source-neutral family term was curated. A follow-up pass should split and ground any exact InterPro, Pfam or NCBIfam families for individual SWP classes without collapsing unrelated exospore proteins.
