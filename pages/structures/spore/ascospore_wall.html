ascospore wall

GO:0005619 · SPORE · ENVELOPE_LAYER · PROPOSED

The specialized cell wall of the ascospore (spore), which is the product of meiotic division. Examples of this component are found in Fungi. [GO:0005619]

Synonyms: fungal-type spore wall

Has part: GO:0005632 GO:0005631 GO:0005630 GO:1990916

Mechanism graphs

Inner polysaccharides and outer layers build a resistant ascospore wall (ASSEMBLY)

In budding yeast, mannan- and glucan-rich inner layers are covered by a chitosan layer and a dityrosine-rich outer layer to build a multilaminar ascospore wall that surrounds the spore plasma membrane and protects the dormant spore.
