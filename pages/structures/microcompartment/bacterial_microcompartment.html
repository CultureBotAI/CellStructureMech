bacterial microcompartment

GO:0031469 · MICROCOMPARTMENT · PROTEIN_SHELLED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A bacterial protein-shelled organelle in which a selectively permeable polyhedral shell encapsulates pathway enzymes to segregate segments of metabolism from the cytosol. [GO:0031469]

Synonyms: BMC; metabolosome

Functions

  • metabolic pathway encapsulation — The protein shell spatially segregates enzyme cargo so BMCs can concentrate useful pathway intermediates or retain toxic and volatile ones.
    • DOI:10.1038/nrmicro.2018.10 Kerfeld et al. 2018 review BMCs as organelles that encapsulate enzymes for autotrophic carbon fixation and heterotrophic substrate catabolism.
  • selective metabolite permeability — Small pores through BMC-H and BMC-T shell tiles control the movement of substrates, products, cofactors, and volatile intermediates across the shell.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.str.2019.01.017 Greber et al. 2019 analyzed BMC-T pores in intact Haliangium shells and modeled them as a gated permeability route through the shell.
    • DOI:10.1038/nrmicro.2018.10 Kerfeld et al. 2018 review BMC shell proteins as selectively permeable pore-containing tiles.

BMC shell proteins close a selectively permeable enzyme compartment (FUNCTION)

BMC-H, BMC-T, and BMC-P proteins assemble into a closed polyhedral shell. The shell encapsulates locus-specific enzymes and uses small pores through its shell tiles to couple pathway sequestration with selective metabolite exchange.
