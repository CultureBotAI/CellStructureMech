retromer complex

GO:0030904 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A conserved hetero-pentameric membrane-associated complex involved in retrograde transport from endosomes to the Golgi apparatus. The budding yeast retromer comprises Vps35p, Vps29p, Vps26p, Vps5p, and Vps17p. The mammalian complex shows slight variation in composition compared to yeast, and comprises SNX1 or SNX2, SNX5 or SNX6, VPS26A or VPS26B, VPS29, and VPS35. [GO:0030904]

Synonyms: retromer; yeast retromer complex
