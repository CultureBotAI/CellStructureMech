Rad17 RFC-like complex

GO:0031389 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A pentameric protein complex related to replication factor C that loads the checkpoint clamp onto DNA at damage sites and functions in DNA damage cell-cycle checkpoints; the large subunit is Rad17 in Schizosaccharomyces pombe and Rad24p in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. [GO:0031389]

Synonyms: Rad17-RFC; Rad17-RLC; Rad24p RFC-like complex; RFC (Rad17)
