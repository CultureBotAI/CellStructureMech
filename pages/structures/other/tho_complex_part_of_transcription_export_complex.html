THO complex part of transcription export complex

GO:0000445 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

The THO complex when it is part of the TREX (TRanscription EXport) complex that is involved in coupling transcription to export of mRNAs to the cytoplasm. In S. cerevisiae, it is composed of four subunits: Hpr1, Tho2, Thp1, and Mft1, while the human complex is composed of 7 subunits. In budding yeast, the tetrameric THO core (Tho2, Hpr1, Mft1, Thp2) travels with elongating RNA polymerase II and recruits the Sub2 RNA helicase and Yra1 adaptor to form the complete TREX complex on nascent pre-mRNAs. [GO:0000445]

Synonyms: TREX-associated THO subcomplex

Part of: transcription export complex (GO:0000346)
