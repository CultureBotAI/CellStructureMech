Noc1p-Noc2p complex

GO:0030690 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A heterodimer associated with 90S and 66S preribosomes. Predominantly, but not exclusively, nucleolar; involved in ribosomal large subunit biogenesis. [GO:0030690]

Part of: 90S preribosome (GO:0030686); preribosome, large subunit precursor (GO:0030687)
