Noc1p-Noc2p complex

GO:0030690 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

Noc1p-Noc2p associates with large-subunit preribosomes (FUNCTION)

In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Noc1p and Noc2p form a heterodimeric Noc complex that associates with 90S and 66S pre-ribosomes during large ribosomal subunit maturation.
