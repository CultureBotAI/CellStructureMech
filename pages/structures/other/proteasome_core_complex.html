proteasome core complex

GO:0005839 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A barrel-shaped self-compartmentalized protease core of proteasomes, built from stacked alpha- and beta-subunit rings that sequester threonine protease active sites in an internal chamber for protein degradation. [GO:0005839]

Synonyms: 20S proteasome; 20S proteasome core particle

Part of: GO:0000502

Mechanism graphs

Stacked alpha and beta rings form a gated proteolytic barrel (FUNCTION)

Proteasome alpha subunits form the two outer rings of a gated barrel, beta subunits form the two inner proteolytic rings, and unfolded substrate entering the internal chamber is cleaved by beta-subunit threonine protease sites.
