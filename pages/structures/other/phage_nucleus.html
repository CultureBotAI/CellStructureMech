phage nucleus

cellstructuremech:phage_nucleus · Other · Protein shelled organelle · Proposed

A jumbo-bacteriophage protein-shelled compartment that assembles in infected bacterial cells, surrounds replicating phage DNA, and spatially separates phage genome replication and transcription from bacterial translation. [DOI:10.1126/science.aal2130]

Synonyms: phage nuclear shell; jumbo bacteriophage nuclear shell; nucleus-like compartment

Functions

  • phage genome compartmentalization — The phage nucleus shell encloses replicating phage DNA and separates viral replication and transcription from ribosomal translation in the infected bacterial cytoplasm.
    • DOI:10.1126/science.aal2130 Chaikeeratisak et al. 2017 found that gp105 shells surround phage DNA while excluding ribosomes.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.celrep.2017.07.064 Chaikeeratisak et al. 2017 showed that nucleus-like compartments and PhuZ spindles are conserved among large Pseudomonas phages.
  • phage DNA antidefense shielding — The chimallin shell blocks access of some bacterial DNA-targeting immune machineries to the phage genome.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41564-019-0612-5 Malone et al. 2020 showed that a phage nucleus protects jumbo-phage DNA from CRISPR-Cas DNA targeting while remaining vulnerable to type III RNA-targeting immunity.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41586-022-05013-4 Laughlin et al. 2022 linked chimallin shell architecture with selective exchange across the jumbo-phage nuclear shell.
