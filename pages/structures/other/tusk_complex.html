TUSK complex

cellstructuremech:tusk_complex · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex containing Usp7, the divergent Skp1-like protein SkpZ, and Tpr86, experimentally characterized in Trypanosoma brucei. It is distinct from cullin ubiquitin-ligase complexes and is implicated in coordinated regulation of surface-protein abundance. [DOI:10.3389/fpara.2023.1118284]

Synonyms: TUSK; TUS complex; TUS deubiquitinase complex

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Functions

  • regulation of surface-protein abundance — In the studied bloodstream-form T. brucei RNAi context, SkpZ depletion reduces Usp7 and Tpr86 levels and decreases total abundance of selected transmembrane surface/endosomal proteins while increasing selected predicted GPI-anchored protein cohorts. Whole-cell proteomics measures abundance, not surface-specific occupancy or direct deubiquitination of each affected protein.
    • DOI:10.3389/fpara.2023.1118284 Results, Table 1 and Figure 4 report qualitative cohort directions after 48-hour SkpZ RNAi; the ISG75 immunoblot corroborates reduced abundance. The Discussion explicitly allows indirect effects for GPI-linked proteins. ESAG7 is a soluble partner of GPI-anchored ESAG6, and ESAG5 anchoring is uncertain; neither is relabeled as a demonstrated GPI-anchored protein here.
