maltose transport complex

GO:1990060 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A pentameric ATP-binding cassette (ABC) importer complex (MalEFGK2) in the bacterial inner membrane and periplasm, composed of the periplasmic maltose/maltodextrin-binding protein MalE, heterodimeric transmembrane permease subunits MalF and MalG, and homodimeric ATP-binding cassette subunits MalK2. [GO:1990060]

Synonyms: maltose ABC transporter complex; maltose ATP-binding cassette transporter complex

Part of: plasma membrane (GO:0005886)

Xrefs: ComplexPortal:CPX-1932

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.
