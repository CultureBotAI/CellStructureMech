SAM complex

GO:0001401 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A large complex of the mitochondrial outer membrane that mediates sorting of some imported proteins to the outer membrane and their assembly in the membrane; functions after import of incoming proteins by the mitochondrial outer membrane translocase complex. [GO:0001401]

Synonyms: mitochondrial sorting and assembly machinery complex; TOB complex

Part of: GO:0005741

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding-yeast mitochondria contain SAM, a sorting and assembly machinery that acts after TOM in the outer-membrane protein biogenesis pathway. [DOI:10.1038/nature01753]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Yeast model in which the mitochondrial outer membrane sorting and assembly machinery was distinguished from TOM and Mas37 was shown to be a SAM-complex constituent. [DOI:10.1038/nature01753]

Functions

  • protein insertion into mitochondrial outer membrane GO:0045040 — The SAM complex acts after TOM to sort and assemble a subset of imported proteins into the mitochondrial outer membrane.
    • GO:0001401 GO:0001401 defines SAM as the mitochondrial outer-membrane machinery that sorts some imported proteins and assembles them into the membrane after TOM import.
    • GO:0045040 GO:0045040 defines insertion of proteins into the mitochondrial outer membrane by large outer-membrane translocase complexes.
    • DOI:10.1038/nature01753 Wiedemann et al. 2003 separated the SAM outer-membrane sorting and assembly step from the upstream TOM translocase step.
