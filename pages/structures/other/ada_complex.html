ADA complex

GO:0140671 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A chromatin remodeling complex that regulates transcription via acetylation primarily of nucleosomal histones H3 and H2B. In budding yeast shares the histone acetylation (HAT) module of ADA2-GCN5-NGG1-SGF29 with the related SAGA complex. [GO:0140671]

Synonyms: ADA HAT complex; ADA histone acetyltransferase complex; Ada2/Gcn5/Ada3 transcription activator complex; scADA
