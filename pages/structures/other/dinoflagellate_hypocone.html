dinoflagellate hypocone

GO:0097614 · OTHER · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The part of a dinoflagellate cell below the cingulum; also referred to as the posterior portion of a dinoflagellate cell. It is separated from the epicone by the cingulum. [GO:0097614]

Synonyms: hypocone; hyposome; hypotheca

Hypocone topology (FUNCTION)

The dinoflagellate hypocone is a cellular anatomical structure that has the dinoflagellate antapex as a part, lies posterior to the epicone, is separated from the epicone by the cingulum, and can appear bilobed when a sulcal notch extends to the antapex.
