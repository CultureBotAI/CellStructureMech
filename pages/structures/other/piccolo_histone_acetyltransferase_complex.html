piccolo histone acetyltransferase complex

GO:0032777 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A heterotrimeric H4/H2A histone acetyltransferase complex with a substrate preference of chromatin over free histones. It contains a subset of the proteins found in the larger NuA4 histone acetyltransferase complex; for example, the S. cerevisiae complex contains Esa1p, Yng2p, and Epl1p. [GO:0032777]

Synonyms: Piccolo NuA4 complex; picNuA4 complex
