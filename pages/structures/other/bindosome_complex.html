bindosome complex

GO:7770042 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A membrane-associated protein complex found in archaea that consists of sugar binding proteins assembled via a type IV pilin-like assembly system. The complex is located at the cell surface and functions in sugar uptake by facilitating the interaction between solute binding proteins and ABC transporters. The complex forms high molecular mass structures (400-600 kDa) that are integrated within or closely associated with the S-layer. [GO:7770042]

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Should BasEF or BasABC be curated as constituents of the mature bindosome complex? GO:7770042 identifies the bindosome as the assembled sugar-binding-protein complex. Zolghadr et al. showed that BasEF and BasABC are type-IV-pilin-like machinery required for functional surface localization of the sugar-binding proteins; BasEF was not required for formation of the extracted 400 and 600 kDa complexes themselves. The Bas system was therefore left out of the mature bindosome component list.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve whether SlaA or other S-layer material should be modeled as a bindosome constituent or as a neighboring envelope scaffold. The 600 kDa S. solfataricus sugar-binding-protein complex co-purified with the main S-layer protein SlaA, whereas the 400 kDa complex contained GlcS and SSO3043 and the GO definition keeps the bindosome scoped to sugar-binding proteins. This pass therefore records the S-layer association without making SlaA a source-neutral bindosome subunit.
