mitochondrial multifunctional assembly

cellstructuremech:mitochondrial_multifunctional_assembly · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A biochemically isolatable protein-lipid assembly of the mitochondrial inner membrane characterized in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. MIMAS brings together metabolite carriers, dehydrogenases, respiratory-chain biogenesis factors and phospholipid enzymes, with stability dependent on phosphatidylethanolamine production. [DOI:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.113772]

Synonyms: MIMAS

Part of: mitochondrial inner membrane (GO:0005743)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — The primary study uses YPH499- or W303-1A-derived strains and isolated mitochondria. Culture conditions vary by experiment. Methods identify a Neurospora crassa AAC construct for import assays, despite Figure 1F naming Aac2; this heterologous precursor does not establish a second canonical organism. Endogenous yeast AAC membership has separate complexome and biochemical support. [DOI:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.113772]

Functions

  • platform for respiratory-chain subunit biogenesis — MIMAS groups respiratory biogenesis factors and transiently accommodates imported Cox13. Its role as a platform preceding incorporation into complex IV is an interpretation of import/chase and Rcf1-association experiments, not direct tracking of transfer between complexes or an obligatory route for all respiratory subunits.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.113772 Figure 4D-G: precursor import/chase distinguishes transient Cox13 association from mature respiratory complexes. Rcf1 point mutants shift its distribution. The source presents broader metabolic integration as a model.
