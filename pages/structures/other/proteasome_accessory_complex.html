proteasome accessory complex

GO:0022624 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex, that caps one or both ends of the proteasome core complex and regulates entry into, or exit from, the proteasome core complex. [GO:0022624]

Part of: GO:0000502

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — COMMON: Archaeal 20S proteasome cores associate with proteasome-associated nucleotidases such as PAN to unfold and translocate substrates. [DOI:10.1042/ETLS20180025]
  • NCBITaxon:201174 Actinomycetota — VARIABLE: Actinobacterial proteasome systems include 20S cores with alternative regulators such as Mpa, Bpa, or Cpa. [DOI:10.3389/fmolb.2019.00023]
  • NCBITaxon:2759 Eukaryota — COMMON: Eukaryotic 26S proteasomes are built from 20S core particles capped by 19S regulatory particles. [DOI:10.1146/annurev-biochem-060410-150257]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:83332 Mycobacterium tuberculosis H37Rv — Actinobacterial model in which Mpa and Bpa regulator routes connect substrate recruitment to the 20S proteasome core. [DOI:10.3389/fmolb.2019.00023]

Functions

  • proteasome core access regulation — Proteasome accessory complexes cap 20S core particles and regulate substrate entry into, or product exit from, the proteolytic chamber.
    • GO:0022624 GO:0022624 defines proteasome accessory complexes as caps that regulate entry into, or exit from, the proteasome core complex.
    • DOI:10.3389/fmolb.2019.00023 Muller and Weber-Ban 2019 review bacterial regulator complexes that associate with the 20S core to recruit and internalize substrates.
