Swi5-Sfr1 complex

GO:0032798 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A conserved DNA recombinase mediator complex that contains two Swi5 monomers and one Sfr1 monomer in Schizosaccharomyces, or orthologs thereof (e.g. Sae3p and Mei5p in Saccharomyces). [GO:0032798]

Synonyms: Sae3-Mei5 complex; Swi5 complex
