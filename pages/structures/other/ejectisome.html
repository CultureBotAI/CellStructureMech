ejectisome

cellstructuremech:ejectisome · OTHER · OTHER · PROPOSED

A cryptophyte extrusive organelle that discharges proteinaceous paracrystalline ribbons or filaments built in part from small ejectisin polypeptides. [DOI:10.1007/s00709-012-0440-4]

ejectisin polypeptidesPROTEINpart ofREVIEWED_LABEL_ONLYUNKNOWNSmall, approximately 6-kDa polypeptides that make up a major component of cryptophycean ejectisomes.
  • DOI:10.1007/s00709-012-0440-4 Ammermann et al. 2013 found 6-kDa polypeptides dominating isolated and reconstituted cryptophyte ejectisome fragments, and introduced the name ejectisins for those major ejectisome components.

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:3027 Cryptophyceae — VARIABLE: Ejectisomes were isolated from Chroomonas and Cryptomonas species, and ejectisin components are described as cryptophycean; this record does not assert that every Cryptophyceae species carries ejectisomes. [DOI:10.1007/s00709-012-0440-4]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:3028 Chroomonas — Cryptophyte genus represented among the cultures used to isolate and characterize discharged ejectisomes and ejectisin-rich ejectisome filaments. [PMID:22868567]
