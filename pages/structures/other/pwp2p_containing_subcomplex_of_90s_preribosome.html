Pwp2p-containing subcomplex of 90S preribosome

GO:0034388 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex that forms a subcomplex of the 90S preribosome and can interact directly with the 5' External Transcribed Spacer (ETS) of the full length pre-rRNA transcript. In S. cerevisiae, it sediments at 25-30 S and is composed of Pwp2p, Dip2p, Utp21p, Utp13p, Utp18p, and Utp6p. [GO:0034388]

Synonyms: 25-30 S subcomplex of 90S preribosome; UTP-B complex

Part of: nucleolus (GO:0005730); 90S preribosome (GO:0030686)

Functions

  • 90S preribosome assembly GO:0034463 — The Pwp2p-containing UTP-B subcomplex supports early assembly of 90S preribosomes and can interact directly with the 5' External Transcribed Spacer of 35S pre-rRNA.
    • GO:0034388 GO:0034388 defines the Pwp2p-containing complex as a subcomplex of the 90S preribosome.
    • GO:0034463 GO:0034463 is the Gene Ontology biological-process term for 90S preribosome assembly.
    • DOI:10.1074/jbc.M404909200 Dosil and Bustelo 2004 found that Pwp2 depletion blocks formation of 90S preribosomes and prevents U3 snoRNP and other 90S particle components from associating with 35S pre-rRNA.
