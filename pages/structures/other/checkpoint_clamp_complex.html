checkpoint clamp complex

GO:0030896 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

Conserved heterotrimeric complex of PCNA-like proteins that is loaded onto DNA at sites of DNA damage. [GO:0030896]

Synonyms: CCC; Rad9-Hus1-Rad1 (9-1-1) clamp complex
