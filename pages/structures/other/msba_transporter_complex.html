MsbA transporter complex

GO:1990199 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An ATP-binding cassette (ABC) transporter complex made up of a dimer of MsbA. Facilitates the export across the plasma membrane of, amongst others, lipid A and lipopolysaccharide. In contrast to most ABC transporter complexes, each chain of the homodimer contains both the transmembrane domain (TMD) and the cytoplasmic ATP-binding domain (NBD). [GO:1990199]

Synonyms: MsbA complex; MsbA dimer
