Noc complex

GO:0030689 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

Any of several heterodimers containing one or two Noc proteins, associated with preribosomal complexes; involved in ribosome biogenesis. A family of nucleolar and nucleoplasmic heterodimeric ribosome-biogenesis factor complexes in Saccharomyces cerevisiae and other eukaryotes composed of Noc (nucleolar complex-associated) proteins, specifically the Noc1p–Noc2p complex and the Noc2p–Noc3p complex. Noc complexes associate with nascent 90S and pre-60S ribosomal particles to coordinate rRNA folding and processing with the intranuclear transport of pre-60S subunits from the nucleolus to the nucleoplasm. [GO:0030689]
