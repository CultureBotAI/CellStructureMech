ERMES complex

GO:0032865 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex that links the endoplasmic reticulum with mitochondria and may have a role in promoting exchange of calcium and phospholipids between the two organelles. [GO:0032865]

Synonyms: ER-mitochondria encounter structure; Mdm10/Mdm12/Mmm1 complex; mitochore; MMM1 complex

Part of: GO:0005741

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding yeast contains ERMES at ER-mitochondria junctions; Kornmann et al. identified the Mmm1/Mdm10/Mdm12/Mdm34 complex as an ER-mitochondria tether. [DOI:10.1126/science.1175088]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Budding-yeast model in which a synthetic tether screen identified Mmm1, Mdm10, Mdm12, and Mdm34 as an ER-mitochondria tethering complex. [DOI:10.1126/science.1175088]

Mechanism graphs

ERMES links the ER with mitochondria (FUNCTION)

ERMES localizes at ER-mitochondria junctions, links the endoplasmic reticulum with the mitochondrial outer membrane, and contains an Mmm1-Mdm12 module that can transfer lipids between membranes in vitro.
