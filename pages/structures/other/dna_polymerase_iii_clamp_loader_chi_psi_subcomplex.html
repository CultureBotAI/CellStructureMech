DNA polymerase III, clamp loader chi/psi subcomplex

GO:0043847 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A dimer composed of the chi and psi subunits which is a subassembly of the DNA polymerase III clamp loader complex and serves as a bridge between the DnaX complex and the single-stranded DNA-binding protein (SSB). [GO:0043847]

Synonyms: DNA polymerase III, DnaX complex, chi/psi subcomplex

Part of: GO:0043846
