DNA polymerase III complex

GO:0009360 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A DNA polymerase III holoenzyme complex organized into a Pol III core, a beta sliding-clamp processivity factor, and a clamp-loader complex that together support processive DNA replication. [GO:0009360]

Synonyms: DNA polymerase III holoenzyme complex
