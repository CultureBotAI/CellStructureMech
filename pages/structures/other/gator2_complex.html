GATOR2 complex

GO:0061700 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A multiprotein subcomplex of the GATOR complex that regulates TORC1 signaling by interacting with the Rag GTPase. In human, this complex consists of WDR24, WDR59, MIOS, SEH1L, and SEC13. In S. cerevisiae, this complex is referred to as SEACAT and contains the Sea2p, Sea3p, Sea4p, Seh1p, Sec13p proteins. [GO:0061700]

Synonyms: SEACAT complex

Part of: GO:0035859

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Keep the GATOR1/SEACIT GAP subcomplex outside this GATOR2/SEACAT record. GO separates GATOR2/SEACAT from GATOR1/SEACIT, and Bar-Peled et al. 2013 separated human GATOR into two subcomplexes; the Iml1/Npr2/Npr3 GAP branch therefore belongs in the distinct GATOR1 record.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Treat Seh1 and Sec13 as GATOR2/SEACAT constituent classes only in this record, despite their other complex contexts. Seh1 and Sec13 are shared beta-propeller proteins, so this record names their SEACAT role without asserting that all Seh1 or Sec13 pools are part of GATOR2.
