Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the clathrin coat of coated pit distinct from the clathrin coat of an endocytic vesicle. GO:0030132 is the AP-2-containing clathrin coat on a clathrin-coated pit, whereas GO:0030128 is the clathrin coat of an endocytic vesicle. This record therefore places GO:0030132 under the broader clathrin coat and models GO:0005905 clathrin-coated pit as the containing structure rather than treating the coated-pit and endocytic-vesicle coats as interchangeable.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve an exact source-neutral grounding for the clathrin triskelion scaffold. The coated-pit coat has an exact GO identifier and AP-2 is modeled as an exact GO subcomplex, but the clathrin triskelion is currently represented at the source-neutral label level until an exact Complex Portal or GO grounding is curated.
