DiaA complex

GO:1990125 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A homotetrameric protein complex consisting of a symmetrical pair of DiaA homodimers. Facilitates DnaA binding to the origin of replication during replication initiation. [GO:1990125]

Synonyms: DiaA homotetramer
