DNA topoisomerase IV complex

GO:0009340 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A bacterial type IIA topoisomerase complex composed of ParC and ParE subunits that can relax supercoiled DNA and supports chromosome segregation. [GO:0009340]

Synonyms: topoisomerase IV
