lipopolysaccharide transport system

GO:0062051 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein-containing complex that functions to transport lipopolysaccharide from its site of synthesis at the cytoplasmic membrane across the periplasm to the outer membrane in an ATP-dependent manner. [GO:0062051]

Synonyms: Lpt machinery; Lpt transenvelope complex

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral family groundings for LptA, LptB, LptC, LptD, LptE, LptF, and LptG in the LPS transport system. The first record uses reviewed gene-family labels from E. coli Lpt literature and avoids mapping to broad ABC transporter, periplasmic beta-jellyroll, lipoprotein, or beta-barrel signatures that would overgeneralize an individual Lpt constituent.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Should MsbA be curated as a constituent of the lipopolysaccharide transport system? MsbA flips nascent LPS across the inner membrane before the Lpt machine extracts LPS and transports it across the periplasm to the outer membrane. MsbA is therefore upstream of, rather than a constituent of, the Lpt transport system.
