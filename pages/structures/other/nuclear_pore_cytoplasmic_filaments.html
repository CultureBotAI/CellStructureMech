Functions

  • cytoplasmic mRNA export platform — The cytoplasmic-filament Nup82/Nup159 platform helps position mRNA-export machinery on the cytoplasmic face of the nuclear pore.
    • DOI:10.1534/genetics.111.127803 Aitchison and Rout 2012 review the yeast Nup82 complex in mRNA export at the cytoplasmic side of the NPC.
