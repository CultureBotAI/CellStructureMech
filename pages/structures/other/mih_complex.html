MIH complex

GO:0120155 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A trimeric complex involved in cytokinesis. Proposed to bridge actomyosin ring contraction and septum synthesis in yeast, resulting in the coordination of these processes, and leading to plasma membrane ingression and fusion. In the yeast Saccharomyces cerevisiae this complex consists of Mlc1p, Iqg1p and Hof1p proteins. [GO:0120155]

Synonyms: Mlc1p-Iqg1p-Hof1p complex

Part of: cellular bud neck (GO:0005935)
