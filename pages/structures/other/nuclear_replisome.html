nuclear replisome

GO:0043601 — nuclear replisome · Other · Nucleoprotein complex · Proposed

A multi-component enzymatic machine at the nuclear replication fork, which mediates DNA replication. Includes DNA primase, one or more DNA polymerases, DNA helicases, and other proteins. The complete multiprotein DNA replication machinery assembled at eukaryotic nuclear replication forks in yeasts, fungi, and protists. Centered on the replicative CMG helicase (Cdc45–Mcm2-7–GINS), the nuclear replisome integrates leading-strand DNA polymerase epsilon, lagging-strand DNA polymerase alpha-primase and delta tethered via Ctf4, the PCNA sliding clamp and RFC clamp loader, RPA, and the fork protection complex (Mrc1–Tof1–Csm3) to coordinate high-fidelity chromosome duplication. [GO:0043601 — nuclear replisome]

Part of: nuclear replication fork (GO:0043596)

Has part: alpha DNA polymerase:primase complex (GO:0005658); DNA replication factor A complex (GO:0005662); nuclear DNA replication factor C complex (GO:0043599); delta DNA polymerase complex (GO:0043625)
