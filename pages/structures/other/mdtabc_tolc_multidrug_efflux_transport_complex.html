MdtABC-TolC multidrug efflux transport complex

cellstructuremech:mdtabc_tolc_multidrug_efflux_transport_complex · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An Escherichia coli Resistance-Nodulation-Division-family cell-envelope efflux pump complex composed of MdtA, MdtB, MdtC, and TolC subunits that exports novobiocin, bile salts, quinolones, fosfomycin, detergents, myricetin, zinc, and other substrates from the cell. [ComplexPortal:CPX-2119]
