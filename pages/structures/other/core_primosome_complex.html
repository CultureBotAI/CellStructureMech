core primosome complex

GO:1990098 · OTHER · NUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein-DNA complex containing at least one DNA helicase and one primase. Can also contain associated proteins. The helicase component continues to unwind the double-stranded DNA (dsDNA) and the primase component synthesizes a RNA primer during initiation or restart of replication. [GO:1990098]

Synonyms: core primosome
