phagophore assembly site

GO:0000407 · OTHER · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

Punctate structures proximal to the endoplasmic reticulum which are the sites where the Atg machinery assembles upon autophagy induction. [GO:0000407]

Synonyms: PAS; pre-autophagosomal structure

Part of: GO:0005737

Functions

  • autophagosome assembly platform — The phagophore assembly site concentrates Atg machinery at a punctate cytoplasmic site to organize phagophore expansion and autophagosome formation.
    • GO:0000045 GO:0000045 defines autophagosome assembly as the formation of a double-membrane-bounded autophagosome.
    • DOI:10.1093/emboj/20.21.5971 Suzuki et al. 2001 showed that PAS organization by concerted APG gene functions is essential for autophagosome formation in yeast.
