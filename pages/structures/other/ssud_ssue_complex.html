SsuD-SsuE complex

GO:1990200 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex containing an alkanesulfonate monooxygenase subunit (SsuD tetramer in E. coli) and a flavin oxidoreductase subunit (SsuE dimer in E. coli). Involved in the utilization of alkanesulfonates as sulfur sources under conditions of sulfate or cysteine starvation. [GO:1990200]

Synonyms: two-component alkanesulfonate monooxygenase system

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve an exact source-neutral family grounding for SsuD alkanesulfonate monooxygenase. The first record represents SsuD by its reviewed E. coli gene name and avoids mapping it to InterPro:IPR050172 because that family also covers RutA monooxygenases.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Should the SsuD-SsuE complex assert a fixed six-subunit stoichiometry? No. The first biochemical study characterized isolated SsuD as a homotetramer and isolated SsuE as a homodimer, while the later interaction study supported a stable SsuD-SsuE complex and proposed a 1:1 monomeric SsuE:SsuD association. This record leaves the assembled complex subunit count unmodeled.
