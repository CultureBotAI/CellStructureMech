nuclear pore

GO:0005643 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A membrane-embedded eukaryotic protein complex that forms a discrete pore in the nuclear envelope where the inner and outer nuclear membranes join and mediates exchange between the nucleus and cytoplasm. [GO:0005643]

Synonyms: nuclear pore complex; NPC

Part of: nuclear envelope (GO:0005635)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Functions

  • nucleocytoplasmic transport — The nuclear pore complex forms the gated channel for macromolecular transport between the nucleus and the cytoplasm.
    • GO:0005643 GO:0005643 defines the nuclear pore as the protein complex that provides an opening in the nuclear envelope.
    • DOI:10.1534/genetics.111.127803 Aitchison and Rout 2012 review yeast NPC organization and transport through it.
