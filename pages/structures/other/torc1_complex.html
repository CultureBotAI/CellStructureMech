TORC1 complex

GO:0031931 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex that contains at least TOR (target of rapamycin) and Raptor (regulatory-associated protein of TOR), or orthologs of, in complex with other signaling components. Mediates the phosphorylation and activation of S6K. In Saccharomyces, the complex contains Kog1p, Lst8p, Tco89p, and either Tor1p or Tor2p. [GO:0031931]

Synonyms: TORC1; TOR complex 1; rapamycin and nutrient-sensitive TOR complex; nutrient sensitive complex
