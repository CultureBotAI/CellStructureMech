cell surface furrow

GO:0097610 · Other · Subcellular region · Proposed

A furrow that may be found on the cell surface. Examples include the cingulum and sulcus found in some dinoflagellates. A persistent invaginated groove or trench in the cortical cell surface of single-celled microbial eukaryotes—including the equatorial cingulum and ventral sulcus furrows of dinoflagellates, as well as the ventral feeding/flagellar grooves of excavates, cryptophytes, and euglenids. Supported by underlying cortical alveoli, epiplastids, and microtubular ribbons, the cell surface furrow channels flagellar beating waves and directs hydrodynamic flow for propulsion and prey capture. [GO:0097610]
