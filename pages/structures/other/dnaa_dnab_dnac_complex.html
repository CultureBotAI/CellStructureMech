DnaA-DnaB-DnaC complex

GO:1990157 · Other · Nucleoprotein complex · Proposed

A protein-DNA complex consisting of the helicase loading complex DnaB-DnaC bound to the DNA-bound DNA replication initiation protein DnaA. Essential for DNA replication initiation. [GO:1990157]
