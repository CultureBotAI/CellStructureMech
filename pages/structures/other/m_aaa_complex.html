Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve separate exact whole-protein family identities for Yta10-type and Yta12-type subunits. Native reviewed UniProt and InterPro checks resolve shared ATP-dependent Zinc Metalloprotease IPR050928 and broader FtsH IPR005936 families; IPR011546 is a domain. These do not distinguish the Yta10 and Yta12 paralog classes, so no broad family or domain is asserted as exact identity.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Distinguish the m-AAA protease from its substrate and the larger prohibitin-protease association. The same 2026 study combines yeast reporter genetics, C. thermophilum heterologous purification, predicted interfaces and human in situ imaging. Treating all as one endogenous yeast structure would conflate species, preparations and entity boundaries.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve native yeast architecture and context-specific stoichiometry from fully read primary structural evidence. EMD-1712 represents a cross-linked detergent sample with C6 symmetry and reported 3:3 subunit counts. Complex Portal leaves copy counts unspecified and lists 1000 kDa, whereas EMDB lists 0.5 MDa for its sample. These source annotations are not reconciled as one physiological mass or fixed family-wide stoichiometry.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Test endogenous substrate processing and the causal route from protease regulation to respiratory peroxide sensitivity. The 2026 processing assay uses a TEF-driven C-terminal Strep reporter and endpoint precursor fractions. Domain replacements can affect more than one molecular property, and growth is a distinct assay. Neither reporter changes nor growth phenotypes alone prove assembly failure, general proteome hyperdegradation or a direct MrpL32-to-stress mechanism.
