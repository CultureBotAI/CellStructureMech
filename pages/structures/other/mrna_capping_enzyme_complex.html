mRNA capping enzyme complex

GO:0031533 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex that consists of an RNA 5' triphosphatase and a guanyl transferase (Cet1p and Ceg1p in S. cerevisiae; Pct1 and Ceg1 in S. pombe) and is involved in mRNA capping. [GO:0031533]
