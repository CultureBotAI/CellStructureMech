AP-1 adaptor complex

GO:0030121 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A heterotetrameric AP-type membrane coat adaptor complex that consists of beta1, gamma, mu1 and sigma1 subunits and links clathrin to the membrane surface of a vesicle; vesicles with AP-1-containing coats are normally found primarily in the trans-Golgi network. In at least humans, the AP-1 complex can be heterogeneric due to the existence of multiple subunit isoforms encoded by different genes (gamma1 and gamma2, mu1A and mu1B, and sigma1A, sigma1B and sigma1C). [GO:0030121]

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent AP-1 as a TGN coat adaptor, not as the complete TGN clathrin coat. GO:0030121 is a subtype of GO:0030131 clathrin adaptor complex and part of GO:0030130 clathrin coat of trans-Golgi network vesicle. This record therefore represents the AP-1 adaptor heterotetramer and links it to the containing TGN clathrin coat through part_of.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Exclude Mil1 from core AP-1 subunits. Whitfield et al. 2016 identify Mil1 as a conserved regulatory protein that binds the Apm2 alternate mu subunit and contributes to membrane recruitment. That supports a regulatory interaction, not stable beta1/gamma/mu1/sigma1 membership in the core AP-1 heterotetramer.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral groundings for the AP-1 beta1, gamma, mu1 and sigma1 subunit classes. GO gives the exact AP-1 complex identity and names its subunit classes, but exact source-neutral InterPro, Pfam or NCBIfam CURIEs for those AP-1-specific classes were not curated in this pass.
