AP-2 adaptor complex

GO:0030122 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A heterotetrameric AP-type membrane coat adaptor complex that consists of alpha, beta2, mu2 and sigma2 subunits, and links clathrin to the membrane surface of a vesicle, and the cargo receptors during receptor/clathrin mediated endocytosis. Vesicles with AP-2-containing coats are normally found primarily near the plasma membrane, on endocytic vesicles. In at least humans, the AP-2 complex can be heterogeneric due to the existence of multiple subunit isoforms encoded by different alpha genes (alphaA and alphaC). [GO:0030122]

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent AP-2 as an adaptor complex within endocytic clathrin coats, not as a complete clathrin coat. GO:0030122 is a clathrin adaptor complex and plasma membrane protein complex that is part of GO:0030128 clathrin coat of endocytic vesicle and GO:0030132 clathrin coat of coated pit. This record therefore represents the AP-2 adaptor heterotetramer and links it to those containing coats through part_of.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent alphaA and alphaC as isoforms of one AP-2 alpha subunit class. The GO definition notes that human AP-2 can be heterogeneric because alphaA and alphaC isoforms are encoded by different genes. The taxon-agnostic component list therefore keeps one AP-2 alpha subunit class rather than making human paralogs separate core component classes.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral groundings for the AP-2 alpha, beta2, mu2 and sigma2 subunit classes. GO gives the exact AP-2 complex identity and names its subunit classes, but exact source-neutral InterPro, Pfam or NCBIfam CURIEs for those AP-2-specific classes were not curated in this pass.
