CAF-1 complex

GO:0033186 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A conserved heterotrimeric protein complex that promotes histone H3 and H4 deposition onto newly synthesized DNA during replication or DNA repair; specifically facilitates replication-dependent nucleosome assembly with the major histone H3 (H3.1). In many species the CAF-1 subunits are designated p150, p60, and p48. [GO:0033186]

Synonyms: chromatin assembly factor 1 complex; chromatin assembly factor-I; CAF-I
