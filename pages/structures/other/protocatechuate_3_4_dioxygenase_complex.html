protocatechuate 3,4-dioxygenase complex

GO:7770085 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex which is capable of protocatechuate 3,4-dioxygenase activity. It consists of an alpha subunit (PcaG) and a beta subunit (PcaH), with the non-heme iron active site formed at the alpha-beta interface. [GO:7770085]

Synonyms: PcaGH complex; PcaHG complex; protocatechuate 3,4-oxygenase complex
