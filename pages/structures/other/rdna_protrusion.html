rDNA protrusion

GO:0030875 · Other · Other · Proposed

Any of the tandem arrays of rDNA localized at the periphery of the nucleus and protruding into the nucleolus, and associated proteins. May be visible as a single or double spot by DAPI staining. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae and Schizosaccharomyces pombe, the rDNA protrusion comprises the tandemly repeated 35S/37S ribosomal DNA array on chromosome XII or III extending from the nuclear chromatin mass into the crescent-shaped nucleolus, bound by Fob1, Sir2/RENT, Net1, Hmo1, and RNA polymerase I. [GO:0030875]

Synonyms: nucleolar rDNA loop

Part of: nucleolar chromatin (GO:0030874)
