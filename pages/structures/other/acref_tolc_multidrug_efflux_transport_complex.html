AcrEF-TolC multidrug efflux transport complex

cellstructuremech:acref_tolc_multidrug_efflux_transport_complex · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An Escherichia coli Resistance-Nodulation-Division-family cell-envelope efflux pump complex composed of AcrE, AcrF, and TolC subunits that exports acriflavines, indoles, and other xenobiotic substrates across the cell envelope. [ComplexPortal:CPX-4265]
