ESCRT complex

GO:0036452 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An endosomal sorting complex involved in membrane fission processes related to sorting of multivesicular bodies (MVB) in the endocytic pathway, cytokinesis and viral budding among other processes. [GO:0036452]

Synonyms: endosomal sorting complex required for transport

Part of: GO:0005768
