UBC13-MMS2 complex

GO:0031372 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A heterodimeric ubiquitin conjugating enzyme complex that catalyzes assembly of K63-linked polyubiquitin chains. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the complex comprises Ubc13p and Mms2p; in human it comprises UBE2N and UBE2V1/UBE2V2; and in plants UBC35/UBC36 and UEV1A/UEV1B/UEV1C/UEV1D-4. [GO:0031372]

Synonyms: BEN-UEV1A complex; UBE2N/UBE2NL complex; UBE2V1/UBE2V2 complex; Uev1A-ben complex; ubc-13-uev-1 complex

Part of: GO:0005737 — cytoplasm (no local record)

Ubc13-Mms2 Heterodimer Assembly and K63-Linked Polyubiquitination in DNA Repair (FUNCTION)

The noncatalytic UEV protein Mms2p forms a specific heterodimer with the catalytic E2 enzyme Ubc13p to constitute the UBC13-MMS2 complex, which executes ubiquitin-conjugating activity to assemble K63-linked polyubiquitin chains for error-free postreplication DNA repair.
