RENT complex

GO:0030869 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex that mediates transcriptional silencing at the rDNA locus (the name derives from regulator of nucleolar silencing and telophase). In Saccharomyces the complex contains Net1p, Sir2p, Cdc14p, and at least one more subunit. [GO:0030869]

Part of: nucleolus (GO:0005730)

Xrefs: ComplexPortal:CPX-1669

    • PMID:11511359 Shou et al. 2001 dissected the Net1p domain that binds and inhibits Cdc14p until mitotic exit.

Mechanism graphs

Net1p scaffolds Sir2p and Cdc14p in the nucleolar RENT complex for rDNA silencing and mitotic exit control (ASSEMBLY)

In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Net1p binds Sir2p and Cdc14p to assemble the nucleolar RENT complex, recruiting Sir2p to mediate rDNA heterochromatin formation while sequestering Cdc14p to regulate exit from mitosis.
