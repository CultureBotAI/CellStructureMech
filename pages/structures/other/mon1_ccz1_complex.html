Mon1-Ccz1 complex

GO:0035658 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex that functions as a guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) and converts Rab-GDP to Rab-GTP. In S. cerevisiae, this complex consists of at least Mon1 and Ccz1, and serves as a GEF for the Rab Ypt7p. [GO:0035658]

Part of: late endosome (GO:0005770)

Xrefs: ComplexPortal:CPX-1674
