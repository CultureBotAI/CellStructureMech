nucleotide-excision repair factor 4 complex

GO:0000113 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

One of several protein complexes involved in nucleotide-excision repair; possesses DNA damage recognition and DNA-dependent ATPase activities. In S. cerevisiae, it is composed of Rad7p and Rad16p. [GO:0000113]

Synonyms: NEF4 complex
