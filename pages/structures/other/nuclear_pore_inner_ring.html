nuclear pore inner ring

GO:0044611 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The first inner-ring record intentionally models only the large Saccharomyces scaffold Nups. Li et al. 2022 also place Nic96 and the Nup57/Nup49/Nsp1 central-channel complex in the near-atomic inner-ring model, so those linker and FG Nups need a separate boundary pass before they are added or explicitly assigned to adjacent nuclear-pore records.
