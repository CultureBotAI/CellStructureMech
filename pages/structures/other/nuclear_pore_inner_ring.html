nuclear pore inner ring

GO:0044611 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A ring-shaped eukaryotic nuclear pore subcomplex built from inner-ring scaffold nucleoporins around the central transport channel. [GO:0044611]

Synonyms: inner ring complex

Part of: nuclear pore (GO:0005643)

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral protein-family groundings for large inner-ring nucleoporin classes. The first inner-ring record leaves these Nup classes reviewed-label only because inner-ring paralogs and ortholog names vary between budding yeast, other fungi, and metazoans; exact InterPro, Pfam, or NCBIfam accessions should be resolved family-by-family.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve whether linker nucleoporins and the central-channel nucleoporin complex should be modeled as components of the nuclear pore inner ring or left to neighboring NPC records. The first inner-ring record intentionally models only the large Saccharomyces scaffold Nups. Li et al. 2022 also place Nic96 and the Nup57/Nup49/Nsp1 central-channel complex in the near-atomic inner-ring model, so those linker and FG Nups need a separate boundary pass before they are added or explicitly assigned to adjacent nuclear-pore records.
