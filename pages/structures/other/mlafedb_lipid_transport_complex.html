MlaFEDB lipid transport complex

cellstructuremech:mlafedb_lipid_transport_complex · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An Escherichia coli inner-membrane ABC transporter complex composed of MlaE, MlaF, MlaD, and MlaB subunits that participates in phospholipid transport during maintenance of outer-membrane lipid asymmetry. [ComplexPortal:CPX-3464]

Synonyms: MlaFEDB complex
