nucleosome

GO:0000786 · Other · Nucleoprotein complex · Proposed

A complex comprised of DNA wound around a multisubunit core and associated proteins, which forms the primary packing unit of DNA into higher order structures. The fundamental repeating nucleoprotein packaging unit of eukaryotic chromatin in yeasts, filamentous fungi, microalgae, and protists, consisting of ~147 base pairs of chromosomal DNA wrapped in 1.65 left-handed superhelical turns around a histone octamer composed of a central (H3–H4)2 tetramer flanked by two H2A–H2B dimers. Nucleosome positioning and post-translational histone modifications govern DNA accessibility for transcription, replication, and repair. [GO:0000786]

Synonyms: cytoplasmic nucleosome; nuclear nucleosome

Part of: GO:0000785 — chromatin (no local record)

Mechanism graphs

Nucleosome assembly, DNA wrapping, and chromatin compaction (ASSEMBLY)

Histone chaperones deposit an (H3–H4)2 tetramer and two H2A–H2B dimers onto ~147 bp of chromosomal DNA to form the canonical nucleosome, compacting genomic DNA and establishing the substrate for epigenetic regulation.
