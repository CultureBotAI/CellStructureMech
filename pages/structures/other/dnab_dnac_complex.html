DnaB-DnaC complex

GO:1990100 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex containing homohexameric DNA helicase DnaB, and the DNA helicase loader DnaC. The helicase loader DnaC delivers DnaB to the chromosomal origin (oriC). [GO:1990100]

Synonyms: DnaB6-DnaC3 complex; DnaB6-DnaC6 complex; helicase-loading complex
