TIM22 mitochondrial import inner membrane insertion complex

GO:0042721 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A multi-subunit mitochondrial inner-membrane complex that mediates insertion of multi-pass inner-membrane precursor proteins carrying internal targeting elements. [GO:0042721]

Synonyms: mitochondrial inner membrane protein insertion complex; Tim22 complex; mitochondrial protein translocase complex
