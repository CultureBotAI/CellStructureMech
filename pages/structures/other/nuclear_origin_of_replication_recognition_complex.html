nuclear origin of replication recognition complex

GO:0005664 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A multisubunit complex that is located at the replication origins of a chromosome in the nucleus. [GO:0005664]

Synonyms: eukaryotic ORC; nuclear ORC

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2759 Eukaryota — COMMON: The ORC protein-family review reports that Orc1-Orc5 appear to be present in all eukaryotes and that Orc6 is poorly conserved outside budding yeast. [PMID:19344485]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Bell and Stillman originally purified the origin recognition complex from budding yeast and assayed origin-specific DNA binding in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. [PMID:1579162]
