TRAPP complex

GO:0030008 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A large complex that acts as a tethering factor involved in transporting vesicles from the ER through the Golgi to the plasma membrane. A TRAPP (transport protein particle) complex has a core set of proteins which are joined by specific subunits depending on the cellular component where a given TRAPP complex is active. The conserved eukaryotic Transport Protein Particle (TRAPP) family of multisubunit vesicle-tethering and Rab guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) complexes in yeasts, fungi, and protists. Built around a shared heptameric core (Bet3p dimer, Bet5p, Trs20p, Trs23p, Trs31p, Trs33p) that forms the catalytic GEF site, TRAPP complexes (TRAPPI, TRAPPII, and TRAPPIII) activate Ypt1/Rab1 and Ypt31/32 GTPases to coordinate ER-to-Golgi transport, intra-Golgi trafficking, and autophagy. [GO:0030008]

Synonyms: transport protein particle; transport protein particle complex; TRAPP1; TRAPP2
