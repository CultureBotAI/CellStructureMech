MIB complex

GO:0140275 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A mitochondrial intermembrane-space-spanning assembly that couples inner-membrane MICOS to outer-membrane SAM and includes a conserved DNAJC11-related protein and metaxin 1. [GO:0140275]

Synonyms: mitochondrial intermembrane space bridging complex; mitofilin complex

Part of: mitochondrial inner membrane (GO:0005743)

Has part: MICOS complex (GO:0061617); SAM complex (GO:0001401)

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Does MIB duplicate MICOS, SAM or MIMAS? MIB denotes the larger bridging assembly; its existing MICOS and SAM records denote subcomplexes. The GO synonym mitofilin complex is retained without treating every mitofilin-containing assembly as MIB.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact DNAJC11-related and metaxin family mappings and strain-matched protein examples. The two component rows describe subcomplexes, not an exhaustive protein inventory. Separate metaxin representation must not double-count a SAM constituent. No individual family or reference-strain accession is asserted before its scope is verified.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Should Mmc1 be encoded as an obligatory MIB constituent? Mmc1 associates with MICOS, but the source separates its activity from MICOS assembly. Association alone does not establish an obligatory MIB subunit.
  • CONTROVERSY (OPEN): Resolve differing mitofilin C-terminal truncation results before adding a domain-level yeast bridging mechanism. Two budding-yeast studies report opposite changes in SAM co-isolation after C-terminal truncation. The fission-yeast example does not resolve that experimental disagreement.
