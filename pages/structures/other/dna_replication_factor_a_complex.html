DNA replication factor A complex

GO:0005662 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A conserved heterotrimeric eukaryotic single-stranded-DNA-binding complex, also known as replication protein A, that supports DNA replication, repair and recombination. [GO:0005662]

Synonyms: replication protein A; RPA
