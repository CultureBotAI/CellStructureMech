mating projection tip polarisome

GO:0031563 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

Protein complex that has a role in determining cell polarity, found at the tip of the mating projection in unicellular fungi exposed to mating pheromone. [GO:0031563]

Part of: mating projection tip (GO:0043332)

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral family groundings for Spa2/Pea2, Bni1, and Bud6 at the mating projection tip. This localized record deliberately reuses the reviewed-label-only Saccharomyces polarisome cohorts from the broader polarisome record instead of guessing InterPro, Pfam, NCBIfam, or UniProtKB family groundings for proteins whose broader homologous families do not exactly delimit the mating-projection-tip complex.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep Cdc42 regulators and Fus1/Fus2 outside the mating-projection-tip polarisome component boundary. Bidlingmaier and Snyder 2004 assayed Cdc24, Bem3, Fus1, and Fus2 together with polarisome mutants during mating-projection cycling, but the exact GO:0031563 structure denotes the localized polarisome complex rather than every Cdc42 regulator or cell-fusion protein that affects projection initiation or termination.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Retain Bud6/Aip3 as a constituent without asserting a periodic mating-projection phenotype edge. GO:0000133 and the broader polarisome record include Bud6/Aip3 in the canonical Saccharomyces polarisome boundary. Bidlingmaier and Snyder 2004 tested bud6 deletion in the same pheromone-induced projection assay but reported that it did not affect projection frequency or morphology, so Bud6 is retained as a constituent and left out of the first phenotype graph until a precise localized Bud6 edge is curated.
