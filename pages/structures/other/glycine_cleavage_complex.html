glycine cleavage complex

GO:0005960 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A glycine-cleavage multienzyme protein complex that reversibly oxidizes glycine through L-, P-, H-, and T-protein components. [GO:0005960]

Synonyms: glycine cleavage system; glycine decarboxylase complex; glycine dehydrogenase (decarboxylating) complex; glycine synthase complex
