Tic complex

GO:0031897 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A chloroplast inner-envelope protein translocon complex that forms the inner-membrane step of nuclear-encoded preprotein import into the chloroplast. [GO:0031897]

Synonyms: TIC complex

Part of: GO:0009706

Taxonomic distribution
