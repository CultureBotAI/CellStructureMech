oral apparatus

GO:0031912 · OTHER · OTHER · PROPOSED

Complex basket- or funnel-like structure used by the cell to collect food and channel it to the cytostome; includes specialized sub-structures made up of closely-spaced cilia and underlying basal bodies and fibrillar systems. [GO:0031912]
