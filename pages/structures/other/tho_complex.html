THO complex

GO:0000347 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A eukaryotic nuclear protein complex that promotes transcription elongation and can act as part of the transcription export complex to couple RNA polymerase II transcription with mRNA export from the nucleus. [GO:0000347]

Functions

  • DNA-templated transcription elongation GO:0006354 — The THO complex promotes transcription elongation, including transcription of repeat-containing genes.
    • GO:0000347 GO:0000347 defines the THO complex as required for transcription elongation through genes containing tandem DNA repeats.
    • GO:0006354 GO:0006354 is the Gene Ontology biological-process term for DNA-templated transcription elongation.
    • PMID:16983072 Voynov et al. 2006 reported that genes with internal repeats require the THO complex for transcription.
  • mRNA export from nucleus GO:0006406 — The THO complex acts within the transcription export complex that couples transcription to mRNA export.
    • GO:0000347 GO:0000347 describes THO as part of the TREX complex that couples transcription to mRNA export to the cytoplasm.
    • GO:0006406 GO:0006406 is the Gene Ontology biological-process term for mRNA export from nucleus.
    • PMID:11979277 Strasser et al. 2002 identified TREX as a conserved complex that couples transcription with mRNA export.
