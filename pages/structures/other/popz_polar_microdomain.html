PopZ polar microdomain

cellstructuremech:popz_polar_microdomain · OTHER · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

A proteinaceous bacterial polar microdomain formed by PopZ polymers at Caulobacter cell poles that anchors the ParB-bound chromosomal origin and organizes pole-specific client proteins. [DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2008.07.015]

Synonyms: Caulobacter PopZ polar subdomain

  • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2008.07.015 Bowman et al. 2008 identified PopZ as a polymeric polar protein that anchors the ParB-bound chromosomal origin complex.
  • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2008.07.016 Ebersbach et al. 2008 independently characterized PopZ as a self-associating polar protein needed for polar organization in Caulobacter.

Functions

  • chromosomal-origin anchoring — The PopZ polar microdomain anchors the ParB-bound chromosomal origin region at the Caulobacter cell pole.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2008.07.015 Bowman et al. 2008 connected PopZ polymers to anchoring of the chromosomal origin/ParB complex at a bacterial cell pole.
  • pole-specific client-protein organization — The PopZ polar microdomain serves as a hub for multiple client proteins that contribute to sequential changes in Caulobacter pole composition.
    • DOI:10.1111/j.1365-2958.2010.07088.x Bowman et al. 2010 showed that the PopZ polar subdomain organizes sequential changes in pole composition and function.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.1602380113 Holmes et al. 2016 identified PopZ as an intrinsically disordered hub for organizing bacterial cell poles.
