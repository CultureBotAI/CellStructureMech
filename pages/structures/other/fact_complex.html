FACT complex

GO:0035101 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A histone chaperone complex that facilitates nucleosome disassembly and reassembly upon DNA or RNA polymerase passage. [GO:0035101]

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Do not model Nhp6 as a core FACT complex constituent. Formosa et al. 2001 reported that Spt16/Cdc68 and Pob3 form a yeast heterodimer and that this heterodimer combines with Nhp6 to form the nucleosome-binding factor SPN, supporting Nhp6 as a DNA-binding cofactor for the yeast SPN activity rather than one of the two FACT core constituents.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral family groundings for the FACT complex constituents. The curated FACT component labels are yeast gene-name-based classes. This pass verified the gene symbols and the Spt16-Pob3 composition but did not verify exact InterPro, Pfam, NCBIfam or UniProt family CURIEs that should be reused across fungi.
