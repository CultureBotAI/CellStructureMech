bacterial pre-replicative complex

GO:0036389 · OTHER · NUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein-DNA complex that forms at the bacterial oriC during the initial step of DNA replication and allows the origin to become competent, or 'licensed', for replication. [GO:0036389]

Synonyms: bacterial pre-RC
