Ssh1 translocon complex

GO:0071261 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An endoplasmic-reticulum translocon complex with a core heterotrimer of alpha, beta, and gamma subunits, represented in budding yeast by Ssh1p, Sbh2p, and Sss1p, that supports cotranslational protein transport across the ER membrane. [GO:0071261]

Synonyms: Ssh1p-Sss1p-Sbh2p complex; Ssh1p complex

Part of: GO:0005789

Functions

  • Ssh1-dependent cotranslational ER protein translocation — The Ssh1 translocon supports cotranslational translocation of a subset of signal-sequence-bearing substrates across the endoplasmic-reticulum membrane.
    • GO:0071261 GO:0071261 defines Ssh1 as a translocon complex involved in the cotranslational pathway of protein transport across the ER membrane.
    • PMID:8612571 Finke et al. 1996 discovered the Ssh1p complex in the Saccharomyces cerevisiae ER membrane and proposed that, unlike Sec61, it functions exclusively in cotranslational protein transport.
    • DOI:10.1091/mbc.01-10-0518 Wittke et al. 2002 showed that Ssh1p physically approaches the SRP receptor beta subunit in vivo and recognizes a subset of ER signal sequences.
