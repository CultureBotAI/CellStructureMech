COG complex

GO:0017119 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A multisubunit tethering complex of the CATCHR family (complexes associated with tethering containing helical rods) that has a role in tethering vesicles to the Golgi prior to fusion. Composed of 8 subunits COG1-8. [GO:0017119]

Synonyms: conserved oligomeric Golgi complex; Golgi transport complex; Sec34/35 complex
