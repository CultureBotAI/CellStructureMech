hyphal tip

GO:0001411 — hyphal tip · Other · Subcellular region · Proposed

The end, or tip, of a fungal hypha, where polarized growth occurs during hyphal elongation. The dome-shaped, rapidly expanding apical region of a fungal or oomycete hypha where polarized exocytosis, cell wall biosynthesis, and cytoskeletal remodeling are concentrated. In filamentous fungi and dimorphic yeasts such as Candida albicans and Ashbya gossypii, the hyphal tip houses the Spitzenkörper and polarisome complex that nucleate actin cables and focus secretory vesicle fusion. [GO:0001411 — hyphal tip]

Has part: spitzenkorper (GO:0031521); hyphal tip polarisome (GO:0031562)
