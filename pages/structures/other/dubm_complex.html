DUBm complex

GO:0071819 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex that forms part of SAGA-type complexes SAGA and SLIK, and mediates deubiquitination of histone H2B. In S. cerevisiae, the DUBm consists of the proteins Ubp8p, Sgf11p, Sus1p, and Sgf73p. [GO:0071819]

Synonyms: deubiquitinating module; deubiquitination module; SAGA DUBm complex

Part of: SAGA complex (GO:0000124); SLIK (SAGA-like) complex (GO:0046695)
