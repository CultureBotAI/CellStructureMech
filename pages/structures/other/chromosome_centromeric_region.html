chromosome, centromeric region

GO:0000775 — chromosome, centromeric region · Other · Nucleoprotein complex · Proposed

The region of a chromosome that includes the centromeric DNA and associated proteins. In mitosis or meiosis II, this region connects the chromosome to spindle microtubules via the kinetochore. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Schizosaccharomyces pombe, and other microbial eukaryotes, the centromeric region comprises specialized CenH3 (Cse4/Cnp1) centromeric nucleosomes, point- or regional-centromere DNA-binding factors (such as the CBF3 complex), pericentromeric cohesin/heterochromatin, and the assembled inner and outer kinetochore complexes. [GO:0000775 — chromosome, centromeric region]

Synonyms: centromere; chromosomal centromeric region

Part of: GO:0005694 — chromosome (no local record)

Has part: kinetochore (GO:0000776); CBF3 complex (GO:0031518)

Centromeric DNA specification, Cse4 chromatin assembly, and kinetochore incorporation in the chromosome, centromeric region (ASSEMBLY)

Centromeric DNA (SO:0000577) specifies assembly of the Cse4/CENP-A centromeric nucleosome platform, which recruits inner (COMA, MIS12/MIND) and outer (Ndc80) kinetochore subcomplexes to form the chromosome, centromeric region (GO:0000775) and mediate chromosome segregation (GO:0007059).
