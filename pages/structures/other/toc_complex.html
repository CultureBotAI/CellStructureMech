Toc complex

GO:0010006 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A chloroplast outer-envelope protein translocon complex that forms the outer-membrane step of nuclear-encoded preprotein import into the chloroplast. [GO:0010006]

Synonyms: TOC complex
