Hda-beta clamp complex

GO:1990085 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex involved in inactivating the function of DnaA and thereby preventing multiple events of replication initiation. In E. coli, this complex is composed of the beta clamp (DnaN) and Hda. [GO:1990085]

Synonyms: Hda-DnaN complex; Hda-dpo3b complex

Has part: GO:0044775
