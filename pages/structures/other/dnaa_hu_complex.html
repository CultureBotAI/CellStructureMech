DnaA-HU complex

GO:1990103 · OTHER · NUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein-DNA complex containing DNA-bound DnaA attached to HU. HU is a dimer encoded by two closely related genes. Essential for the initiation of replication in bacteria; stimulates the DnaA-dependent unwinding of oriC. [GO:1990103]

Synonyms: DnaA-HU-DNA complex
