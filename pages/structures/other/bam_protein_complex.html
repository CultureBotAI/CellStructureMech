Bam protein complex

GO:1990063 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A bacterial outer-membrane beta-barrel protein assembly machine in which the BamA beta-barrel core and associated outer-membrane lipoproteins fold and insert beta-barrel proteins into the outer membrane. [DOI:10.1146/annurev-biochem-061408-144611]

Synonyms: BAM complex; beta-barrel assembly machinery complex; OMP complex

BAM folds and inserts beta-barrel OMP substrates (FUNCTION)

The outer-membrane BamA beta barrel and associated BamB-E lipoproteins form a machine that receives unfolded beta-barrel substrates and catalyzes their folding and insertion into the outer membrane.
