endoplasmic reticulum Sec complex

GO:0031205 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An endoplasmic reticulum membrane-associated complex involved in the translocation of proteins that are targeted to the ER. In yeast, this complex consists of two subcomplexes, namely, the Sec61 complex and the Sec62/Sec63 complex. [GO:0031205]

Functions

  • posttranslational ER protein import — The ER Sec complex brings the Sec61 protein-conducting channel and the Sec62/Sec63 subcomplex together at the ER membrane to support Kar2/BiP-coupled posttranslational import of substrate proteins into the endoplasmic reticulum.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Should individual Sec61p, Sbh1p, Sss1p, Sec62p, Sec63p, Sec71p, or Sec72p subunits be duplicated as direct ER Sec complex components? GO:0031205 is the parent ER Sec complex made from Sec61 and Sec62/Sec63 subcomplexes in yeast. The individual Sec61p/Sbh1p/Sss1p and Sec62p/Sec63p/Sec71p/Sec72p proteins belong on those lower-level subcomplex records, so this parent record uses has_part links to avoid duplicating the same components at two hierarchy levels.
