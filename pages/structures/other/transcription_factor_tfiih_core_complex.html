transcription factor TFIIH core complex

GO:0000439 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The 7 subunit core of TFIIH that is a part of either the general transcription factor holo-TFIIH or the nucleotide-excision repair factor 3 complex. In S. cerevisiae/humans the complex is composed of: Ssl2/XPB, Tfb1/p62, Tfb2/p52, Ssl1/p44, Tfb4/p34, Tfb5/p8 and Rad3/XPD. [GO:0000439]

Synonyms: SSL2-core TFIIH complex; core TFIIH complex
