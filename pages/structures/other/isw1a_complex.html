Isw1a complex

GO:0036436 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An Isw1 complex that binds DNA and has nucleosome-stimulated ATPase activity. In S. cerevisiae, contains an Isw1p ATPase subunit in complex with Ioc3p. [GO:0036436]

Synonyms: ISW1a
