Rpd3S complex

GO:0032221 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A eukaryotically conserved histone deacetylase complex which deacetylates histones across gene coding regions. Composed of a catalytic histone deacetylase subunit, a chromodomain protein, a SIN3 family co-repressor, and a WD repeat protein (Clr6p, Alp13p, Pst2p, and Prw1p respectively in Schizosaccharomyces; Rpd3p, Sin3p, Ume1p, Rco1p and Eaf3 in Saccharomyces; homologs thereof in other species). [GO:0032221]

Synonyms: Clr6-CII complex; Clr6 histone deacetylase complex II; Clr6 histone deacetylase complex II'; Clr6S complex; Rpd3C(S); Rpd3S; Sin3S

Part of: GO:0000228 GO:0000785
