transcription factor TFIIK complex

GO:0070985 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A transcription factor complex that forms part of the holo TFIIH complex. In Saccharomyces/human, TFIIK contains Ccl1p/Cyclin H, Tfb3p/MAT1 and Kin28p/CDK7. [GO:0070985]

Synonyms: TFIIK complex; cyclin H-CDK7 complex; Mcs6/Mcs2/Pmh1 complex
