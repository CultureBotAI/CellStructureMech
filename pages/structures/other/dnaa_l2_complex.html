DnaA-L2 complex

GO:1990082 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex that inhibits unwinding of DNA at the origin of replication and assembly of the pre-primosome. In E. coli, this complex is composed of DnaA and of the ribosomal protein L2. [GO:1990082]
