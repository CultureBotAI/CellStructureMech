origin recognition complex

GO:0000808 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A multisubunit complex that is located at the replication origins of a chromosome. The conserved hexameric AAA+ ATPase protein complex (Orc1–Orc6) that binds specifically to chromosomal replication origins (such as autonomously replicating sequences in Saccharomyces cerevisiae or AT-rich origin tracts in Schizosaccharomyces pombe) throughout the cell cycle. In G1 phase, the origin recognition complex recruits Cdc6 and Cdt1 to load head-to-head double hexamers of the Mcm2–7 replicative helicase onto DNA. [GO:0000808]

Synonyms: ORC; origin of replication recognition complex

Part of: GO:0005694 — chromosome (no local record)
