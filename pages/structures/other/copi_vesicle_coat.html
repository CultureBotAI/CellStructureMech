COPI vesicle coat

GO:0030126 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A COPI membrane vesicle coat complex found associated with Golgi membranes; it forms on COPI-coated vesicles that mediate Golgi-to-endoplasmic-reticulum retrograde transport. [GO:0030126]

Synonyms: coatomer

Part of: COPI-coated vesicle (GO:0030137)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.
