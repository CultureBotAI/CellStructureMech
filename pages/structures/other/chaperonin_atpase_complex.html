chaperonin ATPase complex

GO:0016465 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A multimeric ATPase complex involved in protein folding. It has a symmetric ring-shaped structure and consists of multiple identical ~60kDa subunits and, in the bacterial complex, several smaller subunits; a central cavity receives unfolded peptides and promotes their folding into the native conformation. [GO:0016465]

Part of: GO:0005829 — cytosol (no local record)
