dinoflagellate apical groove

GO:0097685 · OTHER · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

A cell surface furrow (or groove) found on a dinoflagellate apex. It typically loops around the apex. [GO:0097685]

Synonyms: apical groove

Part of: GO:0097613

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2864 Dinophyceae — VARIABLE: GO:0097685 places this apical cell-surface furrow in Dinophyceae; the apical groove is a named morphology of dinoflagellate apices. [GO:0097685]
  • NCBITaxon:77300 Margalefidinium polykrikoides — COMMON: Cochlodinium polykrikoides, the species examined in the 2010 electron-microscopy study, has a U-shaped apical groove at the dorsal side of the epicone. [DOI:10.1111/j.1550-7408.2010.00491.x]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:77300 Margalefidinium polykrikoides — Dinophycean model described as Cochlodinium polykrikoides in the electron-microscopy study that characterized the apical groove and flagellar apparatus. [DOI:10.1111/j.1550-7408.2010.00491.x]
