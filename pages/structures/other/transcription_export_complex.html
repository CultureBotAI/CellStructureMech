transcription export complex

GO:0000346 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

The transcription export (TREX) complex couples transcription elongation by RNA polymerase II to mRNA export. The complex associates with the polymerase and travels with it along the length of the transcribed gene. TREX is composed of the THO transcription elongation complex as well as other proteins that couple THO to mRNA export proteins. The TREX complex is known to be found in a wide range of eukaryotes, including S. cerevisiae and metazoans. The conserved eukaryotic transcription-export (TREX) multiprotein complex that couples RNA polymerase II transcription elongation with nascent mRNP packaging and nuclear export. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae and other microbial eukaryotes, the TREX complex is assembled from the pentameric THO core complex (Tho2, Hpr1, Mft1, Thp2, Tex1), the DEAD-box RNA helicase Sub2 (UAP56), and the RNA-binding export adaptor Yra1 (ALYREF), which recruits the Mex67–Mtr2 export receptor. [GO:0000346]

Synonyms: TREX complex
