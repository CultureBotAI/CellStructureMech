glutamate synthase complex

GO:0031026 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A complex that possesses glutamate synthase activity. The iron-sulfur flavoprotein glutamine:2-oxoglutarate amidotransferase (GOGAT) enzyme complex that catalyzes the reductive transfer of the amide amino group from L-glutamine to 2-oxoglutarate to yield two molecules of L-glutamate. Operating alongside glutamine synthetase in the high-affinity GS-GOGAT ammonia assimilation pathway across bacteria, archaea, yeasts, and microalgae, the glutamate synthase complex couples an amidotransferase site with FMN and [3Fe-4S]/[4Fe-4S] clusters through an intramolecular ammonia tunnel. [GO:0031026]
