HDA1 complex

GO:0070823 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A tetrameric histone deacetylase complex that contains a Class II deacetylase catalytic subunit. In S. cerevisiae it is composed of two Hda1p subunits along with Hda2p and Hda3p. [GO:0070823]
