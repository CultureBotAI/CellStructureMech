cellular bud

GO:0005933 · Other · Other · Proposed — Drafted from literature to fill a coverage gap; not yet reviewed. How records are made

A cellular outgrowth formed during budding reproduction that remains connected to a mother cell while it grows into a new individual. [GO:0005933]

Synonyms: bud; growing bud

Has part: cellular bud membrane (GO:0033101); cellular bud neck (GO:0005935); cellular bud tip (GO:0005934)

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0027 — Bud

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding yeast forms mother-attached cellular buds during mitotic polarized growth before cytokinesis separates mother and daughter. [DOI:10.3389/fcell.2016.00123]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Budding-yeast model in which Pea2 and Spa2 polarity proteins localize to polarized-growth sites during budding. [DOI:10.1083/jcb.135.3.725]

Functions

  • budding growth — The cellular bud is the daughter-cell outgrowth that expands by polarized growth before cytokinesis separates it from the mother yeast cell.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.135.3.725 Valtz and Herskowitz 1996 studied bipolar and mating-projection growth through Saccharomyces polarity proteins that localize to budding sites.
    • DOI:10.3389/fcell.2016.00123 Glomb and Gronemeyer 2016 review the mother-bud neck as the cortical cytokinesis site organized during budding-yeast growth.

Mechanism graphs

Cellular bud topology (FUNCTION)

The yeast cellular bud has a surrounding bud membrane, a distal bud tip, and a bud neck that constricts the connection between the mother and daughter cell.
