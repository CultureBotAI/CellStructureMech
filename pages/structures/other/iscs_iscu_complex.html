IscS-IscU complex

GO:1990330 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A heterotetrameric protein complex involved in the sulfur transfer during iron-sulfur cluster assembly and in the modification of tRNA wobble positions. In E. coli it consists of a central IscS dimer with the IscU protomers attached to one of the IscS units each via a disulfide (-SSH) group. [GO:1990330]

IscS-IscU Complex Assembly, Persulfide Transfer, and Fe-S Cluster Biogenesis (FUNCTION)

The cysteine desulfurase IscS dimer preferentially binds the disordered conformation of apo-IscU to form the IscS-IscU complex, catalyzing Cys328-mediated persulfide sulfur transfer to drive iron-sulfur cluster assembly and tRNA wobble uridine modification.
