FMN reductase complex

GO:1990202 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex capable of FMN reductase activity. Reduces FMN to FMNH2 in a NAD(P)H-dependent manner. In E.coli, consists of a SsuE dimer. [GO:1990202]

Synonyms: FMN reductase complex, NAD(P)H-dependent; SsuE complex
