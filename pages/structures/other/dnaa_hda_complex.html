DnaA-Hda complex

GO:1990083 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex that inactivates the function of DnaA by inhibiting the phosphorylation of DnaA-ADP to DnaA-ATP and thereby preventing multiple events of replication initiation. In E. coli, this complex is composed of DnaA and Hda. [GO:1990083]
