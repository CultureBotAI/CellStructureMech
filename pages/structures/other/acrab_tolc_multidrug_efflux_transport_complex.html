AcrAB-TolC multidrug efflux transport complex

cellstructuremech:acrab_tolc_multidrug_efflux_transport_complex · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An Escherichia coli tripartite multidrug efflux pump complex composed of AcrB inner-membrane RND transporters, AcrA periplasmic adaptor proteins, and the TolC outer-membrane channel that exports xenobiotics across the cell envelope. [ComplexPortal:CPX-4263]
