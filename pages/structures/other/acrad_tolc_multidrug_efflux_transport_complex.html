AcrAD-TolC multidrug efflux transport complex

cellstructuremech:acrad_tolc_multidrug_efflux_transport_complex · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An Escherichia coli RND-family cell-envelope efflux pump complex composed of AcrA, AcrD, and TolC subunits that exports aminoglycosides and other xenobiotic substrates across the cell envelope. [ComplexPortal:CPX-4264]
