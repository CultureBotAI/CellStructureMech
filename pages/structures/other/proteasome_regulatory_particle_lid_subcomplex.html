proteasome regulatory particle, lid subcomplex

GO:0008541 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

The subcomplex of the proteasome regulatory particle that forms the peripheral lid, which is added on top of the base subcomplex. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae and other microbial eukaryotes, the nine-subunit 19S lid subcomplex comprises the PCI-domain scaffolds Rpn3, Rpn5, Rpn6, Rpn7, Rpn9, and Rpn12, the MPN-domain heterodimer Rpn8-Rpn11, and Sem1, positioning the Zn2+-dependent deubiquitinase Rpn11 directly above the Rpt ATPase pore to cleave ubiquitin chains en bloc during translocation. [GO:0008541]

Synonyms: 19S proteasome lid subcomplex

Part of: proteasome regulatory particle (GO:0005838 — proteasome regulatory particle)
