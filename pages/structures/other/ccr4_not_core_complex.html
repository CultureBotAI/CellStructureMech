CCR4-NOT core complex

GO:0030015 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

The core of the CCR4-NOT complex. In Saccharomyces the CCR4-NOT core complex comprises Ccr4p, Caf1p, Caf40p, Caf130p, Not1p, Not2p, Not3p, Not4p, and Not5p. [GO:0030015]

Synonyms: 1.0 MDa CCR4-NOT complex

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Functions

  • poly(A)-specific ribonuclease activity GO:0004535 — CCR4-NOT supplies the major Ccr4/Caf1-dependent cytoplasmic poly(A)-specific exonuclease activity in budding yeast.
    • GO:0004535 GO:0004535 is the Gene Ontology molecular-function term for poly(A)-specific ribonuclease activity.
    • DOI:10.1016/s0092-8674(01)00225-2 Tucker et al. 2001 showed that Ccr4 and Caf1 are critical components of the major Saccharomyces cerevisiae cytoplasmic mRNA deadenylase.
  • nuclear-transcribed mRNA catabolic process, deadenylation-dependent decay GO:0000288 — CCR4-NOT participates in deadenylation-dependent mRNA decay by shortening cytoplasmic mRNA poly(A) tails.
    • GO:0000288 GO:0000288 is the Gene Ontology biological-process term for deadenylation-dependent decay of nuclear-transcribed mRNAs.
    • DOI:10.1016/s0092-8674(01)00225-2 Tucker et al. 2001 showed that Ccr4 and Caf1 are required for normal mRNA deadenylation in vivo.

CCR4-NOT supports yeast cytoplasmic mRNA deadenylation (FUNCTION)

In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the nine-subunit 1.0 MDa CCR4-NOT core complex carries Ccr4/Caf1-dependent mRNA deadenylase activity that shortens poly(A) tails.
