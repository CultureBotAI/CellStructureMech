ribulose bisphosphate carboxylase complex

GO:0048492 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A complex containing either both large and small subunits or just small subunits which carries out the activity of producing 3-phosphoglycerate from carbon dioxide and ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate. The multi-subunit ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate carboxylase/oxygenase (RuBisCO) enzyme complex that catalyzes the primary carbon-fixation step of the Calvin-Benson-Bassham cycle by carboxylating ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate with CO2 to yield two molecules of 3-phosphoglycerate. In cyanobacteria, purple bacteria, chemoautotrophic bacteria, and microalgae, Form I RuBisCO assembles as a hexadecameric L8S8 complex (eight catalytic RbcL/CbbL large subunits and eight RbcS/CbbS small subunits) often concentrated within bacterial carboxysomes or algal pyrenoids. [GO:0048492]

Synonyms: RubisCO complex
