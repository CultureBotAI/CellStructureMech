nucleolar chromatin

GO:0030874 · Other · Nucleoprotein complex · Proposed

The portion of nuclear chromatin associated with the nucleolus; includes the DNA encoding the ribosomal RNA. The specialized chromosomal chromatin domain located within the nucleolus of yeasts, filamentous fungi, and protists, encompassing the tandemly repeated ribosomal DNA (rDNA) array (such as the ~150 rDNA repeats on chromosome XII of Saccharomyces cerevisiae). Nucleolar chromatin alternates between open, Hmo1-bound, nucleosome-depleted repeats actively transcribed by RNA polymerase I and silent, nucleosome-packed repeats maintained by the RENT (Net1–Cdc14–Sir2) histone deacetylase complex to suppress illegitimate rDNA recombination. [GO:0030874]

Part of: nuclear chromosome (GO:0000228); nucleolus (GO:0005730)

Has part: rDNA protrusion (GO:0030875)
