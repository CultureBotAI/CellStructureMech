Atg12-Atg5-Atg16 complex

GO:0034274 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A heterohexameric ubiquitin-like E3 ligase complex localized to the phagophore assembly site (pre-autophagosomal structure, PAS) in yeast and other eukaryotes, formed by two copies of the covalent Atg12-Atg5 conjugate bound to a homodimer of coiled-coil Atg16; stimulates Atg3-mediated conjugation of Atg8 to phosphatidylethanolamine (PE) and scaffolds membrane shaping during autophagosome biogenesis. [GO:0034274]

Part of: phagophore assembly site (GO:0000407)

Xrefs: ComplexPortal:CPX-1849
