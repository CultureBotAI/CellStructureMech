ribonuclease H2 complex

GO:0032299 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex that possesses ribonuclease H activity, in which the catalytic subunit is a member of the RNase H2 (or HII) class. For example, in Saccharomyces the complex contains Rnh201p, Rnh202p and Rnh203p. [GO:0032299]

Synonyms: RNase H2 complex

Functions

  • RNA-DNA hybrid ribonuclease activity GO:0004523 — The ribonuclease H2 complex catalyzes endonucleolytic cleavage of RNA in RNA-DNA hybrids.
