AP-4 adaptor complex

GO:0030124 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An AP-type membrane coat adaptor complex that consists of beta4, epsilon, mu4 and sigma4 subunits and is found associated with membranes in the trans-Golgi network; it is not clear whether AP-4 forms clathrin coats in vivo. [GO:0030124]

Functions

  • AP-4-associated trans-Golgi network cargo sorting — AP-4 is a trans-Golgi-network-associated adaptor complex implicated in recognition and sorting of cargo proteins with tyrosine-based motifs.
    • DOI:10.1091/mbc.10.8.2787 Hirst et al. 1999 identified AP-4, localized it to nonclathrin-coated vesicles near the trans-Golgi network, and reported that its mu4 subunit specifically interacts with a tyrosine-based sorting signal.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Avoid modeling AP-4 as a constitutive clathrin-coat adaptor. GO:0030124 makes AP-4 a child of AP-type membrane coat adaptor complex but explicitly notes that it is unclear whether AP-4 forms clathrin coats in vivo. This record therefore keeps AP-4 under the AP-type parent while avoiding a universal AP-4-to-clathrin-coat edge or a clathrin-coat part_of assertion.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral groundings for the AP-4 beta4, epsilon, mu4 and sigma4 subunit classes. GO gives the exact AP-4 complex identity and names its subunit classes, but exact source-neutral InterPro, Pfam or NCBIfam CURIEs for those AP-4-specific classes were not curated in this pass.
