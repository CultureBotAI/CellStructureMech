Swr1 complex

GO:0000812 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A multisubunit INO80-type chromatin-remodeling complex that catalyzes ATP-dependent exchange of nucleosomal H2A-H2B dimers for H2AZ-H2B dimers. [DOI:10.1126/science.1090701]

Synonyms: SWR-C; SWR1 complex

Part of: nuclear chromosome (GO:0000228)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Functions

  • ATP-dependent H2AZ histone chaperone activity GO:0140849 — The SWR1 complex catalyzes ATP-dependent replacement of canonical H2A-H2B dimers with H2AZ-H2B dimers in nucleosomes.

    • DOI:10.1126/science.1090701 Mizuguchi et al. 2004 biochemically characterized ATP-driven H2AZ exchange by the budding-yeast SWR1 complex.
  • chromatin remodeling GO:0006338 — The Swr1 complex is an INO80-type chromatin-remodeling complex that deposits H2AZ into chromatin.
    • GO:0006338 GO:0006338 is the Gene Ontology biological-process term for chromatin remodeling.
    • GO:0000812 GO:0000812 defines the Swr1 complex as a multisubunit protein complex involved in chromatin remodeling.
