cytostome

GO:0031910 · OTHER · OTHER · PROPOSED

Stable, specialized structure for the ingestion of food by the cell into phagosomes. [GO:0031910]

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:5693 Trypanosoma cruzi — VARIABLE: The cytostome-cytopharynx complex has been structurally characterized in T. cruzi epimastigotes. [DOI:10.1242/jcs.135491]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5693 Trypanosoma cruzi — Kinetoplastid parasite whose epimastigote cytostome-cytopharynx complex has been reconstructed in three dimensions. [DOI:10.1242/jcs.135491]

Functions

  • endocytic cargo uptake — The T. cruzi epimastigote cytostome admits extracellular macromolecular cargo into the endocytic route that passes through an early endosomal network before reservosome storage.
    • DOI:10.1078/0171-9335-00112 Porto-Carreiro et al. 2000 traced endocytic cargo entering through the cytostome and later reaching reservosomes in T. cruzi epimastigotes.
