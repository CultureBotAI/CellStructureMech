nuclear chromosome

GO:0000228 · OTHER · NUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A chromosome that encodes the nuclear genome and is found in the nucleus of a eukaryotic cell during the cell cycle phases when the nucleus is intact. [GO:0000228]

Synonyms: nuclear interphase chromosome
