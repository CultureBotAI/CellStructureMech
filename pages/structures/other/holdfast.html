holdfast

cellstructuremech:holdfast · OTHER · OTHER · PROPOSED

A polar extracellular adhesive matrix of Caulobacter vibrioides composed of secreted holdfast polysaccharide that is anchored at the old cell pole or stalk tip and enables irreversible surface attachment. [DOI:10.1128/JB.00112-19]

Synonyms: Caulobacter holdfast; holdfast adhesin

Functions

  • irreversible surface adhesion — The holdfast glues the old pole or stalk tip to abiotic surfaces during the Caulobacter surface-attachment program.
    • DOI:10.1128/mBio.00294-17 Sprecher et al. 2017 studied the cohesive properties that make the Caulobacter holdfast an adhesive surface organelle.
  • polar holdfast anchoring — Hfa anchoring proteins retain the secreted holdfast matrix at the old cell pole or stalk tip instead of allowing it to be released into the medium.
    • DOI:10.1128/JB.00112-19 Sulkowski et al. 2019 show that Hfa proteins form a multiprotein anchoring complex for old-pole holdfast retention.
