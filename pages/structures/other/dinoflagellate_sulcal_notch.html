dinoflagellate sulcal notch

GO:0097618 · OTHER · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

A dinoflagellate sulcus that extends all the way to the posterior end of the cell (also known as antapex). The presence of a sulcal notch makes the dinoflagellate hypocone appear bilobed. [GO:0097618]

Synonyms: dinoflagellate sulcus notch; sulcal notch

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2864 Dinophyceae — VARIABLE: GO:0097618 defines the sulcal notch as a dinoflagellate sulcus that reaches the posterior antapex and can make the hypocone appear bilobed. [GO:0097618]
