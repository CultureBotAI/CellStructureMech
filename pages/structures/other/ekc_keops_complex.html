EKC/KEOPS complex

GO:0000408 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A multiprotein complex conserved across Archaea and Eukarya (composed of Kae1, Bud32, Cgi121, Pcc1, and in fungi/metazoa Gon7) that catalyzes the biosynthesis of the essential N(6)-L-threonylcarbamoyladenine (t6A) modification at position 37 of ANN-decoding tRNAs and supports telomere maintenance and transcription. [GO:0000408]

Synonyms: KEOPS/EKC complex; endopeptidase-like kinase chromatin-associated protein complex; kinase, putative endopeptidase and other proteins of small size protein complex

Part of: GO:0005737 — cytoplasm (no local record)

Xrefs: ComplexPortal:CPX-995
