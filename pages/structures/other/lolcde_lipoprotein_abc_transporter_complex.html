LolCDE lipoprotein ABC transporter complex

cellstructuremech:lolcde_lipoprotein_abc_transporter_complex · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An Escherichia coli inner-membrane ABC transporter complex composed of one LolC transmembrane subunit, two LolD ATP-binding subunits, and one LolE transmembrane subunit that uses LolD ATP hydrolysis to release outer membrane-specific lipoproteins from the inner membrane for periplasmic trafficking. [ComplexPortal:CPX-4262]

Synonyms: LolCDE complex
