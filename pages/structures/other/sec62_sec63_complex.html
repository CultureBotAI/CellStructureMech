Sec62/Sec63 complex

GO:0031207 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An endoplasmic-reticulum protein complex that supports posttranslational targeting of proteins to the ER and is represented in yeast by a Sec62p, Sec63p, Sec71p, and Sec72p tetramer. [GO:0031207]

Synonyms: ER protein translocation subcomplex; Sec62/63 complex; Sec63 complex

Part of: endoplasmic reticulum Sec complex (GO:0031205); endoplasmic reticulum membrane (GO:0005789)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

GO exactly grounds the Sec62/Sec63 complex and its yeast Sec62p, Sec63p, Sec71p, and Sec72p constituents, but exact source-neutral family CURIEs were not curated in this pass.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Should Sec61-channel subunits or Kar2/BiP be included as Sec62/Sec63 complex components? GO:0031207 denotes the Sec62p/Sec63p/Sec71p/Sec72p subcomplex. The Sec61 heterotrimer combines with it in the broader ER Sec complex, and Kar2/BiP powers posttranslational import from the lumen, but neither is part of the Sec62/Sec63 tetramer.
