NatA complex

GO:0031415 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A conserved complex that catalyzes the transfer of an acetyl group to an N-terminal Ser, Ala, Gly, or Thr residue of a protein acceptor molecule. In Saccharomyces the complex includes Nat1p and Ard1p, and may contain additional proteins. [GO:0031415]

Synonyms: N-terminal acetyltransferase A complex
