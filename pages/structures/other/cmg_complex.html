CMG complex

GO:0071162 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex that contains the GINS complex, Cdc45p, and the heterohexameric MCM complex, and that is involved in unwinding DNA during replication. [GO:0071162]

Synonyms: unwindosome
