1,3-beta-D-glucan synthase complex

GO:0000148 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex that catalyzes the transfer of a glucose group from UDP-glucose to a (1->3)-beta-D-glucan chain. [GO:0000148]

Synonyms: 1,3-beta-glucan synthase complex; (1->3)-beta-glucan synthase complex
