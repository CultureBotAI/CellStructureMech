outer kinetochore

GO:0000940 · Other · Subcellular region · Proposed

The region of a kinetochore most external to centromeric DNA; this outer region mediates kinetochore-microtubule interactions. [GO:0000940]

Synonyms: condensed chromosome outer kinetochore; condensed nuclear chromosome outer kinetochore; outer kinetochore of condensed chromosome; outer kinetochore of condensed nuclear chromosome; outer kinetochore plate

Part of: kinetochore (GO:0000776)

Has part: MIS12/MIND type complex (GO:0000444); Ndc80 complex (GO:0031262)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep individual MIS12/MIND and Ndc80 subunits, lineage-specific outer kinetochore complexes, inner kinetochore assemblies, and checkpoint regulators outside the generic outer kinetochore component list. The generic outer kinetochore is represented by conserved, named subcomplexes; individual protein families are curated inside those subcomplex records, lineage-restricted outer kinetochore complexes stay in their own records, and nonconstituent checkpoint factors should be reviewed separately.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve the complete outer kinetochore subcomplex census across yeasts and vertebrates. This record links the GO-exact MIS12/MIND and Ndc80 records that are already curated, but it does not claim that they exhaust the outer kinetochore in every lineage.
