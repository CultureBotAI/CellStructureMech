outer kinetochore

GO:0000940 · OTHER · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The region of a kinetochore most external to centromeric DNA; this outer region mediates kinetochore-microtubule interactions. [GO:0000940]

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep individual MIS12/MIND, Ndc80, and DASH subunits, inner kinetochore assemblies, and checkpoint regulators outside the outer kinetochore component list. The outer kinetochore is a region assembled from named subcomplexes; its individual protein families are curated inside those subcomplex records, and nonconstituent checkpoint factors should be reviewed separately.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve the complete outer kinetochore subcomplex census across yeasts and vertebrates. This record links the GO-exact MIS12/MIND, Ndc80, and DASH records that are already curated, but it does not claim that they exhaust the outer kinetochore in every lineage.
