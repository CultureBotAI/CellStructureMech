replisome

GO:0030894 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A multi-component enzymatic machine at the replication fork which mediates DNA replication. Includes DNA primase, one or more DNA polymerases, DNA helicases, and other proteins. Across Bacteria, Archaea, and microbial eukaryotes, the replisome couples replicative DNA helicase unwinding (DnaB, MCM, or CMG) with RNA primer synthesis, clamp-loader-mediated sliding clamp assembly (beta clamp or PCNA), and high-processivity leading- and lagging-strand DNA polymerases. [GO:0030894]

Synonyms: DNA replisome complex

Part of: replication fork (GO:0005657 — replication fork)
