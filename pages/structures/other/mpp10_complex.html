Mpp10 complex

GO:0034457 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex that forms a subcomplex of the 90S preribosome. In S. cerevisiae, it is composed of Mpp10p, Imp3p and Imp4p. [GO:0034457]
