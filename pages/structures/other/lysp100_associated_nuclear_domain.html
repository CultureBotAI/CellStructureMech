LYSP100-associated nuclear domain

GO:0016606 · OTHER · OTHER · PROPOSED

A nuclear body that is enriched in the lymphoid cell-specific protein LYSp100B; LANDs are globular, electron-dense structures and are morphologically distinct from the annular structures characteristic of PML bodies. [GO:0016606]

Synonyms: LANDs

Part of: GO:0031981

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:9606 Homo sapiens — VARIABLE: LANDs were defined around human lymphoid-cell LYSP100, with HeLa transfection used to show that LYSP100B can generate LAND-like nuclear dots. [PMID:8695863]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:9606 Homo sapiens — Human LYSP100/LYSp100B was cloned and localized to globular, electron-dense LAND nuclear domains distinct from PML nuclear bodies. [PMID:8695863]

Functions

  • Bright-dependent immunoglobulin heavy-chain enhancer regulation — LYSp100B/Sp140 can interact with the Bright/ARID3A matrix-attachment region-binding transcription factor and is redistributed from LANDs while stimulating Bright transactivation through the immunoglobulin heavy-chain intronic enhancer.
    • DOI:10.1093/emboj/19.15.4123 Zong, Das and Tucker 2000 connected LYSp100B, Bright and LAND redistribution with matrix-attachment-region-dependent IgH enhancer transcription in lymphoid cells.
