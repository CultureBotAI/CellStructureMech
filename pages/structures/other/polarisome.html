polarisome

GO:0000133 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A fungal cell-polarity protein complex that localizes to sites of polarized growth and directs localized actin-filament assembly, including the Saccharomyces Bni1/Spa2/Pea2/Bud6 polarisome. [GO:0000133]
