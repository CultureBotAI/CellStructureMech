ESCRT II complex

GO:0000814 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An endosomal sorting complex required for transport and functions downstream of ESCRT I complex. It consists of the class E vacuolar protein sorting (Vps) proteins and is required for the membrane recruitment of ESCRT III complex and binds to ubiquitinated cargoes. [GO:0000814]

Synonyms: ESCRT-II complex; endosomal sorting complex required for transport

Part of: endosome membrane (GO:0010008)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.
