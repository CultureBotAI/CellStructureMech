RSC-type complex

GO:0016586 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A SWI/SNF-type complex that contains a bromodomain containing-protein, such as yeast Rsc1 or Rsc4 or mammalian PB1/BAF180. The RSC complex is generally recruited to RNA polymerase III promoters and is specifically recruited to RNA polymerase II promoters by transcriptional activators and repressors; it is also involved in non-homologous end joining. [GO:0016586]

Synonyms: PBAF complex; Polybromo- and BAF containing complex; SWI/SNF complex B

Mechanism graphs

RSC supports ATP-dependent chromatin remodeling (FUNCTION)

In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the Sth1 ATPase and accessory RSC subunits assemble into an ATP-dependent remodeler that engages nucleosomes through the core and Arp-module lobes.
