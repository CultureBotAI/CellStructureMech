Ino80 complex

GO:0031011 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A multisubunit protein complex that contains the Ino80p ATPase; exhibits chromatin remodeling activity. [GO:0031011]

Synonyms: INO80 chromatin remodeling complex; INO80-C; Ino80.com

Part of: nuclear chromosome (GO:0000228)
