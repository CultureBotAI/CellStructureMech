exomer complex

GO:0034044 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex that acts as a selective cargo adaptor mediating the selective transport of specific membrane proteins from the trans-Golgi network (TGN) to the plasma membrane. [GO:0034044]

Synonyms: Chs5/6 complex

Chs5 and ChAP subunits assemble exomer for TGN export (ASSEMBLY)

In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the Chs5 scaffold assembles with Chs6-family ChAP cargo-binding subunits to form exomer, which supports selected export from the trans-Golgi network to the plasma membrane.
