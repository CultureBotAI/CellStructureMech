CORVET complex

GO:0033263 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A multimeric protein complex that acts as an endosomal tethering complex (CORVET = class C core vacuole/endosome tethering) by cooperating with Rab GTPases to capture endosomal vesicles and trap them prior to the action of SNAREs; the complex is involved in endo-lysosomal biogenesis and required for transport between endosome and vacuole. The Saccharomyces cerevisiae complex contains Vps8p, Vps3p, Pep5p, Vps16p, Pep3p, and Vps33p. [GO:0033263]

Synonyms: class C core vacuole/endosome tethering complex

CORVET supports yeast endosomal tethering (FUNCTION)

In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Vps3 and Vps8 join Vps11, Vps16, Vps18 and Vps33 to form the CORVET complex, an endosomal tethering complex that interacts with Rab5 homolog Vps21 during endo-lysosomal biogenesis.
