Set1C/COMPASS complex

GO:0048188 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A conserved protein complex that catalyzes methylation of histone H3. In Saccharomyces the complex contains Shg1p, Sdc1p, Swd1p, Swd2p, Swd3p, Spp1p, Bre2p, and the trithorax-related Set1p; in mammals it contains the catalytic subunit (SETD1A or SETD1B), WDR5, WDR82, RBBP5, ASH2L/ASH2, CXXC1/CFP1, HCFC1 and DPY30. [GO:0048188]

Synonyms: COMPASS complex; Set1C; Set1/COMPASS complex
