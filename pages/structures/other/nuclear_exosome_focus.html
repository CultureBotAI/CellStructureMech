nuclear exosome focus

GO:1990251 · Other · Other · Proposed

An nuclear body involved in nuclear mRNA surveilllance. Contains at least Mmi1, or an ortholog of it, and the nuclear exosome. [GO:1990251]

Synonyms: Mmi1 nuclear focus

Part of: nuclear lumen (GO:0031981)
