proteasome regulatory particle, base subcomplex

GO:0008540 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

The subcomplex of the proteasome regulatory particle that directly associates with the proteasome core complex. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae and other microbial eukaryotes, the 19S base subcomplex consists of the heterohexameric AAA+ ATPase motor ring (Rpt1-Rpt6) together with the large scaffolding subunits Rpn1 and Rpn2 and the ubiquitin receptors Rpn10 and Rpn13, which capture ubiquitinated substrates, mechanically unfold them, and open the alpha-ring gate of the 20S core particle. [GO:0008540]

Synonyms: 19S proteasome base subcomplex

Part of: proteasome regulatory particle (GO:0005838 — proteasome regulatory particle)
