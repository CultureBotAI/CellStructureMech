PotABCD spermidine ABC transporter complex

cellstructuremech:potabcd_spermidine_abc_transporter_complex · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An Escherichia coli PotABCD ATP-binding cassette importer composed of two PotA ATP-binding subunits, one PotB permease, one PotC permease, and one PotD periplasmic substrate-binding protein that imports spermidine and can also import putrescine. [ComplexPortal:CPX-4383]

Synonyms: Spermidine ABC transporter complex; Spermidine-preferential ABC transporter complex; potABCD complex
