chaperonin-containing T-complex

GO:0005832 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A multisubunit ring-shaped complex that mediates protein folding in the cytosol without a cofactor. [GO:0005832]

Synonyms: TRiC; TCP-1 ring complex; CCT complex

Part of: GO:0005829 — cytosol (no local record)
