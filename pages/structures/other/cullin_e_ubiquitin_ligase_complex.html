Cullin-E ubiquitin ligase complex

cellstructuremech:cullin_e_ubiquitin_ligase_complex · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A cullin-RING protein complex defined by the Cullin-E scaffold, experimentally characterized in Trypanosoma brucei by co-isolation with Rbx1, Skp1.2 and kelch-domain proteins. The associated kelch proteins are candidate client-recognition subunits; simultaneous occupancy and fixed stoichiometry have not been established. [DOI:10.1038/s41598-025-32077-9]

Functions

  • coordination of organelle replication — Cullin-E RNAi in the studied T. brucei cells produces an increasing fraction with one kinetoplast and two nuclei (1K2N); imaged 1K2N cells also retain a single Golgi apparatus. This supports a role in coordinating division events, but does not identify the molecular pathway or demonstrate that any affected protein is a direct ligase substrate.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41598-025-32077-9 The paper's own Cullin-E knockdown Results and Figure 6B-D provide DAPI counts and GRASP immunofluorescence. The function is bounded to these observations, not the paper's conflicted attribution of older WDR1/PLK work to Cullin-E.
