Functions

  • outer membrane vesicle-mediated export — Outer membrane vesicles bud from the cell surface to export outer-membrane and periplasmic molecules into the extracellular environment.
    • DOI:10.1038/nrmicro3525 Schwechheimer and Kuehn 2015 review outer membrane vesicle biogenesis and functions in Gram-negative bacteria.
  • outer membrane vesicle intercellular delivery — The vesicle carries bacterial envelope and lumen cargo to other cells, supporting interactions with host cells and neighboring microbes.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41579-018-0112-2 Toyofuku, Nomura and Eberl 2019 review bacterial membrane vesicles as extracellular carriers that move biomolecules among bacteria and to host cells.

Discussions

The first OMV record models the conserved vesicle boundary and cargo concept but leaves the heterogeneous LPS, phospholipid, beta-barrel and soluble-cargo sets ungrounded until exact component identifiers can be verified.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Gram-positive bacterial membrane vesicles and outer-inner membrane vesicles are outside the exact GO:0061701 boundary. GO:0061701 denotes Gram-negative outer membrane vesicles. This record therefore excludes broader bacterial extracellular vesicle classes that originate from the cytoplasmic membrane or contain both outer and inner membrane.
