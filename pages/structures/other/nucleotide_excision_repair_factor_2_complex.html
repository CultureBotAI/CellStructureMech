nucleotide-excision repair factor 2 complex

GO:0000111 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

One of several protein complexes involved in nucleotide-excision repair; possesses damaged DNA binding activity. In S. cerevisiae, it is composed of Rad4p and Rad23p. [GO:0000111]

Synonyms: NEF2 complex
