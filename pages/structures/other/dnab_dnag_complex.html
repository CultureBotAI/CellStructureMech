DnaB-DnaG complex

GO:1990156 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex containing homohexameric DnaB helicase, and DnaG (a primase). Facilitates the unwinding of double-stranded DNA and the synthesis of RNA primer sequences during DNA replication and repair in Prokaryotes. [GO:1990156]

Synonyms: DnaB-DnaG primosome complex

Part of: GO:1990098
