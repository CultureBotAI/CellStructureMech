PAN complex

GO:0031251 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A complex that possesses poly(A)-specific ribonuclease activity; catalyzes the message-specific shortening of mRNA poly(A) tails. Contains at least two subunits, known as Pan2p and Pan3p in Saccharomyces. [GO:0031251]

Synonyms: poly(A) nuclease complex

Part of: GO:0005737
