mitochondrial prohibitin complex

GO:0035632 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

Inner-mitochondrial-membrane macromolecular complex composed of repeating prohibitin 1 and prohibitin 2 heterodimeric subunits. [GO:0035632]

Synonyms: mitochondrial inner membrane prohibitin complex

Part of: GO:0005743

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding-yeast mitochondria contain a membrane-associated prohibitin supercomplex built from PHB1 and PHB2 subunits. [DOI:10.1110/ps.0212602]
