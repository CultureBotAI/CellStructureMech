dinoflagellate epicone

GO:0097613 · OTHER · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The part of a dinoflagellate cell above the cingulum; also referred to as the anterior portion of a dinoflagellate cell. It is separated from the hypocone by the cingulum. [GO:0097613]

Synonyms: epicone; episome; epitheca

Epicone topology (FUNCTION)

The dinoflagellate epicone is a cellular anatomical structure that has the dinoflagellate apex and apical groove as parts, lies anterior to the hypocone, and is separated from the hypocone by the cingulum.
