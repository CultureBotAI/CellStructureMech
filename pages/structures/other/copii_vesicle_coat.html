COPII vesicle coat

GO:0030127 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A COPII coat protein complex that assembles on endoplasmic-reticulum membranes to bud COPII-coated ER-to-Golgi transport vesicles; in budding yeast it contains Sar1p, Sec23p, Sec24p, Sec13p, and Sec31p. [GO:0030127]
