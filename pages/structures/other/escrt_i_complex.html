ESCRT I complex

GO:0000813 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An endosomal sorting complex required for transport. It consists of the class E vacuolar protein sorting (Vps) proteins and interacts with ubiquitinated cargoes. [GO:0000813]

Synonyms: ESCRT-I complex; endosomal sorting complex required for transport

Part of: GO:0010008
