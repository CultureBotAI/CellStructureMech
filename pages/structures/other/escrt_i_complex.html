ESCRT I complex

GO:0000813 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An endosomal sorting complex required for transport. It consists of the class E vacuolar protein sorting (Vps) proteins and interacts with ubiquitinated cargoes. [GO:0000813]

Synonyms: ESCRT-I complex; endosomal sorting complex required for transport

Part of: endosome membrane (GO:0010008)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Evaluate Mvb12-family and other lineage-specific ESCRT-I subunits before completing the curated component list. This first record starts with the Vps23/Vps28/Vps37 core identified in 2001. Chu et al. 2006 and Curtiss et al. 2007 later identified Mvb12 as an additional yeast ESCRT-I subunit, so Mvb12-family and other lineage-specific components need a dedicated source review before they are added or excluded.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Do not model Vps27 as a constituent component of ESCRT-I. Vps27 recruits ESCRT-I to endosomal membranes during MVB sorting and is therefore upstream of the constituent Vps23/Vps28/Vps37 ESCRT-I component list in this first record.
