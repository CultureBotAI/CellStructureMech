MdtBC Complex

GO:1990203 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex containing two transmembrane subunits; a MdtB dimer and one unit of MdtC. Capable of exporting substrates across the cell membrane. Involved in conferring antibiotic resistance of Gram-negative bacteria by transporting drugs across the membrane. [GO:1990203]

Synonyms: multidrug efflux pump MdtBC
