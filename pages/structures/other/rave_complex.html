RAVE complex

GO:0043291 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A multisubunit complex that in Saccharomyces is composed of three subunits, Rav1p, Rav2p and Skp1p. Acts transiently to catalyze assembly of cytoplasmic V1 with membrane embedded V0 to form the V-ATPase holoenzyme. [GO:0043291]

Synonyms: regulator of the (H+)-ATPase of the vacuolar and endosomal membranes

Part of: GO:0005737

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding yeast uses a cytoplasmic RAVE complex containing Rav1p, Rav2p and Skp1p to support V-ATPase V1-V0 assembly. [DOI:10.1038/35070067]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Yeast model in which RAVE was isolated as a Skp1-associated complex and connected to glucose-dependent V-ATPase assembly. [DOI:10.1038/35070067]
