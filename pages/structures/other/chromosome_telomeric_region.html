chromosome, telomeric region

GO:0000781 — chromosome, telomeric region · Other · Nucleoprotein complex · Proposed

The terminal region of a linear chromosome that includes the telomeric DNA repeats and associated proteins, required for replication and stability of the chromosome end. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Schizosaccharomyces pombe, and other microbial eukaryotes, the chromosome, telomeric region comprises terminal telomeric DNA repeats bound by sequence-specific duplex repeat-binding and telomere-associated proteins (such as Rap1 and Taz1) that form a protective nucleoprotein cap at chromosome ends and mediate telomere organization. [GO:0000781 — chromosome, telomeric region]

Synonyms: telomere; telomeric chromatin

Mechanism graphs

Telomeric DNA repeat recognition and nucleoprotein assembly of the chromosome, telomeric region (ASSEMBLY)

Terminal telomeric repeat DNA (SO:0000624) recruits duplex telomere-binding protein Taz1 in Schizosaccharomyces pombe and TE2IP/Rap1 (directly in Saccharomyces cerevisiae and via Taz1 in S. pombe) to assemble the chromosome, telomeric region (GO:0000781) and mediate telomere organization (GO:0032200).
