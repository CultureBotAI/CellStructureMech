chromosome, telomeric region

GO:0000781 — chromosome, telomeric region · Other · Nucleoprotein complex · Proposed

The terminal region of a linear chromosome that includes the telomeric DNA repeats and associated proteins, required for replication and stability of the chromosome end. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Schizosaccharomyces pombe, and other microbial eukaryotes, the telomeric region is bound by sequence-specific duplex and single-strand repeat-binding proteins (Rap1, Taz1, Cdc13/Pot1) and nuclear-envelope tethering factors (Ndj1, Bqt1-Bqt2, SUN-KASH complexes) that protect chromosome ends from fusion and mediate nuclear positioning. [GO:0000781 — chromosome, telomeric region]

Synonyms: telomere; telomeric chromatin

Part of: GO:0005694 — chromosome (no local record)

Has part: nucleosome (GO:0000786)

  • Support of homolog pairing and meiotic recombination — The budding-yeast tether/bouquet system supports efficient homolog pairing and gene conversion, without making clustering alone the exclusive cause. Mediated by chromosome, telomeric region (GO:0000781) at the terminal nucleoprotein cap of the chromosome, telomeric region.
    • DOI:10.26508/lsa.202000824 Mps2-depleted and csm4-deleted cells show delayed pairing at LYS4 and reduced gene conversion at ARG4/HIS4. Tethering, motion and clustering effects are not experimentally isolated from one another.
  • Support of meiotic spindle formation — Maintained fission-yeast telomere-SPB attachment supports normal SPB behavior and spindle formation. Mediated by chromosome, telomeric region (GO:0000781) at the terminal nucleoprotein cap of the chromosome, telomeric region.
    • DOI:10.1038/celldisc.2017.41 Premature Bqt1 destruction increases spindle defects. Some bouquet-deficient cells still divide successfully, so no absolute requirement or universal fungal function is asserted.
