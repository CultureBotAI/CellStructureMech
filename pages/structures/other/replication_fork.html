replication fork

GO:0005657 — replication fork · Other · Nucleoprotein complex · Proposed

The Y-shaped region of a replicating DNA molecule, resulting from the separation of the DNA strands and in which the synthesis of new strands takes place. Also includes associated protein complexes. The Y-shaped nucleoprotein junction on a replicating chromosome where parental double-stranded DNA is unwound by the replicative DNA helicase into leading and lagging single-stranded templates and copied by the replisome in bacteria, archaea, and microbial eukaryotes. When stalled by DNA lesions or dNTP depletion, the replication fork recruits RPA, the 9-1-1 (Ddc1–Mec3–Rad17) checkpoint clamp, and Mec1/ATR kinases to stabilize the fork and prevent replication catastrophe. [GO:0005657 — replication fork]
