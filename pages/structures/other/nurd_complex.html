NuRD complex

GO:0016581 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An approximately 2 MDa multi-subunit complex that exhibits ATP-dependent chromatin remodeling activity in addition to histone deacetylase (HDAC) activity, and has been shown to establish transcriptional repression of a number of target genes in vertebrates, invertebrates and fungi. Amongst its subunits, the NuRD complex contains histone deacetylases, histone binding proteins and Mi-2-like proteins. The Nucleosome Remodeling and Deacetylase (NuRD) family of multiprotein chromatin silencing complexes (exemplified in microbial eukaryotes by the fission yeast Schizosaccharomyces pombe SHREC complex) that couples class II histone deacetylase activity (Clr3) with CHD/Mi-2-family ATP-dependent chromatin remodeling (Mit1). Together with scaffold subunits Clr1, Clr2, and the AAA+ ATPase chaperone Cdc20/Ccq1, NuRD/SHREC deacetylates H3K14ac and positions nucleosomes to compact heterochromatin. [GO:0016581]

Synonyms: NRD complex; nucleosome remodeling and histone deacetylation complex; Mi-2 complex
