phenylacetyl-CoA 1,2-epoxidase complex

GO:0062077 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A PaaA/PaaB/PaaC/PaaE multicomponent monooxygenase complex that catalyzes the NADPH- and oxygen-dependent epoxidation of phenylacetyl-CoA to 2-(1,2-epoxy-1,2-dihydrophenyl)acetyl-CoA. [GO:0062077]

Synonyms: paaABCE complex

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Should PaaD be modeled as a constituent of the phenylacetyl-CoA 1,2-epoxidase complex? No. GO:0062077 uses the paaABCE complex synonym and QuickGO annotates PaaA, PaaB, PaaC and PaaE, but not PaaD, to the complex. Grishin et al. 2011 found that PaaD did not form a stable complex with the other proteins in the tested pull-downs and was not necessary for in vitro PaaABCE catalysis, while noting that it may assist maturation of the monooxygenase in vivo.
