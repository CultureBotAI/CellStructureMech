DNA polymerase III, core complex

GO:0044776 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A DNA polymerase III core subassembly made of alpha, epsilon and theta subunits that provides polymerase and 3'-5' exonuclease proofreading activities within the DNA polymerase III holoenzyme. [GO:0044776]

Part of: DNA polymerase III complex (GO:0009360)

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:562 Escherichia coli — Maki and Kornberg reconstituted the E. coli DNA polymerase III holoenzyme from purified subunits and assigned alpha, epsilon and theta to the Pol III core. [PMID:3283127]

Functions

  • DNA-directed DNA polymerase activity GO:0003887 — The DNA polymerase III core carries DNA-template-directed polymerase activity; beta-clamp and clamp-loader subassemblies make that catalytic core processive within the holoenzyme.
    • GO:0044776 GO:0044776 names polymerase activity as one activity carried by the DNA polymerase III core complex.
    • PMID:3283127 Maki and Kornberg 1988 reported that the Pol III core possesses polymerase activity while auxiliary subunits provide processivity.
