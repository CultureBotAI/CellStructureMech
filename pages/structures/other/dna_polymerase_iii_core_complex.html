DNA polymerase III, core complex

GO:0044776 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A DNA polymerase III core subassembly made of alpha, epsilon and theta subunits that provides polymerase and 3'-5' exonuclease proofreading activities within the DNA polymerase III holoenzyme. [GO:0044776]
