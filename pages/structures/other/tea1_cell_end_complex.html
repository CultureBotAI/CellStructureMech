Tea1 cell-end complex

GO:0031500 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A high molecular weight complex characterized in S. pombe containing the cell-end anchoring protein Tea1. This complex is transported to the cell ends by microtubules and is involved in bipolar growth and the maintennce of normal cell polarity. [GO:0031500]

Part of: polarisome (GO:0000133)
