ESCRT III complex

GO:0000815 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A complex with membrane scission activity that plays a major role in many processes where membranes are remodelled - including endosomal transport (vesicle budding), nuclear envelope organisation (membrane closure, mitotic bridge cleavage), and cytokinesis (abscission). [GO:0000815]

Synonyms: ESCRT-III complex; endosomal sorting complex required for transport
