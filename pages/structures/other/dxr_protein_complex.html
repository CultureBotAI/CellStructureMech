Dxr protein complex

GO:1990065 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex that is involved in the MEP pathway of IPP biosynthesis. It catalyzes the NADP-dependent rearrangement and reduction of 1-deoxy-D-xylulose-5-phosphate (DXP) to 2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 4-phosphate (MEP). [GO:1990065]

Synonyms: 1-deoxy-D-xylulose 5-phosphate reductoisomerase complex
