GroEL-GroES complex

GO:1990220 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

Bacterial chaperonin complex consisting of a heptameric 10kDa chaperonin subunit GroES and a tetradecameric (2x7) 60kDa chaperonin subunit GroEL. The 60kDa subunit possesses ATPase activity while the holo-enzyme is responsible for the correct folding of proteins. [GO:1990220]

Synonyms: bacterial chaperonin ATPase complex; bacterial chaperonin complex
