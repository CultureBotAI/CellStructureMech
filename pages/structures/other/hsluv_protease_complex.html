HslUV protease complex

GO:0009376 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex that possesses ATP-dependent protease activity; consists of an ATPase large subunit with homology to other ClpX family ATPases and a peptidase small subunit related to the proteasomal beta-subunits of eukaryotes. In the E. coli complex, a double ring-shaped homohexamer of HslV is capped on each side by a ring-shaped HslU homohexamer. [GO:0009376]

Synonyms: ClpYQ protease complex

Part of: GO:0005737 — cytoplasm (no local record)
