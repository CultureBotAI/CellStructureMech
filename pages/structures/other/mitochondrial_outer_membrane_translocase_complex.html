mitochondrial outer membrane translocase complex

GO:0005742 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A large complex of the mitochondrial outer membrane that mediates transport of proteins into all mitochondrial compartments. [GO:0005742]

Synonyms: GIP complex; mitochondrion outer membrane translocase complex
