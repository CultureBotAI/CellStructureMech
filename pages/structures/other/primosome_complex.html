primosome complex

GO:1990077 · Other · Nucleoprotein complex · Proposed

A protein-DNA complex essential for initiation or restart of DNA replication in bacteria. At a minimum, it contains a DNA helicase and associated protein(s) which unwind the double-stranded DNA (dsDNA) and move along a single strand of DNA in the 5' to 3' direction. Typically, it also contains a primase which synthesizes a RNA primer. [GO:1990077]

Synonyms: primosome

Part of: replisome (GO:0030894)
