  • ATP-dependent protein disaggregase activity GO:0140545 — Supports resolubilization of heat-aggregated reporter protein in isolated yeast mitochondria. Recovery into a soluble fraction is not itself evidence of refolding or restored reporter enzymatic activity.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Separate the Hsp78 oligomer from its subunit, cooperating chaperones and potential clients. Hsp78 is one gene product but forms a distinct mitochondrial oligomer. Copurification does not establish invariant structural membership.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve an exact source-neutral Hsp78-type component family if an authority supplies one. The nine integrated InterPro matches for reviewed P33416 include broader ClpA/B families, domains and sites; using those as an exact Hsp78 identity would overstate specificity.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve preparation-dependent oligomeric states and source-internal construct/protocol discrepancies before asserting a fixed copy number or residue-specific mechanism. Figure 1A and Results give E216Q/E614Q for Hsp78TR, whereas Methods give E612Q for the second substitution. High-salt SEC is described with NaCl in Results and KCl in Methods, and the compared buffers also differ in ATP and magnesium. Figure 2D and Methods differ in aggregation and centrifugation conditions.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Separate purified recombinant Hsp78TR assays from yeast complementation in the causal graph. The initial graph reused a state labelled Hsp78TR complementation for an ATPase assay performed on bacterially expressed purified protein. Correct edge notes did not repair the mismatched intervention subject.
