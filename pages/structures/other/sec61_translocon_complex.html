Sec61 translocon complex

GO:0005784 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A heterotrimeric endoplasmic-reticulum membrane translocon that forms a protein-conducting channel for co- and post-translational substrate movement across or into the ER membrane. [GO:0005784]

Synonyms: Sec61 complex; ER Sec61 translocon

Part of: GO:0005789

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Should Sec62, Sec63, Kar2, or ribosomes be included as Sec61 translocon complex components? GO:0005784 exactly denotes the heterotrimeric Sec61 channel. Sec62, Sec63, and Kar2 participate in posttranslational transport with Sec61, and ribosomes engage Sec61 during cotranslational transport, but those factors are associated machinery rather than constituents of the Sec61 core.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Should the Sec61 beta subunit's Rtn1p-containing pool make Rtn1p a Sec61 translocon component? Sbh1p can interact with Rtn1p in complexes that do not contain Sec61p or Sss1p, so that ER-shaping pool is outside the Sec61 translocon boundary.
