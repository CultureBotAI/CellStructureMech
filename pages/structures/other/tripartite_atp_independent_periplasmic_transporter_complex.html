tripartite ATP-independent periplasmic transporter complex

GO:0031317 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A complex consisting of two membrane proteins and one extracytoplasmic solute receptor. Such transporters transport a variety of substrates without direct ATP power, instead using energy from ion gradients. [GO:0031317]

Synonyms: TRAP transporter complex; TRAP-T transporter complex

Functions

  • ion-gradient-dependent solute uptake — Couples extracytoplasmic receptor binding to secondary active solute import without direct ATP hydrolysis.
    • GO:0031317 GO:0031317 defines TRAP transporter complexes by transport of varied substrates without direct ATP power and with energy from ion gradients.
    • DOI:10.1128/JB.179.17.5482-5493.1997 Forward et al. 1997 found that R. capsulatus DctPQM succinate uptake tracked membrane potential rather than ATP abundance.
