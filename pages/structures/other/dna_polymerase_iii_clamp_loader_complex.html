DNA polymerase III, clamp loader complex

GO:0043846 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A heptamer that includes the tau and gamma products of the dnaX gene and the chi/psi subcomplex. Confers structural asymmetry that allows the polymerase to replicate both leading and lagging strands. [GO:0043846]

Synonyms: DNA polymerase III, DnaX complex; DNA polymerase III, DnaX subcomplex; clamp loader complex

Part of: DNA polymerase III complex (GO:0009360)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Functions

  • DNA clamp loader activity GO:0003689 — The DNA polymerase III clamp-loader complex opens the beta sliding-clamp ring and loads it around primer-template DNA using its ATPase motor.
    • GO:0003689 GO:0003689 defines DNA clamp loader activity as ATP-hydrolysis-driven opening and closing of a sliding clamp around DNA duplex.
    • PMID:11525729 Jeruzalmi et al. 2001 described the E. coli gamma complex as an AAA+ ATPase that loads the beta sliding clamp onto DNA.
  • DNA replication GO:0006260 — The clamp-loader complex helps the Pol III holoenzyme coordinate leading- and lagging-strand DNA replication.
    • GO:0043846 GO:0043846 defines the clamp-loader complex by its structural asymmetry in leading- and lagging-strand replication.
    • GO:0006260 GO:0006260 is the Gene Ontology biological-process term for DNA replication.
