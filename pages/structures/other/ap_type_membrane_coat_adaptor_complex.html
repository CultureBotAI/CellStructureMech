AP-type membrane coat adaptor complex

GO:0030119 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

Any of several heterotetrameric complexes that link clathrin (or another coat-forming molecule, as hypothesized for AP-3 and AP-4) to a membrane surface; they are found on coated pits and coated vesicles, and mediate sorting of cargo proteins into vesicles. Each AP complex contains two large (a beta and one of either an alpha, gamma, delta, or epsilon) subunits (110-130 kDa), a medium (mu) subunit (approximately 50 kDa), and a small (sigma) subunit (15-20 kDa). [GO:0030119]

Synonyms: clathrin adaptor

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Represent AP-type membrane coat adaptor complex as part of membrane coat rather than as a membrane-coat subtype. GO:0030119 is a membrane protein complex that is part of GO:0030117 membrane coat. This record therefore keeps GO:0098796 as its is-a parent and uses part_of for GO:0030117 instead of making the AP adaptor complex a subclass of the coat it helps assemble.

Kaur et al. 2017 support the presence and upstream mucocyst-biogenesis role of a Tetrahymena AP-3 complex. That evidence anchors this AP-type adaptor record, while the mucocyst record continues to exclude AP-3 from mature mucocyst components because the cited role is maturation rather than stable membership in docked mucocysts.
