mitochondrial inner membrane peptidase complex

GO:0042720 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

Protease complex of the mitochondrial inner membrane, consisting of at least two subunits, involved in processing of both nuclear- and mitochondrially-encoded proteins targeted to the intermembrane space. [GO:0042720]

Synonyms: mitochondrion inner membrane peptidase complex; IMP

Part of: GO:0005743

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding-yeast mitochondria contain an inner-membrane peptidase complex whose Imp1 and Som1 proteins physically interact. [DOI:10.1007/s004380051192]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Budding-yeast model used to identify Som1 as a third IMP peptidase-complex subunit alongside Imp1 and Imp2. [DOI:10.1007/s004380051192]

Functions

  • mitochondrial protein processing GO:0034982 — IMP contributes to mitochondrial protein processing by cleaving inner-membrane protease substrates that are targeted to the intermembrane space.
    • GO:0042720 GO:0042720 defines IMP as an inner-membrane protease complex for nuclear- and mitochondrially encoded proteins targeted to the intermembrane space.
    • GO:0034982 GO:0034982 denotes peptide cleavage of mitochondrial proteins, including cleavage contributing to import.
    • DOI:10.1016/s0167-4889(02)00265-3 Gakh et al. 2002 reviewed inner membrane peptidase as one of the conserved mitochondrial peptidases that processes specific precursor-polypeptide subsets.
