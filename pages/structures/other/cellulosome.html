cellulosome

GO:0043263 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An extracellular membraneless organelle built around scaffoldin and dockerin-bearing enzymes that synergistically degrade insoluble plant-cell-wall polysaccharides. [GO:0043263]

Synonyms: cellulosomal complex; multienzyme cellulolytic complex

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: Cellulosomes occur in a subset of anaerobic plant-polysaccharide-degrading bacteria and are absent from most bacterial lineages. [DOI:10.1146/annurev-biochem-091208-085603]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:1515 Acetivibrio thermocellus — The cited literature studies this organism under the Clostridium thermocellum name; it is the prototype cellulosome-forming bacterium. [DOI:10.1146/annurev.micro.57.030502.091022]

Functions

  • plant cell wall polysaccharide degradation — Scaffoldin-bound carbohydrate-active enzymes cooperate to hydrolyze insoluble cellulose and hemicellulose.
    • DOI:10.1146/annurev-biochem-091208-085603 Fontes and Gilbert 2010 review the spatial organization of cellulosomal enzymes for synergistic plant cell-wall deconstruction.

Mechanism graphs

Cohesin-dockerin binding assembles a cellulose-degrading machine (ASSEMBLY)

A primary scaffoldin concentrates dockerin-bearing hydrolases through cohesin-dockerin interactions, and the assembled complex binds insoluble cellulose so its enzymes can hydrolyze plant cell-wall polysaccharides cooperatively.
