cellulosome

GO:0043263 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An extracellular membraneless organelle built around scaffoldin and dockerin-bearing enzymes that synergistically degrade insoluble plant-cell-wall polysaccharides. [GO:0043263]

Synonyms: cellulosomal complex; multienzyme cellulolytic complex

Mechanism graphs

Cohesin-dockerin binding assembles a cellulose-degrading machine (ASSEMBLY)

A primary scaffoldin concentrates dockerin-bearing hydrolases through cohesin-dockerin interactions, and the assembled complex binds insoluble cellulose so its enzymes can hydrolyze plant cell-wall polysaccharides cooperatively.
