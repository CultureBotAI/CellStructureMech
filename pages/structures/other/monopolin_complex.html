monopolin complex

GO:0033551 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex required for clamping microtubule binding sites, ensuring orientation of sister kinetochores to the same pole (mono-orientation) during meiosis I. [GO:0033551]

Synonyms: monopolin subcomplex Csm1/Lrs4; Pcs1/Mde4 complex

Part of: kinetochore (GO:0000776)
