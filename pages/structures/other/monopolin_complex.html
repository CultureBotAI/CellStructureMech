monopolin complex

GO:0033551 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex required for clamping microtubule binding sites, ensuring orientation of sister kinetochores to the same pole (mono-orientation) during meiosis I. In the yeast S. cerevisiae this complex consists of Csm1p, Lrs4p, Hrr25p and Mam1p; in S. pombe Pcs1 and Mde4 have been identified as subunits. [GO:0033551]

Synonyms: monopolin subcomplex Csm1/Lrs4; Pcs1/Mde4 complex

Part of: GO:0000776
