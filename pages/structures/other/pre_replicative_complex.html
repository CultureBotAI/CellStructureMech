pre-replicative complex

GO:0036387 · Other · Nucleoprotein complex · Proposed

A protein-DNA complex that forms at the origin of replication during the initial step of DNA replication and allows the origin to become competent, or 'licensed', for replication. A multiprotein-DNA nucleoprotein assembly formed at chromosomal replication origins prior to S-phase initiation in eukaryotes, archaea, or bacteria that licenses the origin for bidirectional DNA synthesis. In eukaryotes (ORC–Cdc6–Cdt1–Mcm2-7) and bacteria (ATP-DnaA filaments recruiting DnaC-bound DnaB helicase hexamers onto oriC), the pre-replicative complex unwinds or poises origin DNA for replicative helicase activation. [GO:0036387]

Synonyms: pre-RC; pre-replication complex
