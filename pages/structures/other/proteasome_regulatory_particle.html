proteasome regulatory particle

GO:0005838 — proteasome regulatory particle · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A multisubunit complex, which caps one or both ends of the proteasome core complex. This complex recognizes and unfolds ubiquitinated proteins, and translocates them to the proteasome core complex. Composed of the proximal 19S base subcomplex (Rpt1-Rpt6 AAA+ ATPase ring and Rpn1/Rpn2/Rpn10/Rpn13 ubiquitin-binding scaffolds) and the distal 19S lid subcomplex (Rpn11-Rpn8 deubiquitinase module and PCI-domain scaffold subunits). [GO:0005838 — proteasome regulatory particle]

Synonyms: PA700-dependent proteasome activator; 19S regulatory particle; PA700 proteasome activator; modulator complex

Part of: proteasome accessory complex (GO:0022624)

Has part: proteasome regulatory particle, base subcomplex (GO:0008540); proteasome regulatory particle, lid subcomplex (GO:0008541)
