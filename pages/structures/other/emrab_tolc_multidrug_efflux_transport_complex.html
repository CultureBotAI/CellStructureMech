EmrAB-TolC multidrug efflux transport complex

cellstructuremech:emrab_tolc_multidrug_efflux_transport_complex · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An Escherichia coli MFS-family cell-envelope efflux pump complex composed of EmrA, EmrB, and TolC subunits that exports hydrophobic xenobiotics across the inner and outer membranes. [ComplexPortal:CPX-4268]

Synonyms: EmrAB-TolC multidrug efflux transport system
