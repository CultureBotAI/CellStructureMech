COMA complex

GO:0000817 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A kinetochore multiprotein complex that bridges the subunits that are in contact with centromeric DNA and the subunits bound to microtubules during kinetochore assembly. In yeast, consists of Ctf19p, Okp1p, Mcm21p, and Ame1p. [GO:0000817]
