DnaB-DnaC-DnaT-PriA-PriB complex

GO:1990158 · OTHER · NUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein-DNA complex consisting of the helicase loading complex DnaB-DnaC, replication restart proteins DnaT, PriA and PriB, and associated DNA. Involved in the restart of DNA replication after a stalled replication fork has been repaired. [GO:1990158]

Synonyms: DnaB-DnaC-DnaT-PriA-PriB preprimosome; phi-X174-type preprimosome
