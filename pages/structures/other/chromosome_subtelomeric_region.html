chromosome, subtelomeric region

GO:0099115 — chromosome, subtelomeric region · Other · Nucleoprotein complex · Proposed

The chromosomal region immediately adjacent to a telomere, including subtelomeric DNA elements (such as X and Y' repeats in budding yeast) and associated nucleosomal heterochromatin. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae and Schizosaccharomyces pombe, the chromosome, subtelomeric region is packaged into silent chromatin by histone H4 K16 deacetylation and Sir2-Sir3-Sir4 or Swi6/HP1 complexes. [GO:0099115 — chromosome, subtelomeric region]

Synonyms: subtelomere; subtelomeric chromatin

Part of: GO:0005694 — chromosome (no local record)

Has part: nucleosome (GO:0000786)

Mechanism graphs

Subtelomeric nucleosomal heterochromatin assembly via SIR and Swi6 complexes (ASSEMBLY)

Subtelomeric DNA (SO:0001997) packaged into nucleosomes (GO:0000786) recruits Rap1, Sir4, Sir2 (deacetylating histone H4 K16), and Sir3 in Saccharomyces cerevisiae—or Swi6 heterochromatin protein in Schizosaccharomyces pombe—to assemble the chromosome, subtelomeric region (GO:0099115) and mediate telomere organization (GO:0032200).
