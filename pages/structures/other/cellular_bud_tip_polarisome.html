Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral family groundings for Spa2, Pea2, and Bud6/Aip3 at the cellular bud tip. This localized record deliberately reuses the reviewed-label-only Saccharomyces polarisome cohorts from the broader polarisome record instead of guessing InterPro, Pfam, NCBIfam, or UniProtKB family groundings whose broader homologous families do not exactly delimit the bud-tip complex.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep Bni1, Aip5, the exocyst, and MAPK signaling proteins outside the first cellular-bud-tip polarisome component boundary. GO:0000133 and later Saccharomyces polarisome studies implicate Bni1, Aip5, and other actin-assembly factors in the broader polarisome, but Sheu et al. 1998 directly support a Spa2/Pea2/Bud6 12S complex and discuss Bni1 only as a possible additional component. The exocyst is a separate bud-tip-localized complex and the MAPK factors tested by Sheu et al. are interacting signaling proteins, not curated constituents of the GO:0031561 structure.
