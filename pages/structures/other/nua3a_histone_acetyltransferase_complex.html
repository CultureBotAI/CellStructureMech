NuA3a histone acetyltransferase complex

GO:1990467 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A NuA3 complex that catalyzes the acetylation of Histone H3. In S. cerevisiae, this complex consists of Eaf6p, Nto1p, Sas3p, Taf14p, Yng1p and associates with H3K4me3 using Yng1p. [GO:1990467]
