MIS12/MIND type complex

GO:0000444 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A multiprotein kinetochore subcomplex that binds to centromeric chromatin and forms part of the outer kinetochore. It helps to recruit outer kinetochore subunits that will bind to microtubules. In humans, it consists of MIS12, DSN1, NSL1 and PMF1. [GO:0000444]

Synonyms: MIND complex; Mis12 complex; Mtw1p Including Nnf1p-Nsl1p-Dsn1p complex; nuclear MIS12/MIND complex

Part of: outer kinetochore (GO:0000940); kinetochore (GO:0000776)
