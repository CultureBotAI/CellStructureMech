Snt2C complex

GO:0070211 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A histone deacetylase complex that is part of the chromatin remodeling machinery. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae this complex contains Snt2p, Ecm5p and Rpd3p. [GO:0070211]

Synonyms: Snt2 complex
