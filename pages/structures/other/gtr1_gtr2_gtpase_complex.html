Gtr1-Gtr2 GTPase complex

GO:1990131 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A heterodimer GTPase complex. In S. cerevisiae, this complex contains Gtr1p and Gtr2p proteins. [GO:1990131]

Synonyms: Gtr1p-Gtr2p complex; Rag GTPase heterodimer

Gtr1-Gtr2 supports yeast TORC1 signaling (REGULATION)

In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Gtr1 and Gtr2 form a Rag-family GTPase heterodimer that localizes with the EGO anchor on the vacuolar membrane and regulates TORC1 signaling.
