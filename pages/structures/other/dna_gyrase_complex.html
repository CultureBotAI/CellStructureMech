DNA gyrase complex

GO:0120217 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

DNA gyrase complex is a bacterial GyrA2-GyrB2 type IIA topoisomerase complex that uses ATP hydrolysis to introduce negative supercoils into DNA and can also relax positive supercoils. [GO:0120217]
