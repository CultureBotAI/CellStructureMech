cell cortex

GO:0005938 · Other · Subcellular region · Proposed

The region of a cell that lies just beneath the plasma membrane and often, but not always, contains a network of actin filaments and associated proteins. [GO:0005938]

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Should actin, Arp2/3, or septin proteins be duplicated as direct cell cortex components? GO:0005938 denotes the sub-plasma-membrane region. Actin cortical patches and septin rings are cortex-localized substructures that already have their own structure records, so actin, Arp2/3, and septin constituents remain on those lower-level records instead of being duplicated as molecular components of the parent region.
