glycine reductase complex

GO:0030700 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex that possesses glycine reductase activity and is usually built from selenoprotein A, selenoprotein B, and protein C subunits. [GO:0030700]
