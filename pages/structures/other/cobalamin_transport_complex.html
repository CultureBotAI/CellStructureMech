cobalamin transport complex

GO:1990191 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A type II ATP-binding cassette (ABC) importer complex (BtuCD-F) in the bacterial inner membrane and periplasm, composed of the periplasmic cobalamin-binding protein BtuF, homodimeric transmembrane permease subunits BtuC2, and homodimeric ATP-binding cassette subunits BtuD2, which mediates ATP-driven import of vitamin B12 (cobalamin) and cobinamide into the cytoplasm. [GO:1990191]

Synonyms: BtuCDF complex; cobalamin-transporting BtuCDF complex; vitamin B12 transport complex; vitamin B12-transporting BtuCDF complex

Part of: plasma membrane (GO:0005886)

Xrefs: ComplexPortal:CPX-2105

BtuCD-F Periplasmic Capture and ATP-Driven Cobalamin Import (FUNCTION)

Following TonB-dependent outer-membrane passage via BtuB, periplasmic BtuF captures cyanocobalamin and docks onto the BtuC2-BtuD2 inner-membrane ABC complex to drive ATP-dependent cytoplasmic import.
