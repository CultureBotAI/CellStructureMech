Antapex-hypocone topology (FUNCTION)

The dinoflagellate antapex is a cellular anatomical structure within the hypocone, has the horn-shaped thecate antapical horn as a narrower child class, and can be reached by the dinoflagellate sulcal notch sulcus subclass.
