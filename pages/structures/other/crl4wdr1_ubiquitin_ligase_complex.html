CRL4WDR1 ubiquitin ligase complex

cellstructuremech:crl4wdr1_ubiquitin_ligase_complex · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A WDR1-containing Cullin4-DDB1 protein complex characterized in procyclic Trypanosoma brucei. The named CRL4WDR1 association participates in regulation of TbPLK protein turnover; its complete catalytic composition and direct ubiquitination of TbPLK have not been established. [DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1006146]

Synonyms: CRL4WDR1

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5691 Trypanosoma brucei — The paper studies procyclic cells: strain 29-13 for inducible RNAi and Lister 427 for endogenous tagging, with tagging also performed in RNAi lines. Two nonoverlapping WDR1 RNAi fragments and two clones per fragment were tested; the paper presents one representative clone after reporting similar phenotypes. The 927 locus identifiers are reference annotations, not the cultured strain. Yeast two-hybrid hosts and recombinant E. coli expression hosts are not canonical organisms for this complex. [DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1006146]

Functions

  • regulation of TbPLK protein turnover — WDR1 depletion slows the loss of TbPLK during a cycloheximide chase and increases its abundance in procyclic T. brucei. This supports a turnover-regulatory role for the named association, but does not establish direct covalent ubiquitination of TbPLK by a purified complex.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1006146 Figure 6A-C measures abundance at 24/48 hours of RNAi and a 0-6-hour chase after 24 hours of induction. Figure 6D detects less polyubiquitinated material in TbPLK immunoprecipitates, but the Results explicitly leave its identity unresolved: TbPLK itself and/or associated proteins.
  • support of bilobe duplication and flagellum attachment — WDR1 RNAi impairs bilobe duplication, basal-body segregation and new flagellum-attachment-zone assembly, with detached flagella at 48 hours. These are perturbation-supported cellular outputs, not proof that every phenotype is mediated exclusively by direct TbPLK ubiquitination.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1006146 Figures 4-5 show basal-body, bilobe, FAZ and attachment phenotypes after 48 hours of WDR1 RNAi. Figure 7 shows persistent phospho-Ser54 TbCentrin2 staining in late-cycle cells at 24 hours. TbPLK overexpression in S4 Figure phenocopies several effects, but is not a rescue or an epistasis test.
