nuclear replication fork

GO:0043596 · Other · Other · Proposed

The Y-shaped region of a nuclear replicating DNA molecule, resulting from the separation of the DNA strands and in which the synthesis of new strands takes place. Also includes associated protein complexes. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Schizosaccharomyces pombe, and other microbial eukaryotes, the nuclear replication fork couples CMG (Cdc45-MCM-GINS) helicase unwinding of chromosomal DNA with leading-strand synthesis by DNA polymerase epsilon, lagging-strand synthesis by DNA polymerase alpha-primase and delta, PCNA clamp loading, and nucleosome reassembly. [GO:0043596]

Part of: nuclear chromosome (GO:0000228)

Has part: nuclear replisome (GO:0043601 — nuclear replisome)
