Elg1 RFC-like complex

GO:0031391 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A pentameric replication factor C-like complex that unloads the proliferating cell nuclear antigen DNA clamp from chromatin and supports telomere length regulation and genome stability in Saccharomyces. [GO:0031391]

Synonyms: Elg1-RFC; Elg1-RLC; RFC (Elg1)
