NuA3 histone acetyltransferase complex

GO:0033100 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A Gcn5-independent multisubunit complex that catalyzes the acetylation of histone H3. The budding yeast complex includes Sas3p, Taf30p, and Yng1p. The yeast Nucleosomal Acetyltransferase of histone H3 (NuA3) multiprotein complex in Saccharomyces cerevisiae and related fungi that specifically acetylates lysine 14 (and lysine 23) of nucleosomal histone H3 to facilitate RNA polymerase II transcription elongation and DNA replication. The core NuA3 complex comprises the MYST-family catalytic subunit Sas3p, the H3K4me3-binding PHD-finger scaffold Yng1p, the H3K36me3-reading PWWP subunit Pdp3p, Nto1p, Eaf6p, and Taf14p. [GO:0033100]

Mechanism graphs

NuA3 histone acetyltransferase complex methylation-coupled H3K14 acetylation (FUNCTION)

Yng1p and Pdp3p reader domains recruit the NuA3 histone acetyltransferase complex to H3K4me3- and H3K36me3-modified nucleosomes, activating Sas3p to acetylate H3K14 and stimulate transcription elongation.
