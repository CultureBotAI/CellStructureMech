glideosome

GO:0160055 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex composed of at least GAP45 (gliding-associated protein), GAP50 and myosin heavy and light chains. Anchored via GAP50 to the inner membrane complex of motile and invasive forms of apicomplexan parasites and regulates parasite gliding motility and invasion of host cells. [GO:0160055]

Synonyms: actin-myosin glideosome

  • host-cell invasion — The glideosome supports active entry of motile apicomplexan stages into host cells.

    • DOI:10.1046/j.1365-2958.2002.03056.x Opitz and Soldati 2002 review the early glideosome model for Toxoplasma gliding and host-cell invasion.

The first glideosome record uses exact Pfam grounding for GAP45 but leaves MyoA, light-chain and GAP50 components reviewed-label-only because broad myosin, EF-hand and metallophosphatase domains do not denote the specific apicomplexan glideosome families.
