sulfite reductase complex (NADPH)

GO:0009337 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A multisubunit iron flavoprotein, which in yeast is composed of 2 alpha and 2 beta subunits. Catalyzes the reduction of sulfite to sulfide. [GO:0009337]

Synonyms: sulphite reductase complex (NADPH)

Part of: GO:0005737 — cytoplasm (no local record)

Mechanism graphs

NADPH-Sulfite Reductase Assembly and Six-Electron Sulfite Reduction (FUNCTION)

Flavin-binding alpha subunits (CysJ/Met10) and siroheme-[4Fe-4S] hemoprotein beta subunits (CysI/Met5) assemble via the SiRHP N-terminal domain and SiRFP NADPH-binding domain to catalyze six-electron sulfite reduction during sulfate assimilation.
