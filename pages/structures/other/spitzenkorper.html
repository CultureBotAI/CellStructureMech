spitzenkorper

GO:0031521 · OTHER · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

A structure within the hyphal tip of filamentous fungi that acts as an organizing center for hyphal growth. [GO:0031521]

Synonyms: Spitzenkörper; SPK

  • NCBITaxon:4751 Fungi — VARIABLE: Recognizable Spitzenkorper occur in diverse hyphal fungi and organize tip growth, but they are not asserted here to be universal across all fungal lineages or growth states. [DOI:10.1128/MMBR.00068-17]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5141 Neurospora crassa — Filamentous-fungal model in which SPA-2/JNS-1 and LAH-2 scaffold modules of the Spitzenkorper have been experimentally dissected. [DOI:10.1038/s41467-020-16712-9]

Functions

  • hyphal tip-growth organization — The Spitzenkorper acts as a vesicle supply center that organizes polarized secretion for fungal hyphal extension.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.mib.2014.04.003 Riquelme and Sanchez-Leon 2014 reviewed the Spitzenkorper as a fungal hyphal growth and morphogenesis organizer.
    • DOI:10.1128/MMBR.00068-17 Riquelme et al. 2018 review polarized hyphal growth and Spitzenkorper-associated vesicle traffic in fungal morphogenesis.
