nuclear pore outer ring

GO:0031080 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A ring-shaped eukaryotic nuclear pore subcomplex built from outer-ring coat nucleoporins on the cytoplasmic and nucleoplasmic faces of the nuclear pore scaffold. [GO:0031080]

Synonyms: Nup84 complex; Nup107-160 complex; Y complex

Part of: nuclear pore (GO:0005643)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Functions

  • nuclear-pore scaffold assembly — The nuclear pore outer ring contributes a coat-nucleoporin scaffold to the assembled NPC.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.0907453106 Seo et al. 2009 studied Nup120 structure and proposed how the Nup84 complex forms rings.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2021.12.015 Akey et al. 2022 resolved the yeast nuclear pore outer-ring architecture.
