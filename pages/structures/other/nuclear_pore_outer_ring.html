nuclear pore outer ring

GO:0031080 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A ring-shaped eukaryotic nuclear pore subcomplex built from outer-ring coat nucleoporins on the cytoplasmic and nucleoplasmic faces of the nuclear pore scaffold. [GO:0031080]

Synonyms: Nup84 complex; Nup107-160 complex; Y complex

Part of: GO:0005643
