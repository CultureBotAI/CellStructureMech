EMC complex

GO:0072546 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A transmembrane protein complex in the endoplasmic reticulum membrane that promotes insertion of newly synthesized proteins into the ER membrane. [GO:0072546]

Synonyms: endoplasmic reticulum membrane protein complex; ER membrane protein complex

Part of: GO:0005789

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding yeast contains an ER-membrane EMC complex; Bai et al. determined a Saccharomyces cerevisiae cryo-EM structure and reported eight EMC subunits. [DOI:10.1038/s41586-020-2389-3]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Budding-yeast model in which cryo-EM resolved the EMC transmembrane-domain insertase architecture. [DOI:10.1038/s41586-020-2389-3]

Functions

  • protein insertion into ER membrane GO:0045048 — EMC promotes insertion and correct topogenesis of selected tail-anchored and multipass transmembrane domains in the ER membrane.
    • GO:0045048 GO:0045048 denotes incorporation of a protein into the endoplasmic-reticulum membrane with proper topogenic orientation.
    • DOI:10.1126/science.aao3099 Guna et al. 2018 showed that purified EMC catalyzes transmembrane-domain insertion of eligible substrates in a reconstituted liposome system.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2018.10.009 Chitwood et al. 2018 found that EMC initiates cotranslational insertion of the first transmembrane domain of tested GPCR substrates and cooperates with Sec61 for later transmembrane-domain insertion.

Mechanism graphs

EMC inserts transmembrane domains into the ER membrane (FUNCTION)

EMC is embedded in the endoplasmic-reticulum membrane, directly inserts selected transmembrane domains, and can cooperate with the Sec61 translocon during cotranslational topogenesis.
