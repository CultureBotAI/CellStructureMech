Cvt complex

GO:0034270 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex that is involved in the Cvt pathway. In budding yeast, the Cvt complex consists of multimers of preApe1p. [GO:0034270]

Synonyms: cytoplasm to vacuole targeting complex; cytoplasm-to-vacuole targeting complex

Part of: GO:0005737
