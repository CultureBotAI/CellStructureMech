prefoldin complex

GO:0016272 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A multisubunit chaperone that is capable of delivering unfolded proteins to cytosolic chaperonin, which it acts as a cofactor for. In humans, the complex is a heterohexamer of two PFD-alpha and four PFD-beta type subunits. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, it also acts in the nucleus to regulate the rate of elongation by RNA polymerase II via a direct effect on histone dynamics. [GO:0016272]

Synonyms: GIM complex

Mechanism graphs

Prefoldin Hexamer Assembly and Chaperonin-Mediated Cytoskeletal Protein Folding (FUNCTION)

Two alpha-class subunits (Pac10p, Gim5p) and four beta-class subunits (Pfd1p, Gim4p, Gim3p, Yke2p) assemble into the heterohexameric prefoldin complex, which captures nonnative actin and tubulin polypeptides and transfers them to cytosolic chaperonin to drive protein folding and tubulin complex assembly.
