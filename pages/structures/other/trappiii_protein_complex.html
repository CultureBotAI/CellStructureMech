TRAPPIII protein complex

GO:1990072 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A complex that functions in anterograde transport at the Golgi and also regulates autophagy. In yeast it includes at least the following subunits: Bet3 (as homodimer), Bet5, Trs20, Trs23, Trs31, Trs33, Trs85. TRAPPIII may include further, as yet undescribed, proteins. [GO:1990072]
