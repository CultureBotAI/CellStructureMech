Seh1-associated complex

GO:0035859 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A GTPase-activating protein (GAP) complex that regulates TORC1 signaling by interacting with the Rag GTPase. In S. cerevisiae the complex contains Seh1p, Sec13p, Npr2p, Npr3p, Iml1p, Mtc5p, Rtc1p, and Sea4p. [GO:0035859]

Synonyms: GATOR complex; SEA complex

Has part: GO:1990130 GO:0061700
