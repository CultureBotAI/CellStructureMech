GARP complex

GO:0000938 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A quatrefoil tethering complex required for retrograde traffic from the early endosome back to the late Golgi and biogenesis of cytoplasmic vesicles. [GO:0000938]

Synonyms: Golgi associated retrograde protein complex; VFT tethering complex; Vps fifty three tethering complex

Mechanism graphs

GARP supports yeast endosome-to-Golgi retrograde transport (FUNCTION)

In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Vps51 joins Vps52, Vps53 and Vps54 to form the GARP complex, a trans-Golgi-network tethering complex that links retrograde endosome-to-Golgi transport to the t-SNARE Tlg1p.
