nuclear pore central transport channel

GO:0044613 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A central eukaryotic nuclear pore substructure composed of FG-repeat channel nucleoporins that forms the selective transport path for RNAs, proteins, and small molecules. [GO:0044613]

Synonyms: central channel

Part of: nuclear pore (GO:0005643)

Functions

  • selective nucleocytoplasmic transport channel — The nuclear pore central transport channel is the FG-repeat-rich path through which nucleocytoplasmic cargoes cross the pore.
    • GO:0044613 GO:0044613 defines the central transport channel as the NPC substructure through which RNAs, proteins, and small molecules pass.
    • DOI:10.1101/cshperspect.a000562 Wente and Rout 2010 review FG-repeat nucleoporins as central NPC transport factors.
