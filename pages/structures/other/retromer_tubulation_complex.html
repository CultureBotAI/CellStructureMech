retromer, tubulation complex

GO:0030905 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The dimeric subcomplex of the retromer, believed to be peripherally associated with the membrane. This dimeric complex is responsible for remodeling endosomal membranes to form a tube-structure to which cargo molecules are selected for recycling. The budding yeast complex comprises Vps5p and Vps17p, and may contain multiple copies of a Vps5p/Vps17p dimer. The mammalian complex contains SNX1 or SNX2 dimerized with SNX5 or SNX6. [GO:0030905]

Synonyms: SNX-BAR dimer; heterodimeric membrane-deforming retromer subcomplex; retromer complex, outer shell
