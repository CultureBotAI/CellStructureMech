SWI/SNF complex

GO:0016514 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A SWI/SNF-type complex that contains 8 to 14 proteins, including both conserved (core) and nonconserved components; contains the ATPase product of the yeast SNF2 or mammalian SMARCA4/BAF190A/BRG1 gene, or an ortholog thereof. [GO:0016514]

Synonyms: SWI-SNF complex

Mechanism graphs

SWI/SNF supports ATP-dependent chromatin remodeling (FUNCTION)

In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the Snf2 ATPase and accessory Swi/Snf subunits assemble into an ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler that changes nucleosome positioning and histone-DNA contacts.
