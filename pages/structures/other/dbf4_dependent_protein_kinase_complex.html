Dbf4-dependent protein kinase complex

GO:0031431 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A heterodimeric protein complex required for the activation of DNA replication origins; comprises a catalytic subunit and a regulatory subunit (in Saccharomyces, Cdc7p and Dbf4p, respectively); complexes identified in other species generally contain proteins related to the Saccharomyces proteins. [GO:0031431]

Synonyms: DDK; Cdc7-Dbf4 complex; Hsk1-Dfp1 kinase complex
