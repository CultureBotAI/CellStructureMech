Isw1b complex

GO:0036437 · Other · Multiprotein complex · Proposed — Drafted from literature to fill a coverage gap; not yet reviewed. How records are made

An Isw1 complex that binds DNA and has nucleosome-stimulated ATPase activity. In S. cerevisiae, contains an Isw1p ATPase subunit in complex with Ioc2p and Ioc4p. [GO:0036437]

Synonyms: ISW1b

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.
