clathrin coat

GO:0030118 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A membrane coat found on coated pits and some coated vesicles; consists of polymerized clathrin triskelions, each comprising three clathrin heavy chains and three clathrin light chains, linked to the membrane via one of the AP adaptor complexes. [GO:0030118]

Synonyms: clathrin cage
