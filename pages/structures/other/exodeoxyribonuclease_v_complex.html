exodeoxyribonuclease V complex

GO:0009338 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

Exodeoxyribonuclease V complex is a RecB-RecC-RecD enzyme complex that combines ATP-dependent DNA unwinding with exonucleolytic cleavage of DNA to produce 5'-phosphooligonucleotides. [GO:0009338]
