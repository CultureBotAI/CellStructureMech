GET complex

GO:0043529 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An endoplasmic reticulum protein-containing complex that is conserved in eukaryotes and mediates the insertion of tail-anchored proteins into the ER membrane. [GO:0043529]

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding yeast contains the GET complex, with Get3 coupling tail-anchored substrates to the Get1/Get2 ER receptor during ER-membrane insertion. [DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2008.06.025]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Budding-yeast model in which Get3 recognition of secretory-pathway tail anchors, Get1/Get2 receptor recruitment to the ER, and direct Get-mediated insertion were connected experimentally. [DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2008.06.025]

Functions

  • tail-anchored membrane protein insertion into ER membrane GO:0071816 — GET mediates post-translational delivery and insertion of newly synthesized tail-anchored proteins into the endoplasmic-reticulum membrane.
    • GO:0071816 GO:0071816 denotes tail-anchored membrane protein insertion into the endoplasmic-reticulum membrane.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2008.06.025 Schuldiner et al. 2008 showed in budding yeast that Get3 recognizes secretory-pathway tail-anchored protein transmembrane domains, that Get1/Get2 recruits Get3-TA complexes to the ER, and that purified Get proteins directly mediate insertion in vitro.
