EmrKY-TolC multidrug efflux transport complex

cellstructuremech:emrky_tolc_multidrug_efflux_transport_complex · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

An Escherichia coli MFS-family cell-envelope efflux pump complex composed of EmrK, EmrY, and TolC subunits that exports xenobiotics, including antimicrobial compounds, from the cell. [ComplexPortal:CPX-4273]

Synonyms: EmrKY-TolC multidrug efflux transport system
