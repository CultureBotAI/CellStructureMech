i-AAA complex

GO:0031942 · OTHER · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

Protease complex of the mitochondrial inner membrane whose catalytic residues lie on the intermembrane space side of the inner membrane; involved in mitochondrial protein turnover. Contains a subunit belonging to the AAA family of ATP-dependent metalloproteases. [GO:0031942]
