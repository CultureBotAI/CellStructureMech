Functions

  • microtubule organization — The SPB nucleates and organizes nuclear and cytoplasmic microtubules in budding yeast.
    • DOI:10.1146/annurev.cellbio.20.022003.114106 Jaspersen and Winey 2004 reviewed the SPB as the budding-yeast microtubule organizing center.
  • bipolar spindle assembly — SPB duplication and separation generate the two organizing centers that build a bipolar mitotic spindle.
    • DOI:10.1146/annurev.cellbio.20.022003.114106 Jaspersen and Winey 2004 reviewed SPB duplication and mitotic-spindle assembly.

SPB duplication creates two spindle organizers (FUNCTION)

A new SPB assembles next to the old SPB, matures as it is inserted into the nuclear envelope, and the duplicated pair separates to organize a bipolar spindle.
