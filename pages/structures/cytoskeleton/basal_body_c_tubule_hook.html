basal body C-tubule hook

cellstructuremech:basal_body_c_tubule_hook · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

A hook-shaped proteinaceous linker at the external B-C interface of a basal-body microtubule triplet. In the distal half of isolated Chlamydomonas reinhardtii basal bodies, hook densities occupy the C1 position, extend across the outer surfaces of C2-C4, and recur along the triplet axis. [DOI:10.1038/emboj.2011.460]

Part of: ciliary basal body (GO:0036064)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Functions

  • external B-C tubule linkage — In the characterized Chlamydomonas distal region, the hook bridges the B4/C1 interface and extends across the outer C2-C4 surfaces. This is a structural connection, not a demonstrated hook-specific requirement for assembly, force resistance or motility.
    • DOI:10.1038/emboj.2011.460 Regional cryo-electron tomography averages and Figure 4B resolve the exterior linker. B4 contact is described in the longitudinal-variation Discussion; molecular identity and assembly chronology remain interpretive.
