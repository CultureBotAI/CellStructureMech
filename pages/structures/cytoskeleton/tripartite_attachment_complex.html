tripartite attachment complex

GO:0120121 · CYTOSKELETON · OTHER · PROPOSED

A three-part cytoskeletal structure in kinetoplastid species linking mitochondrial DNA organised in a kinetoplast through the mitochondrial membranes to the basal body. [GO:0120121]

Synonyms: TAC

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5691 Trypanosoma brucei — Kinetoplastid model in which the TAC was defined ultrastructurally and later dissected by p166, TAC40 and TAC102 localization and knockdown studies. [DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1005586]

Functions

  • basal-body-linked kinetoplast segregation — The TAC physically links the kinetoplast DNA disk to the flagellar basal bodies so that basal-body segregation helps position duplicated mitochondrial genomes.
    • DOI:10.1091/mbc.E02-08-0525 Ogbadoyi, Robinson and Gull 2003 described a trans-membrane structural linkage responsible for mitochondrial genome positioning and segregation by flagellar basal bodies.
    • DOI:10.1038/sj.emboj.7601956 Zhao et al. 2008 linked p166 depletion to kinetoplast missegregation.
