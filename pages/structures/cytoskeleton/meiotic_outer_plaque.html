meiotic outer plaque

cellstructuremech:meiotic_outer_plaque · CYTOSKELETON · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A sporulation-specific cytoplasmic plaque that forms on meiosis II spindle pole bodies in budding yeast after outer-plaque remodeling and nucleates prospore membrane assembly. [DOI:10.1093/emboj/19.14.3657]

Synonyms: meiosis II outer plaque; MOP

Part of: GO:0005816

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding yeast is the characterized model in which sporulation-specific proteins remodel the cytoplasmic SPB outer plaque into a meiotic outer plaque. [DOI:10.1091/mbc.12.6.1611]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Budding-yeast MOP assembly, vesicle docking, and prospore membrane initiation have been dissected genetically and cytologically. [DOI:10.1091/mbc.E10-07-0563]

Functions

  • prospore membrane initiation — The meiotic outer plaque nucleates de novo prospore membrane formation on the cytoplasmic face of meiosis II spindle pole bodies.
    • GO:0031322 GO:0031322 defines prospore-specific SPB remodeling as recruitment of sporulation-specific proteins to the outer plaque to form the MOP.
    • DOI:10.1093/emboj/19.14.3657 Knop and Strasser 2000 analyzed the budding-yeast SPB outer plaque as the assembly site for prospore membranes.
  • prospore vesicle docking — The MOP acts as a docking complex for prospore-membrane precursor vesicles during Saccharomyces sporulation.
    • DOI:10.1091/mbc.E10-07-0563 Mathieson et al. 2010 used Mpc54 mutants to separate vesicle association from docking at the MOP and linked docking to exocyst recruitment.
