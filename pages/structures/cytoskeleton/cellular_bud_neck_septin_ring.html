cellular bud neck septin ring

GO:0000144 · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A ring-shaped structure that forms at the site of cytokinesis in the bud neck of a budding cell; composed of members of the conserved family of filament forming proteins called septins as well as septin-associated proteins. In S. cerevisiae, this structure forms at the time of bud emergence and the septins show a high rate of exchange. [GO:0000144]

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the cellular bud neck septin ring distinct from the bud-neck septin collar and split septin rings. GO:0000144 denotes the dynamic initial bud-neck ring that forms at bud emergence, GO:0032174 denotes the fixed hourglass-shaped collar that persists for most of the cell cycle, and GO:0032177 denotes the two split rings formed from the collar at cytokinesis. This record models only the initial ring state.
