hyphal septin band

GO:0032163 · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

A septin band, i.e. a diffuse ring composed of a series of septin bars running parallel to the long axis of the cell, located at the junction between the mother cell and the germ tube (hypha) of a fungal cell growing filamentously. [GO:0032163]

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the hyphal septin band distinct from the generic septin band and from localized septin-ring structures. GO:0032163 denotes only the diffuse septin-bar band at the junction between a filamentous fungal mother cell and germ tube. GO:0032158 is the broader noncytokinetic septin-band class, while GO:0032172 and GO:0032168 denote ring-shaped septin structures within germ tubes or hyphae.
