cortical microtubule cytoskeleton

GO:0030981 — cortical microtubule cytoskeleton · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

The part of the microtubule cytoskeleton that lies just beneath the plasma membrane in the cell cortex. In fission yeast (Schizosaccharomyces pombe), ciliates, apicomplexans, and microalgae, the cortical microtubule cytoskeleton includes astral and cortical microtubule arrays that interact with cortical dynein and plus-end capture factors to drive nuclear movement and maintain cell-cortex polarity. [GO:0030981 — cortical microtubule cytoskeleton]

Synonyms: cortical microtubule array

Part of: GO:0015630 — microtubule cytoskeleton (no local record); cell cortex (GO:0005938)

Has part: horsetail-astral microtubule array (GO:0032117); microtubule (GO:0005874)
