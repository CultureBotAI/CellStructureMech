bactofilin filament

cellstructuremech:bactofilin_filament · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

A cytoskeletal filament assembled from beta-helical bactofilin proteins. In bacteria, these filaments provide scaffolds for species-specific cellular organization, including polar stalk biogenesis in Caulobacter vibrioides. [DOI:10.1038/s41564-019-0544-0]

Synonyms: bactofilin cytoskeleton; Caulobacter BacA/BacB polar bactofilin scaffold

Part of: GO:0005856 — cytoskeleton (no local record)

Functions

  • Caulobacter stalk biogenesis scaffold — The BacA/BacB bactofilin structure in Caulobacter vibrioides recruits the PbpC peptidoglycan synthase to the stalked pole for stalk biogenesis.
    • DOI:10.1038/emboj.2009.358 Kuhn et al. 2010 showed that Caulobacter BacA and BacB recruit PbpC to the stalked pole and support stalk morphogenesis.
