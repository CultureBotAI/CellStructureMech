basal body triplet base

cellstructuremech:basal_body_triplet_base · CYTOSKELETON · OTHER · PROPOSED

A linker in the proximal region of a microbial ciliary basal body that connects the pinhead to the A-C linker. In Paramecium tetraurelia reconstructions it extends to the ArmB region of the A-C linker. [DOI:10.15252/embj.2020106246]

Synonyms: centriole triplet base

Part of: GO:0036064

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5888 Paramecium tetraurelia — Ninefold-symmetrized cryo-ET cross sections and a focused subtomogram average resolve the pinhead-to-A-C-linker connection. Methods describe cortical units isolated from d4-2 and Delta-CenBP1; Figure 5 is not assigned to one strain. The proximal-region analysis used 11 uncompressed tomograms containing 16 centrioles with intact proximal regions. [DOI:10.15252/embj.2020106246]
  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — In situ cryo-ET of FIB-milled mat3-4 cells and ninefold-symmetrized cross sections show the link in Figure 4. The Paramecium focused-map measurement is not transferred to this species. [DOI:10.15252/embj.2020106246]

Functions

  • ciliary basal body organization GO:0032053 — Provides a structural connection between the pinhead and A-C linker in the imaged proximal region. This is an observed spatial arrangement, not proof of a mechanical stiffness, essentiality or assembly time course.
    • DOI:10.15252/embj.2020106246 Figures 4 and 5 and the accompanying Results identify the connecting density; the Paramecium average locates its A-C-linker contact on ArmB.
