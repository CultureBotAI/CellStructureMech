septin ring

GO:0005940 · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

A tight ring-shaped septin-based cytoskeletal structure that forms at the cell cortex in the division plane, including the bud-neck septin ring of budding yeast and related fungal septin rings at septation sites. [GO:0005940]

Part of: cell cortex (GO:0005938)

Mechanism graphs

Budding-yeast septin hetero-octamers polymerize into a cortical ring (ASSEMBLY)

In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, septin proteins assemble into hetero-octameric rods, the rods polymerize into septin filaments, and septin filaments organize at the bud-neck cortex to form the septin ring.
