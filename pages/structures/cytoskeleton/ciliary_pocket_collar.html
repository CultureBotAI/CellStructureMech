ciliary pocket collar

GO:1990900 · CYTOSKELETON · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A constriction site at the junction of the plasma, flagellar and flagellar pocket membranes where the flagellum emerges from the cell body. Observed in some unicellular eukaryotic species such as Chlamydomonas, Giardia and Trypanosoma. [GO:1990900]

Synonyms: cilium pocket collar; flagellar pocket collar; flagellum pocket collar; FPC

Part of: GO:0020016

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5691 Trypanosoma brucei — Trypanosome model in which BILBO1 RNAi established that FPC assembly is required for flagellar-pocket biogenesis. [DOI:10.1371/journal.pbio.0060105]

Functions

  • flagellar pocket neck scaffolding — The FPC forms a cytoskeletal collar at the flagellar pocket neck where the flagellum exits the cell body.
    • DOI:10.3390/cells5010009 Perdomo et al. 2016 reviewed the T. brucei FPC as an essential cytoskeletal component at the neck of the flagellar pocket.
  • flagellar pocket biogenesis — BILBO1-dependent FPC assembly supplies a cytoskeletal framework required to build the trypanosome flagellar pocket.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.pbio.0060105 Bonhivers et al. 2008 showed that BILBO1 RNAi prevents formation of new FPC and FP structures in procyclic T. brucei and is lethal in procyclic and bloodstream forms.
  • pocket-hook-microtubule coupling — FPC4 connects BILBO1-containing collar material with the hook complex and the microtubule quartet near the flagellar pocket.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1006710 Albisetti et al. 2017 placed FPC4 at the FPC, hook complex and proximal microtubule quartet and proposed a tripartite association among those structures.
