ParM filament

cellstructuremech:parm_filament · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A plasmid-encoded bacterial actin-like cytoskeletal filament assembled from ParM that is captured and stabilized by ParR-parC plasmid-centromere complexes to support active segregation of low-copy-number plasmids such as Escherichia coli R1. [DOI:10.1093/emboj/cdf320]

Synonyms: R1 ParM filament; bacterial actin-like ParM filament

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: Actin-like ParM partition filaments are plasmid-encoded bacterial systems and are not asserted to be universal across Bacteria. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro2425]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:562 Escherichia coli — Host species for the low-copy-number R1 plasmid whose ParMRC partition system defined ParM filament-driven plasmid segregation. [DOI:10.1093/emboj/cdf320]

Functions

  • R1 plasmid segregation — ParR-parC complexes on paired R1 plasmids capture ParM filaments, and stabilized filament elongation moves sister plasmids apart before host-cell division.
    • DOI:10.1093/emboj/cdf320 Moller-Jensen et al. 2002 connected ParM filament assembly to active segregation of the R1 plasmid.
    • DOI:10.1126/science.1229091 Gayathri et al. 2012 reconstituted the antiparallel ParM filament spindle that drives plasmid segregation.
