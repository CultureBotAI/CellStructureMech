spindle pole body satellite

cellstructuremech:spindle_pole_body_satellite · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A transient cytoplasmic precursor of the daughter spindle pole body that forms at the distal tip of the half bridge during spindle pole body duplication in budding yeast. [DOI:10.1146/annurev.cellbio.20.022003.114106]

Synonyms: SPB satellite

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Budding-yeast reference system for the half-bridge-associated SPB satellite precursor that seeds daughter spindle pole body assembly. [DOI:10.1083/jcb.145.4.809]

Functions

  • daughter spindle pole body precursor — The SPB satellite is an early cytoplasmic intermediate that nucleates assembly of the daughter spindle pole body next to the existing SPB.
