axonemal microtubule doublet ribbon

GO:0160115 · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

A three-protofilament ribbon formed by A-tubule protofilaments A11, A12 and A13 plus associated microtubule inner proteins that separates the A- and B-tubule lumens within an axonemal doublet microtubule. [GO:0160115]

Part of: axonemal doublet microtubule (GO:0097545)

Functions

  • A-tubule ribbon stabilization — The A11-A13 protofilament ribbon and its associated MIPs form a stable axonemal doublet substructure that separates the A- and B-tubule lumens.
    • GO:0160115 GO defines the ribbon by A11-A13 protofilaments and associated MIPs that stabilize those protofilaments within the axonemal doublet.
    • DOI:10.1242/jcs.20.2.405 Linck 1976 found that flagellar doublet extraction leaves stable ribbons of three protofilaments with 16-nm axial repeats.
