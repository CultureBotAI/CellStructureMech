axonemal microtubule doublet ribbon

GO:0160115 · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A three-protofilament ribbon formed by A-tubule protofilaments A11, A12 and A13 plus associated microtubule inner proteins that separates the A- and B-tubule lumens within an axonemal doublet microtubule. [GO:0160115]
