BtubAB bacterial microtubule

cellstructuremech:btubab_bacterial_microtubule · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A bacterial cytoskeletal microtubule assembled from BtubA and BtubB tubulin homologs that form tube-like filaments in Prosthecobacter cells and in purified BtubA/B reactions. [DOI:10.1371/journal.pbio.1001213]

Synonyms: bacterial microtubule; bMT

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral grounding for Prosthecobacter BtubA and BtubB bacterial tubulins. This first record curates bacterial tubulins from BtubAB-forming Prosthecobacter without asserting that a broad FtsZ/tubulin-family GTPase domain or eukaryotic tubulin-family accession exactly denotes full-length BtubA/B microtubule subunits.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral grounding for BtubC/Bklc proteins. This first record curates BtubC as membrane-linking associated machinery for BtubAB bacterial microtubules without asserting that a tetratricopeptide-repeat or kinesin-light-chain-like domain accession exactly denotes this Prosthecobacter protein class.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): BtubC is associated Btub cytoskeleton machinery rather than an alternating bacterial tubulin subunit. BtubC decorates the outside of the BtubAB mini-microtubule and links Btub cytoskeletal filaments to membranes; BtubA and BtubB are the tubulin homologs that make the alternating protofilament polymer.
