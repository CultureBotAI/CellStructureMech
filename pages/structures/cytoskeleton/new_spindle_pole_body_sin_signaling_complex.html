new spindle pole body SIN signaling complex

GO:0160067 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A SIN signaling complex associated with the new mitotic spindle pole body during anaphase and characterized by the presence activated (GTP bound) GTPase (Spg1 in fission yeast) to activate SIN signaling. [GO:0160067]

Synonyms: new spindle pole body SIN signalling complex

Functions
