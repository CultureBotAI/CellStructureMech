Entodiniomorphid subkinetal myoneme system

cellstructuremech:entodiniomorphid_subkinetal_myoneme_system · CYTOSKELETON · OTHER · PROPOSED

A system of calmyonemin-containing contractile filament bundles associated with the bases of retractile apical ciliature in entodiniomorphid ciliates, characterized in Eudiplodinium maggii and Entodinium bursa. This record denotes the subkinetal bundles, not all other calmyonemin-positive arrays or the cilia themselves. [DOI:10.1016/S0248-4900(94)80014-6]

Synonyms: subkinetal myonemes

Functions

  • Apical ciliature retraction — The subkinetal contractile bundles support withdrawal of apical ciliature during stress-associated retraction; the molecular force-generating mechanism remains unresolved here.
    • DOI:10.1002/cm.970270208 Abstract places converging myonemes beneath retractile apical ciliary zones.
    • DOI:10.1016/S0248-4900(94)80014-6 Abstract explicitly describes subkinetal myonemes as contractile bundles powering ciliature retraction.
