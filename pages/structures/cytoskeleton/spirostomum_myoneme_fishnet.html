Spirostomum myoneme fishnet

cellstructuremech:spirostomum_myoneme_fishnet · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

A mesh-like cortical myoneme characterized in Spirostomum, containing intersecting centrin/spasmin-rich fibrillar bundles and giant Sfi1-like scaffold proteins. The extended network shortens during calcium-triggered cell contraction and is distinct from adjacent microtubule bundles and from an isolated scaffold-centrin protein complex. [DOI:10.1126/sciadv.add6550]

Synonyms: Spirostomum mesh-like contractile fibrillar system; myoneme

Functions

  • Calcium-triggered cell contraction — The cortical myoneme network contributes to rapid whole-cell shortening after calcium stimulation. This contractile role does not establish the complete molecular shortening mechanism or the energy source for subsequent re-extension.
    • DOI:10.1126/sciadv.add6550 In the RNAi experiments, a minority of type-E distorted cells appeared only in GSBP-targeted conditions and failed to contract during observation in 1 mM calcium. Mesh disruption was imaged in selected GSBP2-RNAi type-E cells. Other abnormal morphologies also occurred in controls and retained contraction.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.2601408123 Published abstract connects the imaged fishnet with calcium-triggered cell shortening and reports repeat-complex compaction in vitro. No full-native-mesh sufficiency claim, exact kinetics or binding stoichiometry is curated.
