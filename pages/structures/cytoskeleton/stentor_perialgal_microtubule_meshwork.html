Stentor perialgal microtubule meshwork

cellstructuremech:stentor_perialgal_microtubule_meshwork · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

An irregular cortical microtubule meshwork characterized in Stentor pyriformis, with gaps occupied by algal endosymbionts in perialgal vacuoles. The named basket system denotes host cytoskeletal architecture, not regular cages, the symbionts themselves, their vacuole membranes, or the longitudinal Km-fiber bundles. [DOI:10.1091/mbc.E24-12-0571]

Synonyms: microtubule basket system

Functions

  • Proposed cortical positioning of endosymbionts — The authors propose that the meshwork helps retain algal endosymbionts near the host cortex; a meshwork-specific causal requirement has not been established.
