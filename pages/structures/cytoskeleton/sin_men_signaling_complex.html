SIN/MEN signaling complex

GO:0160065 · CYTOSKELETON · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex associated with the mitotic spindle pole body during interphase and mitosis and comprises of the proteins of the septation initiation signaling network (SIN) of fission yeast or mitotic exit network (MEN) of budding yeast, organized by two scaffold/adaptor proteins. [GO:0160065]

Synonyms: SIN/MEN signalling complex; MEN signaling complex; SIN signaling complex

Part of: GO:0061499

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4896 Schizosaccharomyces pombe — COMMON: Fission yeast is the model organism for the septation initiation network branch of this SPB-associated complex; no broader fungal distribution is asserted here. [DOI:10.1038/emboj.2010.317]
  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding yeast is the model organism for the mitotic exit network branch of this SPB-associated complex; no broader fungal distribution is asserted here. [DOI:10.1016/j.semcdb.2012.04.001]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Reference budding-yeast system for the MEN branch of the yeast mitotic-exit signaling network. [DOI:10.1016/j.semcdb.2012.04.001]

Functions

  • SIN/MEN mitotic-exit signaling — The SPB-associated SIN/MEN signaling complex organizes septation-initiation and mitotic-exit signaling proteins in fission and budding yeast.
    • GO:0160065 GO:0160065 defines a mitotic-SPB-associated complex composed of septation initiation network or mitotic exit network proteins.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.semcdb.2012.04.001 Hergovich and Hemmings 2012 reviewed yeast SIN and MEN pathways as Hippo-related signaling pathways that control mitotic exit and cytokinesis.
