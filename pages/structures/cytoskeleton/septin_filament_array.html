septin filament array

GO:0032160 · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

Arrays of septin filaments, or bars, found in a series of filamentous structures. Such structures have been observed in the prospore membrane during spore formation in S. cerevisiae and in the chlamydospore membrane during chlamydospore formation in C. albicans. [GO:0032160]
