aster

GO:0005818 · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

An array of microtubules emanating from a spindle pole MTOC that do not connect to kinetochores. A radial, star-shaped array of cytoplasmic astral microtubules nucleated from a microtubule organizing center—such as the outer plaque of a yeast spindle pole body or a protist basal body/centrosome—with microtubule plus ends radiating toward the cell cortex. In budding and fission yeasts, filamentous fungi, and protists, the aster interacts with cortical dynein and Kar9/Bim1 capture machinery to position the mitotic spindle and drive nuclear migration. [GO:0005818]

  • dynein-driven meiotic oscillatory nuclear movement GO:0030989 — Mediated by aster (GO:0005818) in the radial astral microtubule aster. Provides the radial microtubule array that leads spindle-pole and nuclear oscillations during S. pombe meiotic prophase, acting with dynein-dynactin machinery.
