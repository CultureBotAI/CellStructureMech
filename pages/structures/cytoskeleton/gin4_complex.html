Gin4 complex

GO:1990317 · CYTOSKELETON · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein complex involved in septin ring formation during mitosis. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae it consists of BNI5, CDC3, CDC10, CDC11, CDC12, GIN4, NAP1 and SHS1. At least 2 GIN4 molecules are involved. [GO:1990317]
