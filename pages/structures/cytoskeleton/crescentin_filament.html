crescentin filament

cellstructuremech:crescentin_filament · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A bacterial intermediate filament-like cytoskeletal filament assembled from crescentin that localizes along the inner curvature of Caulobacter vibrioides cells and helps maintain their curved cell shape. [DOI:10.1073/pnas.2309984121]

Synonyms: CreS filament; bacterial intermediate filament-like crescentin filament

Functions

  • Caulobacter cell-curvature maintenance — The crescentin filament tracks along the inner curvature of Caulobacter cells and maintains the vibrioid or crescent cell shape.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.tim.2017.03.006 Woldemeskel and Goley 2018 review crescentin as the CreS-encoded Caulobacter cytoskeletal protein for curvature whose deletion yields straight rods.
    • DOI:10.1101/gad.1795509 Charbon et al. 2009 followed crescentin assembly, organization and dynamics in vivo.
