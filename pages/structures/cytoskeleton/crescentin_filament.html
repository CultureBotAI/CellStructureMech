crescentin filament

cellstructuremech:crescentin_filament · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A bacterial intermediate filament-like cytoskeletal filament assembled from crescentin that localizes along the inner curvature of Caulobacter vibrioides cells and helps maintain their curved cell shape. [DOI:10.1016/S0092-8674(03)00935-8]

Synonyms: CreS filament; bacterial intermediate filament-like crescentin filament

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:155892 Caulobacter vibrioides — Curved freshwater bacterium in which CreS loss produces straight rod-shaped cells. [DOI:10.1016/S0092-8674(03)00935-8]

Functions

  • Caulobacter cell-curvature maintenance — The crescentin filament tracks along the inner curvature of Caulobacter cells and maintains the vibrioid or crescent cell shape.
    • DOI:10.1016/S0092-8674(03)00935-8 Ausmees et al. 2003 showed that cells lacking crescentin become straight rods and that crescentin localizes to the inner cell curvature.
    • DOI:10.1101/gad.1795509 Charbon et al. 2009 followed crescentin assembly, organization and dynamics in vivo.
