Functions

  • doublet microtubule stabilization — Inner-sheath MIPs bind the luminal surfaces of axonemal doublets and help stabilize A- and B-tubule protofilament lattices against bending stress.
    • GO:0160110 GO defines the inner sheath as an A/B-tubule MIP network that helps stabilize the doublet microtubule.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-019-09051-x Owa et al. 2019 found that Chlamydomonas FAP45 and FAP52 are B-tubule luminal MIPs whose loss destabilizes axonemal doublet B tubules.
