meiotic nuclear membrane microtubule tethering complex

GO:0034993 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A nuclear membrane protein complex which connects the nuclear outer and inner membranes together, and links links the nuclear lumen to cytoplasmic microtubules during meiosis. A specialized nuclear-envelope-spanning multiprotein tethering complex assembled during meiotic prophase in fission yeast (Schizosaccharomyces pombe) and related fungi that links clustered meiotic telomeres (via Bqt1–Bqt2) through the inner nuclear membrane SUN protein Sad1 and outer nuclear membrane KASH protein Kms1 to cytoplasmic dynein (Dhc1) and the hrs1/mcp6-organized horsetail microtubule array. [GO:0034993]

Synonyms: LINC complex; LInker of Nucleoskeleton and Cytoskeleton complex; SUN-KASH complex

Part of: GO:0034992 — microtubule organizing center attachment site (no local record)
