TubZ filament

cellstructuremech:tubz_filament · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A plasmid-encoded bacterial tubulin-like cytoskeletal filament assembled from TubZ GTPase subunits that supports pBtoxis plasmid partitioning in Bacillus thuringiensis. [DOI:10.1101/gad.1546107]

Functions

  • pBtoxis plasmid partitioning — TubZ filaments interact with TubR-tubC partition complexes to support pBtoxis DNA partitioning and stable plasmid inheritance in Bacillus thuringiensis.
    • DOI:10.1101/gad.1546107 Larsen et al. 2007 connected TubZ treadmilling to stable inheritance of the Bacillus thuringiensis pBtoxis plasmid.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.1003817107 Ni et al. 2010 analyzed TubR DNA binding and TubZ recruitment in the pBtoxis partition system.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral grounding for plasmid-encoded filament-forming TubZ GTPases. This first record curates TubZ from the Bacillus thuringiensis pBtoxis partition system without asserting that a broad tubulin/FtsZ GTPase domain accession exactly denotes TubZ filament-forming proteins.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): TubR and tubC centromere DNA are graph-scoped partition adapters rather than TubZ filament constituents. TubR bound to the pBtoxis tubC centromere site engages TubZ filaments in the TubZRC partition system, but this centromeric complex is cargo-coupling machinery rather than a TubZ polymer subunit.
