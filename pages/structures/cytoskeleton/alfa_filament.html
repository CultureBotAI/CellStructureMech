AlfA filament

cellstructuremech:alfa_filament · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

A plasmid-encoded bacterial actin-like cytoskeletal filament assembled from AlfA ATPases that supports segregation of pLS32-family plasmids in Bacillus subtilis. [DOI:10.1038/sj.emboj.7601443]

Functions

  • pLS32-family plasmid segregation — AlfA filaments work with the AlfB adaptor and the parN centromere to support active segregation of pLS32-family plasmid DNA in Bacillus subtilis.
    • DOI:10.1038/sj.emboj.7601443 Becker et al. 2006 showed that AlfA is needed for active segregation of pBET131, a pLS32 derivative.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.1304127111 Polka et al. 2014 connected AlfA dynamics to AlfB and parN-dependent plasmid positioning.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral grounding for pLS32-family AlfA actin-like ATPases. This first record curates AlfA from the Bacillus subtilis pLS32-family partition system without asserting that a broad actin ATPase or bacterial actin-like family accession exactly denotes filament-forming AlfA proteins.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): AlfB and parN are graph-scoped centromere adaptors rather than AlfA filament constituents. AlfB bound to parN centromere DNA regulates AlfA filament organization in the partition system, but the component list keeps only the AlfA polymer subunit as a constituent of the filament itself.
