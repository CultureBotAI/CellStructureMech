bilobe structure

GO:0120120 · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

A cytoskeletal structure in some kinetoplastid species linking the structures of the ciliary pocket collar and the flagellum attachment zone. [GO:0120120]

Synonyms: kinetoplastid flagellar hook complex

Part of: GO:0005856 — cytoskeleton (no local record)

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:5691 Trypanosoma brucei — COMMON: The bilobe has been resolved cytologically in T. brucei as a hooked and hairpin-shaped cytoskeletal structure near the flagellar pocket collar, microtubule quartet and flagellum attachment zone filament. [DOI:10.1128/EC.05287-11]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5691 Trypanosoma brucei — Trypanosome model in which TbMORN1, TbCentrin2, TbCentrin4 and TbLRRP1 localized to the bilobe, with TbMORN1 and TbCentrin4 delineating the hooked and hairpin regions around the pocket-collar and FAZ-associated cytoskeleton. [DOI:10.1128/EC.05287-11]

Functions

  • flagellar-pocket and FAZ cytoskeletal linkage — The bilobe is positioned between the flagellar pocket collar and the flagellum attachment zone and is arranged around the microtubule quartet plus FAZ filament.
    • GO:0120120 GO defines the bilobe as a cytoskeletal structure in some kinetoplastids linking the ciliary pocket collar and flagellum attachment zone.
    • DOI:10.1128/EC.05287-11 Esson et al. 2012 resolved the TbMORN1/TbCentrin4 bilobe hairpin around the microtubule quartet and FAZ filament, and placed the hooked TbMORN1 arm above the BILBO1-marked flagellar pocket collar.
    • DOI:10.3390/cells4040726 Morriswood 2015 reviewed the bilobe as a flagellum-associated cytoskeletal structure of kinetoplastids.
