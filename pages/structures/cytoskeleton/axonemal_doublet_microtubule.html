axonemal doublet microtubule

Functions

  • axonemal doublet scaffold — The nine Chlamydomonas axonemal doublet microtubules provide the circumferential scaffold for axonemal dyneins, radial spokes, nexin links and flagellar bending.
    • DOI:10.1126/science.1128618 Nicastro et al. 2006 resolved the 9+2 Chlamydomonas axoneme and its doublet-associated dynein, radial-spoke and linker densities by cryoelectron tomography.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2019.09.030 Ma et al. 2019 resolved the native Chlamydomonas axonemal doublet with associated radial-spoke and nexin-dynein regulatory-complex densities.
