subpellicular microtubule

GO:0020025 · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

Functions

  • pellicle microtubule scaffold — Subpellicular microtubules form a longitudinal cortical microtubule scaffold beneath the inner membrane pellicle complex and alongside the pellicular intramembranous-particle lattice in Toxoplasma.
    • DOI:10.1242/jcs.110.1.35 Morrissette, Murray and Roos 1997 resolved a 32 nm periodicity in isolated Toxoplasma subpellicular microtubules and the overlying pellicle particle lattice.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.0020013 Hu et al. 2006 described the Toxoplasma subpellicular microtubules as cytoskeletal elements closely associated with the cytoplasmic face of the IMC.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact component boundaries and source-neutral groundings for subpellicular microtubule end proteins, plus-end factors, and IMC particle-lattice linkers. Morrissette, Murray and Roos resolved an intramembranous-particle lattice aligned with Toxoplasma subpellicular microtubules, and Hu et al. localized proteins such as TgDLC, TgMORN1 and TgCentrin2 near subpellicular microtubule ends or daughter scaffolds. This record leaves those factors out until exact conserved constituent, linker, marker or assembly-factor boundaries are curated.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep GO:0020025 scoped to apicomplexan subpellicular microtubules rather than kinetoplastid cortical microtubule arrays that share the same plain-English name. The phrase subpellicular microtubule is also used in Trypanosoma literature, but GO:0020025 is taxon-constrained to Apicomplexa and defined by apicomplexan IMC and conoid topology. This record therefore curates only the apicomplexan structure.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Use the exact basal SPMT-origin ring in the attachment graph. The former graph grounded the generic polar-ring name to GO:0020031, whose definition denotes the anterior conoid-fiber-origin ring. GO:0020025 and GO:0020032 identify the basal SPMT-origin topology, in agreement with the primary anatomical distinction.
