cryptophyte cortical centrin network

cellstructuremech:cryptophyte_cortical_centrin_network · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

A peripheral centrin-positive cytoskeletal network observed in cryptophyte cells by ultrastructure expansion microscopy. It forms a regular hexagonal mesh in Rhinomonas nottbeckii and shows variable network patterns among Rhodomonas isolates. The assembly is distinct from basal-body-associated centrin structures and from the periplast itself. [DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2025.09.027]
