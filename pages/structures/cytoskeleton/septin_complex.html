septin complex

GO:0031105 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex containing septins. Typically, these complexes contain multiple septins and are oligomeric. [GO:0031105]

Part of: septin cytoskeleton (GO:0032156 — septin cytoskeleton); cell cortex (GO:0005938)

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep oligomeric septin complexes distinct from higher-order septin filaments, rings, and collars. GO:0031105 denotes oligomeric septin protein complexes. GO:0005940 denotes higher-order cortical septin rings at division planes, and GO:0032173 denotes tubular septin collars assembled from ordered filament arrays.
