Arp2/3 protein complex

GO:0005885 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A stable protein complex that contains two actin-related proteins, Arp2 and Arp3, and five novel proteins (ARPC1-5), and functions in the nucleation of branched actin filaments. A conserved seven-subunit protein complex (comprising actin-related proteins Arp2 and Arp3 together with Arc40/ARPC1, Arc35/ARPC2, Arc19/ARPC3, Arc18/ARPC4, and Arc15/ARPC5) that binds to the side of a pre-existing mother actin filament and nucleates a daughter filament at a characteristic 70-degree angle. In yeasts, amoebae, and filamentous fungi, the Arp2/3 protein complex generates branched actin networks that drive clathrin-mediated endocytosis, phagocytosis, and pseudopod extension. [GO:0005885]

Part of: GO:0015629 — actin cytoskeleton (no local record)
