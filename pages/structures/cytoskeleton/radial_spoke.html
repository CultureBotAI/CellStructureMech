radial spoke

GO:0001534 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A ciliary and flagellar axonemal protein complex with a stalk attached to each outer-doublet A-tubule and a head that projects toward the central-pair microtubule sheath. [GO:0001534]

Part of: axoneme (GO:0005930)

Mechanism graphs

Head, neck and stalk organize the radial spoke (FUNCTION)

In Chlamydomonas flagella, the radial-spoke base binds an outer-doublet A-tubule, the stalk connects the base to the neck, and the neck connects to the head, which projects toward the central pair to help regulate axonemal beating.
