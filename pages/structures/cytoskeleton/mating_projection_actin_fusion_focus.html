mating projection actin fusion focus

GO:1990819 · CYTOSKELETON · OTHER · PROPOSED

A focus at the mating projection tip where the cell wall is degraded during cytogamy. Actin filaments form an aster-like structure from this location. [GO:1990819]

Fus1-nucleated actin asters concentrate hydrolase delivery for wall dissolution (FUNCTION)

During Schizosaccharomyces pombe mating, Fus1 nucleates actin filaments at the projection tip and the resulting actin fusion focus works with type V myosins to concentrate hydrolases at the cell-fusion site.
