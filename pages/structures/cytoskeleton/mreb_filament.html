MreB filament

cellstructuremech:mreb_filament · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

The actin-like cytoskeletal filament of rod-shaped bacteria. MreB polymerises into short membrane-associated filaments that circle the cell at an angle to its long axis and move processively around it, carrying the elongasome — the peptidoglycan synthases that widen the sidewall — so that new wall material is inserted where the filaments travel. It is a homologue of eukaryotic actin by fold, not by sequence identity. [DOI:10.1146/annurev-genet-102108-134845]

Synonyms: bacterial actin

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: Present in many rod-shaped and curved lineages and absent from most cocci, but the correlation is not tight: Actinobacteria and Rhizobiales are rod-like or curved and lack MreB altogether. [DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2018.02.050]
  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — RARE: Actin-fold proteins occur in archaea, but the MreB-elongasome system is a bacterial arrangement. [DOI:10.1146/annurev-genet-102108-134845]

  • KNOWLEDGE_GAP (OPEN): The filament guides the elongasome, and the schema has no relation that says so. It was briefly recorded as xrefs: [cellstructuremech:elongasome], but the schema defines xrefs as true equivalence and the two are not equivalent, so the claim was false as written and removed (#119).
