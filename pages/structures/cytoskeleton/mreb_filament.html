MreB filament

cellstructuremech:mreb_filament · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

The actin-like cytoskeletal filament of rod-shaped bacteria. MreB polymerises into short membrane-associated filaments that circle the cell at an angle to its long axis and move processively around it, carrying the elongasome — the peptidoglycan synthases that widen the sidewall — so that new wall material is inserted where the filaments travel. It is a homologue of eukaryotic actin by fold, not by sequence identity. [DOI:10.1146/annurev-genet-102108-134845]

Synonyms: bacterial actin

MreB and RodZ orient sidewall synthesis to preserve a rod (FUNCTION)

MreB subunits form membrane-associated filaments that RodZ couples to the elongasome. Those filaments orient circumferential sidewall peptidoglycan synthesis, and the resulting feedback keeps rod-shaped cells from widening into spheres.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): This is the corpus's first minted identifier — is the scheme right? GO has no cellular-component term for MreB or for a bacterial actin cytoskeleton, so docs/CURATION.md requires minting with the nearest broader term as a parent. The local part is a lowercase slug of the label rather than a counter, because a slug needs no allocator and reads in a URL; the cost is that it looks stale if the preferred label ever changes, which is acceptable since identifiers are never renamed.
  • KNOWLEDGE_GAP (OPEN): The filament guides the elongasome, and the schema has no relation that says so. MreB filaments carry the RodA-PBP2 synthases around the cell; that is the point of the structure. It was briefly recorded as xrefs: [cellstructuremech:elongasome], but the schema defines xrefs as true equivalence and the two are not equivalent, so the claim was false as written and removed (#119). Until a relation exists, the link lives in the RodZ component role and in this discussion.
