ciliary basal body

GO:0036064 · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

A membrane-tethered, short cylindrical array of microtubules and associated proteins found at the base of a eukaryotic cilium or flagellum that derives from a centriole, anchors the cilium and nucleates axonemal microtubule growth. [GO:0036064]

Synonyms: cilial basal body; cilium basal body; kinetosome; microtubule basal body; basal body

Part of: cilium (GO:0005929)

These observations distinguish normal triplet architecture from malformed basal-body formation but do not establish complete loss of all triplet material in every functional ciliary basal body. Mechanism graphs

The cartwheel organizes the triplet microtubule cylinder (ASSEMBLY)

A proximal cartwheel organizes ninefold basal-body symmetry, nine triplet microtubule blades form the basal-body cylinder, and the assembled basal body anchors the cilium and nucleates axonemal microtubules. This is a nonmechanistic summary. This graph summarizes major Chlamydomonas basal-body substructures and leaves cartwheel protein stoichiometry, centriole maturation, transition fibers and rootlet attachments for later component-level curation. The graph describes normal wild-type architecture, not an absolute substructure-essentiality claim or the morphology of every mutant basal body.
