Functions

  • A-tubule doublet scaffold — The complete A axonemal microtubule forms the 13-protofilament microtubule wall that supports B-tubule attachment, inner- and outer-junction contact, radial-spoke anchoring, and dynein-arm attachment.
    • GO:0097649 GO defines the A axonemal microtubule as the complete 13-protofilament tubule of the axonemal outer doublet.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2019.09.030 Ma et al. 2019 resolved the native Chlamydomonas doublet microtubule lattice with A-tubule-associated inner-sheath, inner-junction, outer-junction, ribbon and external densities.

Mechanism graphs

A axonemal microtubule supports the outer doublet (FUNCTION)

In Chlamydomonas, the complete A tubule is the 13-protofilament microtubule of the axonemal outer doublet, connects to the incomplete B tubule through inner and outer junction structures, and carries radial-spoke and dynein-arm attachments.
