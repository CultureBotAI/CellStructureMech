cellular bud neck split septin rings

GO:0032177 · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

Two separate septin rings that are formed from the septin collar at the time of cytokinesis in cells that divide by budding. These two rings are thought to delineate a special compartment in which factors involved in cytokinesis are concentrated. [GO:0032177]
