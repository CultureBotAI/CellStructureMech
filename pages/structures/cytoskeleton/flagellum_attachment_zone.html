flagellum attachment zone

GO:0120119 · CYTOSKELETON · OTHER · PROPOSED

A network of cytoskeletal and membranous connections responsible for the lateral attachment of the cilium to the cell body in some trypanosomatid species. [GO:0120119]

Synonyms: FAZ

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:5691 Trypanosoma brucei — COMMON: The FAZ runs alongside the attached flagellum in procyclic and bloodstream T. brucei forms and has been dissected by RNAi, tagging and microscopy. [DOI:10.1016/j.pt.2015.12.010]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5691 Trypanosoma brucei — Trypanosome model in which FAZ1, FAZ2 and CC2D were localized to the FAZ filament and depleted to test their roles in FAZ filament integrity, flagellum attachment and cell morphology. [DOI:10.1242/jcs.168377]

Functions

  • lateral flagellum-cell-body attachment — The FAZ links the motile cilium to the trypanosome cell body along the lateral flagellum attachment path.
    • DOI:10.1111/j.1550-7408.1999.tb04592.x Kohl et al. 1999 followed assembly of the flagellum attachment zone complex during the T. brucei cell cycle.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.pt.2015.12.010 Sunter and Gull 2016 reviewed the FAZ as the structure that mediates lateral attachment of the T. brucei flagellum to the cell body.
  • trypanosome cell morphogenesis — FAZ assembly and length help regulate trypanosome cell shape, cell length and positions of single-copy organelles.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.pt.2015.12.010 Sunter and Gull 2016 reviewed FAZ length and composition as regulators of trypanosome morphology and organelle positioning.
  • flagellum attachment zone organization — New FAZ proteins are incorporated near the proximal base of the FAZ filament as the trypanosome builds a daughter FAZ.
    • DOI:10.1242/jcs.168377 Zhou et al. 2015 found proximal incorporation of FAZ filament proteins during FAZ filament assembly.

FAZ filament proteins maintain lateral flagellum attachment (FUNCTION)

FAZ1, FAZ2 and CC2D are curated T. brucei FAZ filament constituents that contribute to the FAZ cytoskeletal filament; the larger FAZ network mediates lateral flagellum attachment and helps set trypanosome morphology.
