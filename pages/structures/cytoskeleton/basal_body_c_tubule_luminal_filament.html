basal body C-tubule luminal filament

cellstructuremech:basal_body_c_tubule_luminal_filament · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

A crescent-shaped proteinaceous filament inside the lumen of a basal-body C-tubule, spanning from protofilament C3 to the C6-C7 junction and occurring in a longitudinally repeated array in the distal half of isolated Chlamydomonas reinhardtii basal bodies. [DOI:10.1038/emboj.2011.460]

Part of: ciliary basal body (GO:0036064)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — Distal-half triplet reconstructions from isolated basal bodies; Figure 4C resolves the luminal filament separately from the exterior hook in Figure 4B. This species example does not imply occurrence in every strain or throughout the basal-body length. [DOI:10.1038/emboj.2011.460]

Functions

  • intraluminal C-tubule linkage — In the characterized Chlamydomonas distal region, the filament spans the C-tubule lumen from protofilament C3 to the C6-C7 junction. This is an observed structural connection, not a demonstrated requirement for stiffness, assembly or motility.
    • DOI:10.1038/emboj.2011.460 Longitudinal-variation Results and Figure 4C show crescent-shaped luminal densities. Mechanical reinforcement and contact with the separate exterior hook are proposed interpretations, not tested filament-specific functions.
