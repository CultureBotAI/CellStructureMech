Native GO wording that microtubules emanate from the SPB is retained, while the 2012 ultrastructure resolves their organization near but distinct from the SPB core.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Separate the array polymer inventory from the organizing-center composition. The initial constituent inventory covers alpha/beta-tubulin polymer classes. The 2012 study localizes Hrs1 and gamma tubulin to the rMTOC and tests Hrs1-dependent organization. These proteins are not automatically asserted to be array-lattice constituents; the center remains external assembly context in the graph. Whole-SPB, nuclear-envelope and bouquet membership is not inferred from spatial proximity or recruitment dependence. This boundary does not deny a physical connection to the array minus ends. A separate source-defined rMTOC record and more detailed membership evidence can refine the representation later.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Extend array-associated machinery and isoform coverage only with direct membership and role evidence. This is a bounded polymer inventory, not a complete HAA proteome. The Atb2 reference sequence is not the experimental strain. Beta-tubulin class composition does not justify an Nda3 example without an inspected isoform-specific experiment. Dynein-dynactin participates in movement, but its detailed array-resident composition and assembly essentiality need separate primary-source review before adding machinery rows. Unpublished observations about dynein-assisted radial rearrangement and suggested Hrs1/NudE similarity in the 2012 Discussion are not treated as verified family or component assignments.
  • KNOWLEDGE_GAP (OPEN): Resolve the physical minus-end attachment and nucleation mechanisms within the organizing region. Ultrastructure, immunolocalization and Hrs1 perturbations establish radial organization but not a purified molecular bridge or sufficiency of an isolated nucleation complex. Yeast-two-hybrid interactions with Kms1 and Alp4 do not establish complete native binding geometry. Gamma-tubulin enrichment is not a census of a fixed ring stoichiometry. Distances and microtubule counts in selected tomograms are assay-specific and are not imported as universal HAA dimensions.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Keep Hrs1 downregulation and meiotic transition phenotypes source-bounded. Hrs1 degradation and phosphorylation jointly downregulate the radial organizing system near meiosis I. Stabilized nonphosphorylatable Hrs1 can delay bipolar spindle formation; this is not a universal complete block, and outcomes depend on temperature and the spindle checkpoint. Phosphomimetic effects do not prove that every identified phosphorylation has the same native role. Ectopic Hrs1 produces HAA-like arrays in vegetative cells but does not turn every such cell into a native meiotic HAA exemplar. The later spindle is not the HAA, and disappearance of array signal is not direct proof that every tubulin subunit is degraded.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Preserve experimental, access and imaging limits. Only S. pombe is an organism exemplar; no clade prevalence, fixed whole-array copy number, trait or pathway relation is inferred. Homothallic live-cell imaging and synchronized engineered pat1 mat-Pc diploid EM experiments are distinct. Main Figure 2 text/captions specify 80 minutes for immuno-EM, whereas supplemental methods and the deletion-control caption specify 120 minutes; no single universal sampling time is assigned. Tomography used high-pressure freezing followed by freeze substitution and resin sections, not vitreous cryo-ET. The 2005 source is abstract-only; the 2012 main text, strain table and supplemental methods were read, but movies, raw data and most supplemental image panels remain uninspected. Figure 4 is unchanged CC BY 3.0 material and mixes live-cell images with biochemical and schematic panels; no dimension is inferred from its unquantified scale bars.
