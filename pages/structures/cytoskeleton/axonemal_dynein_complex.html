axonemal dynein complex

GO:0005858 · CYTOSKELETON · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A dynein complex found in eukaryotic cilia and flagella; the motor domain heads interact with adjacent microtubules to generate a sliding force which is converted to a bending motion. [GO:0005858]

Part of: GO:0005930

Axonemal dynein complexes drive microtubule sliding (FUNCTION)

In Chlamydomonas flagellar axonemes, inner and outer dynein arms are axonemal dynein complexes that project from outer doublet microtubules and drive interdoublet microtubule sliding.
