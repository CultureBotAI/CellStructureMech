septin cytoskeleton

GO:0032156 — septin cytoskeleton · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

The part of the cytoskeleton (the internal framework of a cell) composed of septins and associated proteins. Includes septin cytoskeleton-associated complexes. The network of GTP-binding septin hetero-oligomeric rods (Cdc3–Cdc10–Cdc11–Cdc12–Shs1 in Saccharomyces cerevisiae) and higher-order filaments, rings, collars, and gauzes associated with the inner face of the plasma membrane in yeasts, filamentous fungi, and green algae. The septin cytoskeleton acts as a cortical diffusion barrier and spatial scaffold that recruits cytokinetic, cell-wall-synthetic, and morphogenesis checkpoint proteins to the bud neck, hyphal branch sites, and prospore membranes. [GO:0032156 — septin cytoskeleton]

Has part: septin complex (GO:0031105); septin band (GO:0032158); cleavage apparatus septin structure (GO:0032161)
