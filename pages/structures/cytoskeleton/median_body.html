median body

GO:0097568 · CYTOSKELETON · OTHER · PROPOSED

A non-membrane bound, semi-organized microtubule array of unknown function found in Giardia species (trophozoite stage). It is located on the dorsal side of the trophozoite, slightly posterior to the ventral disc. [GO:0097568]

Synonyms: parabasal body

Mechanism graphs

Tubulin microtubules and Kinesin-13 shape the Giardia median body (REGULATION)

In Giardia trophozoites, alpha- and beta-tubulins polymerize into a semi-organized median-body microtubule array. Kinesin-13 and EB1 localize to the median body, and dominant-negative Kinesin-13 changes median-body volume.
