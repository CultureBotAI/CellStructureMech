outer plaque of mitotic spindle pole body

GO:0061499 · CYTOSKELETON · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

One of three laminate structures that form the mitotic spindle pole body; the outer plaque is in the cytoplasm. [GO:0061499]

Part of: GO:0044732

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding yeast is a characterized fungal model for the cytoplasmic outer plaque of the mitotic spindle pole body. [DOI:10.1146/annurev.cellbio.20.022003.114106]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Budding-yeast reference system in which the layered mitotic SPB and its cytoplasmic microtubule-nucleating outer plaque have been reviewed. [DOI:10.1146/annurev.cellbio.20.022003.114106]

Functions

  • mitotic cytoplasmic microtubule organization — The mitotic outer plaque is the cytoplasmic face of the budding-yeast SPB that organizes mitotic cytoplasmic microtubule nucleation.
    • DOI:10.1146/annurev.cellbio.20.022003.114106 Jaspersen and Winey 2004 reviewed the budding-yeast SPB outer plaque as the cytoplasmic site for microtubule nucleation.
