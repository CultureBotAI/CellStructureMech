outer dynein arm

GO:0036157 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An axonemal dynein complex that forms the outer dynein arm on ciliary and flagellar outer doublet microtubules and contains dynein heavy, intermediate and light chains. [GO:0036157]

Part of: axoneme (GO:0005930)
