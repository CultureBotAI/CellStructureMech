radial spoke stalk

GO:0001536 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

Protein complex forming the elongated portion of the radial spoke between the base which binds to the A-tubule of each microtubule outer doublet and the neck which connects to the spoke head within the ciliary or flagellum axoneme. [GO:0001536]

Part of: radial spoke (GO:0001534)

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the radial spoke stalk distinct from the whole radial spoke and adjacent head and neck subcomplexes. GO:0001536 denotes the elongated radial-spoke portion between the A-tubule-binding base and the neck, GO:0001534 denotes the complete radial spoke, GO:0120343 denotes the radial spoke neck, and GO:0001535 denotes the radial spoke head.
