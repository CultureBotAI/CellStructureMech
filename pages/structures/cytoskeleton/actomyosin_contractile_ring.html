actomyosin contractile ring

GO:0005826 · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A cytokinetic cytoskeletal ring composed of actin filaments, myosin, and accessory proteins that assembles beneath the plasma membrane and constricts during cell division. [GO:0005826]

Synonyms: contractile ring; cytokinetic actomyosin ring
