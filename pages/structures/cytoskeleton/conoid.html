conoid

GO:0020010 · CYTOSKELETON · OTHER · PROPOSED

A spiral cytoskeletal structure located at the apical end of the apical complex in some apicomplexan parasites. Fibers form a left-handed spiral, and are comprised of tubulin protofilaments organized in a ribbon-like structure that differs from the conventional tubular structure characteristic of microtubules. [GO:0020010]

Functions

  • apical cytoskeletal scaffolding — The conoid forms a spiral tubulin-based scaffold at the apex of the apical complex.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.200112086 Hu et al. 2002 resolved Toxoplasma conoid fibers as tubulin protofilament ribbons organized into a spiral.
  • conoid-associated host cell entry and exit — The conoid-associated MyoH motor supports Toxoplasma gliding motility, host-cell entry, and host-cell exit.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1005388 Graindorge et al. 2016 found that MyoH is indispensable for Toxoplasma entry and exit from host cells.
