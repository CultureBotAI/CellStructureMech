axonemal microtubule doublet inner junction

GO:0160113 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A structure that joins the B10 protofilament of the B tubule to the A1 protofilament of the A tubule within an axonemal microtubule doublet. [GO:0160113]

Part of: axonemal doublet microtubule (GO:0097545)

  • GO:0160113 GO defines the axonemal microtubule doublet inner junction by its position between the B-tubule B10 protofilament and the A-tubule A1 protofilament.
  • DOI:10.1091/mbc.E13-08-0464 Yanagisawa et al. 2014 identified Chlamydomonas FAP20 as an inner-junction component by mutant electron microscopy and cryo-ET localization.

Functions

  • A/B-tubule junction stability — The inner-junction protein bridge connects the B-tubule B10 protofilament to the A-tubule A1 protofilament and helps stabilize the axonemal doublet for ciliary and flagellar motility.
    • GO:0160113 GO defines the inner junction as the structure that joins the B10 protofilament to A1 within an axonemal doublet.
    • DOI:10.1091/mbc.E13-08-0464 Yanagisawa et al. 2014 reported that Chlamydomonas fap20 axonemes lack the inner junction and are liable to disintegrate during beating.
    • DOI:10.1091/mbc.E19-01-0063 Dymek et al. 2019 found that loss of PACRG and FAP20 removes the inner-junction bridge and reduces dynein-driven microtubule sliding.
