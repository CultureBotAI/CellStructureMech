nucleus-basal body connector

cellstructuremech:nucleus_basal_body_connector · CYTOSKELETON · OTHER · PROPOSED

A centrin-containing fibrous root system that links flagellar basal bodies to the nuclear surface. It is a specialized nucleus-associated rootlet system, not the entire basal apparatus or the fibers connecting basal bodies to one another. [DOI:10.1083/jcb.101.5.1903]

Synonyms: NBBC; nucleo-basal body connector; basal body-nucleus connector; rhizoplast; system II fiber
