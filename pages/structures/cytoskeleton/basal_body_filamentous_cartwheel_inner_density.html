basal body filamentous cartwheel inner density

cellstructuremech:basal_body_filamentous_cartwheel_inner_density · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed — Drafted from literature to fill a coverage gap; not yet reviewed. How records are made

An apparently continuous filamentous subassembly extending longitudinally within the lumen of a microbial basal-body cartwheel hub. It is morphologically distinguishable from the surrounding hub rings and spokes and from discontinuous cartwheel inner-density units. [DOI:10.15252/embj.2020106249]

Synonyms: filamentous cartwheel inner density; filamentous CID; fCID

Part of: basal body cartwheel (cellstructuremech:basal_body_cartwheel)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:285686 Teranympha mirabilis — Native basal bodies from hindgut flagellates of Reticulitermes speratus were examined by cryo-ET and subtomogram averaging. Preparation began with mixed Trichonympha agilis and Teranympha mirabilis cells, with a sedimentation step enriching the latter. Whole-cell morphology distinguished resin-embedded species; purified cryo-ET basal bodies were assigned using fCID presence or absence. This retains the source's morphology-based attribution, not an independent molecular identification. Figure 4 resolves fCID in both 64% and 36% cartwheel subvolume classes, which can coexist within one basal body. [DOI:10.15252/embj.2020106249]
