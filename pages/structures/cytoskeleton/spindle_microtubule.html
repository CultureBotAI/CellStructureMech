Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep spindle pole bodies and kinetochores as adjacent organizing or attachment structures rather than molecular components of the spindle microtubule. GO:0005876 denotes the anchored tubulin polymer that is part of a spindle. Spindle pole bodies and kinetochores physically interact with spindle microtubules in budding yeast, but each is a separately GO-denoted structure rather than a molecular constituent of the microtubule filament.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral groundings for spindle microtubule motors and microtubule-associated proteins. This record captures the GO-backed tubulin-polymer boundary for the spindle microtubule itself and defers the taxon-specific spindle motor and microtubule-associated protein set.
