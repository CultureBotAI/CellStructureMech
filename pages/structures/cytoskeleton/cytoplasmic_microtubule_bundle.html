cytoplasmic microtubule bundle

GO:1905720 · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

A microtubule bundle located in the cytoplasm. In fission yeast (Schizosaccharomyces pombe) and other microbial eukaryotes, cytoplasmic microtubule bundles consist of parallel or antiparallel alpha/beta-tubulin polymers nucleated by gamma-tubulin adaptor complexes (Mto1-Mto2) and crosslinked by Ase1/MAP65 family proteins and CLASP (Cls1/Peg1) to maintain cytoplasmic organization and cell-tip polarity. [GO:1905720]

Synonyms: cytoplasmic MT bundle

Part of: GO:0015630 — microtubule cytoskeleton (no local record); GO:0005737 — cytoplasm (no local record)

Has part: cytoplasmic microtubule (GO:0005881)

  • Polarity reestablishment after quiescence — Elongation of the pre-existing bundle and restoration of microtubule dynamics support timely return of polarity factors and actin organization to cell tips in refed S. pombe. Mediated by cytoplasmic microtubule bundle (GO:1905720) within the crosslinked cytoplasmic microtubule bundle.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.201502025 Figures 5 and 7: refeeding, MBC treatment, alp14 deletion and cold-treated mto1 deletion distinguish regrowth and polarity defects. The interventions also affect new microtubules, so they do not isolate an exclusive bundle-only pathway.

Mechanism graphs

Cytoplasmic microtubule bundle nucleation, MAP crosslinking, and cell-tip polarity maintenance (FUNCTION)

Alpha- and beta-tubulin polymers nucleated by Mto1-Mto2 adaptors, crosslinked by Ase1, and regulated by Cls1/Peg1, Alp7, XMAP215 (Alp14/Dis1), Klp2, and Tea1 assemble into cytoplasmic microtubule bundles (GO:1905720) that support cell-tip polarity reestablishment.
