axonemal nexin link

GO:0005931 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex in ciliary and flagellar axonemes that interconnects neighboring outer doublet microtubules. [GO:0005931]

Synonyms: axonemal interdoublet link; nexin-dynein regulatory complex; N-DRC

Part of: axoneme (GO:0005930)
