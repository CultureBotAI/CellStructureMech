distal connecting fiber

cellstructuremech:distal_connecting_fiber · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

A centrin-containing fibrous bridge connecting the distal regions of paired flagellar basal bodies in green algae. It is distinct from proximal connecting fibers and nucleus-directed rootlets; cross-striation differs between the documented examples. [DOI:10.1083/jcb.105.2.903]

Synonyms: distal basal body connecting fiber; distal striated fiber; DSF; distal fiber

Functions

  • interbasal-body linkage — The distal fiber forms a physical bridge between paired flagellar basal bodies, separately from the proximal fibers and nucleus-directed connectors.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.105.2.903 Ultrastructure and Figure 9 distinguish the interbasal bridge from adjacent root structures.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.119.6.1613 Distal half-fiber assembly and continuity between the basal bodies are assessed separately from other centrin structures.
  • calcium-dependent basal-body reorientation — Distal-fiber contraction reorients paired basal bodies in Spermatozopsis similis. Chlamydomonas preparations show smaller, reversible calcium-dependent angle changes; the two experimental responses are not treated as identical.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.105.2.903 Reorientation persists when beating is blocked or axonemes are mechanically removed, supporting a separate contractile apparatus.
    • DOI:10.1002/(SICI)1097-0169(1998)41:1<49::AID-CM4>3.0.CO;2-A Primary abstract reports calcium-dependent interaxonemal-angle changes in pf18 and pf18fa1 preparations and lack of that response in pf18vfl2. Full paper unread; vfl2 is not a selective distal-fiber perturbation.
