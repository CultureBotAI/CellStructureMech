ciliary centrin arm

GO:0120269 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A rod-shaped protein complex containing Centrin4 protein that flanks the flagellum attachment zone (FAZ) filament and the quartet microtubules. [GO:0120269]

Part of: flagellar pocket (GO:0020016)

Functions

  • hook-complex and FAZ filament flanking — The ciliary centrin arm flanks the flagellum attachment zone filament and the microtubule quartet in the T. brucei flagellar pocket-associated cytoskeleton.
    • GO:0120269 The GO definition places the ciliary centrin arm beside the FAZ filament and quartet microtubules.
    • DOI:10.3390/cells4040726 Morriswood 2015 described the Centrin4-positive centrin arm alongside the MORN1-positive hook complex, distinguishing these terms within the historical trypanosome bilobe region.
  • flagellum inheritance support — Hook-complex integrity and ciliary centrin arm assembly support flagellum inheritance in T. brucei.
    • DOI:10.1074/jbc.RA120.014237 Pham et al. 2020 connected maintenance of hook-complex integrity and centrin arm assembly to flagellum inheritance in T. brucei.
