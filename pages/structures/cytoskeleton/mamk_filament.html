MamK filament

cellstructuremech:mamk_filament · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

An actin-like bacterial cytoskeletal filament assembled from MamK proteins that runs alongside magnetosomes in magnetotactic bacteria and helps organize the organelles into chains. [DOI:10.1126/science.1123231]

Synonyms: MamK actin-like filament; magnetosome-associated MamK filament

  • DOI:10.1002/pro.2979 Bergeron et al. 2017 reported a cryo-EM structure of a polymerized MamK filament.

Functions

  • magnetosome chain organization — Organizes magnetosome organelles into an intracellular chain aligned with the cell axis.
    • DOI:10.1126/science.1123231 Komeili et al. 2006 showed that MamK is required for proper subcellular organization of magnetosome chains in AMB-1.
    • PMID:20487281 Katzmann et al. 2010 connected loss of MamK with magnetosome formation and chain-assembly defects in MSR-1.
