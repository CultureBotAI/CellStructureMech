inner plaque of spindle pole body

GO:0005822 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

One of three laminate structures that form the spindle pole body; the inner plaque is in the nucleus. [GO:0005822]

Part of: spindle pole body (GO:0005816)
