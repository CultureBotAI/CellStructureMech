basal ring of apical complex

GO:0020032 · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

An electron dense ring at the most posterior position of the apical complex, from which the subpellicular microtubules originate; formed during an invasive life cycle stage of an apicomplexan parasite. [GO:0020032]

Synonyms: lower polar ring of apical complex; posterior polar ring of apical complex

Part of: apical complex (GO:0020007)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:5811 Toxoplasma gondii — COMMON: Toxoplasma tachyzoites have a lower polar ring at the origin of the subpellicular microtubules. The conoid extends and retracts relative to this ring. [DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.0020013]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5811 Toxoplasma gondii — Apicomplexan model in which the conoid fibers, polar rings, intraconoid microtubules and subpellicular microtubules of the apical complex were resolved as distinct cytoskeletal elements. [DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.0020013]

Functions

  • subpellicular microtubule origin — The basal ring of the apical complex marks the origin of the subpellicular microtubules; it is distinct from the preconoidal rings at the conoid tip.
    • GO:0020032 Gene Ontology defines the basal ring of the apical complex by the origin of the subpellicular microtubules.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.0020013 Hu et al. 2006 distinguish the polar ring from which the 22 subpellicular microtubules originate from the distal preconoidal rings.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact basal-ring resident proteins and their source-neutral family groundings. This first basal-ring record captures the GO-delimited lower polar ring and its subpellicular-microtubule origin topology. It leaves protein components out until direct evidence places candidate proteins in the mature basal ring and an exact taxon-neutral family scope can be reviewed.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the basal or posterior polar ring distinct from the anterior polar ring of the apical complex. GO:0020032 denotes the lower/posterior/preconoidal ring from which subpellicular microtubules originate, whereas GO:0020031 denotes the anterior polar ring from which conoid fibers originate. The two terms therefore represent adjacent but separate apical-complex rings.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Separate the GO synonym conflict from the demonstrated basal-ring anatomy. The original discussion faithfully recorded a GO:0020032 exact synonym, preconoidal ring of apical complex. Hu 2006 instead places preconoidal rings at the distal conoid tip and the SPMT-origin polar ring at the base. Hu 2002 distinguishes the moving anterior ring/conoid from the lower ring. Thus the GO synonym conflicts with the primary anatomy and cannot establish biological equivalence.
