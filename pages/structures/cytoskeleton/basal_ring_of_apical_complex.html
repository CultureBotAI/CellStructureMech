basal ring of apical complex

GO:0020032 · CYTOSKELETON · OTHER · PROPOSED

An electron dense ring at the most posterior position of the apical complex, from which the subpellicular microtubules originate; formed during an invasive life cycle stage of an apicomplexan parasite. [GO:0020032]

Synonyms: lower polar ring of apical complex; posterior polar ring of apical complex; preconoidal ring of apical complex

Part of: GO:0020007

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:5811 Toxoplasma gondii — COMMON: Toxoplasma tachyzoite daughter scaffolds contain lower polar or preconoidal rings at the posterior end of the conoid, where subpellicular microtubules originate. [DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.0020013]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5811 Toxoplasma gondii — Apicomplexan model in which the conoid fibers, polar rings, intraconoid microtubules and subpellicular microtubules of the apical complex were resolved as distinct cytoskeletal elements. [DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.0020013]

Functions

  • subpellicular microtubule origin — The basal ring of the apical complex marks the posterior preconoidal ring from which apicomplexan subpellicular microtubules originate.
    • GO:0020032 Gene Ontology defines the basal ring of the apical complex by the origin of the subpellicular microtubules.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.0020013 Hu et al. 2006 localized the lower polar rings and the subpellicular microtubules in the Toxoplasma daughter apical scaffold.
