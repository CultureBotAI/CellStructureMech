Stentor Km fiber

cellstructuremech:stentor_km_fiber · CYTOSKELETON · OTHER · PROPOSED

A longitudinal cortical bundle of overlapping microtubule ribbons in Stentor, characterized ultrastructurally in S. coeruleus. Ribbons originate beside successive basal-body pairs and overlap alongside a ciliary row; the bundle is distinct from the deeper contractile myonemes. [DOI:10.1083/jcb.57.3.704]

Synonyms: km fibers

Part of: GO:0030863
