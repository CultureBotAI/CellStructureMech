cortical actin cytoskeleton

GO:0030864 — cortical actin cytoskeleton · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

The part of the actin cytoskeleton that lies just beneath the plasma membrane. In budding yeast (Saccharomyces cerevisiae), fission yeast, and other fungi and protists, the cortical actin cytoskeleton comprises Arp2/3-nucleated branched actin cortical patches and cortical actin filaments that drive endocytic vesicle internalization and cell-cortex remodeling. [GO:0030864 — cortical actin cytoskeleton]

Synonyms: submembranous actin cytoskeleton

Part of: GO:0015629 — actin cytoskeleton (no local record); cell cortex (GO:0005938)

Has part: actin cortical patch (GO:0030479); Arp2/3 protein complex (GO:0005885)
