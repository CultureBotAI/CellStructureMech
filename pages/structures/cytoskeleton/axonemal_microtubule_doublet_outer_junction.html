axonemal microtubule doublet outer junction

GO:0160114 · CYTOSKELETON · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A junctional axonemal doublet substructure that links the B-tubule B1 protofilament to A-tubule protofilaments A10 and A11. [GO:0160114]

Functions

  • outer-junction B-tubule stabilization — Outer-junction proteins span the A10, A11, B1 and B2 protofilaments of the axonemal doublet and help keep the incomplete B-tubule stably attached to the A-tubule.
    • GO:0160114 GO defines the outer junction as the structure joining B1 to A10 and A11 in the axonemal doublet.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-023-37868-0 Kubo et al. 2023 used Tetrahymena CFAP77/OJ2 structural modeling and CFAP77 knockout tomograms to connect the outer-junction proteins to outer-junction stability.
