spindle pole

GO:0000922 · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

Either of the ends of a spindle, where spindle microtubules are organized; usually contains a microtubule organizing center and accessory molecules, spindle microtubules and astral microtubules. In yeasts, fungi, microalgae, and protists, the two opposite spindle poles contain the spindle pole body or centrosomal MTOC together with minus-end focusing kinesin-14 motors, NuMA/Pkl1/Mto1 factors, and gamma-tubulin ring complexes that anchor kinetochore, interpolar, and astral microtubule minus ends. [GO:0000922]

Synonyms: mitotic spindle pole

Part of: spindle (GO:0005819 — spindle)

Has part: spindle pole body (GO:0005816)
