meiotic spindle pole body

GO:0035974 · CYTOSKELETON · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The microtubule organizing center that forms as part of the meiotic cell cycle; functionally homologous to the animal cell centrosome. [GO:0035974]

Functions

  • meiotic microtubule organization — Meiotic spindle pole bodies organize microtubules during the meiotic cell cycle.
    • GO:0035974 GO:0035974 defines the meiotic spindle pole body as the microtubule-organizing center formed as part of the meiotic cell cycle.
  • prospore membrane initiation site — In budding yeast, meiosis II SPBs carry a meiotic outer plaque that nucleates prospore membrane formation.
    • GO:0031322 GO:0031322 defines prospore-specific SPB remodeling as recruitment of sporulation-specific proteins to the outer plaque to form the meiotic outer plaque.
    • DOI:10.1093/emboj/19.14.3657 Knop and Strasser 2000 analyzed the budding-yeast SPB outer plaque as the assembly site for prospore membranes.
