ventral disc crossbridge

GO:0097595 · CYTOSKELETON · OTHER · PROPOSED

A fibrous structure that horizontally links adjacent dorsal microribbons in the ventral disc of Giardia species trophozoites. [GO:0097595]

Part of: GO:0097597

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:5741 Giardia intestinalis — COMMON: Giardia intestinalis is the diplomonad species in which ventral-disc crossbridges were mapped between adjacent dorsal microribbons. [DOI:10.1371/journal.pone.0043783]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5741 Giardia intestinalis — Parasitic diplomonad model used for electron-tomographic mapping of crossbridges in the ventral-disc microribbon lattice. [DOI:10.1371/journal.pone.0043783]

Functions

  • ventral disc microribbon lattice linkage — Crossbridges link neighboring Giardia ventral-disc dorsal microribbons into the lateral microribbon lattice above the microtubule spiral.
    • GO:0097595 Gene Ontology defines ventral-disc crossbridges as horizontal links between adjacent microribbons.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.pone.0043783 Schwartz et al. 2012 describe the crossbridges linking neighboring dorsal microribbons in the Giardia ventral disc.
