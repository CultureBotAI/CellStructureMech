ventral disc supernumerary microtubule array

GO:0097596 · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

A partial spiral microtubule array associated with the Giardia trophozoite ventral disc and distinct from its main microtubule array. Schwartz et al. 2012 reconstructed a short right-handed fragment lying ventral to the main array. [DOI:10.1371/journal.pone.0043783]

Part of: ventral disc (GO:0097597)

Has part: microtubule (GO:0005874)

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral grounding for the alpha- and beta-tubulins and accessory proteins that build the Giardia ventral-disc supernumerary microtubule array. This first ventral-disc supernumerary-microtubule record keeps only a broad alpha- and beta-tubulin component because no exact source-neutral CURIE was verified for the tubulins or accessory microtubule-associated proteins specific to the supernumerary array.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Preserve conflicting source descriptions of supernumerary-array position and handedness. GO:0097596 describes a partial left-handed array generally dorsal to the main array. The cited Schwartz et al. 2012 reconstruction instead shows a ventral array in Figure 1F and describes a short right-handed fragment in Results. Hagen et al. 2011 describe a left-handed partial array with slightly dorsal or ventral localization in Figure 5.

Evidence

  • GO:0097596 Gene Ontology cellular-component term for the Giardia ventral-disc supernumerary microtubule array.

  • DOI:10.1371/journal.pntd.0001442 Hagen et al. 2011 describe a partial left-handed supernumerary array; Figure 5 reports tagged supernumerary proteins slightly dorsal or ventral to the main array. These descriptions are retained as a conflict with Schwartz et al. 2012, not combined into a universal geometry.
