nuclear membrane microtubule tethering complex

GO:0106094 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A nuclear membrane protein complex that connects the nuclear inner and outer membranes and links nuclear structures across the nuclear envelope to cytoplasmic microtubules. In Schizosaccharomyces pombe and other fungi, nuclear membrane microtubule tethering complexes composed of the inner nuclear membrane SUN-domain protein Sad1 and outer nuclear membrane KASH-domain linker Kms1 (bridged during meiosis to telomeres by Bqt1-Bqt2) couple chromosomes and spindle pole body attachment sites across the nuclear envelope to cytoplasmic microtubule motors. [GO:0106094]

Synonyms: nuclear envelope microtubule tethering complex

Part of: nuclear envelope (GO:0005635); GO:0034992 — microtubule organizing center attachment site (no local record)

Functions

  • telomere organization GO:0032200 — Tethers telomere-bound Bqt1-Bqt2 connectors to the inner nuclear membrane SUN protein Sad1 and outer membrane KASH protein Kms1, clustering chromosome ends at the nuclear envelope during meiotic prophase in Schizosaccharomyces pombe.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2006.01.048 Chikashige et al. 2006 show that Bqt1-Bqt2 connect telomeric Rap1 to Sad1 to tether telomeres to the nuclear envelope.
    • DOI:10.1038/celldisc.2017.41 Live-cell imaging and conditional Bqt1 degradation show that the Bqt1-Bqt2-Sad1 tether maintains telomere attachment at the nuclear envelope.
  • Support of meiotic spindle formation — Maintained fission-yeast telomere-nuclear envelope tethering via Bqt1-Bqt2 and Sad1-Kms1 supports normal spindle pole body behavior and meiotic spindle formation.
    • DOI:10.1038/celldisc.2017.41 Premature Bqt1 degradation disrupts telomere-envelope tethering and increases meiotic spindle defects in S. pombe.
