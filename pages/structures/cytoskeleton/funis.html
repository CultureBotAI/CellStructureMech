funis

cellstructuremech:funis · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed — Drafted from literature to fill a coverage gap; not yet reviewed. How records are made

A Giardia trophozoite cytoskeletal structure composed of short microtubule arrays that emanate from the caudal flagellar axonemes, anchor to dense rods running parallel to the posterior-lateral flagella, and after posterior-lateral flagellar emergence anchor to the epiplasm beneath the contractile tail membrane. [DOI:10.1016/j.jsb.2004.01.017]
