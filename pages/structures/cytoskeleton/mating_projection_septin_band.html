mating projection septin band

GO:0032162 · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

A septin band, i.e. a diffuse ring composed of a series of septin bars running parallel to the long axis of the cell, located at the neck of a shmoo (mating projection). [GO:0032162]

Part of: mating projection (GO:0005937); septin cytoskeleton (GO:0032156 — septin cytoskeleton)

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the diffuse mating-projection septin band distinct from the ring-shaped GO:0032175 mating projection septin ring. GO:0032162 denotes a diffuse ring composed of parallel septin bars at the shmoo neck, whereas GO:0032175 denotes a ring-shaped septin structure. Longtine et al. 1998 describe Saccharomyces pheromone-response septins as a fuzzy band or bars, so this record captures the band morphology and leaves the GO:0032175 record separate.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve an exact source-neutral grounding for the grouped septin proteins component, if one exists. GO:0032162 supports septins as structural constituents of the shmoo-neck band, but septins are a multi-paralog family and this record deliberately avoids guessing one InterPro, Pfam, or NCBIfam accession for the complete fungal septin cohort.
