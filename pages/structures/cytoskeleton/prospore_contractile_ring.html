prospore contractile ring

GO:0032157 · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

A contractile ring composed of actin filaments and myosin that forms beneath the plasma membrane of the prospore envelope in meiotic cells in preparation for completing cytokinesis. [GO:0032157]

Synonyms: actomyosin ring; cytokinetic ring; meiotic contractile ring

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the prospore contractile ring distinct from generic actomyosin contractile rings and from prospore septin rings. GO:0032157 denotes an actin- and myosin-containing contractile ring that forms beneath the prospore-envelope plasma membrane during meiotic cytokinesis. GO:0005826 denotes the broader actomyosin contractile ring class, GO:0110086 denotes the broader meiotic actomyosin contractile ring class, and GO:0032169 denotes a separate prospore septin ring assembled from septins and septin-associated proteins.
