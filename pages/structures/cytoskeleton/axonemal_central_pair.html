axonemal central pair

GO:0097540 · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A pair of asymmetric C1 and C2 singlet microtubules at the center of the 9+2 axonemal central apparatus. [GO:0097540]

Synonyms: axonemal microtubule central pair; axoneme central pair; axoneme microtubule central pair; central pair; central-pair microtubules

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — Green-algal model whose flagellar central pair complex has been resolved in situ by cryoelectron tomography and subtomogram averaging. [DOI:10.1002/cm.21094]

Functions

  • C1/C2 central microtubule core — The axonemal central pair supplies the asymmetric C1/C2 singlet-microtubule core from which central-pair projections extend and around which the bridge network is organized.
    • GO:0097540 GO defines the axonemal central pair as the inner microtubule pair of the 9+2 axoneme.
    • DOI:10.1002/cm.21094 Carbajal-Gonzalez et al. 2013 resolved the Chlamydomonas central pair complex with asymmetric C1 and C2 microtubules, bridges and projections.
