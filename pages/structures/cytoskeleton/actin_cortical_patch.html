actin cortical patch

GO:0030479 · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

An endocytic patch formed from branched actin filaments just beneath the plasma membrane that assembles, moves, and disassembles rapidly. [GO:0030479]

Synonyms: actin patch

Part of: cell cortex (GO:0005938); cortical actin cytoskeleton (GO:0030864 — cortical actin cytoskeleton)
