telocentrosome

cellstructuremech:telocentrosome · CYTOSKELETON · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A telomere-associated microtubule organizing assembly formed at the nuclear envelope during meiotic entry in Schizosaccharomyces pombe. It recruits a gamma-tubulin nucleation complex to SUN/KASH-linked telomeres and generates cytoplasmic microtubules that support their clustering at the spindle pole body; it is distinct from the spindle pole body and the completed telomere bouquet. [DOI:10.1083/jcb.201207168]

Synonyms: meiotic telomeric MTOC

Functions

  • microtubule nucleation by microtubule organizing center GO:0051418 — Nucleates cytoplasmic microtubules at telomere-associated sites during the tested meiotic conditions.
