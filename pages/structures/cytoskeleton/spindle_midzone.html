spindle midzone

GO:0051233 · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

The area in the center of the spindle where the spindle microtubules from opposite poles overlap. In yeasts, fungi, and microbial eukaryotes, the spindle midzone is organized by antiparallel microtubule crosslinkers (Ase1/PRC1), kinesin-5 motors (Cin8, Kip1), and the chromosomal passenger complex (Ipl1/Aurora B, Sli15/INCENP, Bir1), driving anaphase B spindle elongation and stabilizing the central spindle. [GO:0051233]

Synonyms: central spindle overlap zone
