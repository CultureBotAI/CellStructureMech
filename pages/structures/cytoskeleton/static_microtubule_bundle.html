static microtubule bundle

GO:0099070 · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

A stable bundle of closely packed microtubules formed in quiescent cells, usually associated with the spindle pole body. In yeast cells entering quiescence, it assembles at the transition from proliferation to quiescence and disassembles rapidly upon cell cycle re-entry. [GO:0099070]

Synonyms: Q-MT bundle; quiescent cell MT bundle; quiescent cell microtubule bundle

Part of: GO:0015630 — microtubule cytoskeleton (no local record)
