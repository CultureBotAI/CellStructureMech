transition zone stellate fiber system

cellstructuremech:transition_zone_stellate_fiber_system · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

A centrin-containing system of luminal fibers forming a nine-pointed stellate array attached to the outer microtubule doublets of the Chlamydomonas ciliary transition zone. It is distinct from the peripheral Y-links and from the central cylinder around which the fibers extend. [DOI:10.1083/jcb.124.5.795]

Synonyms: transition zone stellate fibers; transition region stellate fibers; stellate structure

Part of: ciliary transition zone (GO:0035869)
