axonemal microtubule

GO:0005879 · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A microtubule in the axoneme of a eukaryotic cilium or flagellum; an axoneme contains nine modified doublet microtubules, which may or may not surround a pair of single microtubules. [GO:0005879]

Part of: GO:0005930

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — COMMON: Chlamydomonas flagellar axonemes contain a 9+2 axonemal microtubule scaffold with nine outer doublets and a central-pair region. [DOI:10.1126/science.1128618]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — Green-algal model whose 9+2 flagellar axonemal microtubule scaffold was resolved by cryoelectron tomography. [DOI:10.1126/science.1128618]

Functions

  • axonemal microtubule scaffold — In Chlamydomonas, specialized axonemal microtubules form the A/B outer doublets and C1/C2 central-pair singlets of the 9+2 flagellar axoneme.
    • GO:0005879 GO:0005879 defines axonemal microtubules as microtubules in the axoneme of a eukaryotic cilium or flagellum.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2019.09.030 Ma et al. 2019 resolved native Chlamydomonas axonemal doublet microtubules with A- and B-tubule protofilament walls.
    • DOI:10.1002/cm.21094 Carbajal-Gonzalez et al. 2013 resolved the Chlamydomonas axonemal central pair with asymmetric C1 and C2 singlet microtubules.
