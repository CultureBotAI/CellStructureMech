gamma-tubulin ring complex

GO:0000931 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A complex of gamma tubulin and associated proteins thought to be formed by multimerization of gamma-tubulin small complexes. An example of this structure is found in Schizosaccharomyces pombe. A large lock-washer-shaped multiprotein complex (gamma-TuRC) assembled from multiple gamma-tubulin small complexes (gamma-TuSCs) together with additional gamma-tubulin ring proteins (GCP4/Gfh1, GCP5/Mod21, GCP6/Alp16) and Mzt1. In fission yeast (Schizosaccharomyces pombe), filamentous fungi, and ciliated protists, gamma-TuRC localizes to spindle pole bodies, interphase and equatorial MTOCs, and basal bodies to nucleate and cap microtubule minus ends. [GO:0000931]

Synonyms: gamma-TuRC; gamma-tubulin large complex, centrosomal; gamma-tubulin large complex, eMTOC; gamma-tubulin large complex, equatorial microtubule organizing center; gamma-tubulin large complex, equatorial microtubule organizing centre; gamma-tubulin large complex, iMTOC; gamma-tubulin large complex, interphase microtubule organizing center; gamma-tubulin large complex, interphase microtubule organizing centre; gamma-tubulin large complex, mitotic spindle pole body; gamma-tubulin large complex, spindle pole body; gamma-tubulin ring complex, centrosomal; gamma-tubulin large complex
