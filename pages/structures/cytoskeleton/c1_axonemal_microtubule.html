C1 axonemal microtubule

GO:1990719 · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

The asymmetric C1 singlet microtubule of the axonemal central pair. [GO:1990719]

Synonyms: C1 tubule

Part of: GO:0097540

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — COMMON: Chlamydomonas flagellar central pair complexes contain an asymmetric C1 singlet microtubule with structurally distinct C1 projections. [DOI:10.1002/cm.21094]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — Green-algal model whose C1 microtubule and C1-associated central-pair projections were resolved by cryoelectron tomography and subtomogram averaging. [DOI:10.1002/cm.21094]

Functions

  • C1 projection scaffold — The C1 axonemal microtubule carries the structurally and biochemically distinct C1a, C1b, C1c, C1d, C1e and C1f central-pair projections.
    • GO:1990719 GO distinguishes the C1 axonemal microtubule from C2 by the differing protein components of their projections.
    • DOI:10.1002/cm.21094 Carbajal-Gonzalez et al. 2013 resolved the Chlamydomonas and Strongylocentrotus C1 microtubule projections, including newly assigned 1e and 1f densities.

Mechanism graphs

C1 axonemal microtubule carries C1 projections (FUNCTION)

In Chlamydomonas, the C1 singlet microtubule is one member of the axonemal central pair and carries C1-specific projections while the central bridge links it to C2.
