gamma-tubulin complex

GO:0000930 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A multiprotein complex composed of gamma-tubulin and other non-tubulin proteins. Gamma-tubulin complexes are localized to microtubule organizing centers, and play an important role in the nucleation of microtubules. The number and complexity of non-tubulin proteins associated with these complexes varies between species. A conserved multi-subunit protein complex containing gamma-tubulin (Tub4p in Saccharomyces cerevisiae) and gamma-tubulin complex proteins (GCPs such as Spc97p and Spc98p) that templates and nucleates microtubule polymerization at microtubule organizing centers, including fungal spindle pole bodies and protist basal bodies. By capping the minus end of nascent microtubules, the gamma-tubulin complex sets microtubule polarity and protofilament number. [GO:0000930]
