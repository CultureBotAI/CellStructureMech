septin cap

GO:0032159 · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A faint structure formed of septins found at the leading edge of growth in germ tubes and hyphae in fungal cells growing filamentously. This cap of septins colocalizes with a region of the plasma membrane that is rich in ergosterol. [GO:0032159]

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the septin cap distinct from narrower germ-tube and hyphal septin caps and from septin rings or collars. GO:0032171 and GO:0032164 are exact child terms for germ-tube and hyphal septin caps, whereas GO:0005940 and GO:0032173 model ring-shaped and collar-shaped septin structures.
