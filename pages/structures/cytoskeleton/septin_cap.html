septin cap

GO:0032159 · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

A faint structure formed of septins found at the leading edge of growth in germ tubes and hyphae in fungal cells growing filamentously. This cap of septins colocalizes with a region of the plasma membrane that is rich in ergosterol. [GO:0032159]

Part of: GO:0005856 — cytoskeleton (no local record)

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the septin cap distinct from narrower germ-tube and hyphal septin caps and from septin rings or collars. GO:0032159 denotes the parent septin-cap structure at the leading edge of filamentous fungal growth. GO:0032171 and GO:0032164 are exact child terms for germ-tube and hyphal septin caps, whereas GO:0005940 and GO:0032173 model ring-shaped and collar-shaped septin structures.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral family groundings for septin-cap septin proteins.
