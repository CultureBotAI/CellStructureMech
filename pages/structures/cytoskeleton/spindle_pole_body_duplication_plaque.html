spindle pole body duplication plaque

cellstructuremech:spindle_pole_body_duplication_plaque · CYTOSKELETON · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A transient cytoplasmic plaque intermediate formed after spindle pole body satellite expansion during daughter spindle pole body assembly in budding yeast. [DOI:10.1146/annurev.cellbio.20.022003.114106]

Synonyms: SPB duplication plaque

Functions

  • daughter spindle pole body plaque intermediate — The duplication plaque is an intermediate plaque in the transition from the SPB satellite to an inserted daughter spindle pole body.
