axoneme

GO:0005930 · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

A microtubule-based bundle with associated proteins that forms the core of a eukaryotic cilium or flagellum. [GO:0005930]

Has part: radial spoke (GO:0001534); axonemal nexin link (GO:0005931); axonemal central apparatus (GO:1990716); inner dynein arm (GO:0036156); outer dynein arm (GO:0036157); axonemal doublet microtubule (GO:0097545)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — COMMON: The 9+2 motile axoneme has been structurally characterized in Chlamydomonas reinhardtii flagella. [DOI:10.1126/science.1128618]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — Green-algal model whose demembranated flagellar axonemes were rapidly frozen for cryoelectron tomography. [DOI:10.1126/science.1128618]

Functions

  • Chlamydomonas flagellar beating — In the Chlamydomonas 9+2 flagellar axoneme, outer-doublet microtubules, axonemal dyneins, radial spokes, central-pair structures, and nexin links form a scaffold for flagellar bending.
    • DOI:10.1126/science.1128618 Nicastro et al. 2006 modeled force generation by dynein within the 9+2 axonemal microtubule scaffold.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.200908067 Heuser et al. 2009 connected the nexin link with axonemal dynein regulation in Chlamydomonas.

Mechanism graphs

Dynein and regulatory complexes bend Chlamydomonas axonemes (FUNCTION)

In Chlamydomonas, dynein arms connect outer doublet microtubules to generate sliding forces; radial spokes, central-pair structures, and axonemal nexin links organize and coordinate the 9+2 flagellar axoneme.
