radial spoke head

GO:0001535 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

Protein complex forming portion of the radial spoke that is orthogonal to the elongated stalk and which projects towards the central pair of microtubules within the ciliary or flagellum axoneme. [GO:0001535]

Part of: radial spoke (GO:0001534)

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the radial spoke head distinct from the whole radial spoke and adjacent neck and stalk subcomplexes. GO:0001535 denotes the terminal protein-complex portion of the radial spoke that projects toward the central pair, GO:0001534 denotes the complete radial spoke, GO:0120343 denotes the radial spoke neck, and GO:0001536 denotes the radial spoke stalk.
