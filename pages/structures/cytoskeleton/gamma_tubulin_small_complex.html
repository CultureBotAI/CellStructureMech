gamma-tubulin small complex

GO:0008275 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A complex usually comprising two gamma-tubulin molecules and two conserved non-tubulin proteins. Some gamma-tubulin small complexes are thought to be the repeating unit making up the core of the gamma-tubulin ring complex. A heterotetrameric core nucleation subcomplex (gamma-TuSC) composed of two molecules of gamma-tubulin (Tub4p in Saccharomyces cerevisiae) bound to one molecule each of GCP2 (Spc97p) and GCP3 (Spc98p). In budding yeast and other fungi lacking GCP4–6, gamma-TuSC is the fundamental building block that oligomerizes into a 13-protofilament helical ring upon binding Spc110p or Spc72p at the spindle pole body. [GO:0008275]

Synonyms: gammaTuSC; Tub4 complex; gamma-tubulin small complex, centrosomal; gamma-tubulin small complex, mitotic spindle pole body; gamma-tubulin small complex, spindle pole body
