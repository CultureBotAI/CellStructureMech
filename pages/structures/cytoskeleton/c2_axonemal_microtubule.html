C2 axonemal microtubule

GO:1990720 · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

The asymmetric C2 singlet microtubule of the axonemal central pair. [GO:1990720]

Synonyms: C2 tubule

Part of: GO:0097540

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — COMMON: Chlamydomonas flagellar central pair complexes contain an asymmetric C2 singlet microtubule with structurally distinct C2 projections. [DOI:10.1002/cm.21094]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — Green-algal model whose C2 microtubule and C2-associated central-pair projections were resolved by cryoelectron tomography and subtomogram averaging. [DOI:10.1002/cm.21094]

Functions

  • C2 projection scaffold — The C2 axonemal microtubule carries the structurally distinct C2a, C2b, C2c, C2d and C2e central-pair projections.
    • GO:1990720 GO distinguishes the C2 axonemal microtubule from C1 by the differing protein components of their projections.
    • DOI:10.1002/cm.21094 Carbajal-Gonzalez et al. 2013 resolved the Chlamydomonas and Strongylocentrotus C2 microtubule projections, including newly assigned 2d and 2e densities.

Mechanism graphs

C2 axonemal microtubule carries C2 projections (FUNCTION)

In Chlamydomonas, the C2 singlet microtubule is one member of the axonemal central pair and carries C2-specific projections while the central bridge links it to C1.
