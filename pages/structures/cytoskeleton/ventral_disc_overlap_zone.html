ventral disc overlap zone

GO:0097592 · CYTOSKELETON · OTHER · PROPOSED

A region of the ventral disc of Giardia species (trophozoite stage) where two portions of the same array of microtubules overlap (the microtubule array makes a complete circle and overlaps on itself). [GO:0097592]

Synonyms: overlap zone; ventral disk overlap zone

Part of: GO:0097597

Taxonomic distribution
