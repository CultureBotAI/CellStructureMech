Functions

  • MCC scaffold assembly — Pil1/Lsp1 eisosome filaments assemble along the cytoplasmic face of MCC membrane domains to help scaffold stable plasma-membrane furrows.

    • DOI:10.1146/annurev-micro-091313-103507 Douglas and Konopka 2014 reviewed MCC/eisosomes as stable plasma-membrane domains in fungi.
