eisosome filament

GO:0036286 · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

A filamentous cortical structure formed, in S. pombe, by the eisosome component Pil1. [GO:0036286]

Part of: eisosome (GO:0032126); GO:0030863 — cortical cytoskeleton (no local record)

Functions

  • MCC scaffold assembly — Pil1/Lsp1 eisosome filaments assemble along the cytoplasmic face of MCC membrane domains to help scaffold stable plasma-membrane furrows.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.201104040 Karotki et al. 2011 connected Pil1/Lsp1 filament assembly to a membrane scaffold at eisosomes.
    • PMID:25002088 Douglas and Konopka 2014 reviewed MCC/eisosomes as stable plasma-membrane domains in fungi.
