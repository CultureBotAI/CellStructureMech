hyphal septin ring

GO:0032168 · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A tight ring-shaped structure that forms in the division plane within hyphae of filamentous fungi at sites where a septum will form; composed of septins as well as septin-associated proteins. [GO:0032168]
