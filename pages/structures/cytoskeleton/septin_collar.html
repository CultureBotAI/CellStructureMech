septin collar

GO:0032173 · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A tubular, hourglass-shaped structure composed of highly ordered arrays of septin filaments; in budding yeast cells, the septin collar forms from the initial septin ring by expanding into the daughter cell. [GO:0032173]
