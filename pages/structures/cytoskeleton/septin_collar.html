septin collar

GO:0032173 · Cytoskeleton · Filament · Proposed

A tubular, hourglass-shaped structure composed of highly ordered arrays of septin filaments; in budding yeast cells, the septin collar forms from the initial septin ring by expanding into the daughter cell. [GO:0032173]

Synonyms: septin hourglass

Discussions

GO:0032173 denotes the broader hourglass-shaped septin filament collar class, while GO:0032174 narrows the same architecture to the cellular bud neck and GO:0062140 narrows it to the pathogenic-fungal hyphae collar at plant plasmodesmata.
