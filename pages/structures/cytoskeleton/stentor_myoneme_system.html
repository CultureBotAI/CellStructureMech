Stentor myoneme system

cellstructuremech:stentor_myoneme_system · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

The cortical system of longitudinal contractile filament bundles, historically termed M bands, characterized in Stentor coeruleus. Its prominent posterior myonemes are distinct from the adjacent microtubular Km fibers; this record does not denote every centrin-containing cortical assembly. [DOI:10.1126/science.177.4047.447]

Synonyms: Stentor M-band system; myonemes

Part of: GO:0030863 — cortical cytoskeleton (no local record)

Functions

  • Rapid cell-body contraction — The myoneme system contributes to shortening of the cell body in response to stimulation.
    • DOI:10.1126/science.177.4047.447 Primary high-speed cinematography follows M bands straightening before regional body shortening. Mechanical and electrical stimulation differ in the spatial progression of contraction; the signal is not directly identified.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cub.2026.06.062 Published abstract reports impaired or incomplete contraction in Sfi1 knockdowns with reduced myoneme cables.
