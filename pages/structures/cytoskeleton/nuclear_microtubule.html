Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep nuclear microtubules distinct from cytoplasmic microtubules and from array-specific spindle microtubules. GO:0005880 denotes microtubules in the nucleus and therefore differs from the cytoplasmic GO:0005881 class. It also differs from the GO:0005876 spindle microtubule class, which is defined by membership in a mitotic or meiotic spindle rather than by nuclear localization alone.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral groundings for nuclear microtubule-associated proteins and nucleation complexes.
