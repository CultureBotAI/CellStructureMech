sinister fiber

cellstructuremech:sinister_fiber · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

A striated, wedge-shaped fibrous linker connecting a flagellar basal body to a five-microtubule s-type root in Spermatozopsis similis. Its BAp95-containing assembly is distinct from the root microtubules, SF-assemblin fibers, centrin fibers and proximal interbasal plates. [DOI:10.1083/jcb.140.5.1149]

Synonyms: sf-fiber; 1sf-fiber; 2sf-fiber

Functions

  • basal-body to s-root linkage — The native fiber physically links a basal body with its five-microtubule s-type root. This structural linkage does not by itself demonstrate control of phototaxis, spindle placement or cleavage-furrow positioning.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.140.5.1149 Results and Figures 7-8 resolve the attachments. The larger 1sf is subdistal on the older basal body; 2sf is proximal on the younger one. Retention of 1sf after mechanical disintegration supports firm basal-body attachment.
