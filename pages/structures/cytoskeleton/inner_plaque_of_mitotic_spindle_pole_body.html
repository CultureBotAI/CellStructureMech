inner plaque of mitotic spindle pole body

GO:0061497 · CYTOSKELETON · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

One of three laminate structures that form the mitotic spindle pole body; the inner plaque is in the nucleus. [GO:0061497]

Functions
