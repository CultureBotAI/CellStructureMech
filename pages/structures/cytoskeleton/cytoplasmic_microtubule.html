cytoplasmic microtubule

GO:0005881 · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

Any microtubule in the cytoplasm of a cell. [GO:0005881]

Synonyms: non-spindle-associated astral microtubule

Part of: GO:0015630

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding yeast spindle pole bodies nucleate cytoplasmic microtubules from their outer plaques. [DOI:10.1146/annurev.cellbio.20.022003.114106]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Reference fungal model in which cytoplasmic microtubules are organized by the cytoplasmic face of the spindle pole body. [DOI:10.1146/annurev.cellbio.20.022003.114106]

Functions

  • cytoplasmic microtubule array organization — Cytoplasmic microtubules form non-nuclear tubulin-polymer arrays that can be organized from cytoplasmic microtubule-organizing centers such as the budding-yeast SPB outer plaque.
    • GO:0005881 GO:0005881 defines cytoplasmic microtubules as microtubules in the cell cytoplasm.
    • DOI:10.1146/annurev.cellbio.20.022003.114106 Jaspersen and Winey 2004 reviewed the budding-yeast SPB outer plaque as the cytoplasmic site for microtubule nucleation.
