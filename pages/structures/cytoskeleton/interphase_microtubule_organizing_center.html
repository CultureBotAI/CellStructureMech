interphase microtubule organizing center

GO:0031021 · Cytoskeleton · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A microtubule organizing center found in interphase cells, which organize a longitudinal array of three to five MT bundles from the nuclear envelope during interphase. Each MT bundle is composed of two to seven MTs arranged in an antiparallel configuration, with the dynamic MT plus ends extending toward the cell tips and stable minus ends near the nucleus. [GO:0031021]

Synonyms: iMTOC; interphase MTOC; interphase microtubule organising center

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Distinguish the organizing center from its arrays and site-specific mechanisms. GO retains a historical NE-oriented definition with bundle counts. Modern S. pombe papers also call microtubule-associated sites iMTOCs. This record follows that named non-SPB interphase organizing entity, not every interphase centrosome, the whole output bundle, eMTOC or nuclear pore. No universal NPC/NE parthood or count is asserted.
  • KNOWLEDGE_GAP (OPEN): Resolve native site architecture and assembly essentiality. Co-localization and engineered nucleation puncta do not establish invariant stoichiometry or a universal gamma-tubulin-ring geometry. Core assembly essentialities remain UNKNOWN rather than being inferred from cell viability or activity. Rsp1 is DISPENSABLE associated machinery because deletion retains newly formed non-SPB microtubules and NE-associated organizing components, despite major defects.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Find exact source-neutral families for the label-only classes. Mto1 and Alp7 matches are domains; Mto2's native InterPro endpoint returns no content. Rsp1's broad family IPR051964 is not exactly the narrow Rsp1-like class. XMAP215 family membership alone cannot establish residence of every member, including Dis1.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep site-specific docking factors separate from invariant core composition. Crm1 and Nup146 are demonstrated NE docking machinery, not evidence for the same composition at all iMTOCs; they remain explicit graph context rather than universal components. RanGTP is functionally required in the docking assay but was not enriched with NE-targeted Mto1, so physical membership is unresolved. Nup82/Nsp1 mass-spectrometry enrichment does not establish universal residence. No direct purified interface, high-affinity NES constant or cargo-handover reaction is asserted.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Do not convert recovery assays into normal-state rates or strict de novo counts. High-dose MBC can leave residual stubs; NE-only time-course selection and drug-induced redistribution limit rate comparisons. Alp14 loss retains short regrowth, and Alp7 loss shifts rather than universally abolishes output. Nup45-GBP tethering is engineered and only partially restores numbers. An Alp7-Mto1-Alp14 assembly is a proposed working model, not a purified stoichiometric complex.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Retain the difference between full-length and 9A1-NE Mto1 targeting. Alp7 supports NE localization of full-length Mto1 during MBC recovery, but is not required for localization of Mto1[9A1-NE] in the docking study. Different-region binding is a proposed reconciliation, not an experimentally resolved combined pathway.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Separate whole-cell Rsp1 loss from lattice-specific delivery and biochemical models. Rsp1 deletion redistributes Mto1 and leaves residual nucleation. Klp2 deletion instead removes much lattice Rsp1 while retaining SPB Rsp1 and does not detectably change NE/cytoplasmic nucleation in the later study. Purified Klp2-Rsp1 transport and Rsp1-Ssa1 effects on Mto1-Mto2/gamma-TuSC association support site-specific regulation, not invariant Klp2/Ssa1 membership. The 2018 turnover/affinity/size-control alternatives are not all resolved by those later assays. Whole-cell concentrations are not MTOC stoichiometry.
  • KNOWLEDGE_GAP (OPEN): Extend taxonomic and native structural evidence without importing inaccessible details. The direct mechanistic exemplar is S. pombe, not a fungal-wide presence frequency. Reference-sequence taxa do not identify every experimental strain. Detailed supplementary files and raw PRIDE datasets were not analyzed. The 2004 definition-linked source was read only as an abstract; it does not support uninspected quantitative or direct-binding claims.
