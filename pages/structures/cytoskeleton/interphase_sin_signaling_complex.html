interphase SIN signaling complex

GO:0160066 · CYTOSKELETON · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A SIN signaling complex associated with the old mitotic spindle pole body during interphase and early M-phase and characterised by the presence active ubiquitin ligase (Dma1 in fission yeast) and GTPase activator (Spg1 in fission yeast) to inactivate SIN signaling. [GO:0160066]

Synonyms: interphase SIN signalling complex

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4896 Schizosaccharomyces pombe — COMMON: Fission yeast is the system in which the interphase SIN signaling complex was defined at the old spindle pole body; no broader fungal distribution is asserted here. [DOI:10.1038/emboj.2010.317]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4896 Schizosaccharomyces pombe — Reference fission-yeast system for the old-SPB SIN configuration containing Dma1 ubiquitin-ligase and Spg1 GTPase-regulatory activities. [DOI:10.1038/emboj.2010.317]

Functions

  • interphase SIN inactivation — The old-SPB interphase SIN signaling complex contains inhibitory Dma1 ubiquitin-ligase and Spg1 GTPase-regulatory activities that keep septation-initiation signaling inactive before anaphase.
    • GO:0160066 GO:0160066 defines this complex by its association with the old mitotic spindle pole body and inactivation of SIN signaling.
    • DOI:10.1038/emboj.2010.317 Johnson and Gould 2011 studied Dma1-dependent regulation of the fission-yeast SIN scaffold Sid4 and Plo1 kinase localization at spindle pole bodies.
