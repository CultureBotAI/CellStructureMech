polar ring of apical complex

GO:0020031 · Cytoskeleton · Other · Proposed

An electron dense ring at the most anterior position of the apical complex, from which the conoid fibers originate; formed during an invasive life cycle stage of an apicomplexan parasite. [GO:0020031]

Synonyms: anterior polar ring of apical complex; upper polar ring of apical complex

Part of: apical complex (GO:0020007)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:5811 Toxoplasma gondii — COMMON: The anterior conoid-fiber-origin ring is represented in the Toxoplasma tachyzoite apical complex; modern preconoidal rings are distinguished from the fixed SPMT-origin APR. [DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.0020013]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5811 Toxoplasma gondii — Model in which distal preconoidal rings, conoid fibers and the separate SPMT-origin polar ring are distinguished. [DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.0020013]

Functions

  • anterior conoid-fiber origin — The anterior ring marks the distal origin of the conoid fibers, rather than the origin of subpellicular microtubules.
    • GO:0020031 The GO definition specifies the anterior ring from which conoid fibers originate.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.0020013 The Introduction places conoid-fiber origins at the distal preconoidal rings, separate from the SPMT-origin polar ring.

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep modern APR composition and gatekeeper evidence separate from GO:0020031. The former component list and graph assigned the fixed multilayer APR to the historical anterior conoid-fiber-origin ring. Tran 2010 places RNG1 below the extruded conoid at the SPMT-origin APR; Ren 2024 places APR2-to-APR7 across fixed APR layers. Koreny 2021 also distinguishes canopy rings from APR1/2. These names and layer numbers cannot be equated directly with the two GO ring identifiers.
