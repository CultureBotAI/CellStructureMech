polar ring of apical complex

GO:0020031 · CYTOSKELETON · OTHER · PROPOSED

An electron dense ring at the most anterior position of the apical complex, from which the conoid fibers originate; formed during an invasive life cycle stage of an apicomplexan parasite. [GO:0020031]

Synonyms: anterior polar ring of apical complex; upper polar ring of apical complex; apical polar ring

Part of: GO:0020007

  • NCBITaxon:5794 Apicomplexa — VARIABLE: The anterior polar ring is formed during invasive apicomplexan life-cycle stages; the Toxoplasma tachyzoite APR is the canonical experimentally resolved example in this record. [DOI:10.1371/journal.pbio.3001081]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5811 Toxoplasma gondii — Apicomplexan model in which APR layers, APR2-to-APR7 localization, and APR2-dependent APR integrity have been experimentally studied. [DOI:10.1073/pnas.2416602121]

Functions

  • apical F-actin flux control — The fixed multilayered apical polar ring helps route F-actin into the pellicular space during conoid extrusion.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.2416602121 Ren et al. 2024 showed that APR2 depletion disrupts APR integrity and leaks F-actin into the cytosol.
  • gliding motility and host cell invasion — The APR2-containing apical polar ring supports normal Toxoplasma gliding motility and host-cell invasion.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.2416602121 Ren et al. 2024 found that conditional APR2 depletion impaired Toxoplasma motility and invasion.
