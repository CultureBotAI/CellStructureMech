germ tube septin cap

GO:0032171 · CYTOSKELETON · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

A faint structure formed of septins found at the leading edge of growth in germ tubes of fungal cells growing filamentously. This cap of septins colocalizes with a region of the plasma membrane that is rich in ergosterol. [GO:0032171]
