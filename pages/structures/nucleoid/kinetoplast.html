kinetoplast

GO:0020023 · NUCLEOID · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

A sub-structure within the large single mitochondrion of kinetoplastid parasites and which is closely associated with the flagellar pocket and basal body of the flagellum. [GO:0020023]

Synonyms: kinetoplast DNA network; kDNA network

Part of: GO:0005739

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0150

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5691 Trypanosoma brucei — Trypanosomatid model in which the disk-shaped kDNA network, duplication cycle and basal-body-linked segregation have been studied by serial-section electron microscopy. [DOI:10.1128/MCB.01176-10]

Functions

  • mitochondrial genome organization — The kinetoplast packages the trypanosomatid mitochondrial genome as a condensed, topologically interlocked network of maxicircles and minicircles.
    • DOI:10.1128/EC.1.4.495-502.2002 Lukes et al. 2002 review the kinetoplast as an improbable network architecture for the kinetoplastid mitochondrial genome.
    • DOI:10.1093/femsre/fuac047 Amodeo et al. 2023 review mitochondrial genome maintenance with a focus on Trypanosoma brucei kDNA.

Mechanism graphs

Minicircles and maxicircles form the condensed kDNA network (ASSEMBLY)

Kinetoplast mitochondrial DNA is built from thousands of topologically interlocked minicircles plus dozens of maxicircles. The catenated DNA network and associated KAP proteins form a condensed disk-shaped kinetoplast inside the single mitochondrion.
