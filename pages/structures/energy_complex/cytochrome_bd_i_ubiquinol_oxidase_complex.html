cytochrome bd-I ubiquinol oxidase complex

cellstructuremech:cytochrome_bd_i_ubiquinol_oxidase_complex · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

Cytochrome bd-I ubiquinol oxidase complex is a membrane-bound CydAB respiratory terminal oxidase with small bd-I accessory subunits that oxidizes ubiquinol, reduces molecular oxygen to water, and contributes to the respiratory proton motive force. [DOI:10.1126/science.aay0967]

Synonyms: cytochrome bd-I oxidase

Functions

  • ubiquinol:oxygen terminal oxidase activity — Cytochrome bd-I oxidizes ubiquinol, reduces molecular oxygen to water, and conserves respiratory-chain energy through a bd-type architecture distinct from heme-copper cytochrome c oxidases.
    • InterPro:IPR002585 InterPro describes CydA-family proteins as alternative oxidases that oxidize ubiquinol and reduce oxygen as part of the electron transport chain.
    • DOI:10.1126/science.aay0967 Safarian et al. 2019 solved the E. coli cytochrome bd-I oxidase structure deposited as PDB:6RKO.
    • PMID:31604309 The PubMed abstract for Safarian et al. 2019 describes cytochrome bd-type quinol oxidases as respiratory-chain enzymes that catalyze molecular-oxygen reduction to water and are structurally unrelated to mitochondrial cytochrome c oxidases.
