cytochrome bd-I ubiquinol oxidase complex

cellstructuremech:cytochrome_bd_i_ubiquinol_oxidase_complex · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

Cytochrome bd-I ubiquinol oxidase complex is a membrane-bound CydAB respiratory terminal oxidase with small bd-I accessory subunits that oxidizes ubiquinol, reduces molecular oxygen to water, and contributes to the respiratory proton motive force. [DOI:10.1126/science.aay0967]

Synonyms: cytochrome bd-I oxidase
