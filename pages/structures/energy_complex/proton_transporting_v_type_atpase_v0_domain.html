proton-transporting V-type ATPase, V0 domain

GO:0033179 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex that forms part of a proton-transporting V-type ATPase and mediates proton transport across a membrane. The V0 complex consists of at least four different subunits (a,c,d and e); six or more c subunits form a proton-binding rotor ring. The integral membrane proton-translocating sector (V0 domain) of the proton-transporting V-type ATPase complex. Assembled in the endoplasmic reticulum of yeasts, fungi, and protists from the a-subunit stator (Vph1p/Stv1p) Containing two hemichannels, a decameric proteolipid c-ring rotor (Vma3p, Vma11p, Vma16p), and coupling subunits d (Vma6p) and e (Vma9p), the V0 domain conducts protons across endomembranes when driven by the V1 rotary motor. [GO:0033179]
