sodium-translocating NADH:quinone reductase complex

GO:0160292 · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

Sodium-translocating NADH:quinone reductase complex is a bacterial respiratory-chain protein complex composed of NqrA, NqrB, NqrC, NqrD, NqrE, and NqrF that transfers electrons from NADH to ubiquinone while exporting sodium ions across the cytoplasmic membrane. [GO:0160292]

Synonyms: Na+-NQR complex; Na(+)-translocating NADH-quinone reductase complex

Functions

  • sodium-pumping NADH:quinone oxidoreductase activity — Na+-NQR couples electron transfer from NADH to ubiquinone with export of sodium ions from the cytoplasm toward the periplasm.
    • GO:0160292 GO:0160292 defines the complex as the bacterial NqrABCDEF protein complex that couples NADH-to-ubiquinone electron transfer to sodium-ion transport from cytoplasm to periplasm.
    • PDB:7XK3 RCSB annotates all six PDB:7XK3 polymer entities with EC 7.2.1.1, NADH:ubiquinone reductase (Na+-transporting).
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-022-31718-1 Kishikawa et al. 2022 solved Vibrio cholerae Na+-NQR cryo-EM structures and describe the enzyme as coupling NADH-to-ubiquinone electron transfer with Na+ pumping.
