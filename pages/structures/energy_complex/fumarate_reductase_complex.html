fumarate reductase complex

GO:0045283 · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A membrane-bound four-subunit respiratory quinol:fumarate reductase complex whose extrinsic catalytic module and membrane-anchor module couple fumarate reduction to quinol oxidation. [GO:0045283]

Synonyms: quinol-fumarate reductase
