potassium ion-transporting ATPase complex

GO:0031004 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A heterotetrameric P-type ATPase complex (KdpFABC) in the prokaryotic plasma membrane that couples ATP hydrolysis in the KdpB P-type ATPase subunit to high-affinity potassium ion import through the channel-like KdpA selectivity filter and inter-subunit intramembrane tunnel, stabilized by KdpC and KdpF. [GO:0031004]

Synonyms: Kdp system complex

Part of: plasma membrane (GO:0005886)

Xrefs: ComplexPortal:CPX-3564
