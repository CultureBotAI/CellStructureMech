phycobilisome

GO:0030089 · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A membrane-associated light-harvesting pigment-protein complex of cyanobacteria and red algae, built from phycobiliprotein core and rod cylinders plus linker polypeptides, that funnels phycobilin excitation energy to photosynthetic reaction centers. [GO:0030089]

Synonyms: PBS

Part of: GO:0042651

Functions

  • photosynthetic light harvesting — Phycobilisomes absorb visible light through phycobilin-bearing rods and transfer excitation through the allophycocyanin core to photosystem II and, in some states, photosystem I.
    • DOI:10.1146/annurev-biophys-062920-063657 Sui 2021 reviews phycobilisome light capture and downhill excitation-energy transfer to photosystems.
  • photoprotection — Some cyanobacterial phycobilisomes switch to quenched or orange-carotenoid-protein-bound states that dissipate excess absorbed energy.
    • DOI:10.1146/annurev-biophys-062920-063657 Sui 2021 reviews phycobilisome quenching and photoprotective states in cyanobacteria.

Mechanism graphs

Rods and linkers funnel bilin excitation through the allophycocyanin core (FUNCTION)

Phycobilins covalently attached to rod phycobiliproteins absorb visible light. Linker proteins order stacked rod and core cylinders so excitation energy moves from rods into the allophycocyanin core and then into chlorophyll-containing photosystems.
