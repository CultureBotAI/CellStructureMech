vanadium-iron nitrogenase complex

GO:0016613 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An alternative nitrogenase enzyme complex containing a vanadium-iron active-site cluster (FeV-cofactor) expressed under molybdenum depletion in species such as Azotobacter vinelandii. Although the GO:0016613 ontology definition retains older generic alpha2-beta2 tetramer wording, structural resolution of the A. vinelandii VFe protein (PDB:5N6Y) shows a heterohexameric alpha2beta2delta2 catalytic dinitrogenase component (VnfD2K2G2) partnered with a homodimeric iron-protein nitrogenase reductase (VnfH2). [DOI:10.1038/nchembio.2428]

Mechanism graphs

VnfD2K2G2 and VnfH2 assembly and catalysis in the vanadium-iron nitrogenase complex (ASSEMBLY)

In Azotobacter vinelandii, VnfD alpha, VnfK beta, and VnfG delta subunits assemble the heterohexameric VFe catalytic component that partners with the homodimeric VnfH reductase to form the vanadium-iron nitrogenase complex and catalyze dinitrogen reduction.
