nitrate reductase complex

GO:0009325 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An enzyme complex that catalyzes the formation of nitrate from nitrite with the concomitant reduction of an acceptor. A molybdenum-cofactor- and iron-sulfur-containing multisubunit oxidoreductase complex (exemplified by the respiratory membrane-bound NarGHI complex in Escherichia coli and denitrifying bacteria, periplasmic NapAB, and assimilatory Nas/Nia complexes in bacteria, fungi, and microalgae) that catalyzes the two-electron reduction of nitrate (NO3-) to nitrite (NO2-). In anaerobic respiration, the membrane-bound nitrate reductase complex couples quinol oxidation in the cytoplasmic membrane to proton-motive force generation. [GO:0009325]

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — COMMON: Widespread across facultatively anaerobic and denitrifying bacteria expressing respiratory (NarGHI), periplasmic (NapAB), or assimilatory (Nas) nitrate reductase complexes. [PMID:12950096]
  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — VARIABLE: Present in denitrifying and nitrate-respiring haloarchaea and hyperthermophilic archaea. [PMID:12950096]

Functions

  • nitrate reductase (quinone) activity GO:0160182 — The NarGHI complex oxidizes quinol to quinone while reducing nitrate to nitrite during anaerobic nitrate respiration.
    • GO:0160182 GO:0160182 defines quinol-dependent nitrate reductase activity as catalysis of quinol plus nitrate to quinone, water, and nitrite.
    • PMID:12910261 Bertero et al. 2003 structurally traced the NarGHI respiratory electron-transfer route from NarI through NarH to the NarG Mo-bisMGD catalytic site.
