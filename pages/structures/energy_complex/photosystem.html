photosystem

GO:0009521 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A complex located in a photosynthetic membrane that consists of a photoreaction center associated with accessory pigments and electron carriers. Examples of this component are found in Arabidopsis thaliana and in photosynthetic bacterial and archaeal species. A pigment-protein supercomplex embedded in the photosynthetic thylakoid or cytoplasmic membranes of cyanobacteria, phototrophic bacteria, and eukaryotic microalgae that harvests light energy via chlorophyll/bacteriochlorophyll and carotenoid antennas and drives photochemical charge separation across a reaction center. Photosystems (including oxygenic Photosystem I and II and anoxygenic type I/II reaction centers) initiate photosynthetic electron transport coupled to ATP and NAD(P)H synthesis. [GO:0009521]

Synonyms: reaction center; reaction centre

Part of: photosynthetic membrane (GO:0034357)

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:32046 Synechococcus elongatus — Model oxygenic cyanobacterium in which the crystal structure of trimeric Photosystem I (PsaA/PsaB reaction center core) was determined. [PMID:11418848]
  • NCBITaxon:32053 Thermostichus vulcanus — Thermophilic cyanobacterium used for atomic-resolution crystallographic determination of native Photosystem II (D1/PsbA and D2/PsbD reaction center core). [PMID:21499260]

Functions

  • photosynthetic electron transport chain GO:0009767 — In oxygenic/cyanobacterial PSI, light excitation of P700 drives electron transfer through iron-sulfur centers toward ferredoxin, with upstream soluble donors restoring the reaction center. This P700 description does not cover all bacterial type-I reaction centers included in the generic GO definition.
    • GO:0009522 GO:0009522 defines PSI as a light-dependent plastocyanin-ferredoxin oxidoreductase in cyanobacteria and chloroplasts.
    • PMID:11418848 Jordan et al. 2001 connected the cyanobacterial PSI structure to light capture and electron transfer.
