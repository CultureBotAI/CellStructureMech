cytochrome o ubiquinol oxidase complex

GO:0009319 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A four-polypeptide respiratory-chain protein complex that possesses cytochrome o ubiquinol oxidase activity and contains associated prosthetic groups. [GO:0009319]

Functions

  • cytochrome bo3 ubiquinol oxidase activity GO:0009486 — Cytochrome bo3 ubiquinol oxidase oxidizes ubiquinol, reduces molecular oxygen to water, and moves protons to the periplasmic side of the respiratory membrane.
    • GO:0009486 GO:0009486 defines the cytochrome bo3 ubiquinol oxidase reaction with ubiquinol as donor, oxygen as acceptor, and proton release on the periplasmic side.
