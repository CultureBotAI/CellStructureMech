dimethyl sulfoxide reductase complex

GO:0009390 · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A membrane-bound anaerobic dimethyl sulfoxide reductase complex that catalyzes the reduction of dimethyl sulfoxide to dimethyl sulfide during respiratory electron transport. [GO:0009390]

Synonyms: dimethyl sulphoxide reductase complex; DMSO reductase complex
