phycobilisome rod

cellstructuremech:phycobilisome_rod · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

Peripheral phycobiliprotein cylinder subassembly of a phycobilisome that binds phycobilin chromophores, absorbs excitation energy, and transfers it inward to the allophycocyanin core. [DOI:10.1038/s41467-022-30962-9]

Synonyms: phycobilisome peripheral rod

Part of: phycobilisome (GO:0030089)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Peripheral rods absorb light and transfer excitation to the core (FUNCTION)

Rod phycobiliproteins bind phycobilin chromophores that capture light. Rod linkers stabilize disk stacking and tune the downhill transfer path toward the allophycocyanin core.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact InterPro, Pfam, NCBIfam, Complex Portal, or ChEBI accessions for phycobilisome rod phycobiliproteins, rod linkers, and bound phycobilins. This first rod child record keeps the mixed rod phycobiliprotein, linker, and chromophore groups reviewed-label-only because a broad phycobiliprotein family would blur rod constituents with core constituents.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the phycobilisome rod distinct from the whole phycobilisome and from the allophycocyanin core. GO:0030089 denotes the complete phycobilisome. The rod is a peripheral phycobiliprotein subassembly of the complete antenna, while the allophycocyanin core is a different central subassembly that accepts rod excitation.
