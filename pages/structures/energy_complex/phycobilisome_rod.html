phycobilisome rod

cellstructuremech:phycobilisome_rod · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

Peripheral phycobiliprotein cylinder subassembly of a phycobilisome that binds phycobilin chromophores, absorbs excitation energy, and transfers it inward to the allophycocyanin core. [DOI:10.1038/s41467-022-30962-9]

Synonyms: phycobilisome peripheral rod

Part of: phycobilisome (GO:0030089)

This first rod child record keeps the mixed rod phycobiliprotein, linker, and chromophore groups reviewed-label-only because a broad phycobiliprotein family would blur rod constituents with core constituents.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the phycobilisome rod distinct from the whole phycobilisome and from the allophycocyanin core. GO:0030089 denotes the complete phycobilisome. The rod is a peripheral phycobiliprotein subassembly of the complete antenna, while the allophycocyanin core is a different central subassembly that accepts rod excitation.
