proton-transporting two-sector ATPase complex, catalytic domain

GO:0033178 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex that forms part of a proton-transporting two-sector ATPase complex and catalyzes ATP synthesis or hydrolysis. The catalytic sector consists of a hexameric head and a central stalk; substrate binding and rotary motion of the central stalk is coupled to proton transport in the membrane sector. [GO:0033178]

Part of: proton-transporting two-sector ATPase complex (GO:0016469)
