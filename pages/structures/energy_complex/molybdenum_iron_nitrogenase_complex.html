molybdenum-iron nitrogenase complex

GO:0016612 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An enzyme complex containing a molybdenum-iron cluster found in many diazotrophic species. It is composed of two component proteins, molybdenum-iron dinitrogenase (MoFe protein, an alpha2beta2 heterotetramer NifD2K2 carrying P-clusters and active-site FeMo-cofactors) and nitrogenase reductase (Fe protein, a homodimer NifH2 carrying a subunit-bridging [4Fe-4S] cluster) that transiently dock to couple MgATP hydrolysis to electron transfer and dinitrogen reduction. [GO:0016612]

Mechanism graphs

Assembly and ATP-coupled dinitrogen reduction by the molybdenum-iron nitrogenase complex (ASSEMBLY)

NifD alpha and NifK beta chains assemble the heterotetrameric MoFe dinitrogenase core that docks with homodimeric NifH1 iron-protein reductase to form the molybdenum-iron nitrogenase complex and catalyze ATP-dependent dinitrogen reduction.
