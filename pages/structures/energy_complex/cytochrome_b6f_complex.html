cytochrome b6f complex

GO:0009512 · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A thylakoid membrane protein complex that oxidizes plastoquinol, reduces a downstream soluble electron carrier for photosystem I, and contributes to proton translocation during oxygenic photosynthetic electron transport. [GO:0009512]

Part of: GO:0042651

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:1117 Cyanobacteriota — COMMON: Cyanobacteria with internal thylakoids use cytochrome b6f as a major thylakoid electron-transport complex between plastoquinol and PSI. [DOI:10.1146/annurev-cellbio-120823-022747]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:103690 Nostoc sp. PCC 7120 = FACHB-418 — Cyanobacterial model used to purify and determine the crystal structure of a stable dimeric cytochrome b6f complex. [DOI:10.1074/jbc.M809196200]

Functions

  • photosynthetic electron transport chain GO:0009767 — Cytochrome b6f oxidizes plastoquinol downstream of PSII, reduces a soluble carrier for PSI, and couples that electron flow to thylakoid proton translocation.
    • GO:0009512 GO:0009512 defines the cytochrome b6f complex by electron transfer from reduced plastoquinone to oxidized plastocyanin with coupled proton translocation.
    • DOI:10.1146/annurev-cellbio-120823-022747 Perez-Boerema et al. 2024 place cytochrome b6f between PSII and PSI in the thylakoid electron-transport chain.

Cytochrome b6f couples thylakoid quinol oxidation to proton transfer (FUNCTION)

In oxygenic thylakoid electron transport, cytochrome b6f receives electrons from the plastoquinone pool, passes them toward PSI through a soluble carrier, and contributes to the proton motive force.
