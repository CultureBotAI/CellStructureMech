proton-transporting ATP synthase complex

GO:0045259 · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The rotary motor enzyme that makes ATP from ADP and phosphate using the ion motive force across the membrane. It has two coupled sectors: a membrane-embedded Fo, whose c-ring turns as protons cross it, and a soluble F1 (alpha3beta3gammadeltaepsilon), whose catalytic beta subunits are driven through their conformational cycle by the rotating gamma subunit. [GO:0045259]

Synonyms: F1Fo ATP synthase
