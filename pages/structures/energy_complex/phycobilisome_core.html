phycobilisome core

cellstructuremech:phycobilisome_core · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

Allophycocyanin-containing central cylinder assembly of a phycobilisome that receives excitation energy from peripheral rods and routes it toward chlorophyll photosystems. [DOI:10.1146/annurev-biophys-062920-063657]

Synonyms: allophycocyanin core

Part of: phycobilisome (GO:0030089)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Mechanism graphs

The allophycocyanin core routes rod excitation to photosystems (FUNCTION)

Peripheral phycobilisome rods transfer captured excitation to the central allophycocyanin core. Core bilins and terminal emitters route that energy toward chlorophyll-containing photosystems.
