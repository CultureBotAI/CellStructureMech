LH2 peripheral light-harvesting antenna complex

cellstructuremech:lh2_peripheral_light_harvesting_antenna_complex · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A peripheral bacteriochlorophyll-containing light-harvesting antenna protein complex of purple bacterial chromatophore membranes that broadens the photosynthetic absorption cross-section and transfers excitation energy toward RC-LH1 reaction-center core complexes. [DOI:10.1038/nature02823]

Synonyms: LH2 antenna complex; peripheral light-harvesting complex 2

Part of: GO:0042717

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — RARE: LH2 antenna complexes occur in the chromatophore photosynthetic membranes of purple bacteria rather than broadly across bacteria. [uniprot.location:SL-0042]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:1063 Cereibacter sphaeroides — Purple photosynthetic bacterium, still commonly cited under the synonym Rhodobacter sphaeroides, whose native chromatophore membranes were imaged with LH2 complexes surrounding RC-LH1-PufX core arrays. [DOI:10.1038/nature02823]

Functions

  • photosynthesis, light harvesting GO:0009765 — The peripheral LH2 antenna broadens bacteriochlorophyll light capture and transfers excitation energy inward toward reaction-center LH1 core complexes.
    • DOI:10.1038/nature02823 Bahatyrova et al. 2004 resolved LH2 antenna complexes around RC-LH1-PufX core arrays in native Rhodobacter sphaeroides photosynthetic membranes.
    • uniprot.location:SL-0042 UniProt SL-0042 lists antenna complexes among the contents of cellular chromatophore membranes.
