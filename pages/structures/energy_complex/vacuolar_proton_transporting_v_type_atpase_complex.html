vacuolar proton-transporting V-type ATPase complex

GO:0016471 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A proton-transporting two-sector ATPase complex found in the vacuolar membrane, where it acts as a proton pump to mediate acidification of the vacuolar lumen. [GO:0016471]

Synonyms: vacuolar hydrogen-translocating V-type ATPase complex

Part of: vacuolar membrane (GO:0005774)

Has part: vacuolar proton-transporting V-type ATPase, V0 domain (GO:0000220); vacuolar proton-transporting V-type ATPase, V1 domain (GO:0000221)
