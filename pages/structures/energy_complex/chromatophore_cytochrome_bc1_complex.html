chromatophore cytochrome bc1 complex

cellstructuremech:chromatophore_cytochrome_bc1_complex · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A purple-bacterial chromatophore membrane cytochrome bc1 protein complex that oxidizes quinol from the chromatophore quinone pool and returns electrons toward reaction-center antenna cores through soluble cytochrome c2 during cyclic photosynthetic electron transport. [DOI:10.1074/jbc.M412088200]

Synonyms: cytochrome bc1 complex

Part of: GO:0042717

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — RARE: Chromatophore cytochrome bc1 complexes occur in purple-bacterial photosynthetic membranes rather than broadly across bacteria. [uniprot.location:SL-0042]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:1063 Cereibacter sphaeroides — Purple photosynthetic bacterium, still commonly cited under the synonym Rhodobacter sphaeroides, whose chromatophore quinone traffic between reaction-center antenna cores and cytochrome bc1 has been modeled. [DOI:10.1074/jbc.M412088200]

Functions

  • photosynthetic electron transport chain GO:0009767 — The chromatophore cytochrome bc1 complex reoxidizes quinol carriers from reaction-center cores and returns electrons toward the reaction center through cytochrome c2.
    • DOI:10.1074/jbc.M412088200 Comayras et al. 2005 modeled chromatophore quinone-domain behavior around cytochrome bc1 and reaction-center antenna complexes.
