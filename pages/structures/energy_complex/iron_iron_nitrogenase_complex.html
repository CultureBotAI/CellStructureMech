iron-iron nitrogenase complex

GO:0016611 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An alternative nitrogenase enzyme complex containing an iron-only active-site cluster (FeFe-cofactor) expressed when both molybdenum and vanadium are depleted in diazotrophs such as Rhodobacter capsulatus and Azotobacter vinelandii. Although the GO:0016611 ontology definition describes an older alpha1-beta2 or alpha2-beta2 dinitrogenase stoichiometry, crystallographic and cryo-EM structures (PDB:8BOQ, PDB:8OIE) demonstrate a heterohexameric alpha2beta2delta2 catalytic component (AnfD2K2G2) partnered with a homodimeric iron-protein nitrogenase reductase (AnfH2). [DOI:10.1038/s41594-023-01124-2]

AnfD2K2G2 and AnfH2 assembly and catalysis in the iron-iron nitrogenase complex (ASSEMBLY)

AnfD alpha, AnfK beta, and AnfG delta chains assemble the heterohexameric FeFe catalytic component that docks with homodimeric AnfH reductase to form the iron-iron nitrogenase complex and catalyze ATP-dependent dinitrogen reduction.
