RC-LH1-PufX core complex

cellstructuremech:rc_lh1_pufx_core_complex · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A purple-bacterial chromatophore membrane protein complex that contains a photochemical reaction center, LH1 antenna proteins and PufX, receives excitation from surrounding LH2 antennae, and reduces quinone during cyclic photosynthetic electron transport. [DOI:10.1038/nature02823]

Synonyms: reaction center-LH1-PufX core complex

Part of: GO:0042717

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:1063 Cereibacter sphaeroides — Purple photosynthetic bacterium, still commonly cited under the synonym Rhodobacter sphaeroides, whose chromatophore membranes contain native RC-LH1-PufX core-complex arrays. [DOI:10.1038/nature02823]

Functions

  • photosynthetic electron transport chain GO:0009767 — The RC-LH1-PufX core receives excitation energy from LH2 antennae, performs photochemical charge separation, and reduces quinone in the purple-bacterial chromatophore membrane.
    • DOI:10.1074/jbc.M412088200 Comayras et al. 2005 modeled excitation transfer and quinone-domain behavior in Rhodobacter sphaeroides reaction-center antenna complexes.
