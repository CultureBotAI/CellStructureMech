NarGHI complex

GO:0044799 · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A cytoplasmic membrane-bound NarGHI nitrate reductase complex with iron-sulfur, heme, and molybdenum-bis(molybdopterin guanine dinucleotide) cofactors that serves as a terminal reductase during anaerobic nitrate respiration. [GO:0044799]

Synonyms: cytoplasmic membrane-bound quinol-nitrate oxidoreductase; nitrate reductase A; Nitrate reductase A complex
