nitrogenase complex

GO:0016610 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A nitrogenase enzyme assembly comprising a metal-cluster-containing dinitrogenase catalytic component and a homodimeric nitrogenase reductase that together support ATP-dependent dinitrogen reduction. Catalytic-component subunit composition varies among molybdenum, vanadium and iron-only nitrogenases. [DOI:10.1038/s41594-023-01124-2]

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.
