nitrogenase complex

GO:0016610 · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A nitrogenase enzyme complex composed of tetrameric dinitrogenase and homodimeric nitrogenase reductase subcomplexes that together catalyze ATP-dependent reduction of dinitrogen to ammonia. [GO:0016610]
