respiratory chain complex IV

GO:0045277 · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

Respiratory chain complex IV is a membrane-bound cytochrome c oxidase complex that transfers electrons from reduced cytochrome c to dioxygen and couples oxygen reduction to proton translocation across a respiratory membrane. [GO:0045277]

Synonyms: cytochrome c oxidase complex; electron transport complex IV
