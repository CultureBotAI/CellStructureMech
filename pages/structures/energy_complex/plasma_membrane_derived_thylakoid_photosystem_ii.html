plasma membrane-derived thylakoid photosystem II

GO:0030096 · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A photosystem II protein complex located in the plasma membrane-derived bacterial thylakoid that contains a P680 reaction center and catalyzes the water-oxidizing, plastoquinone-reducing step of oxygenic photosynthesis. [GO:0030096]

Synonyms: cyanobacterial PSII

Part of: GO:0031676

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:1117 Cyanobacteriota — COMMON: Cyanobacteria with internal thylakoids carry PSII complexes in plasma membrane-derived thylakoid membranes as part of oxygenic photosynthetic electron transport. [DOI:10.1146/annurev-cellbio-120823-022747]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:103690 Nostoc sp. PCC 7120 = FACHB-418 — Cyanobacterial model used for single-particle EM of intact phycobilisomes associated with photosystem II assemblies. [DOI:10.1038/cr.2015.59]

Functions
