formate hydrogenlyase complex

cellstructuremech:formate_hydrogenlyase_complex · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A membrane-bound Escherichia coli formate hydrogenlyase complex in which FdhF and HycB-HycG subunits couple formate oxidation to proton reduction, yielding carbon dioxide and hydrogen gas. [DOI:10.1038/s41467-022-32831-x]

Synonyms: FHL complex

Functions

  • formate hydrogenlyase activity — The membrane-bound FHL complex oxidizes formate to carbon dioxide and transfers the released electrons through the Hyc module to reduce protons to molecular hydrogen.
    • PMID:36104349 Steinhilper et al. 2022 describe E. coli FHL as a membrane-bound complex that couples formate oxidation by FdhF to proton reduction at the Hyc hydrogenase module.
