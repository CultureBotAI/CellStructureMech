methyl coenzyme M reductase complex

GO:0044674 · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

Methyl coenzyme M reductase complex is a hexameric archaeal alpha2 beta2 gamma2 enzyme complex that catalyzes coenzyme B-dependent reduction of methyl-coenzyme M to methane during the terminal step of methanogenesis. [GO:0044674]

MCR reduces methyl-coenzyme M to methane (FUNCTION)

The alpha2 beta2 gamma2 methyl coenzyme M reductase complex catalyzes the final methanogenesis reaction, consuming coenzyme B and methyl-coenzyme M while releasing methane and coenzyme M-coenzyme B heterodisulfide.
