archaeal proton-transporting A-type ATPase complex

GO:1990490 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed — Drafted from literature to fill a coverage gap; not yet reviewed. How records are made

A large proton-transporting two-sector ATPase protein complex that catalyzes the synthesis or hydrolysis of ATP by a rotational mechanism, coupled to the transport of protons across a membrane and is found in Archaea. [GO:1990490]

Synonyms: A-type ATPase protein complex; archaeal A-type ATPase protein complex

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.
