formate dehydrogenase complex

GO:0009326 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An enzyme complex that catalyzes the dehydrogenation of formate to produce carbon dioxide (CO2). A multisubunit molybdenum/tungsten-cofactor- and iron-sulfur-cluster-containing oxidoreductase complex in bacteria and archaea (including respiratory membrane-bound Fdh-N/FdnGHI and Fdh-O/FdoGHI in Escherichia coli and cytoplasmic NAD+/F420-linked formate dehydrogenases in methanogens and methylotrophs) that catalyzes the two-electron oxidation of formate (HCOO-) to CO2 and H+. Respiratory formate dehydrogenase complexes couple periplasmic formate oxidation to menaquinone reduction via a redox loop that conserves energy as proton-motive force. [GO:0009326]
