chlorosome baseplate antenna complex

cellstructuremech:chlorosome_baseplate_antenna_complex · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A bacteriochlorophyll a-CsmA light-harvesting protein-pigment array on one face of green bacterial chlorosomes that accepts excitation energy from chlorosome bacteriochlorophyll aggregates and couples it toward membrane reaction-center complexes. [DOI:10.1038/ncomms12454]

Synonyms: CsmA baseplate antenna; CsmA bacteriochlorophyll a baseplate; protein-pigment baseplate complex

Part of: GO:0046858 GO:0033105
