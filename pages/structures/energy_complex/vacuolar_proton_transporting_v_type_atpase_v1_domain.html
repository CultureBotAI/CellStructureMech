vacuolar proton-transporting V-type ATPase, V1 domain

GO:0000221 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

The V1 domain of a proton-transporting V-type ATPase found in the vacuolar membrane. [GO:0000221]

Synonyms: vacuolar hydrogen ion-transporting ATPase V1 domain

Part of: vacuolar proton-transporting V-type ATPase complex (GO:0016471)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding yeast vacuoles use a cytosolic V1 sector that hydrolyzes ATP and couples to the membrane V0 sector in the vacuolar V-ATPase. [DOI:10.1128/MMBR.70.1.177-191.2006]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Yeast model for the V1 catalytic sector of the vacuolar V-ATPase and its coupling to the V0 sector. [DOI:10.1128/MMBR.70.1.177-191.2006]

Functions

  • V1-sector ATP hydrolysis — Within the intact vacuolar V-ATPase, the V1 domain forms the cytosolic catalytic sector that hydrolyzes ATP and drives rotation coupled to V0 proton translocation.
    • GO:0033180 GO:0033180 identifies V1 domains as ATP-hydrolyzing V-ATPase sectors.
    • DOI:10.1038/nrm2272 Forgac 2007 reviews ATP hydrolysis in V1 as the source of rotary V-ATPase proton-pump activity.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent the V1 domain as the ATP-hydrolyzing sector of the intact vacuolar V-ATPase rather than as a standalone membrane proton pump. GO:0000221 denotes the V1 domain, which GO places part-of the whole vacuolar V-ATPase complex; the V1 sector couples ATP hydrolysis to the V0 proton-translocation sector in the intact rotary enzyme.
