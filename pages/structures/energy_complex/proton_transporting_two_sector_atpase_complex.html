proton-transporting two-sector ATPase complex

GO:0016469 · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A large protein complex that catalyzes the synthesis or hydrolysis of ATP by a rotational mechanism, coupled to the transport of protons across a membrane. The complex comprises a membrane sector (F0 or V0) that carries out proton transport and a cytoplasmic compartment sector (F1 or V1) that catalyzes ATP synthesis or hydrolysis. [GO:0016469]

Synonyms: hydrogen-transporting two-sector ATPase complex; vacuolar hydrogen-transporting ATPase

Has part: proton-transporting two-sector ATPase complex, proton-transporting domain (GO:0033177); proton-transporting two-sector ATPase complex, catalytic domain (GO:0033178)
