light-harvesting complex

GO:0030076 · ENERGY_COMPLEX · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein-pigment complex that may be closely or peripherally associated to photosynthetic reaction centers that participate in harvesting and transferring radiant energy to the reaction center. [GO:0030076]

Synonyms: antenna complex

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral grounding for antenna protein and pigment groups before splitting the generic component rows. This parent record spans biliprotein, bacteriochlorophyll and chlorophyll antenna systems; one broad protein-family or ChEBI accession would either be too narrow for the GO:0030076 parent or too broad to denote only light-harvesting constituents.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Curate LH2, RC-LH1 core, phycobilisome, chlorosome baseplate and other lineage-specific antenna architectures as child records rather than components of this generic GO parent. GO:0030076 covers several protein-pigment architectures with different subunits, pigments, membrane contexts and excitation paths; purple-bacterial chromatophores alone distinguish peripheral LH2 antennae from RC-LH1 reaction-center core antennae, so this parent record intentionally captures only the shared antenna boundary.
