formate dehydrogenase N complex

cellstructuremech:formate_dehydrogenase_n_complex · Energy complex · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A nitrate-inducible, membrane-bound formate:quinone oxidoreductase complex whose FdnG, FdnH, and FdnI subunits oxidize formate and pass electrons to a membrane quinone during anaerobic respiration. [ComplexPortal:CPX-1975]

Synonyms: Formate dehydrogenase-N complex; FDH-N complex; FdnGHI complex; nitrate-inducible formate dehydrogenase complex; formate:quinone oxidoreductase
