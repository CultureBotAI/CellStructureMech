curli secretion complex

GO:0062155 · SECRETION_SYSTEM · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A protein-containing complex that serves as a channel for the secretion of curli. Curli are a fibers that serve as a major component of the extracellular matrix of pellicle biofilms. [GO:0062155]
