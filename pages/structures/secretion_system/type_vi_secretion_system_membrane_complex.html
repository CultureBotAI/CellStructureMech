type VI secretion system membrane complex

cellstructuremech:type_vi_secretion_system_membrane_complex · Secretion system · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A bacterial type VI secretion system subassembly in which TssJ-, TssL-, and TssM-family proteins form an envelope-spanning membrane core of the secretion apparatus. [DOI:10.1038/nature14667]

Synonyms: T6SS membrane complex; TssJLM membrane complex; TssJ/TssL/TssM membrane complex; type VI secretion membrane core complex

Part of: type VI protein secretion system complex (GO:0033104)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Functions

  • T6SS membrane-core anchoring — The TssJLM membrane complex anchors the type VI secretion apparatus in the cell envelope and recruits the cytoplasmic baseplate.
    • DOI:10.1038/nature14667 Durand et al. 2015 determined the architecture and biogenesis of the TssJLM membrane core complex.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.pgen.1005545 Brunet et al. 2015 proposed multiple contacts between the TssEFGK-VgrG baseplate and the TssJLM membrane complex.
