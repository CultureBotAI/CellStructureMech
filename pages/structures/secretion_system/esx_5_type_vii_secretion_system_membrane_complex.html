ESX-5 type VII secretion system membrane complex

cellstructuremech:esx_5_type_vii_secretion_system_membrane_complex · SECRETION_SYSTEM · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A mycobacterial ESX-5 type VII secretion inner-membrane complex in which EccB5, EccC5, EccD5, EccE5, and MycP5 assemble into a multi-megadalton nanomachine that exports Esx and PE/PPE-family protein substrates. [DOI:10.1038/s41586-021-03517-z]

Synonyms: mycobacterial ESX-5 secretion system membrane complex; ESX-5 membrane complex; T7SS membrane complex

Mechanism graphs

EccB5/EccD5/MycP5 build the ESX-5 chamber over EccC5 (FUNCTION)

Six EccB5, six EccE5, twelve EccD5, six EccC5, and three MycP5 copies form the ESX-5 inner-membrane core. EccB5 and MycP5 build the periplasmic dome, EccD5 supplies much of the transmembrane core, and EccC5 ATPase domains sit on the cytosolic side where they are positioned to drive type VII substrate export.
