Holo-translocon SecYEG-SecDF-YajC-YidC complex

cellstructuremech:holo_translocon_secyeg_secdf_yajc_yidc_complex · Secretion system · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

An Escherichia coli Sec-pathway plasma-membrane holo-translocon complex in which SecY, SecE, SecG, SecD, SecF, YajC, and YidC assemble as a heteroheptamer that supports protein secretion and membrane-protein insertion. [ComplexPortal:CPX-1095]

Synonyms: HTL complex

Has part: SecYEG translocon complex (cellstructuremech:secyeg_translocon_complex)

Mechanism graphs

Sec holo-translocon couples transport and insertion (FUNCTION)

SecYEG, SecDF-YajC, and YidC assemble as the bacterial holo-translocon, where the SecYEG core supplies the pore and lateral gate, SecDF supports proton-motive-force assisted translocation, and YidC cooperates with the lateral gate for membrane-protein insertion.

The existing SecYEG translocon record captures the heterotrimeric protein-conducting channel core, while CPX-1095 scopes the E. coli holo-translocon as SecYEG assembled with SecDF-YajC and YidC. The larger holo-translocon is therefore a distinct supercomplex that has the SecYEG core as a named part.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Should SecA be modeled as a constituent of the SecYEG-SecDF-YajC-YidC holo-translocon? SecA drives post-translational substrates through Sec-family channels, but CPX-1095 scopes the holo-translocon itself to SecYEG, SecDF-YajC, and YidC.
