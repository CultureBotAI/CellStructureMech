type III protein secretion system complex

GO:0030257 · Secretion system · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

The injectisome: a syringe-like envelope-spanning machine that delivers effector proteins from the bacterial cytoplasm directly into a eukaryotic host cell. Rings in the inner and outer membranes surround an export apparatus; an extracellular needle carries the secretion channel to a translocon pore in the host membrane. It is evolutionarily related to the flagellar export apparatus. [GO:0030257]

Synonyms: injectisome

Mechanism graphs

The export gate feeds unfolded effectors into a host-cell conduit (FUNCTION)

Inner- and outer-membrane rings align the export apparatus with the needle filament, creating a conduit through the bacterial envelope. The export gate recognizes unfolded substrates, the narrow needle conducts them away from the basal body, and the translocon completes the channel in the target-cell membrane.

Find a source that states a length, or record that it varies too much between species to carry as one value.
