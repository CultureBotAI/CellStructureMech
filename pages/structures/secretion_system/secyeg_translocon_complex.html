SecYEG translocon complex

cellstructuremech:secyeg_translocon_complex · SECRETION_SYSTEM · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A bacterial cytoplasmic-membrane Sec-pathway protein-conducting channel built from SecY, SecE, and SecG, through which unfolded secretory proteins or nascent membrane proteins move during translocation or membrane insertion. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro.2016.161]

Synonyms: SecYEG translocon; SecYEG channel; bacterial SecYEG complex

Part of: GO:0031522

SecA drives preproteins through SecYEG (FUNCTION)

SecY and SecE form the stabilized channel core, SecG completes the bacterial SecYEG heterotrimer, and the SecA ATPase drives unfolded preproteins through the SecY pore during post-translational secretion.
