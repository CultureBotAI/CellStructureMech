type IV secretion system complex

GO:0043684 · SECRETION_SYSTEM · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A bacterial envelope-spanning secretion complex related to conjugation machines that uses VirB-family channel, pilus, ATPase and coupling-protein subassemblies to translocate DNA and protein substrates. [GO:0043684]

Synonyms: T4SS complex; VirB/VirD4 secretion system
