type II protein secretion system complex

GO:0015627 · Secretion system · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A Gram-negative bacterial envelope-spanning secretion complex in which an inner-membrane platform and ATPase drive pseudopilus-mediated export of folded periplasmic substrates through an outer-membrane secretin. [GO:0015627]

Synonyms: T2SS complex; type 2 secretion system

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.
