type I protein secretion system complex

GO:0030256 · SECRETION_SYSTEM · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A Gram-negative bacterial trans-envelope protein secretion complex in which an inner-membrane ABC transporter and membrane-fusion protein recruit a TolC-like outer-membrane channel for one-step export of C-terminal-signal substrates. [GO:0030256]

Synonyms: T1SS complex; type I secretion system

Mechanism graphs

An ABC-MFP-TolC conduit exports type I substrates (FUNCTION)

The inner-membrane ABC transporter recognizes substrates and cooperates with a membrane-fusion protein to recruit a TolC-family outer-membrane factor, forming a continuous channel that exports substrates across both Gram-negative membranes.
