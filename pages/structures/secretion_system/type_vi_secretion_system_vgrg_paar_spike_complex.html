type VI secretion system VgrG/PAAR spike complex

cellstructuremech:type_vi_secretion_system_vgrg_paar_spike_complex · Secretion system · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A bacterial type VI secretion system tip subassembly in which trimeric VgrG proteins cap the Hcp tube and associate with PAAR-repeat proteins to sharpen the puncturing spike. [DOI:10.1128/microbiolspec.PSIB-0031-2019]

Synonyms: T6SS VgrG/PAAR spike complex; VgrG/PAAR spike; type VI secretion system spike

Part of: type VI protein secretion system complex (GO:0033104)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Functions

  • T6SS spike-mediated puncturing — The VgrG/PAAR spike caps the Hcp tube as the sharpened puncturing tip that is fired outward during type VI secretion.
    • DOI:10.1038/nature12453 Shneider et al. 2013 showed that PAAR proteins sharpen the T6SS VgrG spike and proposed a contraction-driven VgrG-PAAR effector-delivery model.
    • DOI:10.1128/microbiolspec.PSIB-0031-2019 Cherrak et al. 2019 review the VgrG/PAAR spike as the cap at the distal end of the Hcp tube.
