type VI secretion system baseplate

cellstructuremech:type_vi_secretion_system_baseplate · SECRETION_SYSTEM · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A bacterial type VI secretion system subassembly in which TssE-, TssF-, TssG-, and TssK-family proteins form a phage-baseplate-like platform that docks to the TssJ/TssL/TssM membrane complex and initiates Hcp tube and contractile-sheath assembly. [DOI:10.1371/journal.pgen.1005545]

Synonyms: T6SS baseplate; TssEFGK-VgrG phage-like baseplate

Part of: GO:0033104

Functions

  • T6SS sheath-tube assembly platform — The type VI secretion baseplate docks to the membrane complex and initiates polymerization of the Hcp tube and its surrounding contractile sheath.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.pgen.1005545 Brunet et al. 2015 proposed that the baseplate is recruited to TssJLM and serves as a platform for tail tube and sheath assembly.
    • DOI:10.15252/embj.201797103 Nazarov et al. 2018 imaged baseplates at the ends of Vibrio cholerae sheath-tube assemblies.

The T6SS baseplate anchors and primes sheath-tube growth (ASSEMBLY)

TssE, TssF, TssG, TssK and VgrG form a phage-like baseplate that docks onto the TssJ/TssL/TssM membrane complex. The docked baseplate then starts Hcp tube polymerization and contractile-sheath extension.
