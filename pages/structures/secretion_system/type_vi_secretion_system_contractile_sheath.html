type VI secretion system contractile sheath

cellstructuremech:type_vi_secretion_system_contractile_sheath · Secretion system · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A bacterial type VI secretion system subassembly in which TssB/VipA- and TssC/VipB-family proteins polymerize into a metastable sheath around the Hcp tube and contract to drive Hcp/VgrG/PAAR spike ejection. [DOI:10.1038/nature10846]

Synonyms: T6SS contractile sheath; TssB/TssC contractile sheath; VipA/VipB sheath

Part of: type VI protein secretion system complex (GO:0033104)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Mechanism graphs

The TssB/TssC sheath extends, fires and is recycled (FUNCTION)

TssB/VipA and TssC/VipB assemble into an extended sheath around the Hcp tube. Sheath contraction fires the T6SS, and ClpV can disassemble the contracted polymer for subunit reuse.
