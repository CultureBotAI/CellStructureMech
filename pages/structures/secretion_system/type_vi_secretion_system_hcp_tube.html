type VI secretion system Hcp tube

cellstructuremech:type_vi_secretion_system_hcp_tube · SECRETION_SYSTEM · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A bacterial type VI secretion system tail-tube subassembly in which Hcp-family proteins stack into hexameric rings that form the inner tube expelled by contractile-sheath firing. [DOI:10.1128/microbiolspec.PSIB-0031-2019]

Synonyms: Hcp inner tube; T6SS Hcp tube; T6SS tail tube

Part of: GO:0033104

Functions

  • T6SS Hcp tube ejection — The Hcp tube is expelled during type VI secretion to deliver the VgrG/PAAR spike and associated effectors out of the cell.
    • DOI:10.1128/microbiolspec.PSIB-0031-2019 Cherrak et al. 2019 review the T6SS as a contractile machine in which sheath contraction ejects the Hcp tube and spike.
