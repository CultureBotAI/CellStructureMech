type IX protein secretion system complex

cellstructuremech:type_ix_protein_secretion_system_complex · SECRETION_SYSTEM · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A Bacteroidota trans-envelope secretion nanomachine that couples an inner-membrane PorL/PorM-family motor to PorK/PorN outer-membrane rings and a SprA/Sov translocon to export folded C-terminal-domain-bearing proteins across the outer membrane. [DOI:10.1099/mic.0.001320]

Synonyms: type IX secretion system; T9SS; Por secretion system

Mechanism graphs

PorL/PorM energizes PorK/PorN-Sov export (FUNCTION)

The PorL/PorM-family inner-membrane motor supplies proton-driven energy, the PorK/PorN ring scaffolds the outer-membrane side, PorW links the ring to Sov, and SprA/Sov forms the outer-membrane translocon that passes folded type IX substrates to a PorV-family shuttle.
