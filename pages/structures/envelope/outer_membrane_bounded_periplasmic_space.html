outer membrane-bounded periplasmic space

GO:0030288 · ENVELOPE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The region between the inner (cytoplasmic or plasma) membrane and outer membrane of organisms with two membranes such as Gram negative bacteria. These periplasmic spaces are relatively thick and contain a thin peptidoglycan layer (PGL), also referred to as a thin cell wall. [GO:0030288]

Synonyms: outer membrane bounded periplasmic space; outer membrane-enclosed periplasmic space

Has part: GO:0009276

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the outer membrane-bounded periplasmic space separate from the Gram-negative-bacterium-type cell wall record. GO:0030288 denotes the entire region between the inner and outer membranes, whereas GO:0009276 denotes the thin peptidoglycan wall located inside that region.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the inner and outer membranes as boundaries of the outer membrane-bounded periplasmic space rather than as periplasm components. GO:0030288 denotes the region between the inner and outer membranes, so those membranes bound the region but are not contained by it.
