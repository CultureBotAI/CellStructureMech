capsule

GO:0042603 · ENVELOPE · ENVELOPE_LAYER · PROPOSED

A highly organized glycocalyx layer attached outside the cell wall of some bacteria and fungi, usually built from capsular polysaccharides and retained on the cell surface rather than released as a loose extracellular polymer. [GO:0042603]

Synonyms: bacterial capsule; polysaccharide capsule; cryptococcal capsule

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: Capsules occur in many Gram-negative and Gram-positive lineages but are serotype- and strain-dependent rather than universal across bacteria. [DOI:10.1146/annurev.micro.50.1.285]
  • NCBITaxon:4751 Fungi — VARIABLE: The Cryptococcus neoformans polysaccharide capsule is a prominent fungal capsule model; capsules are not a universal fungal cell-wall layer. [DOI:10.1128/CMR.00001-12]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:562 Escherichia coli — E. coli K-antigen capsules are the classic Gram-negative model for ABC-transporter-dependent capsule export. [DOI:10.1146/annurev.biochem.75.103004.142545]
  • NCBITaxon:1313 Streptococcus pneumoniae — Encapsulated pneumococcal strains are a primary model for capsule-mediated complement evasion and anti-phagocytic activity. [DOI:10.1128/IAI.00881-09]
  • NCBITaxon:5207 Cryptococcus neoformans — The thick glucuronoxylomannan-rich capsule is the major surface virulence structure of this fungal yeast. [DOI:10.1128/CMR.00001-12]

Functions

  • anti-phagocytic surface shielding — Capsules mask the cell surface from opsonization and reduce uptake by professional phagocytes in encapsulated pathogens.
    • DOI:10.1128/IAI.00881-09 Hyams et al. 2010 showed that the Streptococcus pneumoniae capsule reduces complement deposition and neutrophil phagocytosis.
  • capsule-mediated cell protection — A retained capsule protects encapsulated cells by shielding the cell envelope from host antimicrobial effectors and environmental stressors.
    • DOI:10.1146/annurev.micro.50.1.285 Roberts 1996 reviews the protective and virulence roles of bacterial capsular polysaccharides.
    • DOI:10.1128/CMR.00001-12 O'Meara and Alspaugh 2012 review capsule-mediated shielding functions in Cryptococcus neoformans.
