mycolate outer membrane

GO:0036407 · ENVELOPE · MEMBRANE · PROPOSED

A mycolic-acid-rich cell outer membrane in mycobacteria and related Corynebacteriales, containing a lipid bilayer and covalently linked long-chain mycolic acids attached to peptidoglycan through an arabinogalactan network. [GO:0036407]

Synonyms: MOM; mycobacterial outer membrane; mycomembrane

Mechanism graphs

Mycolates form a covalently anchored outer membrane (ASSEMBLY)

Long-chain mycolic acids are esterified to arabinogalactan, which links the hydrophobic mycolate layer to peptidoglycan. Extractable lipids and outer-membrane proteins pack into that mycolate-rich membrane to form a low-permeability envelope boundary.
