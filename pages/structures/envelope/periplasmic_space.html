periplasmic space

GO:0042597 · ENVELOPE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The region between the inner (cytoplasmic) and outer membrane (Gram-negative Bacteria) or cytoplasmic membrane and cell wall (Fungi and Gram-positive Bacteria). [GO:0042597]

Synonyms: periplasm

Part of: GO:0005576

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0200

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:562 Escherichia coli — Canonical Gram-negative model whose periplasm lies between the cytoplasmic and outer membranes and houses the thin peptidoglycan wall. [DOI:10.1101/cshperspect.a000414]
  • NCBITaxon:1280 Staphylococcus aureus — Gram-positive model in which cryo-electron microscopy resolved a native periplasmic space between the plasma membrane and thick wall. [PMID:16428405]

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the generic periplasmic space record separate from the outer membrane-bounded periplasmic space record. GO:0042597 is the broader periplasmic-space class covering Gram-negative, Gram-positive, and fungal topologies, while GO:0030288 is the narrower outer membrane-bounded subclass.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Do not list boundary membranes or walls as taxon-agnostic components of the generic periplasmic space. The periplasmic space is the region delimited by envelope layers; the delimiting layers vary across Gram-negative bacteria, Gram-positive bacteria, and fungi and are not uniformly contained by the generic region.

Evidence

  • GO:0042597 Gene Ontology cellular-component term for the generic periplasmic space, with the exact synonym periplasm and the UniProt Subcellular Location xref SL-0200.
  • uniprot.location:SL-0200 UniProt Subcellular Location entry for Periplasm, mapped to GO:0042597.
  • DOI:10.1101/cshperspect.a000414 Silhavy, Kahne and Walker 2010 review the Gram-negative envelope, where the periplasm lies between the inner and outer membranes.
  • PMID:16428405 Matias and Beveridge 2006 used cryo-electron microscopy to visualize the native cell-wall organization and periplasmic space in Staphylococcus aureus.
