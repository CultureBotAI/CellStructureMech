slime layer

GO:0030114 · ENVELOPE · ENVELOPE_LAYER · PROPOSED

A slime layer is an easily removed, diffuse, unorganized layer of extracellular material that surrounds a cell. Specifically this consists mostly of exopolysaccharides, glycoproteins, and glycolipids. [GO:0030114]
