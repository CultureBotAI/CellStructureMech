fungal-type cell wall

GO:0009277 · ENVELOPE · ENVELOPE_LAYER · PROPOSED

A rigid but dynamic carbohydrate- and glycoprotein-rich wall surrounding the fungal plasma membrane, built from a variable cross-linked matrix of glucans, wall glycoproteins, and often chitin and kept distinct from fungal capsules outside the wall. [GO:0009277]

Synonyms: chitin- and beta-glucan-containing cell wall; fungal cell wall

Functions

  • osmotic protection and morphogenesis — The cross-linked wall resists turgor pressure and maintains fungal cell shape during polarized growth and division.
    • DOI:10.1016/B978-0-12-407677-8.00002-6 Free 2013 reviews fungal cell walls as protective structures required for cell integrity and morphogenesis.
  • fungal surface interaction — Outer wall glycoproteins and exposed polysaccharides form the cell surface that contacts hosts, substrates, and neighboring cells.
    • DOI:10.1128/microbiolspec.FUNK-0035-2016 Gow, Latge and Munro 2017 review the cell wall as the primary fungal surface for host and environmental interactions.

Glucans, chitin and mannoproteins form a fungal wall matrix (ASSEMBLY)

In well-studied fungal walls, branched beta-glucans form a cross-linked matrix with chitin and mannoproteins. This carbohydrate-rich matrix surrounds the plasma membrane to maintain shape and provide a fungal cell surface.
