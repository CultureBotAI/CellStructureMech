Cajal body

GO:0015030 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · OTHER · PROPOSED

A class of nuclear body, first seen after silver staining by Ramon y Cajal in 1903, enriched in small nuclear ribonucleoproteins, and certain general RNA polymerase II transcription factors; ultrastructurally, they appear as a tangle of coiled, electron-dense threads roughly 0.5 micrometers in diameter; involved in aspects of snRNP biogenesis; the protein coilin serves as a marker for Cajal bodies. Some argue that Cajal bodies are the sites for preassembly of transcriptosomes, unitary particles involved in transcription and processing of RNA. [GO:0015030]

Synonyms: coiled body; Gemini of coiled bodies; Gems

Part of: GO:0031981
