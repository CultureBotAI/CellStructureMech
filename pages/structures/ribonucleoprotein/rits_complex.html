RITS complex

GO:0030958 · Ribonucleoprotein · Ribonucleoprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex required for heterochromatin assembly; contains an Argonaute homolog, a chromodomain protein, and at least one additional protein; named for RNA-induced initiation of transcriptional gene silencing. [GO:0030958]

Part of: GO:0000785 — chromatin (no local record); nuclear chromosome (GO:0000228)

Functions

  • heterochromatin formation GO:0031507 — Uses Ago1-bound siRNAs and the Chp1 H3K9me-binding chromodomain to engage nascent pericentromeric transcripts and recruit the Clr4 methyltransferase and RDRC machinery for heterochromatin assembly.
    • GO:0030958 GO:0030958 defines the RITS complex as required for heterochromatin assembly.
    • PMID:14704433 Verdel et al. 2004 showed that RITS is required for H3K9 methylation and Swi6/HP1 recruitment at centromeric heterochromatin.
  • regulatory ncRNA-mediated gene silencing GO:0031047 — Mediates co-transcriptional and transcriptional gene silencing at heterochromatic repeats via siRNA-guided recognition of nascent noncoding transcripts.
    • PMID:14704433 Verdel et al. 2004 demonstrated siRNA-dependent transcriptional gene silencing by the RITS complex.
    • PMID:15607976 Motamedi et al. 2004 showed that RITS couples siRNA-guided nascent transcript recognition to RDRC-dependent dsRNA synthesis and silencing.

Mechanism graphs

Ago1, Chp1, Tas3, and siRNAs assemble the RITS complex to direct heterochromatin formation (ASSEMBLY)

In Schizosaccharomyces pombe, Tas3 bridges Ago1 and Chp1 together with Ago1-bound siRNAs to assemble the RITS ribonucleoprotein complex, which promotes heterochromatin formation and regulatory ncRNA-mediated gene silencing.
