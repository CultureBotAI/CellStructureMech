RNP body

GO:0140365 · Ribonucleoprotein · Other · Proposed

A ribonucleoprotein granule located in the cytoplasm of bacteria, minimally containing the RNase E protein and RNA molecules. Bacterial RNP-bodies are similar to eukaryotic P-bodies and stress granules. [GO:0140365]

Synonyms: BR-body; bacterial RNP body

Part of: GO:0005737 — cytoplasm (no local record)

  • NCBITaxon:565050 Caulobacter vibrioides NA1000 — All Caulobacter strains in the 2018 study derive from NA1000, historically called C. crescentus NA1000. Fluorescent-fusion imaging, RNase E domain and binding-site mutants, and RNA-depletion experiments establish BR-body assembly in this background. [DOI:10.1016/j.molcel.2018.08.003]

Functions

  • mRNA catabolic process GO:0006402 — During the assayed Caulobacter growth conditions, BR-bodies preferentially organize poorly translated mRNAs and decay enzymes, stimulating initial endonucleolytic cleavage and subsequent degradation. This does not assert identical RNA fate under every stress condition.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.molcel.2020.04.001 Global half-life profiling and paired RNA-seq/qRT-PCR comparisons distinguish condensation-dependent initial cleavage from the contribution of recruited exoribonucleases to decay of intermediates.

RNA-dependent condensation couples BR-body assembly to RNA cleavage (FUNCTION)

Caulobacter RNase E and available mRNA promote condensation; the resulting bodies stimulate RNA cleavage, which enables their dynamic disassembly.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Keep the BR-body condensate distinct from one RNA degradosome and from eukaryotic granules. The existing bacterial degradosome record describes a protein complex with an E. coli-centered component set. BR-bodies are mesoscale RNase E/RNA condensates whose assembly persists after several degradosome-client binding sites are removed. Eukaryotic P-bodies and stress granules are analogies, not exact identities.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Establish exact taxon-neutral family groundings for the RhlB and aconitase client classes. The verified strain-specific UniProt mappings identify the assayed loci, but domain and broad superfamily matches cannot substitute for exact whole-protein family groundings. Current UniProt calls CCNA_01923 RhpA, whereas the paper and gene symbol call it RhlB; this nomenclature difference is explicitly retained.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Distinguish the verified NA1000 example from species-wide prevalence. A species-level VARIABLE presence row would imply patchiness across lineages that the curated NA1000 experiments do not establish. A qualifying note cannot remove that assertion from the structured field.
