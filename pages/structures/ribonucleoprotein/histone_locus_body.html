histone locus body

GO:0035363 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · OTHER · PROPOSED

A nuclear body associated with the histone gene locus that is thought to contain all of the factors necessary for histone mRNA transcription and pre-mRNA processing. In Drosophila, U7 snRNP is located in the histone locus body rather than the distinct Cajal body. [GO:0035363]

Synonyms: HLB

Part of: GO:0031981

Functions

  • replication-dependent histone mRNA biogenesis — Histone locus bodies concentrate replication-dependent histone transcription and pre-mRNA processing factors at the histone gene cluster.
    • DOI:10.1080/15476286.2016.1265198 Duronio and Marzluff 2017 reviewed HLB assembly and function in cell-cycle-regulated histone gene expression.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.201504043 Tatomer et al. 2016 showed that concentrating pre-mRNA processing factors in the HLB facilitates efficient Drosophila histone mRNA biogenesis.
