U1 snRNP

GO:0005685 · Ribonucleoprotein · Ribonucleoprotein complex · Proposed

A ribonucleoprotein complex that contains small nuclear RNA U1, a heptameric ring of Sm proteins, as well as several proteins that are unique to the U1 snRNP, most of which remain associated with the U1 snRNA both while the U1 snRNP is free or assembled into a series of spliceosomal complexes. [GO:0005685]

Synonyms: snRNP U1
