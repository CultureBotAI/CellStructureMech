omega speckle

GO:0035062 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · OTHER · PROPOSED

A nucleoplasmic speckle distributed in the interchromatin space of cells in close proximity to chromatin. Omega speckles are distinct from interchromatin granules and contain heterogeneous nuclear RNA-binding proteins (hnRNPs). [GO:0035062]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:7227 Drosophila melanogaster — Drosophila melanogaster cells were used to define omega speckles as hsr-omega nuclear-transcript foci that colocalize with hnRNPs and remain distinct from SR-protein interchromatin granules. [DOI:10.1242/jcs.113.19.3485]

Functions

  • hnRNP trafficking and availability regulation — Drosophila omega speckles organize hsr-omega-n noncoding RNA and hnRNP proteins in nuclear foci that regulate the trafficking and availability of hnRNPs and related RNA-binding proteins.
    • DOI:10.1242/jcs.113.19.3485 Prasanth et al. 2000 concluded from hsr-omega localization, hnRNP colocalization and hsr-omega-nullosomic disruption that omega speckles organize hnRNP trafficking and availability.
