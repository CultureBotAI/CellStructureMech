bacterial degradosome

GO:1990061 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The degradosome is a protein complex playing a key role in mRNA degradation and RNA processing. It includes a RNA helicase, a 3'-5' phosphate-dependent PNPase and a RNase E bound-enolase. [GO:1990061]

Synonyms: RNA degradosome; Escherichia coli RNA degradosome
