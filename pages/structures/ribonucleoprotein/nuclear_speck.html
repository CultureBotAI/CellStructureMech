nuclear speck

GO:0016607 · Ribonucleoprotein · Other · Proposed

A discrete extra-nucleolar subnuclear domain, 20-50 in number, in which splicing factors are seen to be localized by immunofluorescence microscopy. [GO:0016607]

Synonyms: nuclear speckle; nuclear speckles; splicing speckle; speckle domain; speckle focus

Part of: nuclear lumen (GO:0031981)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Functions

  • pre-mRNA splicing factor organization — Nuclear speckles concentrate pre-mRNA splicing factors and dynamically supply them to active transcription sites.
    • DOI:10.1038/387523a0 Misteli, Caceres and Spector 1997 showed dynamic exchange of an SR splicing factor between nuclear speckles and transcription sites in living cells.
    • DOI:10.1101/cshperspect.a000646 Spector and Lamond 2011 reviewed nuclear speckles as subnuclear domains enriched in pre-mRNA splicing factors.
