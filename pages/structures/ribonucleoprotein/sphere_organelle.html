sphere organelle

GO:0071601 · Ribonucleoprotein · Other · Proposed — Drafted from literature to fill a coverage gap; not yet reviewed. How records are made

A nuclear body that is found in the germinal vesicles of amphibian oocytes, and consist of three major parts: a remarkably spherical body about 5-10 pm in diameter, smaller spherical or nearly spherical granules on the surface, and inclusions of various sizes that strongly resemble the surface granules. The parts of the sphere organelle have distinct compositions, including splicing snRNAs and proteins. [GO:0071601]

Part of: nuclear lumen (GO:0031981)

SPH-1 and U7 snRNA mark sphere organelles (ASSEMBLY)

Xenopus laevis sphere organelles contain cortex-localized coilin-related SPH-1 protein and U7 snRNA, and increased U7 snRNA can induce coiled-body-like structures in oocyte nuclei.
