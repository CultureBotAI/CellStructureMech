TmaR condensate

cellstructuremech:tmar_condensate · Ribonucleoprotein · Other · Proposed

A polar, membraneless ribonucleoprotein condensate organized by TmaR in Escherichia coli that recruits phosphotransferase-system enzyme I and participates in spatial regulation of sugar utilization. [DOI:10.1016/j.celrep.2025.115436]

Synonyms: TmaR-EI polar condensate

Functions

  • Spatial regulation of sugar utilization — Polar retention of EI by TmaR limits EI-dependent sugar consumption in the tested E. coli backgrounds.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.celrep.2025.115436 Figures 1-3 combine localization and interaction perturbations with consumption assays. F8L and V11I recruitment-defective TmaR variants show elevated glucose consumption. This is not a direct measurement of transport flux or the catalytic state of each condensed EI molecule.
  • Flagellar transcript protection — The primary report associates protection of flagella-related transcripts in TmaR condensates with control of flagella production and motility.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.celrep.2023.113393 Supported at the level of the readable final-paper abstract. Transcript-specific half-lives, exact cargo membership and downstream causal edges await full-text assessment.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Distinguish the TmaR scaffolded condensate from a protein, one EI-TmaR complex and the H-body. Shared polar location and stress-induced co-condensation do not imply identical structure identity or permanent parthood.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Resolve TmaR at its issuing family authority rather than substituting a generic sequence family. InterPro's DUF496 and unreviewed UPF0265 descriptions alone do not establish the intended functional class.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Keep scaffold/client essentiality and native organism scope separate from heterologous reconstitution. EI and TmaR require co-expression for the tested yeast condensates, unlike native bacterial TmaR foci. Plasmid cross-species complementation is not endogenous taxonomic prevalence. Post-PR review found that calling HPr merely a modeled EI partner conflated established EI-HPr interaction with the proposed TmaR-dependent exclusion mechanism.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Do not promote docking, consumption measurements or discordant significance descriptions into stronger claims. The 2025 interface is a model with mutational support, not an experimentally solved EI-TmaR complex. Figure 3E marks K95E, whereas the Results text singles out A84G among patch-II variants.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Assess the full 2023 primary experiments before adding specific RNA cargo or transcript-stability mechanisms. The verified abstract supports RNA-assisted assembly and flagellar transcript protection but not an exhaustive cargo list, quantitative half-lives or universal necessity.
