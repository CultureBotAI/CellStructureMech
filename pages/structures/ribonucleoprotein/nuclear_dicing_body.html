nuclear dicing body

GO:0010445 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · OTHER · PROPOSED

A small round nuclear body, measuring 0.2-0.8 microns in diameter that is diffusely distributed throughout the nucleoplasm. Several proteins known to be involved in miRNA processing have been localized to these structures. D-bodies are thought to be involved in primary-miRNA processing and/or storage/assembly of miRNA processing complexes. [GO:0010445]

Synonyms: D body; D-body

Part of: GO:0031981
