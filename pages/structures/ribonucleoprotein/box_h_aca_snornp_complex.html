box H/ACA snoRNP complex

GO:0031429 · Ribonucleoprotein · Ribonucleoprotein complex · Proposed

A box H/ACA small nucleolar ribonucleoprotein complex located in the nucleolus that catalyzes pseudouridylation of ribosomal RNA residues. The complex is composed of four different core proteins that assemble onto a H/ACA guide RNA scaffold that identifies specific uridines in rRNA for modification during ribosome synthesis. A conserved ribonucleoprotein complex composed of a hairpin-hinge-hairpin-tail box H/ACA small nucleolar RNA (snoRNA in eukaryotes or sRNA in Archaea) bound by four core proteins: the catalytic pseudouridine synthase Cbf5p/dyskerin (aCbf5), Nop10p, Gar1p, and Nhp2p (L7Ae in Archaea). Guided by antisense base-pairing in the pseudouridylation pocket of the H/ACA RNA, the complex catalyzes site-specific isomerization of uridine to pseudouridine in pre-rRNA and snRNAs. [GO:0031429]

Synonyms: box H/ACA small nucleolar ribonucleoprotein complex; box H/ACA snoRNP pseudouridylase complex

Part of: nucleolus (GO:0005730)

Box H/ACA snoRNP guide-directed rRNA pseudouridylation (ASSEMBLY)

Nhp2/L7Ae, Nop10, and Gar1 stabilize the catalytic synthase Cbf5 on a box H/ACA guide RNA, which base-pairs with nascent pre-rRNA to position target uridines for isomerization into pseudouridine.
