precatalytic spliceosome

GO:0071011 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A spliceosomal complex that is formed by the recruitment of a preassembled U5-containing tri-snRNP to the prespliceosome. Although all 5 snRNPs are present, the precatalytic spliceosome is catalytically inactive. The precatalytic spliceosome includes many proteins in addition to those found in the associated snRNPs. [GO:0071011]

Synonyms: mammalian spliceosomal complex B; mammalian spliceosomal complex B1; yeast spliceosomal complex A2-1
