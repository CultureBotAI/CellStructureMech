box H/ACA RNP complex

GO:0072588 · Ribonucleoprotein · Ribonucleoprotein complex · Proposed

A ribonucleoprotein complex containing a small RNA with an H/ACA box sequence motif that catalyzes pseudouridylation of RNA molecules. Most members of this family use guide RNAs to direct the site of pseudouridylation of target RNAs (for example, box H/ACA snoRNPs and box H/ACA scaRNPs) although some members do not modify other RNAs (for example, telomerase RNP). [GO:0072588]

Synonyms: sRNP complex; box H/ACA snoRNP pseudouridylase complex
