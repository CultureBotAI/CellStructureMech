nuclear stress granule

GO:0097165 · Ribonucleoprotein · Other · Proposed

A dense aggregation in the nucleus composed of proteins and RNAs that appear when the cell is under stress. [GO:0097165]

Synonyms: nuclear stress body; nSB; HSF1 granule

Part of: nuclear lumen (GO:0031981)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:9606 Homo sapiens — Human cultured cells were used to connect HSF1-positive nuclear stress granules with RNA polymerase II transcription of satellite III repeats during heat shock. [DOI:10.1083/jcb.200306104]

Functions

  • stress-induced satellite III RNP organization — Nuclear stress granules assemble on satellite III repeats in heat-shocked human cells, retain satellite III repeat RNAs and accumulate pre-mRNA processing factors.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.200306104 Jolly et al. 2004 showed that satellite III RNAs are transcribed in HSF1-containing nuclear stress granules and remain associated with the 9q12 region.
    • DOI:10.1101/cshperspect.a000695 Biamonti and Vourc'h 2010 reviewed nuclear stress bodies as heat-shock-induced subnuclear organelles formed on satellite III arrays.

HSF1 and satellite III RNAs organize nuclear stress granules (ASSEMBLY)

In heat-shocked human cells, HSF1 binds satellite III repeats, satellite III repeats are transcribed into RNAs retained at the locus, and pre-mRNA processing factors accumulate in the resulting nuclear stress granule.
