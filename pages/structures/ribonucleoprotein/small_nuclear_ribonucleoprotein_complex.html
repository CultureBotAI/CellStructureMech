small nuclear ribonucleoprotein complex

GO:0030532 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A ribonucleoprotein complex that contains at least one small nuclear RNA (snRNA) and associated proteins. Small nuclear ribonucleoproteins are typically named for the snRNA constituents they contain, such as U1 snRNP, U4/U6 snRNP, or 7SK snRNP; many become part of spliceosomes, while others regulate transcription elongation or replication-dependent histone pre-mRNA 3'-end processing. [GO:0030532]

Synonyms: small nuclear ribonucleoprotein; snRNP
