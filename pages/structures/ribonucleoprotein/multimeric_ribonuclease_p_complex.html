multimeric ribonuclease P complex

GO:0030681 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A ribonuclease P complex that generally contains a single RNA molecule and several protein molecules. Examples of this complex are found in Archaeal species. [GO:0030681]

Synonyms: multimeric RNase P complex
