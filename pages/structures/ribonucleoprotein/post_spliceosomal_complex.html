post-spliceosomal complex

GO:0071020 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A spliceosomal complex that is formed following the second splicing event and contains the spliced product, the excised intron, and three snRNPs, including U5. [GO:0071020]

Synonyms: mammalian spliceosomal complex C2; yeast spliceosomal complex A2-3
