paraspeckles

GO:0042382 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · OTHER · PROPOSED

Discrete subnuclear bodies in the interchromatin nucleoplasmic space, often located adjacent to nuclear specks. 10-20 paraspeckles are typically found in human cell nuclei. [GO:0042382]

Part of: GO:0031981
