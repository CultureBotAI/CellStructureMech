paraspeckles

GO:0042382 · Ribonucleoprotein · Other · Proposed

Discrete subnuclear bodies in the interchromatin nucleoplasmic space, often located adjacent to nuclear specks. 10-20 paraspeckles are typically found in human cell nuclei. [GO:0042382]

Part of: nuclear lumen (GO:0031981)

Functions

  • nuclear RNA retention — Paraspeckles are NEAT1-built nuclear bodies associated with retention of selected RNAs in the nucleus.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.200906113 Bond and Fox 2009 reviewed paraspeckles in relation to nuclear retention of RNA.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.tibs.2017.12.001 Fox et al. 2018 reviewed paraspeckles as long-noncoding-RNA nuclear bodies assembled through phase separation.
