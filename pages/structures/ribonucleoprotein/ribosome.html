ribosome

GO:0005840 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The ribonucleoprotein particle that translates mRNA into protein. The bacterial (70S) ribosome comprises a small (30S) subunit built on 16S rRNA, which decodes the mRNA, and a large (50S) subunit built on 23S and 5S rRNA, whose 23S rRNA catalyses peptide bond formation. Archaeal and eukaryotic cytosolic ribosomes are homologous but larger. [GO:0005840]

Synonyms: 70S ribosome

Has part: GO:0015935 GO:0015934
