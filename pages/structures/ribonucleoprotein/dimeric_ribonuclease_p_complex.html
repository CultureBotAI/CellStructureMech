dimeric ribonuclease P complex

GO:0030680 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A ribonuclease P complex that contains a single RNA molecule that is necessary and usually sufficient for catalysis, and a single protein molecule. [GO:0030680]

Synonyms: dimeric RNase P complex
