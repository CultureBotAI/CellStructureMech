small ribosomal subunit

GO:0015935 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The smaller of the two subunits of a ribosome. [GO:0015935]

Synonyms: ribosomal small subunit

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:274 Thermus thermophilus — Bacterial source of the 3.05 A 30S small-subunit crystal structure used to resolve 16S rRNA and 30S protein placement. [DOI:10.1038/35030006]
  • NCBITaxon:83333 Escherichia coli K-12 — Reference bacterial strain for the curated Complex Portal 30S small ribosomal subunit composition, CPX-3802. [ComplexPortal:CPX-3802]

Functions

  • mRNA decoding — The bacterial 30S subunit monitors codon-anticodon geometry and selects cognate aminoacyl-tRNAs during translation.
    • DOI:10.1126/science.1060612 Ogle et al. 2001 show 16S rRNA A1492, A1493, and G530 rearrangements around the codon-anticodon helix in the 30S decoding site.
