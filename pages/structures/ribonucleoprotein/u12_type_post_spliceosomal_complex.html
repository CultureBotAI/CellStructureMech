U12-type post-spliceosomal complex

GO:0071022 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A spliceosomal complex that is formed following the second splicing event and contains the spliced product, the excised intron, and three snRNPs, U5, U12 and U6atac. [GO:0071022]

Synonyms: minor post-spliceosomal complex; AT-AC post-spliceosomal complex; mammalian U12-type spliceosomal complex C2; yeast U12-type spliceosomal complex A2-3
