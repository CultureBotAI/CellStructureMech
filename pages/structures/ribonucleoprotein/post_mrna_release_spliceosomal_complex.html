post-mRNA release spliceosomal complex

GO:0071014 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A spliceosomal complex that is formed following the release of the spliced product from the post-spliceosomal complex and contains the excised intron and three snRNPs, either U2 or U12, U5, and either U6 or U6atac. [GO:0071014]

Synonyms: mammalian spliceosomal complex I
