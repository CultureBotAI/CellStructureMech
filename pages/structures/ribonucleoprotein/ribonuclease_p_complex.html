ribonuclease P complex

GO:0030677 · Ribonucleoprotein · Ribonucleoprotein complex · Proposed

A ribonucleoprotein complex that catalyzes cleavage of the leader sequence of precursor tRNAs (pre-tRNAs), generating the mature 5' end of tRNAs. [GO:0030677]

Synonyms: RNase P complex

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve source-neutral component boundaries across RNase P complex lineages. GO:0030677 captures the shared RNase P ribonucleoprotein identity and pre-tRNA 5-prime leader cleavage function, while its term comment flags that chloroplast complexes called RNase P appear not to contain RNA. Exact RNA and protein components should be curated on source-specific dimeric, multimeric, nucleolar, cyanelle and mitochondrial children before assigning source-neutral constituents to this generic parent; chloroplast or other RNA-free RNase P-like complexes need separate protein-only modeling instead of inheriting a generic RNase P RNA component.
