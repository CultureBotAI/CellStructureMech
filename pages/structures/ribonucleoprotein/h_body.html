H-body

cellstructuremech:h_body · Ribonucleoprotein · Other · Proposed

A stress-responsive, polar, membraneless ribonucleoprotein condensate organized by Hfq in Escherichia coli, with polyphosphate-modulated assembly and enrichment of polyadenylated transcripts and RNA-processing proteins. [DOI:10.1371/journal.pbio.3003775]

Synonyms: Hfq condensate

Part of: GO:0005737 — cytoplasm (no local record)

This is an experimentally demonstrated strain example, not a prevalence assertion across E. coli or bacteria. [DOI:10.1371/journal.pbio.3003775]

Functions

  • RNA and protein sequestration — Concentrates polyadenylated transcripts and selected RNA-processing proteins during nitrogen starvation. PolyP-dependent body organization is associated with selective transcript stabilization; the genetic comparisons do not isolate condensation from all other Hfq and polyP activities.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.pbio.3003775 Figures 4H-I and 5C,E directly localize RNase E, RhlB and poly(A) RNA. Transcript half-life and poly(A)-tail measurements in WT versus ppk deletion strains support selective preservation, but the Discussion calls for separation-of-function mutants before attributing survival specifically to condensation.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Distinguish H-body from BR-body, one Hfq hexamer and unrelated polyphosphate stores. Shared RNA or RNase E membership does not establish identical condensate identity. GO:0140365 has a broad minimal-component definition but an exact BR-body synonym and Caulobacter RNase E-scaffold provenance; it does not specifically denote Hfq-organized H-bodies.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Keep direct localization evidence separate from candidate proteomic associations. The native-gel screen can include polyP-binding proteins that do not interact with Hfq. The final paper reports mostly diffuse enolase fluorescence, unlike a stronger interpretation of the preprint.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Do not equate polyphosphate promotion with universal necessity or transcript preservation with proven RNase inhibition. Residual Hfq foci persist without polyP during nitrogen starvation, whereas effects are stronger under 1.17 M NaCl. Knockouts affect multiple processes and cannot uniquely assign survival or transcript-stability changes to condensation.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Establish an exact source-neutral family grounding for the TmaR pole-localizer class. Verified sequence-family membership alone does not show that the wider DUF496 or UPF0265 families are coextensive with the named TmaR functional class.
