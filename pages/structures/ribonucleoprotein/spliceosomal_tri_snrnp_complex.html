spliceosomal tri-snRNP complex

GO:0097526 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A spliceosomal snRNP complex that is formed by the association of the U4/U6 (or U4atac/U6atac) snRNP with the U5 snRNP. [GO:0097526]

U4/U6-family and U5 snRNPs form spliceosomal tri-snRNPs (ASSEMBLY)

Spliceosomal tri-snRNP complexes form when either the major U4/U6 snRNP or the minor U4atac/U6atac snRNP associates with U5 snRNP.
