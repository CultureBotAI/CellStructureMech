U12-type post-mRNA release spliceosomal complex

GO:0071019 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A spliceosomal complex that is formed following the release of the spliced product from the post-spliceosomal complex and contains the excised intron and the U12, U5 and U6atac snRNPs. [GO:0071019]

Synonyms: minor post-mRNA release spliceosomal complex; AT-AC post-mRNA release spliceosomal complex; mammalian U12-type spliceosomal complex I
