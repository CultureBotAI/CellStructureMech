exosome (RNase complex)

GO:0000178 · Ribonucleoprotein · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A ribonuclease complex with 3'-to-5' exoribonuclease activity that participates in RNA processing and degradation. [GO:0000178]

Synonyms: exosome multienzyme ribonuclease complex; RNA exosome

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.
