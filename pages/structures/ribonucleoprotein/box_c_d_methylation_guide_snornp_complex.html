box C/D methylation guide snoRNP complex

GO:0031428 · Ribonucleoprotein · Ribonucleoprotein complex · Proposed

A ribonucleoprotein complex containing a box C/D type RNA that is capable of ribose-2'-O-methylation of target RNAs. Box C/D type RNAs are widespread in eukaryotes and in Archaea, suggesting that an RNA-based guide mechanism for directing specific RNA 2'-O-ribose methylations was present in the common ancestor of Archaea and Eukarya. A conserved ribonucleoprotein complex composed of a box C/D guide snoRNA (or archaeal box C/D sRNA) containing kink-turn C/D and C'/D' motifs bound by the kink-turn binding protein Snu13p (L7Ae in Archaea), the scaffold subunits Nop56p and Nop58p (Nop5 in Archaea), and the SAM-dependent 2'-O-ribose methyltransferase Nop1p/fibrillarin. The complex base-pairs with target pre-rRNA to direct site-specific 2'-O-methylation five nucleotides upstream of box D or D'. [GO:0031428]

Synonyms: box C/D snoRNP ribose 2'-O methylase complex; box C/D sRNP complex; box C/D small nucleolar ribonucleoprotein complex; box C/D snoRNP complex; box C/D snoRNP ribose-2'-O-methyltransferase complex
