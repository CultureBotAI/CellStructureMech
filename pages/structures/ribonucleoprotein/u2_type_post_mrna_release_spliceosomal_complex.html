U2-type post-mRNA release spliceosomal complex

GO:0071008 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A spliceosomal complex that is formed following the release of the spliced product from the post-spliceosomal complex and contains the excised intron and the U2, U5 and U6 snRNPs. [GO:0071008]

Synonyms: major post-mRNA release spliceosomal complex; U2/U5/U6 snRNP complex; U2/U5/U6 tri-snRNP complex; GT-AG post-mRNA release spliceosomal complex; mammalian U2-type spliceosomal complex I
