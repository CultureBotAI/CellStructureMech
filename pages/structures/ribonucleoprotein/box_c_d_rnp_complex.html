box C/D RNP complex

GO:0170049 · Ribonucleoprotein · Ribonucleoprotein complex · Proposed — Drafted from literature to fill a coverage gap; not yet reviewed. How records are made

A ribonucleoprotein complex containing a small RNA with a C/D box sequence motif that functions in the modification and processing of precursor ribosomal RNAs. Most members of this family direct site-specific 2'-O-methylation of substrate RNAs (box C/D methylation guide snoRNPs and scaRNPs), while others, such as the U3, U8, U14, and U22 snoRNPs, mediate endonucleolytic cleavage and folding of pre-rRNA. [GO:0170049]

Synonyms: SNORD ribonucleoprotein complex; SNORNP
