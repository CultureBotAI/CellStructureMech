Lsm2-8 complex

GO:0120115 · Ribonucleoprotein · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

Heteroheptameric Sm-like protein ring complex composed of Lsm2, Lsm3, Lsm4, Lsm5, Lsm6, Lsm7 and Lsm8 or their orthologs that associates with U6-family spliceosomal snRNAs. [GO:0120115]

Part of: U6 snRNP (GO:0005688); U6atac snRNP (GO:0005691)

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the Lsm2-8 protein ring distinct from complete U6-family snRNPs and from individual Lsm proteins. GO:0120115 denotes the heteroheptameric Lsm2-8 protein complex, whereas GO:0005688 and GO:0005691 denote complete U6 and U6atac small nuclear ribonucleoprotein complexes that contain snRNA and additional proteins.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral groundings for the individual Lsm2, Lsm3, Lsm4, Lsm5, Lsm6, Lsm7 and Lsm8 protein families. GO:0120115 establishes the seven named Sm-like subunits of the Lsm2-8 complex, but this first child-complex record keeps them as a reviewed-label-only aggregate instead of guessing exact InterPro, Pfam, NCBIfam or UniProtKB groundings for individual Lsm proteins.
