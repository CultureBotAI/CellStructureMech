Lsm2-8 complex

GO:0120115 · Ribonucleoprotein · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

Heteroheptameric Sm-like protein ring complex composed of Lsm2, Lsm3, Lsm4, Lsm5, Lsm6, Lsm7 and Lsm8 or their orthologs that associates with U6-family spliceosomal snRNAs. [GO:0120115]

Part of: U6 snRNP (GO:0005688); U6atac snRNP (GO:0005691)
