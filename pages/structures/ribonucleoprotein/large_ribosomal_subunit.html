large ribosomal subunit

GO:0015934 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The larger of the two subunits of a ribosome. Two sites on the ribosomal large subunit are involved in translation, namely the aminoacyl site (A site) and peptidyl site (P site). [GO:0015934]

Synonyms: ribosomal large subunit

Part of: GO:0005840
