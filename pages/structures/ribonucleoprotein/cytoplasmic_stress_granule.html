cytoplasmic stress granule

GO:0010494 · RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN · OTHER · PROPOSED

A dense aggregation in the cytosol composed of proteins and RNAs that appear when the cell is under stress. [GO:0010494]

Synonyms: cytoplasmic mRNP granule; stress granule

Functions

  • mRNP triage during stress — Cytoplasmic stress granules concentrate nontranslating mRNPs and translation-initiation components when yeast cells encounter glucose deprivation or heat shock.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.200807043 Buchan, Muhlrad and Parker 2008 connected yeast stress granules to remodeling of mRNPs that had accumulated in preexisting P-bodies.
    • DOI:10.1242/jcs.045104 Grousl et al. 2009 found that robust heat shock induces Saccharomyces stress granules with eIF3 and 40S ribosomal subunit markers.

Yeast mRNPs and RNA-binding proteins assemble stress granules (ASSEMBLY)

In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, stalled mRNP cargo, Pab1/Pub1/Pbp1 RNA-binding proteins, and heat-shock-localized eIF3/40S translation-initiation components concentrate in cytoplasmic stress granules, while P-bodies promote stress-granule assembly during glucose deprivation.
