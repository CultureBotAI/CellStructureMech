periplasmic flagellum

GO:0055040 · APPENDAGE · APPENDAGE · PROPOSED

A bacterial-type flagellar appendage of spirochetes whose filaments reside in the periplasmic space between the cytoplasmic and outer membranes, attach near the cell poles, and rotate to deform the cell cylinder for motility. [DOI:10.3390/biom10040550]

Synonyms: endoflagellum; periplasmic flagellar filament
