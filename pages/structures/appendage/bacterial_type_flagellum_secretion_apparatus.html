bacterial-type flagellum secretion apparatus

GO:0120102 · Appendage · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A flagellum-specific type III export apparatus at the bacterial basal body, comprising a membrane-associated FlhA/FlhB/FliP/FliQ/FliR export gate and a cytoplasmic FliH/FliI/FliJ ATPase complex that support export of rod, hook, and filament subunits through the growing flagellum. [DOI:10.1016/j.bbamcr.2013.09.005]

Synonyms: bacterial-type flagellum export apparatus; flagellar type III export apparatus; flagellar type III export gate complex

Part of: bacterial-type flagellum basal body (GO:0009425)

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: Flagellum-specific type III export apparatuses occur in bacteria that assemble bacterial-type flagella and are absent from bacteria that lack the flagellar system. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro1887]
  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — ABSENT: Archaeal archaella are type-IV-pilus-related rotary filaments rather than bacterial flagella and do not use a FlhA/FlhB/FliP/FliQ/FliR export gate. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:90371 Salmonella enterica subsp. enterica serovar Typhimurium — Source organism for the cryo-EM reconstruction of the FliP5-FliQ4-FliR1 export-gate core. The FlhB-containing complex cited elsewhere was reconstructed from Vibrio mimicus. [DOI:10.1038/s41594-018-0086-9]

Functions

  • flagellar axial-subunit export — The apparatus secretes unfolded rod, hook and filament substrates from the cytoplasm through the narrow central channel of the growing bacterial flagellum.
    • DOI:10.1128/JB.181.5.1388-1394.1999 Minamino and Macnab 1999 classified Salmonella flagellar export substrates for the apparatus.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.bbamcr.2013.09.005 Minamino 2014 reviewed export of flagellar axial proteins through the bacterial flagellar type III pathway.
  • membrane export-gate assembly — FliP and FliR form a helical export-gate core with peripheral FliQ subunits. FlhB associates around the core base, and FlhA connects the gate with cytoplasmic substrate-delivery machinery.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41594-018-0086-9 Kuhlen et al. 2018 reconstructed Salmonella Typhimurium FliP5-FliQ4-FliR1 by cryo-EM: FliP and FliR form a helical core with peripheral FliQ subunits. The structure does not by itself measure assembly order.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-020-15071-9 Kuhlen et al. 2020 resolved Vibrio mimicus FliP5-FliQ4-FliR1-FlhB1. FlhB contacts the helical core and wraps around its base; this is distinct from the earlier Salmonella FliPQR structure.
    • DOI:10.1007/82_2019_170 Minamino et al. 2019 reviewed the ordered assembly pathway of the flagellar type III export apparatus.
  • ATPase- and ion-coupled export — The FliH/FliI/FliJ ATPase complex promotes efficient proton-motive-force-driven export. In the cited Salmonella experiments, gates with a compromised ATPase system could additionally use sodium motive force, while the intact ATPase-assisted system showed no sodium dependence under the tested conditions.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1005495 Minamino et al. 2016 found sodium-dependent export in ATPase-compromised Salmonella, including FliH/FliI or FliJ defects; the intact FliH/FliI/FliJ system showed no sodium dependence under the tested conditions and supported efficient proton-motive-force-driven export.
    • DOI:10.1038/s42003-024-06081-0 Kinoshita et al. 2024 found that FliH and FliI help FlhA enforce ordered protein export.

Mechanism graphs

Membrane gate assembly drives flagellar substrate export (FUNCTION)

FliP and FliR form a helical core with peripheral FliQ subunits and FlhB around its base. FlhA connects this gate to the cytoplasmic FliH/FliI/FliJ ATPase machinery. Efficient intact-system export is proton-motive-force driven in the cited Salmonella experiments; sodium coupling was demonstrated with ATPase-system defects.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Should FliO be curated as a conserved constituent of the mature FlhA/FlhB/FliP/FliQ/FliR export-gate core? GO:0120102 includes FliO in its legacy inventory. Fukumura et al. 2017 studied FliO-assisted isolated FliP hexamers and detergent-dependent associations, not a FliP5-FliQ4-FliR1-FlhB1 reconstruction. Later structures resolved Salmonella FliP5-FliQ4-FliR1 (Kuhlen 2018) and Vibrio mimicus FliP5-FliQ4-FliR1-FlhB1 (Kuhlen 2020), supporting exclusion of transient assembly factor FliO from the conserved mature core.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Should the broad InterPro FliI/YscN family ground the FliI component? InterPro currently offers a mixed FliI/YscN type III secretion ATPase family and three clade-specific flagellar FliI entries. The broad entry overstates this flagellum-specific component, while any one clade-specific entry understates its bacterial scope.
