bacterial-type flagellum secretion apparatus

GO:0120102 · APPENDAGE · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A flagellum-specific type III export apparatus at the cytoplasmic side of the bacterial MS ring that uses a FlhA/FlhB/FliP/FliQ/FliR membrane export gate and a FliH/FliI/FliJ cytoplasmic ATPase complex to export rod, hook, and filament subunits through the growing flagellum. [DOI:10.1016/j.bbamcr.2013.09.005]

Synonyms: bacterial-type flagellum export apparatus; flagellar type III export apparatus; flagellar type III export gate complex

Part of: GO:0009425

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: Flagellum-specific type III export apparatuses occur in bacteria that assemble bacterial-type flagella and are absent from bacteria that lack the flagellar system. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro1887]
  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — ABSENT: Archaeal archaella are type-IV-pilus-related rotary filaments rather than bacterial flagella and do not use a FlhA/FlhB/FliP/FliQ/FliR export gate. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:90371 Salmonella enterica subsp. enterica serovar Typhimurium — Classical genetic and biochemical model for flagellar type III export and source organism for the reconstituted FliP5-FliQ4-FliR1-FlhB1 export-gate core. [DOI:10.1371/journal.pbio.2002281]

Functions

  • flagellar axial-subunit export — The apparatus secretes unfolded rod, hook and filament substrates from the cytoplasm through the narrow central channel of the growing bacterial flagellum.
    • DOI:10.1128/JB.181.5.1388-1394.1999 Minamino and Macnab 1999 classified Salmonella flagellar export substrates for the apparatus.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.bbamcr.2013.09.005 Minamino 2014 reviewed export of flagellar axial proteins through the bacterial flagellar type III pathway.
  • membrane export-gate assembly — FliP assembly seeds the FliP/FliQ/FliR membrane core, which combines with FlhA and FlhB to form the flagellar export gate.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.pbio.2002281 Fukumura et al. 2017 determined the FliP5-FliQ4-FliR1-FlhB1 core stoichiometry.
    • DOI:10.1007/82_2019_170 Minamino et al. 2019 reviewed the ordered assembly pathway of the flagellar type III export apparatus.
  • ATPase- and ion-coupled export — A soluble FliH/FliI/FliJ ATPase complex and proton or sodium motive force activate the export gate and drive efficient processive export.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1005495 Minamino et al. 2016 showed that the flagellar export gate can use proton and sodium gradients.
    • DOI:10.1038/s42003-024-06081-0 Kinoshita et al. 2024 found that FliH and FliI help FlhA enforce ordered protein export.

Mechanism graphs

Membrane gate assembly drives flagellar substrate export (FUNCTION)

FliP, FliQ and FliR form the small hydrophobic membrane core, FlhB caps that core, FlhA completes the membrane gate, FliH, FliI and FliJ assemble the cytoplasmic ATPase complex that couples to FlhA, and ATP plus ion motive force drive rod, hook and filament subunits through the MS-ring-embedded export apparatus.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Should FliO be curated as a conserved constituent of the mature FlhA/FlhB/FliP/FliQ/FliR export-gate core? GO:0120102's legacy definition lists FliO with the secretion apparatus membrane proteins, and FliO promotes FliP assembly in the Salmonella system. However, the biochemically reconstituted Salmonella core contains FliP5-FliQ4-FliR1-FlhB1, the final gate is modeled with a FlhA nonamer, and FliO is not part of that conserved final membrane-gate stoichiometry.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Should the broad InterPro FliI/YscN family ground the FliI component? InterPro currently offers a mixed FliI/YscN type III secretion ATPase family and three clade-specific flagellar FliI entries. The broad entry overstates this flagellum-specific component, while any one clade-specific entry understates its bacterial scope.
