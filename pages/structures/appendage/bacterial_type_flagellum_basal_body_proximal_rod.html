bacterial-type flagellum basal body, proximal rod

GO:0009429 · APPENDAGE · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The portion of the central rod of the bacterial-type flagellar basal body that is proximal to the cell membrane; the proximal rod connects the distal rod to the flagellar motor. [GO:0009429]

Synonyms: flagellar basal body, proximal rod; flagellin-based flagellum basal body, proximal rod

Part of: GO:0030694

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — COMMON: Proximal rods occur in bacterial-type flagellar basal bodies but are absent from bacteria without flagellar loci. [DOI:10.1128/JB.181.23.7149-7153.1999]
  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — ABSENT: Archaeal archaella are type-IV-pilus-related appendages and do not contain the FliE/FlgB/FlgC/FlgF proximal rod. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:90371 Salmonella enterica subsp. enterica serovar Typhimurium — Classical genetic, biochemical and structural model for proximal-rod composition and its connection to FliF and FlgG. [DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2021.03.057]

Functions

  • motor to distal-rod mechanical coupling — The proximal rod links the FliF/MS-ring side of the motor to the FlgG distal rod to carry rotation into the external hook-filament assembly.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2021.03.057 Tan et al. 2021 resolved FliE and FlgB contacts that couple the MS ring to the helical rod.
    • DOI:10.1128/JB.182.11.3029-3036.2000 Minamino et al. 2000 placed the FliE-FlgB junction at the proximal end of the rod.
  • proximal rod segment formation — FliE, FlgB, FlgC and FlgF provide the motor-proximal axial subunits of the segmented rod upstream of the distal FlgG segment.
    • DOI:10.1016/0022-2836(90)90365-S Homma et al. 1990 model the Salmonella rod as sequential FlgB/FlgC/FlgF proximal segments followed by the FlgG distal segment.
    • DOI:10.1016/0022-2836(92)90958-m Kubori et al. 1992 used Salmonella assembly intermediates to place FliE and the FlgB/FlgC/FlgF/FlgG proteins in the rod assembly pathway.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Should FlgG, the MS ring, or the flagellar export apparatus be curated as proximal-rod components? The mature GO:0009429 proximal rod is scoped to the FliE/FlgB/FlgC/FlgF segment. FlgG forms the distal rod, the FliF MS ring is motor-proximal to FliE, and the flagellar type III export apparatus secretes axial substrates but is not a mature proximal-rod subunit.
