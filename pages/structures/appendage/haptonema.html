haptonema

cellstructuremech:haptonema · APPENDAGE · APPENDAGE · PROPOSED

An elongated, plasma-membrane-bounded, microtubule-supported appendage of haptophyte algae that emerges from the flagellar pole and can coil rapidly during haptonemal motility or participate in particle capture. [DOI:10.1242/bio.036590]

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2830 Haptophyta — COMMON: The haptonema is a microtubule-based motile organelle described as unique to haptophyte algae. [DOI:10.1242/bio.036590]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:35142 Haptolina hirta — Marine haptophyte, previously Chrysochromulina hirta, in which the haptonema was observed attaching to food particles during phagotrophic feeding. [DOI:10.2216/i0031-8884-30-6-563.1]

Functions

  • rapid haptonemal coiling — The haptonemal microtubule bundle can undergo rapid calcium-dependent conformational changes that coil the appendage in haptophyte cells.
    • DOI:10.1242/bio.036590 Nomura et al. 2019 used microtubule stabilization to show that Ca2+-dependent microtubule conformational changes are required for rapid haptonemal coiling.
  • haptonemal particle capture — The haptonema can attach to food particles and bring them toward the haptophyte cell surface during feeding.
    • DOI:10.2216/i0031-8884-30-6-563.1 Kawachi et al. 1991 observed haptonema-mediated food-particle capture in Haptolina hirta.
    • DOI:10.2216/i0031-8884-34-3-193.1 Kawachi and Inouye 1995 characterized functional roles of the haptonema during feeding by Chrysochromulina spinifera.
