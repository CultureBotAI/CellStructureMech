bacterial-type flagellum hook-filament junction

GO:0009422 · APPENDAGE · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The FlgK/FlgL hook-associated protein junction at the distal end of the bacterial-type flagellar hook that couples the flexible hook to the flagellin filament and buffers mechanical stress while initiating filament assembly. [DOI:10.1038/s41564-025-02037-0]

Synonyms: flagellar hook-filament junction; FlgK-FlgL hook-filament junction

Part of: GO:0009288

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: FlgK/FlgL hook-filament junctions occur in bacteria that assemble complete bacterial-type flagella and are absent from lineages that lack the rotary flagellar system. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro1887]
  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — ABSENT: Archaeal archaella are type-IV-pilus-related appendages and do not contain the FlgK/FlgL junction proteins of bacterial flagella. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:28901 Salmonella enterica — Source species for the native FlgK/FlgL hook-filament junction resolved with its surrounding hook, filament and FliD cap. [DOI:10.1038/s41564-025-02037-0]

Functions

  • hook-filament coupling — FlgK and FlgL form a short junction between the flexible hook and the rigid flagellin filament.
    • DOI:10.1128/jb.162.1.183-189.1985 Homma and Iino 1985 localized HAP1 and HAP3 between the hook and filament in Salmonella.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41564-025-02037-0 Einenkel et al. 2025 resolved the FlgK/FlgL junction connecting the native Salmonella hook to the filament.
  • mechanical stress buffering — The FlgK/FlgL junction mechanically separates the curved hook from the filament and limits stress transfer into the filament lattice.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41564-025-02037-0 Einenkel et al. 2025 structurally modeled the hook-filament junction as a buffer that prevents hook-derived stress from propagating into the filament.
  • filament assembly initiation — The junction provides the distal hook platform on which the FliD cap assembles to initiate ordered flagellin incorporation into the growing filament.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41564-025-02037-0 Einenkel et al. 2025 resolved a Campylobacter hook-filament junction with the FliD cap before filament assembly and native Salmonella cap-filament intermediates.

Physical properties

  • SUBUNIT_COUNT: 22 (11 FlgK/HAP1 plus 11 FlgL/HAP3 subunits in the Salmonella hook-filament junction) [DOI:10.1038/s41564-025-02037-0]

Mechanism graphs

FlgK and FlgL connect the hook to the filament (FUNCTION)

FlgK assembles next to the hook and FlgL next to the filament to form a two-layer hook-filament junction. The junction joins the flexible hook to the rigid filament, supports FliD cap placement during filament initiation, and buffers hook-derived mechanical stress.
