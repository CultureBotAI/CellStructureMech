Functions

  • outer-membrane bushing anchoring — The L ring sits at the outer-membrane side of the LP-ring bushing around the distal rod in Gram-negative flagellar basal bodies.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-021-24715-3 Yamaguchi et al. 2021 placed the Salmonella FlgH L ring at the outer side of the LP ring.
  • LP-ring bushing support — Together with the P ring, the L ring forms a bushing that surrounds the distal rod and supports rotation of the flagellar drive shaft.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-021-24715-3 Yamaguchi et al. 2021 structurally resolved the LP ring around the distal rod in Salmonella hook-basal-body complexes.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Should the P ring, distal rod, lipopolysaccharide, outer membrane, FlgA, or FlgJ be curated as L-ring components? The mature GO:0009427 L ring is the FlgH ring at the outer-membrane side of the LP-ring bushing. FlgI forms the adjacent P ring, the FlgG distal rod runs through the bushing, lipopolysaccharide and the cell outer membrane host the L ring, and FlgA and FlgJ act during outer-ring or rod assembly rather than remaining constituents of the mature L ring.
