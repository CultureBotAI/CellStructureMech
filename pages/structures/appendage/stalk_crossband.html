stalk crossband

cellstructuremech:stalk_crossband · Appendage · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A disk-like multiprotein diffusion barrier that spans the Caulobacter stalk cross-section. StpABCD-containing stalk crossbands partition the prostheca into compartments by limiting exchange of soluble and membrane proteins between the cell body and stalk segments. [DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2012.10.046]

Synonyms: stalk band; Caulobacter crossband

Part of: prostheca (cellstructuremech:prostheca)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

  • NCBITaxon:155892 Caulobacter vibrioides — UNIVERSAL: Crossbands form along the Caulobacter stalk as part of the stalked-cell program. [DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2012.10.046]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:565050 Caulobacter vibrioides NA1000 — Model strain used to identify the proteinaceous StpABCD diffusion barriers in the stalk. [DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2012.10.046]

Functions

  • stalk diffusion barrier — Crossbands restrict soluble and membrane-protein exchange between the cell body and the prostheca, and between neighboring stalk compartments.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2012.10.046 Schlimpert et al. 2012 show that crossbands block diffusion across Caulobacter stalk compartments.

Mechanism graphs

StpABCD proteins assemble stalk-compartment diffusion barriers (ASSEMBLY)

In Caulobacter, StpA and StpB form the crossband scaffold; StpC and StpD tighten its diffusion barrier. Crossbands span the prostheca, and intact barriers limit soluble and membrane-protein exchange across stalk compartments.
