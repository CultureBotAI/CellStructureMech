OmcZ cytochrome nanowire

cellstructuremech:omcz_cytochrome_nanowire · APPENDAGE · APPENDAGE · PROPOSED

An extracellular electrically conductive Geobacter sulfurreducens filament assembled from the octaheme c-type cytochrome OmcZ; each subunit contributes a branched heme arrangement with a surface-exposed heme site to the cytochrome polymer. [DOI:10.7554/eLife.81551]

Synonyms: OmcZ nanowire; OmcZ filament

Functions

  • branched-heme long-range electron transfer — OmcZ subunits arrange covalently bound hemes in a branched network with a surface-exposed heme site per subunit, providing a structural path for long-range electron transfer in conductive OmcZ filaments.
    • DOI:10.7554/eLife.81551 Wang et al. 2022 contrasted OmcZ with linear OmcS and OmcE heme chains and connected the OmcZ branched, surface-exposed heme arrangement with the filament's high conductivity.
