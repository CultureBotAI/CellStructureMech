9+2 motile cilium

GO:0097729 — 9+2 motile cilium · Appendage · Appendage · Proposed

A motile cilium where the axoneme has a ring of nine outer microtubule doublets plus two central microtubules (and is therefore called a 9+2 axoneme). A canonical motile cilium (or eukaryotic flagellum) whose axoneme consists of nine peripheral microtubule doublets surrounding a central pair of singlet microtubules (the 9+2 arrangement). Conserved across Chlamydomonas reinhardtii, Tetrahymena thermophila, and Trypanosoma brucei, the 9+2 motile cilium integrates central pair projections, radial spokes, nexin-dynein regulatory complexes, and dynein arms to produce planar or ciliary waveforms. [GO:0097729 — 9+2 motile cilium]

Synonyms: motile 9+2 cilium; conventional motile cilium; sperm flagellum

Has part: paraflagellar rod (GO:0097740)
