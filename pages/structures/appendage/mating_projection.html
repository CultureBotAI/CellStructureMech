mating projection

GO:0005937 · APPENDAGE · APPENDAGE · PROPOSED

The projection formed by unicellular fungi in response to mating pheromone. [GO:0005937]

Synonyms: shmoo; conjugation tube

Has part: GO:0070250 GO:0043332 GO:0001400 GO:0032175 GO:0032162

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — VARIABLE: Haploid budding-yeast cells extend mating projections after pheromone exposure rather than during every growth state. [DOI:10.1091/mbc.3.4.429]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Budding-yeast model in which pheromone treatment induces a polarized mating projection containing Spa2-positive tips. [DOI:10.1091/mbc.3.4.429]

Functions

  • mating-projection polarized growth — The mating projection concentrates polarized-growth machinery at its tip to build a pheromone-induced fungal outgrowth.
    • GO:0005937 GO:0005937 defines the projection as the structure formed by unicellular fungi in response to mating pheromone.
    • DOI:10.1091/mbc.3.4.429 Read et al. 1992 used actin and beta-tubulin mutants to analyze Saccharomyces cerevisiae mating-projection formation.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.200307065 Bidlingmaier and Snyder 2004 studied polarisome components during cyclic initiation and termination of yeast mating-projection growth.

Mechanism graphs

Mating-projection polarity topology (FUNCTION)

The fungal mating projection has a surrounding membrane, a polarized-growth tip, a basal region, septin structures at the neck, and a tip-localized polarisome.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep polarisome and septin proteins on their own structure records rather than duplicating them as mating projection components. GO:0005937 identifies the whole projection, while GO child terms identify localized membrane, tip, base, septin, and polarisome substructures.
