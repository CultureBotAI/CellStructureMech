transverse flagellum

GO:0097608 · Appendage · Appendage · Proposed

A motile cilium found in dinoflagellates. It coils around the cell and provides the forward thrust for motility. It is often contained in a furrow called the cingulum, and emerges from a flagellar pore located in the cingulum. The ribbon-like anterior/equatorial eukaryotic flagellum of dinoflagellates that emerges from the ventral flagellar pore and beats within the horizontal cingulum furrow encircling the cell. It contains a 9+2 axoneme paired with a contractile striated paraflagellar rod (paraxonemal strand) that holds the flagellum in a helical wave, generating both forward thrust and rotational torque during swimming. [GO:0097608]

Synonyms: transverse cilium
