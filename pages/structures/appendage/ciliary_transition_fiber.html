ciliary transition fiber

GO:0097539 · Appendage · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A nine-bladed, propeller-like protein complex that links the distal end of the basal body and the cilium to the plasma membrane. Functions in protein sorting and gating (i.e. active and passive transport of proteins in and out of the cilium). [GO:0097539]

Synonyms: centriolar distal appendage; cilial transition fiber; cilial transition fibre; ciliary transition fibre; cilium transition fiber; cilium transition fibre; distal appendage of basal body; distal appendage of centriole; distal appendage of mother centriole; transition fiber; transition fibre

Part of: ciliary basal body (GO:0036064)

Functions

  • basal-body membrane linkage — The radial transition-fiber complex links the distal basal body and cilium to the plasma membrane.
    • GO:0097539 The exact cellular-component definition supplies this anatomical linking function; no individual protein is assigned direct lipid binding.
  • support of new flagellum assembly — Transition-fiber-associated proteins contribute to normal new-flagellum assembly in procyclic trypanosomes.
    • DOI:10.1242/jcs.261692 RNAi of FBF1 and five other mapped proteins impairs new-flagellum assembly. This does not isolate fiber docking, prove loss of every blade, or establish a specific IFT-entry reaction.
