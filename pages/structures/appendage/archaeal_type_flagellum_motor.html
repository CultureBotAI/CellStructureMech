archaeal-type flagellum motor

cellstructuremech:archaeal_type_flagellum_motor · APPENDAGE · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A membrane-associated ATP-powered motor complex of the archaeal-type flagellum that assembles and rotates the external archaellin filament. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]

Synonyms: archaellum motor; archaeal flagellum motor

Part of: GO:0097589

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — VARIABLE: Archaellum motors occur in archaeal lineages that encode the archaeal-type flagellum and are absent from archaea that lack the motility operon. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]
  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — ABSENT: Bacterial flagellar motors are non-homologous rotary machines built from FliF/FliG/FliM/FliN rotors and Mot/Pom stators rather than archaellum Arl/Fla subassemblies. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:2285 Sulfolobus acidocaldarius — Crenarchaeal source for biochemical analysis of FlaX scaffold ring formation and interaction with the motor ATPase FlaI. [DOI:10.1074/jbc.M112.414383]
  • NCBITaxon:2261 Pyrococcus furiosus — Euryarchaeal source for in situ cryo-ET of basal archaellum machinery at the archaeal cell envelope. [DOI:10.7554/eLife.27470]

Functions

  • ATP-powered archaellum assembly — The motor uses ATP hydrolysis by ArlI/FlaI to assemble archaellin subunits at the base of the growing archaeal-type flagellum filament.
    • DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023 Albers and Jarrell 2015 review ATP-powered archaellum filament assembly by the basal motor.
  • ATP-powered archaellum rotation — The motor rotates the assembled archaellin filament to drive archaeal-type flagellum-dependent motility.
    • DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023 Albers and Jarrell 2015 review ATP-powered rotation of the archaellum as the basis of archaeal swimming.
