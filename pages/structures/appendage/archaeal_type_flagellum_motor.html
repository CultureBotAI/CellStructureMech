archaeal-type flagellum motor

cellstructuremech:archaeal_type_flagellum_motor · Appendage · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A membrane-associated ATP-powered motor complex of the archaeal-type flagellum that assembles and rotates the external archaellin filament. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]

Synonyms: archaellum motor; archaeal flagellum motor

Part of: archaeal-type flagellum (GO:0097589)

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — VARIABLE: Archaellum motors occur in archaeal lineages that encode the archaeal-type flagellum and are absent from archaea that lack the motility operon. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]
  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: Litorilinea aerophila DSM 25763 forms archaellin filaments and swims after growth on motility plates. Other Chloroflexota candidates are supported by genome surveys, not equivalent experimental observations. Its motor architecture is inferred from loci and predicted ArlI/ArlJ structures, not a directly resolved bacterial motor. [DOI:10.1038/s41564-025-02110-8]

Canonical examples

Functions

  • ATP-powered archaellum assembly — The motor uses ATP hydrolysis by ArlI/FlaI to assemble archaellin subunits at the base of the growing archaeal-type flagellum filament.
    • DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023 Albers and Jarrell 2015 review ATP-powered archaellum filament assembly by the basal motor.
  • ATP-powered archaellum rotation — The motor rotates the assembled archaellin filament to drive archaeal-type flagellum-dependent motility.
    • DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023 Albers and Jarrell 2015 review ATP-powered rotation of the archaellum as the basis of archaeal swimming.

Discussions

This first motor record follows Arl/Fla nomenclature and intentionally leaves the ATPase/membrane core, ArlH/FlaH regulator, envelope anchor, and cytosolic scaffold modules label-only until exact family or complex identifiers can be verified.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Archaellins and prearchaellin maturation enzymes are out of scope for the motor record. The archaellum motor is the basal ATP-powered machine. Archaellins are the polymerized filament subunits, and PibD/FlaK-like prepilin peptidases act on archaellin precursors before filament assembly rather than forming part of the basal motor.
