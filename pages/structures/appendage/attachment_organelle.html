attachment organelle

GO:0033099 · Appendage · Appendage · Proposed

A membrane-bounded polar cell extension in Mycoplasma species that concentrates cytadherence and cytoskeletal proteins for host-cell attachment, gliding motility, and organelle-linked cell division. [GO:0033099]

Synonyms: terminal organelle

Has part: attachment organelle membrane (GO:0033111)

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:544448 Mycoplasmatota — VARIABLE: Attachment organelles are characterized in the Mycoplasma pneumoniae cluster within Mycoplasmatota, not universally across the phylum. Chen et al. use historical Mollicutes terminology; current NCBI taxonomy places the M129 example in Mycoplasmoidales outside class Mollicutes (NCBITaxon:31969). [DOI:10.1186/s12866-025-04320-w]
  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — RARE: The characterized attachment organelle is restricted to a small mycoplasma lineage and is not a general bacterial appendage. [DOI:10.1186/s12866-025-04320-w]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:272634 Mycoplasmoides pneumoniae M129 — The Nakane et al. structural mapping work used the M129 type strain, named Mycoplasma pneumoniae M129 in that literature. [DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1005299]

Functions

  • host-cell cytadherence — Polar clustering of P1-family and P30 adhesins lets cells attach to host tissue surfaces.
    • DOI:10.1046/j.1365-2958.2003.03899.x Krause and Balish 2004 review the terminal organelle as the polarized structure that mediates M. pneumoniae cytadherence.
    • DOI:10.1128/JB.181.4.1079-1087.1999 Romero-Arroyo et al. 1999 showed P30 is required for normal cytadherence.
  • attachment-organelle-dependent gliding motility — The polar protrusion supports M. pneumoniae gliding over host surfaces. Coupling of internal-core deformation to surface adhesins is a proposed mechanism, not directly established by the cited structural imaging.
    • DOI:10.1128/mBio.00243-16 Kawamoto et al. 2016 measured structural periodicity in frozen-hydrated attachment organelles by electron cryotomography and proposed a gliding mechanism from the observed variation. The cryotomograms do not directly image internal-core deformation during live gliding.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1005299 Nakane et al. 2015 integrated surface and internal-core protein maps into a model for gliding motility.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1009621 Nakane et al. 2021 linked engineered HMW2 length to organelle length and gliding speed. Dual-color fluorescence tracking at 50 Hz in 20 cells did not detect large stepwise displacement. This limits the large-deformation model without excluding all smaller conformational changes.

Internal-core proteins organize a polar adhesin-bearing attachment organelle (FUNCTION)

HMW2, P65, and HMW3 organize the terminal button and paired plates of the internal core. The polar extension positions P1/P40/P90 and P30 adhesins on the surface, coupling cytadherence with attachment-organelle-dependent gliding.
