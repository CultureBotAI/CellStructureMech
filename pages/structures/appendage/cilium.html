cilium

GO:0005929 · Appendage · Appendage · Proposed

A specialized eukaryotic organelle that consists of a filiform extrusion of the cell surface and of some cytoplasmic parts; it is largely bounded by an extrusion of the plasma membrane, contains a longitudinal microtubule array, and is anchored to a basal body. [GO:0005929]

Synonyms: eukaryotic flagellum; microtubule-based flagellum

Has part: axoneme (GO:0005930); ciliary membrane (GO:0060170); ciliary basal body (GO:0036064); ciliary transition zone (GO:0035869)

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Should bacterial-type flagella or archaeal-type flagella be merged under the cilium record? GO:0005929 explicitly scopes cilium to the membrane-sheathed, microtubule-based eukaryotic organelle. Bacterial-type flagella and archaeal-type flagella are nonhomologous prokaryotic appendages with separate GO identities and separate CellStructureMech records.
