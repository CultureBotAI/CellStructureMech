cilium

GO:0005929 · APPENDAGE · APPENDAGE · PROPOSED

A specialized eukaryotic organelle that consists of a filiform extrusion of the cell surface and of some cytoplasmic parts; it is largely bounded by an extrusion of the plasma membrane, contains a longitudinal microtubule array, and is anchored to a basal body. [GO:0005929]

Synonyms: eukaryotic flagellum; microtubule-based flagellum

Bacterial-type flagella and archaeal-type flagella are nonhomologous prokaryotic appendages with separate GO identities and separate CellStructureMech records.
