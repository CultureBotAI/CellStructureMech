type I pilus

GO:0140621 · APPENDAGE · APPENDAGE · PROPOSED

A chaperone-usher bacterial pilus assembled from a helical FimA rod tipped by the adaptor subunits FimG and FimF and the mannose-binding FimH adhesin. In Escherichia coli, the type I pilus is polymerized through the outer-membrane FimD usher and terminated by a basal FimI subunit that anchors the completed rod to the assembly platform. [GO:0140621]

Synonyms: type 1 pilus; type 1 fimbria; mannose-sensitive pilus

Mechanism graphs

FimC and FimD polymerize the type I pilus tip and rod (ASSEMBLY)

FimC carries folded pilus subunits to the outer-membrane FimD usher. FimD accepts the FimH adhesin, FimG adaptor, and FimF adaptor before adding FimA repeatedly into the helical rod, then the FimI terminator caps the basal end and anchors the rod at the usher.
