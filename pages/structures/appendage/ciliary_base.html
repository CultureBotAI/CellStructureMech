ciliary base

GO:0097546 · Appendage · Subcellular region · Proposed

Area of the cilium (also called flagellum) where the basal body and the axoneme are anchored to the plasma membrane. The ciliary base encompasses the distal part of the basal body, transition fibers and transition zone and is structurally and functionally very distinct from the rest of the cilium. In this area proteins are sorted and filtered before entering the cilium, and many ciliary proteins localize specifically to this area. The specialized proximal region of a eukaryotic cilium or flagellum encompassing the basal body docking site, transition fibers, and ciliary transition zone in ciliated protists and microalgae. The ciliary base acts as a molecular gate and assembly hub where intraflagellar transport (IFT) trains are loaded and membrane diffusion between the plasma membrane and ciliary membrane is regulated. [GO:0097546]

Synonyms: cilial base; cilium base; flagellar base; flagellum base

Part of: cilium (GO:0005929)
