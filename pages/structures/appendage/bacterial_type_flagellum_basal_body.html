bacterial-type flagellum basal body

GO:0009425 · APPENDAGE · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The basal cell-envelope substructure of a bacterial flagellum, consisting of a rod, a series of rings, Mot stator proteins, the switch complex, and the proximal flagellum-specific type III export apparatus. [GO:0009425]

Synonyms: bacterial flagellum basal body; bacterial flagellar basal body

Part of: GO:0009288

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — COMMON: Bacterial-type flagellar basal bodies are restricted to bacteria with bacterial flagella and absent from strains and lineages that have lost the organelle. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro1887]
  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — ABSENT: Archaea swim with archaella, which are type-IV-pilus-related machines rather than bacterial-type flagellar basal bodies. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:90371 Salmonella enterica subsp. enterica serovar Typhimurium — Classical genetic and cryo-EM model for bacterial basal-body assembly, the MS/C rotor, the LP-ring bushing, and rod-driven torque transmission. [DOI:10.1038/s41564-021-00895-y]

Functions

  • flagellum anchoring in the cell envelope — The basal-body rings and rod span the inner membrane, peptidoglycan and, in Gram-negative bacteria, the outer membrane to mount the flagellar rotor.
    • DOI:10.1146/annurev.micro.57.030502.090832 Macnab 2003 reviews assembly of the basal body as the envelope-spanning foundation for the bacterial flagellum.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41564-021-00895-y Johnson et al. 2021 resolved the intact Salmonella basal body spanning the inner membrane, cell wall and outer membrane.
  • rotary torque transmission — Ion-driven stator units act on the C ring so the MS ring and basal-body rod rotate and transmit torque to the hook and filament.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2021.03.057 Tan et al. 2021 resolved structural couplings by which the MS ring, FliE, FlgB, distal rod and LP ring support torque transmission through the flagellar motor.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41564-020-0788-8 Deme et al. 2020 structurally modeled the MotA5MotB2 stator as the ion-driven unit that drives the FliG rotor.
