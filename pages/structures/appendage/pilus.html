pilus

GO:0009289 · APPENDAGE · APPENDAGE · PROPOSED

A proteinaceous hair-like appendage on the surface of bacteria ranging from 2-8 nm in diameter. [GO:0009289]

Synonyms: fimbria; fimbriae; fimbrium; pili

Functions

  • bacterial surface interaction — Bacterial pili project from the cell surface and, depending on subtype, mediate adhesion, cell-cell interactions, biofilm formation, conjugation, DNA uptake or twitching motility.
    • uniprot.location:SL-0113 UniProt Subcellular Location entry SL-0113 summarizes adhesion, motility, cell-cell interaction, biofilm, conjugation and DNA uptake roles for bacterial pili.
    • DOI:10.1038/nrmicro.2017.40 Hospenthal, Costa and Waksman 2017 review the adhesive, motility and DNA-uptake roles of Gram-negative bacterial pili.
