OmcE cytochrome nanowire

cellstructuremech:omce_cytochrome_nanowire · APPENDAGE · APPENDAGE · PROPOSED

An extracellular Geobacter sulfurreducens cytochrome nanowire assembled from OmcE tetraheme c-type cytochrome subunits and resolved by cryo-EM as a helical filament whose hemes pack within the filament core. [DOI:10.1038/s41564-022-01159-z]

Synonyms: OmcE cytochrome filament; OmcE nanowire

Functions
