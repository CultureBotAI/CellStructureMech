archaeal-type flagellum

GO:0097589 · Appendage · Appendage · Proposed

A non-membrane-bounded motility organelle, also called the archaellum, characterized in Archaea and in the bacterium Litorilinea aerophila; a membrane-associated motor drives swimming through rotation of extracellular type-IV-pilin-like archaellin filaments. [DOI:10.1038/s41564-025-02110-8]

Synonyms: archaellum; archaeal flagellum

Mechanism graphs

ATP-driven basal assembly builds and rotates the archaellum filament (ASSEMBLY)

Prearchaellins are processed and assembled by an ATP-powered ArlI/ArlJ motor. ArlH and lineage-specific scaffold proteins organize that motor; envelope-side FlaF/FlaG proteins are proposed to anchor it to the S-layer. Motor-driven filament rotation propels archaeal cells.
