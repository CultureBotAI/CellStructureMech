archaeal-type flagellum

GO:0097589 · APPENDAGE · APPENDAGE · PROPOSED

A non-membrane-bounded archaeal motility organelle, also called the archaellum, whose ATP-powered membrane motor rotates one or more glycosylated type-IV-pilin-like archaellin filaments outside the cell to drive swimming. [GO:0097589]

Synonyms: archaellum; archaeal flagellum

Mechanism graphs

ATP-driven basal assembly builds and rotates the archaellum filament (ASSEMBLY)

Prearchaellins are processed and added to the base of a helical filament by an ATP-powered ArlI/ArlJ motor. ArlH and lineage-specific scaffold proteins organize that motor, envelope proteins anchor it, and motor-driven rotation of the assembled filament propels archaeal cells.
