flagella connector

GO:0120118 · APPENDAGE · OTHER · PROPOSED

A mobile transmembrane junction at the tip of the flagellum of some kinetoplastid species linking the tip of a new growing flagellum to an older flagellum. [GO:0120118]

Synonyms: FC

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:5691 Trypanosoma brucei — VARIABLE: The flagella connector is a division-stage junction in procyclic Trypanosoma brucei that links the tip of the new flagellum to the old flagellum. [DOI:10.1242/jcs.00995]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5691 Trypanosoma brucei — African trypanosome model in which the flagella connector was defined as a motile transmembrane junction and later resolved by electron and cryo-electron tomography. [DOI:10.1371/journal.pntd.0004312]

Functions

  • new-to-old flagellum linkage — The FC couples the distal tip of the growing new flagellum to the flank of the old flagellum during T. brucei flagellum biogenesis.
    • DOI:10.1242/jcs.00995 Briggs et al. 2004 characterized the T. brucei flagella connector as a mobile transmembrane junction linking the new and old flagella.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.pntd.0004312 Hoog et al. 2016 resolved FC layers between the new and old flagella by electron tomography and cryo-electron tomography.
  • new-flagellum tip tracking — The FC remains associated with the tip of the assembling new flagellum while moving along the old flagellum.
    • DOI:10.1126/science.1063775 Moreira-Leite et al. 2001 first described the motile junction that connects the growing new flagellum to the old flagellum during trypanosome cell division.
    • DOI:10.1242/jcs.03203 Davidge et al. 2006 used intraflagellar-transport mutants to dissect FC movement from extension of the new flagellar axoneme.
  • distal flagellar-tip coupling — FC-associated proteins couple the connector membrane junction to microtubules at the distal tips of the assembling and mature trypanosome flagella.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.1703553114 Varga et al. 2017 identified FC constituents and assigned kinesin-family proteins to the links between the FC membrane junction and microtubules at the distal new- and old-flagellum tips.
