mating projection base

GO:0001400 · APPENDAGE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The region where the mating projection meets the bulk of the cell, in unicellular fungi exposed to mating pheromone. [GO:0001400]

Synonyms: base of shmoo tip; conjugation tube base

Part of: GO:0005937
