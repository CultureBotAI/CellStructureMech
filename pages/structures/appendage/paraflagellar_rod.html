paraflagellar rod

GO:0097740 · APPENDAGE · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

A large lattice-like axial structure found in some flagellated protists which extends alongside the axoneme. Protein components of the paraflagellar rod are likely implicated, among other, in adenine nucleotide signaling and metabolism, and in calcium signaling. [GO:0097740]

Synonyms: PFR; paraxial rod

Part of: GO:0097729

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2759 Eukaryota — RARE: The paraflagellar rod is restricted to a small set of flagellated protist lineages rather than a conserved feature of eukaryotic cilia. [PMID:7961827]
  • NCBITaxon:33682 Euglenozoa — VARIABLE: Within Euglenozoa, PFRs are prominent in kinetoplastid flagella, but they can be greatly reduced and cryptic in some endosymbiont-bearing kinetoplastids. [DOI:10.1128/EC.4.3.516-525.2005]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5702 Trypanosoma brucei brucei — Trypanosome model in which deletion of cytoplasmic PFR-AF1 or PFR-AF2 showed that both assembly factors are required for paraflagellar rod formation. [DOI:10.1242/jcs.242271]
  • NCBITaxon:5665 Leishmania mexicana — Genetic model in which PFR1 and PFR2 knockouts established that both major proteins are required for native PFR formation and flagellar motility. [DOI:10.1242/jcs.112.16.2753]

Functions

  • flagellar waveform control — The PFR provides an extra-axonemal scaffold needed for normal kinetoplastid flagellar beating and cell motility.
    • DOI:10.1038/35300 Bastin et al. 1998 disrupted the PFR in T. brucei and linked PFR loss to a trypanosome motility defect.
    • PMID:9497035 Santrich et al. 1997 showed that a Leishmania mexicana PFR2 knockout lacks most PFR structure and has altered flagellar beat waveform and forward swimming.
  • extra-axonemal PFR assembly — New PFR lattice proteins are delivered into the flagellum and incorporated into the elongating paraflagellar rod, a process that requires cytoplasmic PFR assembly factors.
    • DOI:10.1128/MCB.19.12.8191 Bastin et al. 1999 followed tagged PFRA during PFR growth and observed a major distal addition site plus minor incorporation along the partially assembled PFR.
    • DOI:10.1242/jcs.242271 Alves et al. 2020 identified cytoplasmic PFR-AF1 and PFR-AF2 as factors required for PFR formation.
  • flagellar signaling platform — The PFR-associated protein cohort includes calcium/calmodulin and adenine-nucleotide signaling or metabolic proteins that can influence flagellar beating.
    • DOI:10.1074/jbc.M808859200 Portman et al. 2009 used RNAi-mutant comparative proteomics to expand the PFR cohort and infer calcium/calmodulin regulatory links among some components.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.ijpara.2009.10.005 Portman and Gull 2010 review links between the PFR proteome, adenine-nucleotide metabolism, calcium signaling and motility regulation.

PFR1 and PFR2 build the paraflagellar rod lattice (ASSEMBLY)

PFR1 and PFR2 are transported into the flagellum and co-assemble into the extra-axonemal paraflagellar rod lattice. Cytoplasmic PFR-AF1 and PFR-AF2 act before flagellar entry and are needed to accumulate the major PFR proteins and form the mature rod.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Should all proteomic PFR candidates be curated as paraflagellar-rod components now? The PFR proteome contains many low-abundance or regulatory proteins in addition to the two major lattice proteins; Portman et al. 2009 identified 20 novel PFR components by RNAi-ablation proteomics and epitope tagging. This first GO:0097740 record therefore enumerates only PFR1/PFR2, while additional candidates need family-by-family curation to avoid folding transient or nearby flagellar proteins into the structural lattice boundary.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Are PFR-AF1 and PFR-AF2 paraflagellar-rod components? PFR-AF1 and PFR-AF2 are cytoplasmic proteins required for PFR formation, but the component boundary in docs/CURATION.md excludes factors that act on a subunit before assembly and then depart.
