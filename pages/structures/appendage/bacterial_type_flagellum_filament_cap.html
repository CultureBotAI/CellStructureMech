Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: FliD/HAP2 filament caps occur in bacteria that assemble completed bacterial-type flagellar filaments and are absent from lineages that lack the rotary flagellar system. [DOI:10.1128/JB.181.23.7149-7153.1999]
  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — ABSENT: Archaeal archaella are type-IV-pilus-related appendages and do not use FliD/HAP2 caps to assemble bacterial-type flagellin filaments. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]

Functions

  • filament tip capping — FliD/HAP2 oligomers cap the distal end of the bacterial flagellar filament.
    • DOI:10.1128/JB.181.23.7149-7153.1999 Macnab 1999 reviews HAP2/FliD as the cap at the distal filament end.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41564-025-02037-0 Einenkel et al. 2025 resolved FliD caps at native Salmonella filament tips.
  • cap-mediated flagellin incorporation — The cap presents a terminal-domain platform that guides exported flagellin into the growing filament lattice.
    • DOI:10.1126/science.290.5499.2148 Yonekura et al. 2000 modeled the bacterial flagellar cap as a rotary promoter of flagellin self-assembly.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.2534343100 Maki-Yonekura et al. 2003 linked HAP2 domain movement to cap-filament complex formation and growth.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41564-025-02037-0 Einenkel et al. 2025 resolved filament-assembly intermediates and connected FliD terminal-domain movement to flagellin incorporation.

Mechanism graphs

FliD caps the filament tip and guides flagellin addition (ASSEMBLY)

FliD/HAP2 oligomerizes into a filament cap at the distal tip of the flagellar filament. The cap sits on the hook-filament junction before filament assembly and later moves at the filament end to guide exported flagellin subunits into the elongating lattice.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Should flagellin, FlgK, or FlgL be curated as filament-cap components? GO:0009421 is scoped to the distal cap rather than the flagellin filament or the FlgK/FlgL hook-filament junction below it. FliD is the cap constituent; flagellin, FlgK and FlgL are neighboring axial proteins.
  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Should the FliD subunit count be curated as universally pentameric? The native Salmonella cap resolved by Einenkel et al. 2025 is pentameric, but Postel et al. 2016 showed that purified bacterial FliD proteins can adopt diverse oligomeric states.
