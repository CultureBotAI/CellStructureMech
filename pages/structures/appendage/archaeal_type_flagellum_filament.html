archaeal-type flagellum filament

cellstructuremech:archaeal_type_flagellum_filament · APPENDAGE · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

An extracellular helical archaellum filament assembled from glycosylated type-IV-pilin-like archaellin subunits and rotated by the archaeal-type flagellum motor to propel archaeal cells. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]

Synonyms: archaellum filament

Part of: GO:0097589

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — VARIABLE: Archaellum filaments occur in archaeal lineages that encode the archaeal-type flagellum; archaellum operons are patchily distributed across Archaea. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]
  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — ABSENT: Bacteria lack archaellin-based archaellum filaments and instead use non-homologous bacterial flagellin filaments or type IV pili. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:667126 Methanocaldococcus villosus — Source archaeon for a high-resolution heteropolymeric archaellum filament structure built from alternating ArlB1 and ArlB2 subunits. [DOI:10.1038/s41467-022-28337-1]

Functions

  • archaeal-type flagellum-dependent cell motility GO:0097590 — Rotation of the assembled archaellum filament propels archaeal cells through liquid or across semi-solid surfaces.
    • DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023 Albers and Jarrell 2015 review archaellum rotation as the archaeal swimming mechanism.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral grounding for filament-forming archaellins. This first filament record follows reviewed Arl/Fla nomenclature but leaves the full archaellin subunit class label-only rather than asserting a broad type-IV pilin family or a lineage-specific archaellin accession as an exact source-neutral grounding.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): ArlI/ArlJ motor proteins are associated assembly and rotation machinery rather than archaellum filament constituents. The filament is the extracellular polymer of archaellin subunits. The basal motor powers assembly and rotates the polymer but is not itself part of the filament.
