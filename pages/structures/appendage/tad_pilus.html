Tad pilus

cellstructuremech:tad_pilus · Appendage · Appendage · Proposed

A tight-adherence (Tad/Flp) subtype of type IV pilus involved in bacterial adhesion and colonization. In the Caulobacter vibrioides model, polar PilA filaments extend and retract during surface exploration and promote holdfast-dependent permanent attachment; this dynamic behavior is not asserted for every Tad system. [DOI:10.1128/mBio.01237-19]

Synonyms: tight-adherence pilus; type IVc pilus; Flp pilus

Functions

  • Tad pilus extension and retraction — The CpaF motor extends and retracts polar PilA filaments so Caulobacter cells can repeatedly probe nearby surfaces.
    • DOI:10.1126/science.aan5706 Ellison et al. 2017 directly observed extension and retraction of labeled Caulobacter Tad pili.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-024-50280-6 Hohl et al. 2024 show bidirectional processing by the Tad pilus CpaF motor.
    • DOI:10.1126/sciadv.aay2591 Ellison et al. 2019 correlate CpaF ATPase mutations with reduced extension/retraction rates, ATP hydrolysis and retraction force in Caulobacter; the tested Asticcacaulis system differs in the fraction of dynamic pili.
  • surface colonization — Cycles of Tad pilus extension, surface contact and retraction help Caulobacter cells remain near a surface long enough to deposit a holdfast.
    • DOI:10.1128/mBio.01237-19 Sangermani et al. 2019 propose that the flagellum and Tad pili cooperate to progressively drive Caulobacter surface colonization.
