prostheca

cellstructuremech:prostheca · APPENDAGE · APPENDAGE · PROPOSED

A tubular projection of the bacterial cell envelope and cytoplasm that extends away from the main cell body in prosthecate bacteria. In stalked Caulobacter cells, the prostheca contains the inner membrane, peptidoglycan and outer membrane layers of the Gram-negative envelope and supports stalk-specific compartmentalization and nutrient uptake. [DOI:10.1073/pnas.0909119107]

Synonyms: bacterial stalk; Caulobacter stalk; prosthecate cell extension

Functions

  • polar envelope extension — Cell-cycle-coupled stalk biogenesis extends the Caulobacter envelope at the old pole after the swarmer-to-stalked transition.
    • DOI:10.1111/j.1365-2958.2005.04970.x Biondi et al. 2006 identify a phosphorelay system that controls Caulobacter stalk biogenesis during cell-cycle progression.
  • nutrient uptake from dilute media — The narrow envelope extension can increase the cell surface available for nutrient uptake in dilute media.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.0602047103 Wagner et al. 2006 test a nutrient-uptake role for bacterial cell-envelope extensions.
    • DOI:10.1046/j.1365-2958.2002.03071.x Ireland et al. 2002 find transport-related proteins in isolated Caulobacter stalks.
  • stalk compartmentalization — Protein diffusion barriers divide the Caulobacter stalk from the cell body and separate stalk segments from each other.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2012.10.046 Schlimpert et al. 2012 show that crossband diffusion barriers compartmentalize the Caulobacter stalk.
