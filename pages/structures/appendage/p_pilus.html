P pilus

cellstructuremech:p_pilus · Appendage · Appendage · Proposed

A chaperone-usher adhesive pilus of uropathogenic Escherichia coli built from a PapA helical rod, a PapG-PapF-PapE tip fibrillum, a PapK adaptor that bridges the fibrillum to the rod, and a basal PapH terminator. The PapD chaperone delivers subunits to the PapC usher, which assembles the pilus, and the distal PapG adhesin binds host glycolipid receptors. [DOI:10.1128/ecosalplus.ESP-0007-2017]

Synonyms: pyelonephritis-associated pilus; P fimbria; Pap pilus

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:562 Escherichia coli — VARIABLE: P pili are chaperone-usher adhesive appendages encoded by pap operons in uropathogenic Escherichia coli rather than universal E. coli structures. [DOI:10.1128/ecosalplus.ESP-0007-2017]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:562 Escherichia coli — Uropathogenic E. coli P pili are the experimental model for PapA rod biogenesis, PapG tip-mediated adhesion, and PapH termination. [DOI:10.1128/ecosalplus.ESP-0007-2017]

Functions

  • P pilus assembly — PapD delivers folded PapG, PapF, PapE, PapK, PapA, and PapH subunits to the PapC usher, which incorporates them in a defined order to build the tip fibrillum, PapK tip-to-rod adaptor, helical rod, and basal terminator of the P pilus.
    • DOI:10.1128/ecosalplus.ESP-0007-2017 Werneburg and Thanassi 2018 review P pilus assembly as an ordered PapD/PapC chaperone-usher pathway.
    • DOI:10.1038/nrmicro.2017.40 Hospenthal, Costa and Waksman 2017 review P pili within the broader chaperone-usher pilus biogenesis pathway.
  • Gal-alpha(1-4)Gal-dependent adhesion — The distal PapG adhesin binds host globoseries glycolipids that present a Gal-alpha(1-4)Gal receptor epitope.
    • DOI:10.1128/ecosalplus.ESP-0007-2017 Werneburg and Thanassi 2018 review PapG-mediated binding of P pili to globoseries glycolipid receptors.

Mechanism graphs

PapC assembles the PapD-delivered P pilus tip and rod (ASSEMBLY)

PapD carries pilus subunits to the PapC usher. PapC initiates assembly with the distal PapG adhesin, then incorporates PapF and PapE to build the tip fibrillum, PapK to bridge the tip to the rod, PapA repeatedly to form the rod, and PapH to terminate and anchor the completed appendage.
