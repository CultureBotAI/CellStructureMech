P pilus

cellstructuremech:p_pilus · APPENDAGE · APPENDAGE · PROPOSED

A chaperone-usher adhesive pilus of uropathogenic Escherichia coli built from a PapA helical rod and a PapG-PapF-PapE-PapK tip fibrillum. The P pilus is assembled by the PapD chaperone and PapC usher and uses its distal PapG adhesin to bind host glycolipid receptors. [DOI:10.1128/ecosalplus.ESP-0007-2017]

Synonyms: pyelonephritis-associated pilus; P fimbria; Pap pilus

Canonical examples

Functions

  • P pilus assembly — The PapD chaperone and PapC usher polymerize PapG, PapF, PapE, PapK, PapA, and PapH subunits in a defined order to build the tip fibrillum, helical rod, and basal terminator of the P pilus.
    • DOI:10.1128/ecosalplus.ESP-0007-2017 Werneburg and Thanassi 2018 review P pilus assembly as an ordered PapD/PapC chaperone-usher pathway.
    • DOI:10.1038/nrmicro.2017.40 Hospenthal, Costa and Waksman 2017 review P pili within the broader chaperone-usher pilus biogenesis pathway.
  • Gal-alpha(1-4)Gal-dependent adhesion — The distal PapG adhesin binds host globoseries glycolipids that present a Gal-alpha(1-4)Gal receptor epitope.
    • DOI:10.1128/ecosalplus.ESP-0007-2017 Werneburg and Thanassi 2018 review PapG-mediated binding of P pili to globoseries glycolipid receptors.

Mechanism graphs

PapD and PapC assemble the P pilus tip and rod (ASSEMBLY)

PapD carries pilus subunits to the PapC usher. PapC initiates assembly with the distal PapG adhesin, then incorporates PapF, PapE, and PapK to build the tip fibrillum, PapA repeatedly to form the rod, and PapH to terminate and anchor the completed appendage.
