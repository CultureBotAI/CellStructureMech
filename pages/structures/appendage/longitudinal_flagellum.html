longitudinal flagellum

GO:0097609 · Appendage · Appendage · Proposed

A motile cilium found in dinoflagellates. It trails the cell and acts as a steering rudder. It is often partially contained in a furrow called the sulcus, and emerges from a flagellar pore located in the sulcus. The posteriorly directed eukaryotic flagellum of dinoflagellates that emerges from the ventral flagellar pore, lies along the longitudinal sulcus furrow, and trails behind the cell body. Together with the transverse flagellum, the longitudinal flagellum beats with planar waves to provide directional steering and forward propulsion in free-living and symbiotic dinoflagellates. [GO:0097609]

Synonyms: longitudinal cilium
