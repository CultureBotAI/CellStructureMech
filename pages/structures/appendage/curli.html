curli

GO:0098774 · Appendage · Appendage · Proposed

Extracellular amyloid fibers assembled from CsgA by the Csg secretion and nucleation machinery that form a pilus subtype in enteric biofilm matrices. [GO:0098774]

Synonyms: curli fimbriae; bacterial amyloid fibers

Functions

  • curli assembly GO:0098775 — CsgA subunits are secreted and nucleated on the cell surface to build an extracellular amyloid fiber.
    • DOI:10.1128/ecosalplus.ESP-0037-2018 The EcoSal Plus review frames curli biogenesis as bacterial amyloid assembly by the type VIII secretion pathway.
  • biofilm matrix adhesion — Curli fibers contribute an adhesive amyloid scaffold to enterobacterial extracellular matrices.
    • DOI:10.1146/annurev.micro.60.080805.142106 Barnhart and Chapman 2006 review curli functions in surface adhesion, cell aggregation, and biofilm formation.

Mechanism graphs

Csg secretion and nucleation build the curli fiber (ASSEMBLY)

The outer-membrane CsgG pore and accessory factors move CsgA to the cell surface, CsgB nucleates CsgA polymerization, and the resulting amyloid polymer forms the curli fiber.
