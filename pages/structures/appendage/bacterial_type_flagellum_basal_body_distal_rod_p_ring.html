Functions

  • peptidoglycan bushing anchoring — The P ring associates with the peptidoglycan layer and provides the peptidoglycan-side portion of the LP-ring bushing around the distal rod.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-021-24715-3 Yamaguchi et al. 2021 placed the Salmonella FlgI P ring in the peptidoglycan-proximal part of the LP ring.
  • LP-ring bushing support — Together with the L ring, the P ring forms a bushing that surrounds the distal rod and supports rotation of the flagellar drive shaft.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-021-24715-3 Yamaguchi et al. 2021 structurally resolved the LP ring around the distal rod in Salmonella hook-basal-body complexes.

Mechanism graphs

FlgI builds the peptidoglycan P-ring bushing (FUNCTION)

FlgI oligomers form the P ring around the distal rod. The P ring associates with peptidoglycan and cooperates with the L ring to make the LP-ring bushing that supports flagellar rod rotation.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Should the L ring, distal rod, FlgA, or FlgJ be curated as P-ring components? The mature GO:0009428 P ring is the FlgI ring at the peptidoglycan-proximal side of the LP-ring bushing. FlgH forms the adjacent L ring, the FlgG distal rod runs through the bushing, and FlgA and FlgJ act during outer-ring or rod assembly rather than remaining constituents of the mature P ring.
