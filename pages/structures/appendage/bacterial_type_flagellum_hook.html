bacterial-type flagellum hook

GO:0009424 · APPENDAGE · FILAMENT · PROPOSED

The portion of the bacterial-type flagellum that connects the filament to the basal body. [GO:0009424]

Synonyms: flagellar hook; flagellin-based flagellum hook

Part of: GO:0009288

Components

ComponentTypePart of / acts onGroundingGenesStoichiometryEssentialRole
hook protein FlgEPROTEINpart ofREVIEWED_LABEL_ONLYflgEESSENTIALPolymerizes into the short, curved tubular hook that connects the basal body to the external filament.
  • DOI:10.1146/annurev.micro.57.030502.090832 Macnab 2003 reviews the hook as the FlgE-built flexible connector between the flagellar basal body and filament.
  • DOI:10.1038/ncomms13425 Matsunami et al. 2016 resolved the Campylobacter jejuni hook as a helical tube built from FlgE.
  • DOI:10.3390/biom9090462 Horvath et al. 2019 resolved the Salmonella flagellar hook and modeled FlgE intermolecular contacts that support universal-joint mechanics.

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — COMMON: FlgE hooks are widespread in bacterial-type flagella but are absent from bacteria without flagellar loci. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro1887]
  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — ABSENT: Archaeal archaella are type-IV-pilus-related appendages and do not use the FlgE hook of bacterial flagella. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:197 Campylobacter jejuni — Source species for the complete cryo-EM structure of a bacterial flagellar hook. [DOI:10.1038/ncomms13425]
  • NCBITaxon:90371 Salmonella enterica subsp. enterica serovar Typhimurium — Model species used to resolve hook intermolecular interactions and native hook supercoiling. [DOI:10.3390/biom9090462]

Functions

  • universal-joint torque transmission — The hook bends while remaining torsionally stiff enough to transmit motor rotation from the basal body to the helical filament.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-019-13252-9 Kato et al. 2019 resolved the native supercoiled Salmonella hook and linked its FlgE lattice to universal-joint bending and torque transmission.
    • DOI:10.3390/biom9090462 Horvath et al. 2019 analyzed Salmonella FlgE contacts that explain hook flexibility during universal-joint function.

FlgE builds the torque-transmitting hook (FUNCTION)

FlgE subunits polymerize into a short curved tube. The resulting hook connects the basal body and filament, bends as the filament changes angle, and transmits motor torque to the filament propeller.
