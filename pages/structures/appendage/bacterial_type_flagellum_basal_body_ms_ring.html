bacterial-type flagellum basal body, MS ring

GO:0009431 · APPENDAGE · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

One of the rings of the bacterial-type flagellar basal body; a double-flanged ring that anchors the basal body to the cytoplasmic membrane. [GO:0009431]

Functions

  • inner-membrane basal-body anchoring — The FliF MS ring forms the cytoplasmic-membrane platform that mounts the bacterial flagellar basal body.
    • DOI:10.1128/JB.181.23.7149-7153.1999 Macnab 1999 reviews the MS ring as the membrane-embedded base of the bacterial flagellar basal body.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-021-24507-9 Kawamoto et al. 2021 structurally resolved the Salmonella MS ring as the core ring of the native basal body.
  • rotor and export-gate templating — The MS ring provides the scaffold on which the C ring assembles and a central opening that accommodates the flagellar export gate.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-021-24507-9 Kawamoto et al. 2021 related Salmonella C-ring symmetry and the export-gate opening to the 34-subunit native MS ring.

Mechanism graphs

FliF builds the membrane MS-ring platform (FUNCTION)

FliF oligomers form the MS ring in the cytoplasmic membrane. The resulting ring anchors the basal body, templates C-ring and export-gate placement, and couples to the axial rod through FliE.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Should the C ring, export apparatus, rod, or accessory collar proteins be curated as MS-ring components? The mature GO:0009431 MS ring is scoped to the FliF ring embedded in the cytoplasmic membrane. The FliG/FliM/FliN C ring assembles on the cytoplasmic face, the flagellar export gate sits in the center of the MS ring, FliE and the rod extend distally from it, and species-specific collar proteins decorate broader basal-body or motor assemblies rather than the core MS ring.
