bacterial-type flagellum rotor complex

GO:0120107 · APPENDAGE · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The bacterial flagellar basal-body rotor built from the membrane-anchored FliF MS ring and the cytoplasmic FliG/FliM/FliN C ring, which together receive torque from stator units, rotate the axial rod, and bias clockwise or counterclockwise motor output. [DOI:10.1016/j.tim.2014.12.011]

Synonyms: bacterial flagellar rotor; flagellar rotor

Part of: GO:0120100

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: MS-ring/C-ring rotor complexes occur in bacteria that encode bacterial-type flagellar basal bodies and are absent from bacteria without the rotary flagellar system. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro1887]
  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — ABSENT: Archaeal archaella are type-IV-pilus-related rotary appendages and do not contain the FliF/FliG/FliM/FliN rotor of bacterial flagella. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:90371 Salmonella enterica subsp. enterica serovar Typhimurium — Source organism for C-ring electron microscopy, native MS-ring cryo-EM, and the FliG/FliM/FliN rotor-architecture model. [DOI:10.1128/JB.00552-06]

Functions

  • MS-ring and C-ring rotor assembly — The C ring assembles on the cytoplasmic face of the FliF MS ring to produce the basal-body rotor.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.89.14.6304 Francis et al. 1992 placed the FliG switch protein on the cytoplasmic face of the flagellar M ring.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-021-24507-9 Kawamoto et al. 2021 imaged the C ring attached to the native Salmonella MS ring.
  • torque reception and rod rotation — Stators apply torque to the C ring through FliG, and the MS-ring/rod connection transmits rotor motion into the axial rod.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.tim.2014.12.011 Minamino and Imada 2015 review the MS ring and C ring as the rotor coupled to stator torque.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2021.03.057 Tan et al. 2021 resolved how the MS ring and proximal rod are coupled for torque transmission.
  • directional switching — The C-ring switch complex biases the flagellar rotor between clockwise and counterclockwise states in response to CheY-P.
    • DOI:10.1038/nature09300 Lee et al. 2010 connected FliG/FliM/FliN torque-ring architecture to rotational switching.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.90.19.8787 Welch et al. 1993 showed that CheY phosphorylation promotes switch-complex binding through FliM.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Should the stator units, export apparatus, or axial rod be curated as rotor-complex components? GO:0120107 is scoped to the membrane-anchored ring and motor switch complex that rotate as the basal-body rotor. Stator units apply torque to the rotor, the rod receives rotation from it, and the export apparatus occupies the MS-ring center, but those neighboring structures are not part of the MS-ring/C-ring rotor complex.
