hamus

cellstructuremech:hamus · APPENDAGE · APPENDAGE · PROPOSED

An archaeal cell-surface appendage composed of proteinaceous helical filaments with periodic prickles and a terminal grappling hook, used by Candidatus Altarchaeum hamiconexum cells to attach to biotic and abiotic surfaces. [DOI:10.1111/j.1365-2958.2005.04294.x]

Synonyms: hami; altiarchaeal grappling hook; nano-grappling hook

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:1803513 Candidatus Altarchaeum hamiconexum — Uncultivated archaeal species whose surface hami were described by electron microscopy, cryo-electron tomography and biofilm ultrastructural analysis. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2014.00397]

Functions

  • biotic and abiotic surface adhesion — Hami project from the archaeal cell surface and mediate strong adhesion to chemically distinct biotic and abiotic surfaces.
    • DOI:10.1111/j.1365-2958.2005.04294.x Moissl et al. 2005 connected each cell's halo of hami with adhesion to surfaces of different chemical composition.
    • DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2014.00397 Perras et al. 2014 localized hami as major contact structures at the SM1/Altarchaeum cell surface in biofilm ultrastructure.
