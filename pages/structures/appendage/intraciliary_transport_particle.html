intraciliary transport particle

GO:0030990 · Appendage · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

Non-membrane-bounded protein particle complex that carries cargo along axonemal microtubules during intraflagellar transport in eukaryotic cilia and flagella. [GO:0030990]

Synonyms: intraflagellar transport particle

Part of: cilium (GO:0005929)

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the intraciliary transport particle distinct from the complete cilium, the axoneme, and IFT motor complexes. GO:0030990 denotes the mobile IFT protein-particle complex that acts inside the cilium, GO:0005929 denotes the complete membrane-sheathed cilium, and GO:0005930 denotes the axonemal microtubule core on which IFT particles move.
