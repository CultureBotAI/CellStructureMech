Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep P1/P40/P90, P30, and internal-core proteins on the whole attachment organelle record rather than duplicating them as attachment organelle membrane components. GO:0033111 and SL-0021 denote the membrane around the organelle. Nakane et al. map P1/P40/P90 and P30 to the broader organelle surface and map HMW2, P65, and HMW3 to the electron-dense internal core, so those proteins remain components of the whole attachment organelle record.
