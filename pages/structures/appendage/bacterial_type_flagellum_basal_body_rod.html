bacterial-type flagellum basal body, rod

GO:0030694 · APPENDAGE · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The central portion of the bacterial-type flagellar basal body, which spans the periplasm and threads through the rings. [GO:0030694]

Synonyms: flagellar basal body, rod; flagellin-based flagellum basal body, rod

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — COMMON: Basal-body rods are found in bacterial-type flagella but are absent from bacteria without flagellar loci. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro1887]
  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — ABSENT: Archaeal archaella are type-IV-pilus-related appendages and do not contain the FliE/FlgB/FlgC/FlgF/FlgG basal-body rod. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2015.00023]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:90371 Salmonella enterica subsp. enterica serovar Typhimurium — Classical genetic and biochemical model for bacterial basal-body rod composition and segment order. [DOI:10.1016/0022-2836(90)90365-S]

Functions

  • axial drive-shaft torque transmission — The basal-body rod connects the MS ring to the hook and transmits rotation as a drive shaft through the cell envelope.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2021.03.057 Tan et al. 2021 resolved FliE/FlgB couplings that transmit torque from the MS ring into the proximal rod.
    • PMID:15136044 Saijo-Hamano et al. 2004 describe the central rod as the drive shaft that transmits torque to the hook and filament.
  • periplasmic axial scaffold — The assembled rod spans the periplasmic portion of the basal body and forms the axial scaffold that is surrounded by the P and L rings.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41564-021-00895-y Johnson et al. 2021 resolved the intact Salmonella basal body and the FlgG distal rod running through the periplasm.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-021-24715-3 Yamaguchi et al. 2021 resolved the Salmonella LP-ring bushing surrounding the distal rod.

Mechanism graphs

Rod proteins assemble the axial drive shaft (FUNCTION)

FliE, FlgB, FlgC and FlgF form the proximal rod and FlgG forms the distal rod. Together they make the central basal-body rod that spans the periplasm and transmits torque from the MS ring to the hook.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Should LP rings, FlgD, or FlgJ be curated as bacterial-type flagellum basal-body rod components? The mature GO:0030694 rod is scoped to the FliE/FlgB/FlgC/FlgF/FlgG axial polymer. The L and P rings surround the distal rod as bushings, whereas FlgD and FlgJ act during axial assembly rather than remaining mature rod subunits.
