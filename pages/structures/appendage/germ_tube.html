germ tube

GO:0032179 — germ tube · Appendage · Appendage · Proposed

The slender tubular outgrowth first produced by most spores in germination. A polarized cylindrical outgrowth emerging from a germinating fungal spore or yeast cell during the transition to filamentous hyphal growth, notably in Candida albicans, Aspergillus fumigatus, and Neurospora crassa. Germ tube emergence and extension depend on Cdc42-mediated polarity establishment, Spitzenkörper/polarisome vesicle delivery, and a basal septin collar/cap. [GO:0032179 — germ tube]

Has part: germ tube septin cap (GO:0032171); germ tube septin ring (GO:0032172)
