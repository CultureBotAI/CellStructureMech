mastigoneme

GO:0097741 · Appendage · Appendage · Proposed

A hair-like structure covering the flagella found in some algae (heterokonts and cryptophytes). It is approximately 15 nm in diameter, and usually consist of a tubular shaft that itself terminates in smaller hairs. It is composed of glycoproteins and, likely, carbohydrates. Mastigonemes may assist in locomotion by increasing the surface area of a flagellum. [GO:0097741]

Part of: cilium (GO:0005929)

Functions

  • context-dependent contribution to swimming — Chlamydomonas mastigoneme-associated swimming phenotypes depend on the tested mutant and assay. Liu et al. 2020 observed slower swimming in an mst1 mutant, whereas Amador et al. 2020 found no significant forward-swimming speed or propulsive-flow benefit from mastigonemes using a different mstg line. A general hydrodynamic contribution is therefore not established.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.202001122 Liu et al. 2020 found that mst1 line LMJ.RY0402.052413 lacked mastigonemes and swam about 15% slower than their control. PKD2 organization and swimming phenotypes support a context-specific association, not a resolved universal hydrodynamic mechanism.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.bpj.2020.05.003 Amador et al. 2020 used mastigoneme-deficient mstg line LMJ.RY0402.136134 and compared it with cw15 and cc125. Mean swimming speed, normalized maximum speed, flagellar waveform and propulsive flows did not show significant enhancement by mastigonemes under these conditions. Increased turning in mstg was observed, so this is not evidence that all motility phenotypes are absent.
