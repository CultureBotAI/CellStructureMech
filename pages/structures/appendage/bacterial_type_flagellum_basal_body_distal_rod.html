bacterial-type flagellum basal body, distal rod

GO:0009426 · APPENDAGE · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The portion of the central rod of the bacterial-type flagellar basal body that is distal to the cell membrane; spans most of the distance between the inner and outer membranes. [GO:0009426]

Synonyms: flagellar basal body, distal rod; flagellin-based flagellum basal body, distal rod

Part of: GO:0030694

Functions

  • periplasmic distal rod axis formation — FlgG forms the distal segment of the central basal-body rod, extending that axis toward the outer envelope side of the cell.
    • DOI:10.1016/0022-2836(90)90365-S Homma et al. 1990 placed FlgG as the distal Salmonella rod protein in the segmented basal-body rod.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41564-021-00895-y Johnson et al. 2021 structurally resolved the FlgG distal rod segment in the intact Salmonella basal body.
  • LP-ring bushing axis — In Gram-negative Salmonella, the distal rod is the rotating FlgG axis surrounded by the P and L rings.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41564-021-00895-y Johnson et al. 2021 resolved the FlgG distal rod in the intact Salmonella flagellar basal body.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41467-021-24715-3 Yamaguchi et al. 2021 resolved the Salmonella LP-ring bushing that surrounds the distal rod.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Should L/P rings, the hook, or rod assembly factors be curated as bacterial-type flagellum distal-rod components? The mature GO:0009426 distal rod is scoped to the FlgG axial polymer even though the GO L- and P-ring terms sit under the distal rod. The L and P rings surround the distal rod as Gram-negative bushings, the hook continues the external axial structure, and export or capping proteins act during assembly rather than remaining mature distal-rod subunits.
