Y-shaped link

GO:0097537 · Appendage · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A Y-shaped protein complex in the ciliary transition zone that connects the cilium axoneme to the ciliary necklace. Both protein sorting and protein gating occur at this point in the cilium allowing some, but not all proteins to enter the cilium. [GO:0097537]

Synonyms: membrane-microtubule complex; Y-link; Y-link structure; Y-shaped assemblage; Y-shaped fiber; Y-shaped fibre; Y-shaped linker

Part of: ciliary transition zone (GO:0035869)

Functions

  • transition-zone membrane tethering — Microtubule-membrane connectors physically couple the transition-zone doublets to the surrounding ciliary membrane. The Chlamydomonas evidence concerns the connector ensemble, not purified Y-links acting alone.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.201006105 Figure 3 compares connector defects with greater and more variable membrane spacing and loss of detergent-resistant membrane attachment in cep290 cells.
