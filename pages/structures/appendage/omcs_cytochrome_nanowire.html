OmcS cytochrome nanowire

cellstructuremech:omcs_cytochrome_nanowire · APPENDAGE · APPENDAGE · PROPOSED

An extracellular electrically conductive Geobacter sulfurreducens PCA filament assembled as a helical array of OmcS six-heme c-type cytochrome monomers whose stacked hemes provide a path for long-range electron transport. [DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2019.03.029]

Synonyms: OmcS nanowire; cytochrome-based bacterial nanowire

  • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2019.03.029 Wang et al. 2019 solved the cryo-EM structure of Geobacter sulfurreducens OmcS nanowires and modeled stacked OmcS monomers as the proteinaceous filament.
  • DOI:10.1038/s42003-019-0448-9 Filman et al. 2019 independently resolved OmcS as head-to-tail stacked monomers in an extracellular Geobacter sulfurreducens cytochrome filament.

Functions

  • stacked-heme long-range electron transfer — OmcS monomers align six covalently bound hemes per subunit into a continuous chain along the filament axis, providing a redox path for long-range electron transfer in purified nanowires.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2019.03.029 Wang et al. 2019 connected the OmcS filament's stacked heme arrangement with micrometer-scale electron transport in purified nanowires.
    • DOI:10.1038/s42003-019-0448-9 Filman et al. 2019 resolved a 3.4 A OmcS filament structure whose continuous heme stack can support long-range electron transport.
