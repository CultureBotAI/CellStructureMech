divisome complex

GO:1990586 · DIVISION_MACHINERY · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The membrane-associated protein machine that constricts a dividing bacterial cell. It is organised by the FtsZ ring — treadmilling filaments of the tubulin homologue FtsZ tethered to the inner membrane — which positions and moves the septal peptidoglycan synthases that build the division septum. [GO:1990586]

Synonyms: Z ring

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — COMMON: FtsZ-based division is the norm; a few lineages (e.g. Chlamydiae, some Planctomycetes) divide without FtsZ. [DOI:10.1146/annurev-biophys-121219-081703]
  • NCBITaxon:2157 Archaea — VARIABLE: Euryarchaeota use FtsZ; some other archaea divide with an ESCRT-III-related system instead. [DOI:10.1146/annurev-biophys-121219-081703]
