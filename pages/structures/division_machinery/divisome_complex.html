divisome complex

GO:1990586 · Division machinery · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

The membrane-associated protein machine that builds the peptidoglycan division septum in FtsZ-dependent bacterial cytokinesis. Membrane-tethered FtsZ filaments organize the division site and help distribute septal synthases; movement during active synthesis is not universally driven by FtsZ treadmilling. [GO:1990586]

Synonyms: Z ring

Part of: GO:0032153 — cell division site (no local record)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Mechanism graphs

FtsZ-dependent distribution and synthesis-dependent motion in the E. coli two-track model (FUNCTION)

E. coli model supported by single-molecule tracking and perturbations: fast, synthesis-inactive FtsW/FtsI follows treadmilling FtsZ, whereas slow movement accompanies active septal synthesis independently of FtsZ treadmilling speed. FtsN promotes the active population. This does not assert identical rate coupling in B. subtilis or all bacteria, or a fully resolved molecular coupling mechanism.
