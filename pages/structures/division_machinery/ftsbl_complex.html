FtsBL complex

GO:1990588 · Division machinery · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex required for prokaryotic cell division (FtsZ-dependent cytokinesis). Part of the divisome. Assembled independently of the other divisome components in the cytoplasm prior to transport to the cell septum. In E. coli consists of FtsB and FtsL. [GO:1990588]

Synonyms: FtsB-FtsL complex

Part of: FtsQBL complex (GO:1990587); GO:0005829 — cytosol (no local record)
