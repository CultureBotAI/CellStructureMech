FtsQBL complex

GO:1990587 · Division machinery · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex required for prokaryotic cell division (FtsZ-dependent cytokinesis). Part of the divisome. Assembled independently of the other divisome components in the cytoplasm prior to transport to the cell septum. In E. coli consists of FtsB, FtsL and FtsQ. [GO:1990587]

Synonyms: FtsB-FtsL-FtsQ complex

Has part: FtsBL complex (GO:1990588)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Functions

  • FtsZ-dependent cytokinesis GO:0043093 — Preassembles from FtsB, FtsL, and FtsQ prior to septal localization and is recruited to the midcell divisome to license FtsW-FtsI septal peptidoglycan synthesis during prokaryotic FtsZ-dependent cytokinesis.
    • GO:1990587 GO:1990587 defines the FtsQBL complex as required for prokaryotic FtsZ-dependent cytokinesis.
    • PMID:15165235 Buddelmeijer and Beckwith 2004 established that the preassembled FtsQBL complex is transported to the septum to drive downstream divisome maturation and cell division.
  • division septum assembly GO:0000917 — Serves as the structural scaffold within the divisome that bridges early septal ring proteins to the late peptidoglycan transglycosylase-transpeptidase machinery (FtsW-FtsI) during division septum assembly.
    • PMID:15165235 Buddelmeijer and Beckwith 2004 showed that FtsQBL complex formation and septal localization are required to recruit downstream septal assembly proteins FtsW and FtsI.
    • PMID:19233928 Gonzalez and Beckwith 2009 showed that distinct periplasmic domains of FtsB within the FtsQBL complex mediate interactions required for divisome construction at the septum.
