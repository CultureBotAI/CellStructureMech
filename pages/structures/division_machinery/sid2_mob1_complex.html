Sid2-Mob1 complex

GO:0034973 · Division machinery · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex that contains a protein kinase (Sid2 in S. pombe) and its regulatory subunit (Mob1). The Sid2p-Mob1p kinase complex is a component of the septation initiation network in fission yeast (called the mitotic exit network in S. cerevisiae) and is required for cytokinesis. The analogous complex in S. cerevisiae is called Dbf2p-Mob1p complex. [GO:0034973]

Synonyms: Dbf2p-Mob1p complex; Sid2-Mob1 kinase complex; Sid2p-Mob1p complex

Part of: spindle pole body (GO:0005816)

Xrefs: ComplexPortal:CPX-26510 ComplexPortal:CPX-1683

Mechanism graphs

Sid2-Mob1 / Dbf2-Mob1 Activation at the Spindle Pole Body and Mitotic Exit Signaling (FUNCTION)

Mob1 binds the N-terminal regulatory region of Sid2/Dbf2 to form the Sid2-Mob1 (Dbf2-Mob1) complex, which is activated at the spindle pole body by Cdc15 and phosphorylates Cfi1/Net1 and Cdc14 to drive mitotic exit.
