elongasome

cellstructuremech:elongasome · DIVISION_MACHINERY · MULTIPROTEIN_COMPLEX · PROPOSED

The sidewall peptidoglycan-synthesis machine of rod-shaped bacteria, also called the Rod complex. A SEDS-family glycosyltransferase (RodA) works with a class B penicillin-binding protein (PBP2) to polymerise and crosslink new wall material into the cylinder, while MreC, MreD and RodZ couple the complex to MreB filaments, which distribute it around the circumference. It is the elongation counterpart of the divisome. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro2677]

Synonyms: Rod complex; RodA-PBP2 complex
