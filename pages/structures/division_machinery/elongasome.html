elongasome

cellstructuremech:elongasome · Division machinery · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

The membrane-associated Rod complex that synthesizes lateral peptidoglycan during rod-shaped growth in model bacteria such as Escherichia coli and Bacillus subtilis. Its SEDS-family RodA polymerase works with an elongation-specific class B penicillin-binding transpeptidase, PBP2 in E. coli. Associated MreB filaments orient synthesis, while wall synthesis and filament motion are coupled; this is not a universal mechanism of rod shape in all bacteria. [DOI:10.1038/nmicrobiol.2016.172]

Synonyms: Rod complex; RodA-PBP2 complex

Mechanism graphs

Rod-system synthesis, MreB rotation and model-specific shape maintenance (FUNCTION)

E. coli RodA/PBP2 synthesize lateral wall, and inhibition experiments couple synthesis to MreB rotation. MreB rotation and synthase movement are separate readouts, not interchangeable with rod shape. RodZ promotes normal MreB rotation, but suppressor backgrounds can retain rod shape without rotation. B. subtilis uses related machinery, including its own elongation-specific transpeptidases; this graph is not a universal inventory or necessity claim.

  • CURATION_TODO (RESOLVED): Which source establishes the RodA polymerase assignment used in the definition? The definition and synonyms now cite inspected 2016 primary evidence. The RodA component now carries Meeske 2016 with verbatim support (#161), so the claim itself is sourced.
