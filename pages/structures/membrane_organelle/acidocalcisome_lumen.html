The acidocalcisome compartment bounded by the acidocalcisomal membrane. [GO:0033985]

Synonyms: acidocalcisomal lumen

Part of: GO:0020022

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0316

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: Acidocalcisome-like compartments have been characterized in bacteria, but they are not universal across bacteria. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro1097]
  • NCBITaxon:2759 Eukaryota — VARIABLE: Acidocalcisomes occur across diverse unicellular eukaryotes and metazoan cell types, so the lumen is present in the subset of eukaryotic cells that carry this organelle. [DOI:10.1128/MMBR.00042-23]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:358 Agrobacterium tumefaciens — Bacterial species whose membrane-bounded, calcium- and polyphosphate-rich acidocalcisome-like volutin granules establish a lumenal storage compartment. [DOI:10.1074/jbc.M304548200]
