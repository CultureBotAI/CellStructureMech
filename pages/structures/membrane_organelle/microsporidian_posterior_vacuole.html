microsporidian posterior vacuole

cellstructuremech:microsporidian_posterior_vacuole · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A membrane-bounded posterior vesicle in a microsporidian spore that expands during germination and is implicated in polar-tube extrusion and sporoplasm expulsion. [DOI:10.14411/fp.2005.014]

Functions

  • polar tube firing support — During spore germination, posterior vacuole expansion is coupled to pressure changes and movement of spore contents toward the everting polar tube.
    • DOI:10.14411/fp.2005.014 Findley et al. 2005 tested the posterior vacuole's role in Spraguea lophii spore hatching.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.fgb.2015.08.007 Troemel and Becnel 2015 observed posterior vacuole expansion late in Edhazardia aedis polar-tube firing.

Mechanism graphs

Posterior vacuole expansion accompanies polar-tube firing (FUNCTION)

The posterior vacuole sits behind the sporoplasm in mature microsporidian spores. During germination it expands as the polar tube fires, a nonmechanistic relationship consistent with a role in moving spore contents into the everting polar tube.
