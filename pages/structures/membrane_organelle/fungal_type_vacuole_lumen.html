Functions

  • vacuolar small-molecule storage — The fungal-type vacuole lumen is the membrane-enclosed space where the vacuole stores amino acids, ions, polyphosphate, and other small molecules.
    • GO:0000324 GO:0000324 defines the fungal-type vacuole as a reservoir for amino acids, divalent cations, ions, polyphosphate, and other small molecules.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.bbamcr.2008.08.003 Li and Kane 2009 review the yeast vacuole as a lysosome-like organelle at the intersection of storage and homeostasis.
  • vacuolar macromolecule degradation — The fungal-type vacuole lumen compartmentalizes acid hydrolases that degrade macromolecular cargo delivered to the vacuole.
    • GO:0000324 GO:0000324 describes the fungal-type vacuole as an acidic compartment containing acid hydrolases and serving as the primary degradation compartment.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.bbamcr.2008.08.003 Li and Kane 2009 review yeast vacuole functions at the endpoint of degradative membrane-trafficking routes.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent the fungal-type vacuole membrane as the boundary of the fungal-type vacuole lumen rather than as a lumen component. GO:0000328 denotes the volume enclosed by the fungal-type vacuole membrane, whereas GO:0000329 denotes that surrounding lipid bilayer.
