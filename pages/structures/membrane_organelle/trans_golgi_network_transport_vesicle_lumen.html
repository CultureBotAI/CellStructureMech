trans-Golgi network transport vesicle lumen

GO:0098564 · Membrane organelle · Subcellular region · Proposed

The volume enclosed within the membrane of a trans-Golgi network transport vesicle. The enclosed aqueous compartment within a trans-Golgi network (TGN) transport vesicle bounded by the vesicle membrane. In yeasts and other microbial eukaryotes, the TGN vesicle lumen houses soluble secretory glycoproteins, cell-wall remodeling enzymes, and vacuolar hydrolase precursors sorted from the late Golgi. [GO:0098564]

Luminal cargo packaging into trans-Golgi network transport vesicles (FUNCTION)

Arf1- and adaptor-driven coat assembly at phosphatidylinositol 4-phosphate-enriched TGN domains concentrates soluble and transmembrane cargo into the nascent trans-Golgi network transport vesicle lumen for post-Golgi trafficking.
