mitochondrial intermembrane space

GO:0005758 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The region between the inner and outer lipid bilayers of the mitochondrial envelope. [GO:0005758]

Synonyms: mitochondrial envelope lumen; mitochondrial membrane lumen

Part of: GO:0005740

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2759 Eukaryota — VARIABLE: Canonical mitochondrial intermembrane spaces occur with canonical mitochondria in eukaryotes, while some anaerobic lineages carry reduced mitochondrion-related organelles instead. [GO:0005758]
  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding-yeast mitochondria contain an intermembrane space traversed by precursor proteins after outer-membrane TOM entry and before downstream inner-membrane sorting. [DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2009.08.005]
