mitosome intermembrane space

cellstructuremech:mitosome_intermembrane_space · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The mitosomal intermembrane space is the space between inner and outer mitosomal membranes. [uniprot.location:SL-0440]

Synonyms: mitosomal intermembrane space; mitosomal periplasm; mitosomal periplasmic space

Part of: cellstructuremech:mitosome_envelope

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0440

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2759 Eukaryota — RARE: Mitosome intermembrane spaces occur in the patchy anaerobic or microaerophilic microbial eukaryotes that retain double-membrane mitochondrion-related mitosomes. [DOI:10.1111/jeu.12922]
  • NCBITaxon:5759 Entamoeba histolytica — COMMON: The Entamoeba mitochondrion-related mitosome was identified as a double-membrane organelle. [DOI:10.1046/j.1365-2958.1999.01414.x]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5759 Entamoeba histolytica — Amoebozoan parasite in which a double-membrane mitochondrion-related mitosome was initially described. [DOI:10.1046/j.1365-2958.1999.01414.x]
