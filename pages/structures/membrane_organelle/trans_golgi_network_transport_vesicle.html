trans-Golgi network transport vesicle

GO:0030140 — trans-Golgi network transport vesicle · Membrane organelle · Membrane bounded organelle · Proposed

A vesicle that mediates transport between the trans-Golgi network and other parts of the cell. A membrane-bounded transport vesicle that buds from the trans-Golgi network (TGN) to sort and deliver biosynthetic secretory and endolysosomal cargo to the plasma membrane, endosomes, or vacuole in yeasts, filamentous fungi, and protists. Vesicle biogenesis is driven by Arf1 GTPase activation, clathrin/adaptor or exomer coat assembly, and phosphatidylinositol-4-phosphate signaling. [GO:0030140 — trans-Golgi network transport vesicle]

Synonyms: TGN transport vesicle; trans-Golgi network constitutive secretory pathway transport vesicle

Has part: trans-Golgi network transport vesicle membrane (GO:0012510); exomer complex (GO:0034044); trans-Golgi network transport vesicle lumen (GO:0098564)
