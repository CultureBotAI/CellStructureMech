coated vesicle

GO:0030135 · Membrane organelle · Membrane bounded organelle · Proposed

Small membrane-bounded organelle formed by pinching off of a coated region of membrane. Some coats are made of clathrin, whereas others are made from other proteins. [GO:0030135]

Has part: vesicle coat (GO:0030120); coated vesicle membrane (GO:0030662)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.
