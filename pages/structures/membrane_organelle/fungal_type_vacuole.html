fungal-type vacuole

GO:0000324 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A vacuole that has both lytic and storage functions. The fungal vacuole is a large, membrane-bounded organelle that functions as a reservoir for small molecules and as the primary compartment for degradation. [GO:0000324]

Synonyms: vacuole, cell cycle-correlated morphology

Functions

  • small-molecule storage and ion homeostasis — The fungal-type vacuole stores amino acids, ions, and other small molecules while helping maintain cellular homeostasis.
    • GO:0000324 GO:0000324 defines the fungal vacuole as a storage reservoir for amino acids, divalent cations, ions, polyphosphate, and other small molecules.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.bbamcr.2008.08.003 Li and Kane 2009 review the yeast vacuole as a lysosome-like organelle at the intersection of storage and homeostasis.
  • vacuolar macromolecule degradation — The acidic vacuolar lumen houses hydrolases that degrade cargo delivered through endocytic, biosynthetic, and autophagic trafficking routes.
    • GO:0000324 GO:0000324 defines the fungal-type vacuole as an acidic compartment containing acid hydrolases and serving as the primary degradation compartment.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.bbamcr.2008.08.003 Li and Kane 2009 review yeast vacuole functions at the endpoint of degradative membrane-trafficking routes.
