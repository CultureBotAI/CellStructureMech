Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent the multivesicular body membrane as the boundary of the lumen rather than as a lumen component. UniProt SL-0340 denotes the MVB compartment bounded by the multivesicular body membrane, whereas GO:0032585 denotes the surrounding lipid bilayer.
