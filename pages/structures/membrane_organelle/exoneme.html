exoneme

GO:0044311 · Membrane organelle · Membrane bounded organelle · Proposed

A dense granule-like organelle of the apical complex of merozoites, released into the parasitophorous vacuole, mediating protease-dependent rupture and parasite exit from the infected erythrocyte. [GO:0044311]

Part of: apical complex (GO:0020007)

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:5833 Plasmodium falciparum — COMMON: Exonemes are directly evidenced in Plasmodium falciparum segmented schizonts and blood-stage merozoites; no broader apicomplexan distribution is asserted in this first record. [DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2007.10.049]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5833 Plasmodium falciparum — Human malaria parasite in which PfSUB1 discharge from exonemes into the parasitophorous vacuole was tied to SERA processing and erythrocyte egress. [DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2007.10.049]

Functions

  • regulated PfSUB1 discharge — Exonemes release PfSUB1 into the parasitophorous vacuole shortly before P. falciparum erythrocyte egress.
    • DOI:10.1111/j.1462-5822.2008.01176.x Blackman 2008 reviews the regulated release of PfSUB1 from exonemes into the PV space just before egress.
    • DOI:10.1038/ncomms4726 Withers-Martinez et al. 2014 describe SUB1 discharge from exonemes into the PV lumen minutes before egress.
  • erythrocyte egress protease delivery — Exoneme discharge delivers mature PfSUB1 to process SERA and MSP substrates that support rupture of the parasitophorous vacuole and erythrocyte membranes.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2007.10.049 Yeoh et al. 2007 connected PfSUB1 exoneme discharge to SERA maturation, host-cell egress, and the invasive capacity of released merozoites.
    • DOI:10.1126/science.aan1478 Nasamu et al. 2017 placed PMX upstream of SUB1 maturation and SERA5/MSP1 processing in the P. falciparum egress cascade.

Plasmepsin X and PfSUB1 connect exonemes to erythrocyte egress (FUNCTION)

PMX localizes to exonemes and supports final maturation of PfSUB1; exonemes discharge PfSUB1 into the PV, where the protease processes SERA/MSP substrates required for P. falciparum egress and post-egress invasion.
