endosome

GO:0005768 · Membrane organelle · Membrane bounded organelle · Proposed

A vacuole to which materials ingested by endocytosis are delivered. [GO:0005768]

Has part: endosome membrane (GO:0010008); endosome lumen (GO:0031904)

Functions

  • endosomal transport GO:0016197 — Endosomes move internalized and biosynthetic membrane cargo through the yeast endomembrane system toward the vacuole.
    • GO:0016197 GO:0016197 defines endosomal transport as directed movement of lumenal or membrane substances mediated by an endosome.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.devcel.2011.05.015 Henne, Buchkovich and Emr 2011 reviewed the ESCRT pathway as a conserved route for endosomal cargo sorting.
  • multivesicular body sorting — Endosomal sorting machinery recognizes ubiquitinated membrane cargo and packages it into intraluminal vesicles of multivesicular bodies.
    • PMID:11511343 Katzmann, Babst and Emr 2001 identified ESCRT-I as a conserved endosomal protein-sorting complex needed for yeast MVB sorting.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.devcel.2004.09.003 Teo et al. 2004 described ESCRT-II as part of the sequentially recruited endosomal ESCRT machinery and mapped direct ESCRT-II binding to the Vps20 subunit of ESCRT-III.
