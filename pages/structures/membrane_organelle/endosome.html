  • endosomal transport GO:0016197 — Endosomes move internalized and biosynthetic membrane cargo through the yeast endomembrane system toward the vacuole.

  • multivesicular body sorting — Endosomal sorting machinery recognizes ubiquitinated membrane cargo and packages it into intraluminal vesicles of multivesicular bodies.
    • DOI:10.1016/S0092-8674(01)00434-2 Katzmann, Babst and Emr 2001 identified ESCRT-I as a conserved endosomal protein-sorting complex needed for yeast MVB sorting.
