Asi complex

GO:0097658 · Membrane organelle · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A nuclear ubiquitin ligase multiprotein complex located in the inner nuclear membrane (INM) that recognizes and ubiquitinates misfolded INM proteins and also some proteins involved in sterol biosynthesis, during ER-associated protein degradation (ERAD). In S. cerevisiae, this complex contains the ubiquitin ligases Asi1p and Asi3p. [GO:0097658]

Part of: nuclear inner membrane (GO:0005637)
