chloroplast stroma

GO:0009570 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The internal space enclosed by the chloroplast double membrane but excluding the thylakoid space. [GO:0009570]

Mechanism graphs

Chloroplast stroma topology and import (FUNCTION)

The chloroplast envelope encloses the stroma, stromal protein import terminates in the stroma after precursor transit across the envelope, and chloroplast nucleoids and plastoglobules occupy the stromal compartment.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the chloroplast stroma separate from thylakoid lumen compartments. GO:0009570 denotes the hydrophilic internal matrix enclosed by the chloroplast envelope, explicitly excluding the thylakoid space that has dedicated chloroplast and plastid lumen terms.
