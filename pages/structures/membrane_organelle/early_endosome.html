early endosome

GO:0005769 · Membrane organelle · Membrane bounded organelle · Proposed

A membrane-bounded organelle that receives incoming material from primary endocytic vesicles that have been generated by clathrin-dependent and clathrin-independent endocytosis; vesicles fuse with the early endosome to deliver cargo for sorting into recycling or degradation pathways. [GO:0005769]

Has part: early endosome membrane (GO:0031901); early endosome lumen (GO:0031905)

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0094 — Early endosome

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Model the Trypanosoma reservosome as a separate downstream organelle rather than as an early-endosome subtype.
