early endosome

GO:0005769 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A membrane-bounded organelle that receives incoming material from primary endocytic vesicles that have been generated by clathrin-dependent and clathrin-independent endocytosis; vesicles fuse with the early endosome to deliver cargo for sorting into recycling or degradation pathways. [GO:0005769]

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Model the Trypanosoma reservosome as a separate downstream organelle rather than as an early-endosome subtype. Porto-Carreiro et al. 2000 placed the early endosomal network upstream of posterior reservosome storage in T. cruzi epimastigotes, so this record keeps the generic GO:0005769 early-endosome identity distinct from the minted reservosome record.
