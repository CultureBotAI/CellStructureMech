food vacuole

GO:0020020 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A vacuole within a parasite used for digestion of the host cell cytoplasm. [GO:0020020]

Synonyms: digestive vacuole

Components

ComponentTypePart of / acts onGroundingGenesStoichiometryEssentialRole
food vacuole membraneLIPIDpart ofREVIEWED_LABEL_ONLYThe acidic organelle-bounding membrane that separates the digestive lumen from the parasite cytosol and carries digestive-vacuole transporters.
  • DOI:10.1083/jcb.113.5.1033 Cowman et al. 1991 localized a Plasmodium falciparum P-glycoprotein homologue to the digestive vacuole.
  • DOI:10.3390/pathogens13030182 Wiser 2024 reviewed the digestive vacuole as an acidic lysosome-like organelle of malaria parasites.

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:5833 Plasmodium falciparum — COMMON: The food vacuole is directly evidenced in Plasmodium falciparum blood-stage parasites; no broader Plasmodium or Apicomplexa scope is asserted in this first record. [DOI:10.1002/prca.200700112]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5833 Plasmodium falciparum — Human malaria parasite whose food-vacuole fraction was isolated and profiled by proteomics. [DOI:10.1002/prca.200700112]

Functions

  • hemoglobin catabolism — The acidic food vacuole receives host hemoglobin and houses proteolytic digestion of that cargo during Plasmodium intraerythrocytic growth.
    • DOI:10.3390/pathogens13030182 Wiser 2024 reviewed the malaria digestive vacuole as the acidic lysosome-like site of host hemoglobin digestion.
    • DOI:10.1002/prca.200700112 Lamarque et al. 2008 isolated Plasmodium falciparum food vacuoles for proteomic analysis.
  • heme detoxification — Heme released by hemoglobin proteolysis is converted into hemozoin crystals inside the digestive vacuole.
    • DOI:10.3390/pathogens13030182 Wiser 2024 reviewed hemozoin formation as the food-vacuole route for detoxifying heme released from digested hemoglobin.
