recycling endosome

GO:0055037 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

An organelle consisting of a network of tubules that functions in targeting molecules, such as receptors transporters and lipids, to the plasma membrane. [GO:0055037]

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Treat TbRAB11 as marker and regulatory evidence rather than as a recycling-endosome structural component. The Trypanosoma brucei evidence uses TbRAB11 localization and perturbation to identify recycling endosomal traffic, but Rab11 is a small GTPase marker/regulator and not a constituent of the lipid-bounded compartment itself.
