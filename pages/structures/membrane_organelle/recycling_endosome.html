recycling endosome

GO:0055037 · Membrane organelle · Membrane bounded organelle · Proposed

An organelle consisting of a network of tubules that functions in targeting molecules, such as receptors transporters and lipids, to the plasma membrane. [GO:0055037]

Synonyms: endosomal recycling compartment; endosome recycling compartment; ERC

Has part: recycling endosome lumen (GO:0034777); recycling endosome membrane (GO:0055038)

Functions

  • endocytic cargo recycling — Recycling endosomes return internalized cargo toward the cell surface; in Trypanosoma brucei, TbRAB11-positive compartments support recycling of transferrin and anti-VSG antibody cargo through the endosomal system.
    • GO:0055037 GO:0055037 defines the recycling endosome by its role in targeting molecules to the plasma membrane.
    • PMID:12744719 Pal et al. 2003 used live-cell recycling assays and Rab mutants to implicate TbRAB11 in Trypanosoma brucei transferrin and anti-VSG antibody recycling.
    • PMID:29582527 Umaer, Bush and Bangs 2018 showed that TbRab11 mediates selective recycling and endocytic trafficking in Trypanosoma brucei.

Recycling endosome topology and Rab11 cargo recycling (FUNCTION)

The recycling endosome is an endosome subtype bounded by a membrane around a lumenal compartment and marked in Trypanosoma brucei by Rab11-positive endosomal structures that carry recycling cargo.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Treat TbRAB11 as marker and regulatory evidence rather than as a recycling-endosome structural component. The Trypanosoma brucei evidence uses TbRAB11 localization and perturbation to identify recycling endosomal traffic, but Rab11 is a small GTPase marker/regulator and not a constituent of the lipid-bounded compartment itself.
