Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep transient phagosome-maturation proteins out of the generic phagocytic vesicle components. The generic phagocytic vesicle is defined by a membrane-bounded vesicle made by phagocytosis. Rab GTPases, V-ATPases, proteases, and actin-associated factors associate with phagosomes in maturation-stage-specific ways, so the first record models only the stable membrane boundary as a constituent and leaves exact trafficking machinery to narrower timing- or organism-specific curation.
