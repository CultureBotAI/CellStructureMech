Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Avoid organelle-lumen parentage for the plasma membrane-derived thylakoid lumen. GO:0031979 denotes the internal compartment of a plasma membrane-derived bacterial thylakoid, while GO:0009579 explicitly scopes thylakoids as membranous structures that are not considered organelles.
