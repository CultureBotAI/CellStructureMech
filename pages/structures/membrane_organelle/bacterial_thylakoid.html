bacterial thylakoid

GO:0030075 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A thylakoid that is derived from and attached to, but not necessarily continuous with, the plasma membrane, and is not enclosed in a plastid. It bears the photosynthetic pigments in photosynthetic cyanobacteria. [GO:0030075]

Synonyms: plasma membrane-derived thylakoid

Functions

  • photosynthetic electron transport chain GO:0009767 — Bacterial thylakoids organize PSII, cytochrome b6f and PSI so light-driven electron transport can move electrons from water toward ferredoxin while contributing to a trans-thylakoid proton gradient.
    • DOI:10.1146/annurev-cellbio-120823-022747 Perez-Boerema et al. 2024 review thylakoid electron transport as the membrane process that converts light into chemical energy.
  • photosynthetic ATP synthesis — The plasma membrane-derived thylakoid membrane separates the bacterial thylakoid lumen from the cytoplasm so proton accumulation can drive ATP synthase.
    • DOI:10.1146/annurev-cellbio-120823-022747 Perez-Boerema et al. 2024 describe proton motive force generation across thylakoid membranes and ATP synthase use of that gradient.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep bacterial thylakoids separate from the generic thylakoid membrane record. GO:0030075 denotes the whole plasma membrane-derived bacterial thylakoid, with GO:0031676 and GO:0031979 as membrane and lumen parts. The existing GO:0042651 record denotes only the generic pigmented membrane of cyanobacterial and plastid thylakoids.
