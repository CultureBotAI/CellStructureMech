thylakoid membrane

GO:0042651 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE · PROPOSED

The pigmented photosynthetic membrane of cyanobacteria and chloroplasts, enriched in photosystems, cytochrome b6f, ATP synthase, plastoquinone and glycolipids, where light-driven electron transport builds the proton motive force for photosynthetic ATP synthesis. [GO:0042651]

Mechanism graphs

Linear electron transport builds a trans-thylakoid proton gradient (FUNCTION)

Photosystem II, plastoquinone, cytochrome b6f and photosystem I form an electron-transport chain in the thylakoid membrane. Electron flow is coupled to proton accumulation in the thylakoid lumen, and ATP synthase converts that proton motive force into ATP.
