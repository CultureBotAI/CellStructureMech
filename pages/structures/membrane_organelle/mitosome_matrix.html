mitosome matrix

cellstructuremech:mitosome_matrix · Membrane organelle · Subcellular region · Proposed — Drafted from literature to fill a coverage gap; not yet reviewed. How records are made

The matrix of a mitosome is the mitosomal internal spaces enclosed by the inner membrane. [uniprot.location:SL-0441 — Mitosome matrix]

Synonyms: mitosomal matrix

Part of: mitosome (GO:0032047)

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0441 — Mitosome matrix

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2759 Eukaryota — RARE: Mitosome matrices occur in the patchy anaerobic or microaerophilic microbial eukaryotes that retain double-membrane mitochondrion-related mitosomes. [DOI:10.1111/jeu.12922]
  • NCBITaxon:5759 Entamoeba histolytica — COMMON: The Entamoeba mitochondrion-related mitosome was identified as a double-membrane organelle whose internal matrix is enclosed by the inner mitosomal membrane. [DOI:10.1046/j.1365-2958.1999.01414.x]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5759 Entamoeba histolytica — Amoebozoan parasite in which mitosomes were initially described as double-membrane mitochondrion-related organelles. [DOI:10.1046/j.1365-2958.1999.01414.x]
