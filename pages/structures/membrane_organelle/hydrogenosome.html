hydrogenosome

GO:0042566 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A spherical, membrane-bounded organelle found in some anaerobic protozoa, which participates in ATP and molecular hydrogen formation. [GO:0042566]

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2759 Eukaryota — RARE: Hydrogenosomes occur patchily in anaerobic microbial eukaryotes and represent independently modified mitochondria rather than a conserved eukaryotic state. [DOI:10.1111/jeu.12922]
  • NCBITaxon:5722 Trichomonas vaginalis — COMMON: The parasite contains abundant hydrogenosomes that have been biochemically and proteomically characterized. [DOI:10.1016/j.ijpara.2011.10.001]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5722 Trichomonas vaginalis — Parabasalid parasite in which hydrogenosomal metabolism, iron-sulfur cluster assembly, protein targeting and the organelle proteome have been experimentally studied. [DOI:10.1016/j.ijpara.2011.10.001]

Functions

  • anaerobic ATP and molecular hydrogen production — Hydrogenosomal pyruvate metabolism supports anaerobic ATP production by substrate-level phosphorylation while releasing molecular hydrogen.
    • PMID:4750424 Lindmark and Muller 1973 isolated the hydrogen-producing organelle that gave hydrogenosomes their name.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.ijpara.2011.10.001 Schneider et al. 2011 cataloged hydrogenosomal enzymes for pyruvate metabolism and substrate-level phosphorylation.
  • hydrogenosomal iron-sulfur cluster assembly — Hydrogenosomes retain mitochondrial-type ISC machinery that assembles iron-sulfur centers.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.0401319101 Sutak et al. 2004 showed that iron-sulfur center assembly occurs in Trichomonas vaginalis hydrogenosomes.
  • hydrogenosomal matrix protein import — Nuclear-encoded hydrogenosomal matrix proteins are targeted to the organelle through N-terminal presequences.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.0500349102 Dolezal et al. 2005 compared targeting signals in trichomonad hydrogenosomes and Giardia mitosomes.
