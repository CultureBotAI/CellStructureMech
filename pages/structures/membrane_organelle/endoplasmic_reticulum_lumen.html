endoplasmic reticulum lumen

GO:0005788 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The volume enclosed by the membranes of the endoplasmic reticulum. [GO:0005788]

Synonyms: ER lumen

Part of: GO:0005783

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding yeast has endoplasmic-reticulum unfolded-protein-response and ERAD pathways coupled to ER protein-folding quality control. [DOI:10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80835-1]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Budding-yeast model for Sec61-dependent protein movement across or into the endoplasmic reticulum membrane that bounds the ER lumen. [DOI:10.1091/mbc.3.2.129]

Functions

  • Sec61-dependent ER protein translocation — Sec61 translocons move substrate polypeptides across or into the endoplasmic reticulum membrane that separates the cytosol from the ER lumen.
    • GO:0005784 GO:0005784 identifies the Sec61 translocon complex as the heterotrimeric ER membrane channel that mediates cotranslational and posttranslational protein translocation.
    • DOI:10.1091/mbc.3.2.129 Stirling et al. 1992 analyzed Saccharomyces cerevisiae sec61 mutants with endoplasmic-reticulum protein-insertion defects.
  • ER lumen protein quality control — The ER lumen is the enclosed compartment whose protein-folding state is monitored by the unfolded-protein-response and ER-associated-degradation pathways.
    • DOI:10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80835-1 Travers et al. 2000 linked yeast unfolded-protein response signaling with ER-associated degradation.
