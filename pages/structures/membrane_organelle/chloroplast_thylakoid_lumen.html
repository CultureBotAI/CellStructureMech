Chloroplast thylakoid lumen topology (FUNCTION)

The chloroplast thylakoid lumen is the internal chloroplast thylakoid compartment enclosed by the thylakoid membrane.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the chloroplast thylakoid membrane as the boundary of the chloroplast thylakoid lumen rather than as a lumen component. GO:0009543 denotes the volume enclosed by a chloroplast thylakoid membrane, whereas GO:0009535 denotes the pigmented membrane that encloses that volume.
