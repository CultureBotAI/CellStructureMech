Mechanism graphs

Cyanelle envelope topology (FUNCTION)

The cyanelle envelope is a double plastid envelope made of cytoplasm-facing outer and stroma-facing inner membranes with a peptidoglycan-containing intermembrane space between them.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the whole cyanelle envelope record separate from the generic GO:0033113 cyanelle membrane fallback term. GO:0033112 identifies the whole double envelope including the intermembrane space, whereas GO:0033113 identifies either envelope lipid bilayer when the exact inner or outer cyanelle membrane is unspecified.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve an exact source-neutral grounding for the peptidoglycan layer in the cyanelle envelope. GO and UniProt place a vestigial peptidoglycan layer in the cyanelle intermembrane space, but the resolved authority terms identify the whole intermembrane space rather than a standalone cyanelle peptidoglycan sheet.
