peroxisome

GO:0005777 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A small organelle enclosed by a single membrane, and found in most eukaryotic cells; it contains peroxidases and other enzymes involved in metabolic processes including free radical detoxification, lipid catabolism and biosynthesis, and hydrogen peroxide metabolism. [GO:0005777]
