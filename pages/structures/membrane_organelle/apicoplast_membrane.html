apicoplast membrane

GO:0160211 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE · PROPOSED

Any of the lipid bilayers that surround an apicoplast and form the apicoplast envelope. [GO:0160211]

Part of: GO:0020011

apicoplast lipid bilayersLIPIDpart ofREVIEWED_LABEL_ONLYESSENTIALGeneric lipid bilayers that form the multi-membrane apicoplast envelope and separate the plastid from the surrounding parasite cytoplasm.
  • GO:0160211 GO:0160211 defines the apicoplast membrane as any of the lipid bilayers that surround an apicoplast and form the apicoplast envelope.
  • DOI:10.1186/1475-2875-12-25 Lemgruber et al. 2013 used cryo-electron tomography to resolve four membranes around the Plasmodium falciparum apicoplast.
  • DOI:10.1098/rstb.2009.0273 Lim et al. 2010 review the four-membrane apicoplast envelope and protein targeting across it.

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:5794 Apicomplexa — VARIABLE: Apicoplast membranes form the multi-bilayer envelope of apicoplasts, which are present in many apicomplexan parasites and absent from some lineages. [DOI:10.1016/j.ijpara.2016.08.005]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5833 Plasmodium falciparum — Human malaria parasite in which cryo-electron tomography resolved the apicoplast as an organelle bounded by four membranes. [DOI:10.1186/1475-2875-12-25]
  • NCBITaxon:5811 Toxoplasma gondii — Apicomplexan model used to show that Tic20 acts at the apicoplast inner membrane and is required for protein import into the plastid. [DOI:10.1073/pnas.0803862105]

Functions

  • apicoplast protein import boundary — Apicoplast membranes host translocons that support import of nuclear-encoded proteins across the surrounding envelope.
    • DOI:10.1098/rstb.2009.0273 Lim et al. 2010 review bipartite targeting signals and import across the four apicoplast membranes.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.1003426 Agrawal et al. 2013 identified an apicoplast-localized ubiquitylation system required for import of nuclear-encoded plastid proteins.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.0803862105 van Dooren et al. 2008 localized TgTic20 to the apicoplast and showed that TgTic20 disruption blocks apicoplast protein import.
