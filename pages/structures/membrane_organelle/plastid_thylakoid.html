Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the whole plastid thylakoid distinct from its membrane and lumen children. GO:0031976 denotes any whole thylakoid within a plastid, while GO:0055035 denotes its lipid bilayer membrane and GO:0031978 denotes the plastid thylakoid lumen.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent plastid thylakoid as a membranous plastid structure without asserting a lineage-specific organelle class. The broader GO:0009579 thylakoid class is intentionally separated from organelles, and its exact plastid child still groups whole-thylakoid compartments that are modelled with different structure_kind values in narrower records.
