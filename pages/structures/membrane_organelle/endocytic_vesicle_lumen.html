Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Use the cytoplasmic vesicle lumen rather than the generic intracellular organelle lumen as the curated parent. GO asserts GO:0071682 as part of an endocytic vesicle and directly under the broad intracellular organelle lumen. Because GO:0030139 endocytic vesicle is a cytoplasmic vesicle, GO:0060205 is the narrower inferred lumen parent that keeps endocytic vesicle lumen with the other cytoplasmic vesicle lumen subtypes in this corpus.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep variable internalized cargo out of the endocytic vesicle lumen record. GO:0071682 denotes the bounded volume inside an endocytic vesicle rather than a stable molecular cargo class, and the cargo enclosed by an endocytic carrier depends on the uptake event and subtype.
