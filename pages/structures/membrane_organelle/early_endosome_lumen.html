Functions

  • early endosome lumen cargo routing — The early endosome lumen is the membrane-enclosed volume inside the early endosome through which newly internalized endocytic cargo is routed.
    • GO:0031905 GO:0031905 defines the early endosome lumen as the volume enclosed by the membrane of an early endosome.
    • GO:0005769 GO:0005769 defines early endosomes as receiving incoming material from primary endocytic vesicles for sorting toward recycling or degradation pathways.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent the early endosome membrane as the boundary of the early endosome lumen rather than as a lumen component. GO:0031905 denotes the volume enclosed by the membrane of an early endosome, whereas GO:0031901 denotes the surrounding lipid bilayer.
