photosynthetic membrane

GO:0034357 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE · PROPOSED

A membrane enriched in complexes formed of reaction centers, accessory pigments and electron carriers, in which photosynthetic reactions take place. [GO:0034357]

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:1117 Cyanobacteriota — COMMON: Most cyanobacteria use thylakoid photosynthetic membranes for oxygenic light reactions; Gloeobacter-like lineages are the major exception because they photosynthesize at the plasma membrane. [DOI:10.1146/annurev-cellbio-120823-022747]
  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: Photosynthetic membranes occur in phototrophic bacterial clades, including cyanobacterial thylakoids and the chromatophore membranes of purple photosynthetic bacteria. [DOI:10.1038/nature02823]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:1148 Synechocystis sp. PCC 6803 — Cyanobacterial model whose thylakoid photosynthetic membranes have been used to map native photosystem organization. [DOI:10.1016/j.molp.2017.09.019]
  • NCBITaxon:1063 Cereibacter sphaeroides — Purple photosynthetic bacterium whose native chromatophore photosynthetic membrane architecture was resolved by atomic force microscopy. [DOI:10.1038/nature02823]
