photosynthetic membrane

GO:0034357 · Membrane organelle · Membrane · Proposed

A membrane enriched in complexes formed of reaction centers, accessory pigments and electron carriers, in which photosynthetic reactions take place. [GO:0034357]

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:1117 Cyanobacteriota — COMMON: Most cyanobacteria use thylakoid photosynthetic membranes for oxygenic light reactions; Gloeobacter-like lineages are the major exception because they photosynthesize at the plasma membrane. [DOI:10.1146/annurev-cellbio-120823-022747]
  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: Photosynthetic membranes occur in phototrophic bacterial clades, including cyanobacterial thylakoids and the chromatophore membranes of purple photosynthetic bacteria. [DOI:10.1038/nature02823]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:1148 Synechocystis sp. PCC 6803 — Cyanobacterial model whose thylakoid photosynthetic membranes have been used to map native photosystem organization. [DOI:10.1016/j.molp.2017.09.019]
  • NCBITaxon:1063 Cereibacter sphaeroides — Purple photosynthetic bacterium whose native chromatophore photosynthetic membrane architecture was resolved by atomic force microscopy. [DOI:10.1038/nature02823]

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral CURIEs for oxygenic PSI/PSII and anoxygenic reaction-center subclasses before splitting the broad reaction-center component. GO:0034357 spans multiple membrane systems whose photochemical cores are not all instances of the same lower-level complex term.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve how far to project membrane parthood for LH2 rings, phycobilisomes and chlorosome baseplates before adding lineage-specific antenna rows under photosynthetic membranes. Purple-bacterial LH2 complexes are integral chromatophore membrane complexes, whereas cyanobacterial phycobilisomes and green-bacterial chlorosome antennae can be peripherally attached to a photosynthetic membrane or baseplate rather than embedded in the bilayer itself.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Keep ATP synthases out of this broad photosynthetic-membrane definition unless a child membrane record explicitly includes them as part of the same curated membrane system. Thylakoid and chromatophore membranes both maintain proton motive force for ATP synthesis, but GO:0034357 is defined by reaction centers, accessory pigments and electron carriers rather than by the downstream ATP synthase.
