mitochondrial matrix

GO:0005759 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The gel-like material, with considerable fine structure, that lies in the matrix space, or lumen, of a mitochondrion. It contains the enzymes of the tricarboxylic acid cycle and, in some organisms, the enzymes concerned with fatty acid oxidation. [GO:0005759]

Synonyms: mitochondrial lumen; mitochondrial stroma

Part of: GO:0005739

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2759 Eukaryota — VARIABLE: Canonical mitochondrial matrices occur with canonical mitochondria in eukaryotes, while some anaerobic lineages carry reduced mitochondrion-related organelles instead. [GO:0005759]
  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding-yeast mitochondria contain matrix and membrane-associated mitochondrial proteins in purified mitochondrial proteomes. [DOI:10.1073/pnas.2135385100]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Budding-yeast model whose purified mitochondrial proteome includes matrix and membrane-associated mitochondrial machinery. [DOI:10.1073/pnas.2135385100]
