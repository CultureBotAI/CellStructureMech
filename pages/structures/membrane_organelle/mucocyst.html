mucocyst

GO:0036000 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A small subcellular vesicle, surrounded by a membrane, in the pellicle of ciliate protozoans that discharges a mucus-like secretion. [GO:0036000]

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Tetrahymena maturation factors such as Mma1p, AP-3 and endosomal syntaxin were not modeled as mature mucocyst components. Mma1p and membrane-trafficking factors can be required for mucocyst biogenesis, but the first record only models cargo or machinery associated with mature docked or discharge-competent mucocysts. Proteins that act upstream in sorting or maturation should be added only if evidence places them in the mature organelle or at the assembled organelle boundary.
