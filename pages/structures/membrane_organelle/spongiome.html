spongiome

GO:0062160 · Membrane organelle · Membrane bounded organelle · Proposed

A network of tubules and vesicles that is part of a contractile vacuole complex and participates in external water discharge during osmoregulation. [GO:0062160]

Synonyms: decorated spongiome

Part of: contractile vacuole complex (GO:0062159)

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:44689 Dictyostelium discoideum — Amoebozoan model in which the contractile-vacuole complex was biochemically resolved into bladder and spongiome membranes. [PMID:8294479]
  • NCBITaxon:44030 Paramecium multimicronucleatum — Ciliate model in which microelectrode measurements and V-ATPase labeling tied decorated-spongiome radial arms to fluid segregation. [DOI:10.1242/jeb.205.20.3261]

Functions

  • contractile-vacuole fluid segregation — V-ATPase-bearing spongiome membranes participate in ion-driven water handling by the contractile-vacuole complex.
    • PMID:8294479 Nolta and Steck 1994 interpreted the V-H+-ATPase-rich Dictyostelium spongiome as a candidate primary energy transducer for pumping water out of the cytoplasm.
    • DOI:10.1242/jeb.205.20.3261 Gronlien et al. 2002 measured contractile-vacuole membrane potential in Paramecium and connected decorated-spongiome V-ATPase to fluid segregation.
    • DOI:10.1016/B978-0-12-407695-2.00002-0 Docampo et al. 2013 review the contractile vacuole complex as an osmoregulatory organelle in protists.

Mechanism graphs

Spongiome V-ATPase supports contractile-vacuole fluid segregation (FUNCTION)

A V-ATPase-rich spongiome is the tubular or vesicular membrane subnetwork of the contractile-vacuole complex; in Paramecium, decorated-spongiome V-ATPase contributes to the electrogenic membrane potential needed for fluid segregation.
