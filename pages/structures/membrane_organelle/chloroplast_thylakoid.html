Chloroplast thylakoid topology (FUNCTION)

The chloroplast thylakoid is an internal chloroplast compartment embedded in the stroma and bounded by a pigmented membrane that encloses the thylakoid lumen.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the chloroplast thylakoid distinct from its bounding membrane and its lumen. GO:0009534 denotes the whole chloroplast thylakoid, while GO:0009535 denotes the pigmented bounding membrane and GO:0009543 denotes the chloroplast thylakoid lumen.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent chloroplast thylakoid as a membranous chloroplast substructure without asserting that it is an organelle. GO:0009579 describes thylakoids as membranous cellular structures and explicitly comments that they are not considered organelles; StructureKindEnum has no non-organelle membrane-bounded-compartment value.
