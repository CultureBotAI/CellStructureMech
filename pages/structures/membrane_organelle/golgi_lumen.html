Golgi lumen

GO:0005796 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The volume enclosed by the membranes of any cisterna or subcompartment of the Golgi apparatus, including the cis- and trans-Golgi networks. [GO:0005796]

Synonyms: Golgi apparatus lumen; Golgi complex lumen

Part of: GO:0005794

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0133

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding-yeast Golgi compartments import GDP-mannose into the Golgi lumen for lumenal mannosylation reactions. [PMID:9395539]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Budding yeast whose Vrg4-dependent GDP-mannose transport supports Golgi-lumen mannosylation. [PMID:9395539]

Functions

  • Golgi lumen glycosylation compartment — The Golgi lumen is the bounded volume inside Golgi cisternae and subcompartments where lumen-facing glycosylation reactions access nucleotide-sugar substrates imported across Golgi membranes.
    • GO:0005796 GO:0005796 defines the Golgi lumen as the volume enclosed by membranes of Golgi cisternae and subcompartments.
    • PMID:9395539 Dean, Zhang and Poster 1997 showed that VRG4 is required for GDP-mannose transport into the Golgi lumen of Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent Golgi membranes and their nucleotide-sugar transporters as lumen boundaries rather than Golgi lumen components. GO:0005796 denotes the bounded volume enclosed by Golgi membranes, whereas GO:0000139 denotes the enclosing lipid bilayers and Vrg4 is a membrane GDP-mannose transporter.
