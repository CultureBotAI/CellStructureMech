magnetosome lumen

GO:0110145 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The volume enclosed by the magnetosome membrane. [GO:0110145]

Part of: GO:0110143

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0511

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — RARE: Magnetosome lumina occur inside magnetosome vesicles in magnetotactic bacterial lineages rather than broadly across Bacteria. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro.2016.99]
  • NCBITaxon:28211 Alphaproteobacteria — VARIABLE: Alphaproteobacterial magnetotactic model strains build magnetosome vesicles with membrane-enclosed mineralization lumina, but magnetotaxis is confined to particular lineages. [DOI:10.1038/nrmicro.2016.99]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:431944 Magnetospirillum gryphiswaldense MSR-1 — Alphaproteobacterial model used for biochemical and proteomic analysis of isolated magnetosome membranes and for genetic analysis of magnetosome vesicle assembly. [PMID:14766587]

Functions

  • magnetosome lumen biomineralization — The magnetosome lumen is the membrane-enclosed compartment in which imported iron is mineralized into a magnetic iron-mineral crystal.
    • DOI:10.1038/nrmicro.2016.99 Uebe and Schuler 2016 review magnetosome vesicles as the compartments in which iron is accumulated and biomineralized as magnetite or greigite.
    • PMID:22007638 Uebe et al. 2011 linked MamB and MamM to magnetite biomineralization and magnetosome membrane assembly in Magnetospirillum gryphiswaldense.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the magnetosome membrane as the boundary of the magnetosome lumen rather than as a lumen component. GO:0110145 denotes the magnetosome lumen, whereas GO:0110146 denotes the lipid bilayer surrounding a magnetosome.
