ferrosome

cellstructuremech:ferrosome · Membrane organelle · Membrane bounded organelle · Proposed

A bacterial lipid-bounded iron-storage organelle that contains iron-, phosphorus- and oxygen-rich material and forms through Fez protein-dependent machinery. [DOI:10.1038/s41586-022-04741-x]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:573370 Solidesulfovibrio magneticus RS-1 — Magnetotactic bacterial strain in which membrane-surrounded iron-phosphorus granules were distinguished ultrastructurally from bullet-shaped magnetosomes. [DOI:10.1073/pnas.1001290107]

Functions

  • anaerobic iron storage — Ferrosomes store intracellular iron for use during anaerobic metabolism.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41586-022-04741-x Grant et al. 2022 tested ferrosome formation and function as an iron-storage organelle during anaerobic growth.
