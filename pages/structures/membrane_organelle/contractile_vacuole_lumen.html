Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2759 Eukaryota — RARE: Contractile-vacuole lumens occur in the patchy subset of unicellular freshwater and soil eukaryotes that build contractile vacuoles. [DOI:10.1016/j.ejop.2024.126078]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:44030 Paramecium multimicronucleatum — Ciliate model in which microelectrodes placed in the contractile vacuole measured lumen-side membrane potential during the filling phase. [DOI:10.1242/jeb.205.20.3261]
  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — Freshwater alga used to quantify contractile-vacuole volume changes and osmolarity-responsive filling and discharge cycles. [DOI:10.1128/EC.00163-14]

Functions

  • contractile-vacuole water accumulation — The contractile vacuole lumen expands as excess water enters the vacuole before periodic systolic discharge.
    • DOI:10.1016/S0074-7696(02)15015-7 Allen and Naitoh 2002 review fluid filling and discharge as phases of the contractile-vacuole cycle.
    • DOI:10.1128/EC.00163-14 Komsic-Buchmann et al. 2014 measured contractile vacuole volume and contraction interval changes in Chlamydomonas under altered osmolarity.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent the contractile vacuolar membrane as the boundary of the lumen rather than as a lumen component.
