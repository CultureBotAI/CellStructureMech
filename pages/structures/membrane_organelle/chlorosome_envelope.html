chlorosome envelope

GO:0033105 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · OTHER · PROPOSED

The structure, composed of a monolayer of glycolipids with embedded proteins, that encloses the pigments and other contents of the chlorosome. [GO:0033105]

Synonyms: chlorosome membrane; chlorosomal envelope

Part of: GO:0046858

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0060

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:1097 Chlorobaculum tepidum — Green sulfur bacterium used to test individual chlorosome envelope protein contributions and to resolve the CsmA baseplate architecture. [DOI:10.1128/JB.186.3.646-653.2004]
  • NCBITaxon:1108 Chloroflexus aurantiacus — Green filamentous bacterium whose thin chlorosomes were used to visualize the relationship between the CsmA baseplate and the surrounding pigment aggregate. [DOI:10.1128/JB.00690-09]

Functions

  • photosynthetic light harvesting — The chlorosome envelope organizes the self-assembled bacteriochlorophyll aggregate and a CsmA baseplate so excitation energy can exit the chlorosome toward membrane reaction centers.
    • DOI:10.1007/s11120-013-9869-3 Orf and Blankenship 2013 review chlorosome envelopes, baseplates, and bacteriochlorophyll aggregates in the light-harvesting antennae of green photosynthetic bacteria.
    • DOI:10.1038/ncomms12454 Nielsen et al. 2016 modeled how the CsmA baseplate transfers excitation energy out of Chlorobaculum tepidum chlorosomes.
