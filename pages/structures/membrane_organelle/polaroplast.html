polaroplast

GO:0160201 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A membrane-bounded organelle found in a microsporidian spore that swells with water and exerts pressure to rupture the polar cap and evert the polar tube. [GO:0160201]

Functions

  • polar tube firing support — During microsporidian spore germination, the polaroplast expands and participates in the membrane movements coupled to polar tube discharge.
    • DOI:10.1007/978-3-030-93306-7_8 Han et al. 2022 reviewed the polaroplast as an anterior membrane organelle associated with polar tube discharge.
    • DOI:10.1186/s13071-023-05908-9 Chen et al. 2023 reviewed the microsporidian polar tube invasion apparatus and its relationship to other spore structures.
  • sporoplasm membrane formation — Polaroplast membrane material travels through the fired polar tube and becomes part of the sporoplasm plasma membrane.
    • DOI:10.1128/mbio.02749-23 Lv et al. 2024 followed polaroplast membrane transport through the polar tube and transformation into the sporoplasm membrane in Nosema bombycis.
