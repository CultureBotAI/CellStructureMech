thylakoid lumen

GO:0031977 · Membrane organelle · Subcellular region · Proposed

The volume enclosed by a thylakoid membrane. The continuous aqueous compartment enclosed by the thylakoid membrane in cyanobacteria and plastid-bearing eukaryotic microalgae (such as Chlamydomonas reinhardtii and diatoms). During oxygenic photosynthesis, water oxidation by the lumenal oxygen-evolving complex of Photosystem II and plastoquinol oxidation by cytochrome b6f pump protons into the thylakoid lumen, generating the acidic ΔpH that drives ATP synthase and housing soluble electron carrier plastocyanin (or cytochrome c6). [GO:0031977]
