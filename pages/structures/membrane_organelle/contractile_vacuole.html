contractile vacuole

GO:0000331 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A specialized vacuole of eukaryotic cells, especially Protozoa, that fills with water from the cytoplasm and then discharges this externally by the opening of contractile vacuole pores. One of its functions is osmoregulatory. [GO:0000331]

Synonyms: CV; central bladder

Part of: GO:0062159

Has part: GO:0031164

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:44689 Dictyostelium discoideum — Amoebozoan model in which vacuolar H+-ATPase dynamics have been followed in the contractile-vacuole complex. [DOI:10.1242/jcs.115.14.2893]

  • NCBITaxon:5693 Trypanosoma cruzi — Kinetoplastid parasite in which acidocalcisomes and the contractile-vacuole complex have been linked to hyposmotic-stress responses. [DOI:10.1074/jbc.M410372200]

Functions

  • osmoregulatory water expulsion — The contractile vacuole fills with excess cytosolic water and periodically discharges that water outside the cell to maintain volume and osmotic balance.
    • DOI:10.1016/S0074-7696(02)15015-7 Allen and Naitoh 2002 review the contractile-vacuole cycle as the osmoregulatory route for expelling cytosolic water.
    • DOI:10.1128/EC.00163-14 Komsic-Buchmann et al. 2014 quantified changes in Chlamydomonas contractile-vacuole pulse frequency and size under different osmotic conditions.
