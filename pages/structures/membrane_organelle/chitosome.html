chitosome

GO:0045009 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

An intracellular membrane-bounded particle found in fungi and containing chitin synthase; it synthesizes chitin microfibrils. Chitin synthase activity exists in chitosomes and they are proposed to act as a reservoir for regulated transport of chitin synthase enzymes to the division septum. [GO:0045009]

Synonyms: chitin synthase vesicle; chitin synthetase microvesicle

Functions

  • chitin synthase storage and delivery — Chitosomes act as intracellular membrane-bounded carriers that store latent fungal chitin synthase and deliver it to cell-surface sites for localized chitin microfibril synthesis.
    • GO:0045009 GO:0045009 describes chitosomes as proposed reservoirs for regulated chitin-synthase transport to the division septum.

  • fungal wall chitin microfibril synthesis — The chitosome-associated synthase pool supports synthesis of chitin microfibrils used in the fungal cell-wall matrix after enzyme delivery to the cell surface.
    • DOI:10.1111/j.1567-1364.2006.00158.x Bartnicki-Garcia 2006 reviews chitosomes as microvesicular carriers of chitin-synthetase activity for fungal wall chitin microfibril construction.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.136.1.95 Santos and Snyder 1997 linked Chs3p localization to polarized chitin synthesis sites in Saccharomyces vegetative and mating cells.

Mechanism graphs

Chitosomes store and deliver chitin synthase (FUNCTION)

A chitosome is a fungal cytoplasmic vesicle that carries latent chitin synthase. In Saccharomyces, Chs3p traffics from the chitosome to the plasma membrane, where localized synthase activity contributes to chitin microfibril synthesis for the fungal cell wall.

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral identifiers for the fungal chitin synthase cargo class and for any chitosome-specific membrane term before grounding these reviewed labels. GO provides an exact whole-chitosome term but no exact chitosome-membrane child term, and the chitosome literature spans several chitin synthase isoenzymes rather than one curated universal cargo family.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep chitin microfibrils as the synthesis product, not as resident chitosome components. Chitosomes are vesicular carriers for chitin synthase activity; chitin microfibrils are synthesized after that activity is deployed for fungal wall construction.
