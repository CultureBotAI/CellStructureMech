Mechanism graphs

Reservosome lumen topology (FUNCTION)

The reservosome lumen is the internal volume of a T. cruzi reservosome and is bounded by the reservosome membrane.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent the reservosome matrix as a separate internal substructure rather than duplicating its membranes, vesicles, or lipid inclusions as direct lumen components. GO:0106124 denotes the volume enclosed by reservosome membranes, whereas GO:0106125 denotes the internal reservosome matrix composed of planar membranes, vesicles, and lipid inclusions.
