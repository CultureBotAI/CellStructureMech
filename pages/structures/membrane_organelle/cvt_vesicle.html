Cvt vesicle

GO:0033107 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A cytosolic vesicle that is enclosed by a double membrane and is implicated in the cytoplasm to vacuole targeting pathway. These vesicles are found in the yeast S. cerevisiae, and contain vacuolar hydrolases, aminopeptidase I (Ape1p) and alpha-mannosidase (Ams1p). [GO:0033107]

Synonyms: cytoplasm to vacuole targeting vesicle; cytoplasm-to-vacuole targeting vesicle

Has part: GO:0033110
