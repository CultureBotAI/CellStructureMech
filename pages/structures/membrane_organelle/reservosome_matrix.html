Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:5693 Trypanosoma cruzi — VARIABLE: The reservosome matrix is present in the reservosomes of T. cruzi epimastigotes; reservosomes are absent from amastigotes and trypomastigotes. [DOI:10.1017/S0031182004005797]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5693 Trypanosoma cruzi — Kinetoplastid parasite whose epimastigote reservosome matrix was analyzed with thin-section, uranyl-acetate, and freeze-fracture electron microscopy. [DOI:10.1002/jemt.20592]

Functions

  • sterol storage in reservosome lipid inclusions — Reservosome lipid inclusions store and mobilize sterols, including cholesterol endocytosed by T. cruzi epimastigotes.
    • DOI:10.1111/j.1574-6968.2002.tb11317.x Cunha-e-Silva et al. 2002 isolated T. cruzi reservosomes and found large amounts of ergosterol and esterified cholesterol in the purified organelles.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.pone.0022359 Pereira et al. 2011 showed that T. cruzi epimastigotes store and mobilize cholesterol in reservosome lipid inclusions.
