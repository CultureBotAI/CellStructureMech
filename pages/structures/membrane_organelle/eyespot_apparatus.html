eyespot apparatus

GO:1990413 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · OTHER · PROPOSED

A small pigmented organelle used in single-celled organisms to detect light. [GO:1990413]

Synonyms: eyespot; stigma

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — Green-algal model in which eyespot-assembly mutants defined loci required for pigmented eyespot formation and placement. [DOI:10.1093/genetics/153.2.721]

Functions

  • directional light detection for phototaxis — The eyespot apparatus localizes photoreceptors and uses carotenoid layers to shade them so a motile cell can infer the direction of incident light.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.1525538113 Ueki et al. 2016 showed that Chlamydomonas eyespot pigment layers determine the phototactic sign by shielding photoreceptors from light condensed through the cell body.
