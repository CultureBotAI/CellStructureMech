eyespot apparatus

GO:1990413 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · OTHER · PROPOSED

A small pigmented organelle used in single-celled organisms to detect light. [GO:1990413]

Synonyms: eyespot; stigma

Canonical examples

Functions

  • directional light detection for phototaxis — The eyespot apparatus uses carotenoid layers to shade photoreceptors so a motile cell can infer the direction of incident light.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.1525538113 Ueki et al. 2016 showed that Chlamydomonas eyespot pigment layers determine the phototactic sign by shielding photoreceptors from light condensed through the cell body.

Carotenoid layers shade channelrhodopsins for directional phototaxis (FUNCTION)

The Chlamydomonas eyespot concentrates carotenoid pigments in reflective granule layers with a localized channelrhodopsin photoreceptor patch so the cell can sense the direction of incident light.
