glycosome

GO:0020015 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5691 Trypanosoma brucei — Kinetoplastid parasite in which glycosome glycolytic enzymes, PEX14-mediated protein import and the metabolic need for compartmentation have been experimentally studied. [DOI:10.1073/pnas.0806664105]

Functions

  • glycolytic enzyme compartmentalization — The glycosome sequesters glycolytic enzymes in a membrane-bounded matrix, protecting trypanosomes from toxic accumulation of glycolytic intermediates.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.0806664105 Haanstra et al. 2008 showed that compartmentation of glycolysis prevents toxic glucose-6-phosphate accumulation in Trypanosoma brucei.
  • glycosomal matrix protein import — Glycosomal peroxins import nuclear-encoded PTS-containing matrix proteins into the glycosome.
    • DOI:10.1046/j.1432-1033.2003.03582.x Choe et al. 2003 showed that PEX14 participates in glycosomal matrix-protein import in Trypanosoma brucei.
