Golgi-associated vesicle

GO:0005798 · Membrane organelle · Membrane bounded organelle · Proposed

Any vesicle associated with the Golgi complex and involved in mediating transport within the Golgi or between the Golgi and other parts of the cell. [GO:0005798]

Synonyms: Golgi vesicle

Has part: Golgi-associated vesicle membrane (GO:0030660)

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep coatomer, Arf-family GTPases, and other subtype-specific coat proteins off the broad Golgi-associated vesicle record. GO:0005798 is a broad Golgi-associated vesicle parent term, whereas GO:0030137 COPI-coated vesicles, GO:0030126 COPI vesicle coats, and other child or coat records carry exact coat architectures.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Add GO's useful Golgi vesicle related synonym while excluding its overbroad vesicular component related synonym. GO:0005798 lists both 'Golgi vesicle' and 'vesicular component' as related synonyms. 'Golgi vesicle' usefully recovers the same Golgi-associated vesicle class, but 'vesicular component' is too generic and would conflate this record with any vesicle.
