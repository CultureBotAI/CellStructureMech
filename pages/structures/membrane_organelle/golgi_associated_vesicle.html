Golgi-associated vesicle

GO:0005798 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep coatomer, Arf-family GTPases, and other subtype-specific coat proteins off the broad Golgi-associated vesicle record. GO:0005798 is a broad Golgi-associated vesicle parent term, whereas GO:0030137 COPI-coated vesicles, GO:0030126 COPI vesicle coats, and other child or coat records carry exact coat architectures.
