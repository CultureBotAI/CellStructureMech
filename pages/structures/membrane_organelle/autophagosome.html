autophagosome

GO:0005776 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A double-membrane-bounded compartment that engulfs endogenous cellular material as well as invading microorganisms to target them to the lytic vacuole/lysosome for degradation as part of macroautophagy. [GO:0005776]

Synonyms: autophagic vacuole; initial autophagic vacuole

Has part: GO:0000421 GO:0034423

Functions

  • macroautophagy — Autophagosomes engulf cytoplasmic material in a double-membrane vesicle and deliver that cargo to a lytic vacuole for degradation.
    • GO:0016236 GO:0016236 defines macroautophagy as the autophagic process that proceeds via autophagosome formation.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.119.2.301 Takeshige et al. 1992 demonstrated starvation-induced autophagy in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
  • autophagosome-vacuole fusion — In yeast, completed autophagosomes fuse with the fungal-type vacuole to release autophagic bodies into the degradative lumen.
    • GO:0061909 GO:0061909 defines autophagosome-lysosome fusion and describes yeast autophagosome fusion with the vacuole.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.119.2.301 Takeshige et al. 1992 used proteinase-deficient yeast mutants to accumulate autophagic bodies in the vacuole.
