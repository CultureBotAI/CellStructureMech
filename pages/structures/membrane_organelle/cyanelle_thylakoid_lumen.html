Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the cyanelle thylakoid membrane as the boundary of the cyanelle thylakoid lumen rather than as a lumen component. GO:0033114 denotes the lumenal compartment bounded by thylakoid membranes, whereas GO:0033115 denotes the cyanelle thylakoid lipid bilayer membrane that bounds that compartment.
