nucleomorph

GO:0033009 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A small, vestigial nucleus found in some plastids that derive from a eukaryotic endosymbiont. Observed in chlorarachniophytes and cryptomonads, which acquired their plastids from a green and red alga respectively. [GO:0033009]

Synonyms: residual endosymbiont nucleus; remnant endosymbiont nucleus

Part of: GO:0009536

  • NCBITaxon:227086 Bigelowiella natans — Chlorarachniophyte model compared with Guillardia theta to explain why a small residue of essential genes remains locked in each nucleomorph. [DOI:10.1038/nature11681]

Functions

  • reduced endosymbiont genome maintenance — The nucleomorph houses the compact remnant genome of an engulfed eukaryotic algal endosymbiont inside a secondary plastid.
    • DOI:10.1038/nature11681 Curtis et al. 2012 found that the Guillardia theta and Bigelowiella natans nucleomorphs retain small sets of essential genes.
  • nucleomorph genome replication — The nucleomorph genome is replicated once per Guillardia theta cell cycle in coordination with plastid replication.
    • DOI:10.1038/s41598-017-02668-2 Onuma et al. 2017 measured host nuclear and nucleomorph DNA synthesis timing in the cryptophyte Guillardia theta.
