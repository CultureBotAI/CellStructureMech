osmiophilic body

GO:0044310 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A membrane-bounded vesicle found predominantly in Plasmodium female gametocytes, that becomes progressively more abundant as the gametocyte reaches full maturity. These vesicles lie beneath the subpellicular membrane of the gametocyte, and the release of their contents into the parasitophorous vacuole has been postulated to aid in the escape of gametocytes from the erythrocyte after ingestion by the mosquito. [GO:0044310]

Functions

  • female gametocyte emergence support — Osmiophilic bodies in mature P. falciparum female gametocytes support efficient emergence from erythrocytes after gametogenesis is induced.
    • DOI:10.1111/j.1365-2958.2007.06039.x De Koning-Ward et al. 2008 found that Pfg377-negative female gametocytes lack the full osmiophilic-body complement and are less efficient at emergence from erythrocytes.
  • mosquito infection support — Normal osmiophilic-body formation contributes to P. falciparum transmission to mosquitoes after a gametocyte-containing blood meal.
    • DOI:10.1111/j.1365-2958.2007.06039.x De Koning-Ward et al. 2008 linked the Pfg377-negative osmiophilic-body defect to a strong reduction in mosquito oocyst formation.
