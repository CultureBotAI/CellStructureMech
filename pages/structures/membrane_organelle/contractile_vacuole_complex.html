contractile vacuole complex

GO:0062159 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · OTHER · PROPOSED

A non-membrane-bounded organelle of eukaryotic cells, especially Protozoa, that fills with water from the cytoplasm and then discharges this externally. One of its functions is osmoregulatory. [GO:0062159]

Synonyms: CVC

Has part: GO:0000331 GO:0062160

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2759 Eukaryota — RARE: Contractile vacuole complexes occur patchily in unicellular eukaryotes, especially freshwater and soil protists, rather than throughout Eukaryota. [DOI:10.1016/j.ejop.2024.126078]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:44689 Dictyostelium discoideum — Amoebozoan model in which the contractile-vacuole complex was biochemically resolved into bladder and spongiome membranes. [PMID:8294479]
  • NCBITaxon:44030 Paramecium multimicronucleatum — Ciliate model in which the decorated-spongiome radial arms of the contractile-vacuole complex were connected to fluid segregation. [DOI:10.1242/jeb.205.20.3261]

Functions

  • osmoregulatory water expulsion — The contractile vacuole complex collects excess cytosolic water and periodically discharges it outside the cell to maintain osmotic balance.
    • GO:0062159 GO:0062159 defines the contractile vacuole complex as an organelle that fills with cytoplasmic water, discharges it externally, and functions in osmoregulation.
    • DOI:10.1016/B978-0-12-407695-2.00002-0 Docampo et al. 2013 review acidocalcisome and contractile-vacuole osmoregulation in protists.
