reservosome

A posterior, membrane-bounded endocytic organelle in Trypanosoma cruzi epimastigotes that receives internalized macromolecular cargo. [DOI:10.1017/S0031182004005797]

Functions

  • endocytic cargo storage — Reservosomes store proteins and lipids delivered by the T. cruzi epimastigote endocytic pathway.
    • DOI:10.1078/0171-9335-00112 Porto-Carreiro et al. 2000 showed that endocytic cargo passes from the cytostome through an early endosomal network before storage in reservosomes.
    • DOI:10.1017/S0031182004005797 Figueiredo et al. 2004 reviewed reservosomes as endocytic compartments in T. cruzi epimastigotes.
  • reservosome hydrolase-associated proteolysis — Reservosomes concentrate lysosomal hydrolases, including cruzipain, in a proteome associated with protein degradation and regulation.
    • DOI:10.1002/pmic.200800730 Sant'Anna et al. 2009 identified cruzipain and other lysosomal hydrolases in purified reservosomes and discussed reservosomes as a major protein degradation and regulation site.
