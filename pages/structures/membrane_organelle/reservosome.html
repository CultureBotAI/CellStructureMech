reservosome

cellstructuremech:reservosome · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

An acidic, membrane-bounded endocytic organelle in Trypanosoma cruzi epimastigotes that stores endocytosed macromolecules and lipids and concentrates lysosomal hydrolases including cruzipain. [DOI:10.1590/S0074-02761999000700015]

Functions

  • endocytic cargo storage — Reservosomes store proteins and lipids delivered by the T. cruzi epimastigote endocytic pathway.
    • DOI:10.1078/0171-9335-00112 Porto-Carreiro et al. 2000 showed that endocytic cargo passes from the cytostome through an early endosomal network before storage in reservosomes.
    • DOI:10.1017/S0031182004005797 Figueiredo et al. 2004 reviewed reservosomes as endocytic compartments in T. cruzi epimastigotes.
  • acidic proteolysis — Reservosomes concentrate lysosomal hydrolases in an acidic lumen where internalized proteins can be degraded.
    • DOI:10.1590/S0074-02761999000700015 Soares 1999 reviewed reservosomes as acidic cruzipain-containing prelysosomal compartments.
    • DOI:10.1002/pmic.200800730 Sant'Anna et al. 2009 profiled the reservosome proteome and recovered several proteins involved in proteolysis.
