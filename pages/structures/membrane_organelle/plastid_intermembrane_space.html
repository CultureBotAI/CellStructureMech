plastid intermembrane space

GO:0009529 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The region between the inner and outer lipid bilayers of the plastid envelope. [GO:0009529]

Synonyms: Plastidic intermembrane space

Part of: GO:0009526

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0212

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:33090 Viridiplantae — COMMON: Green algae and land plants carry primary plastids whose double envelopes enclose an intermembrane space between the inner and outer plastid membranes. [DOI:10.1098/rstb.2009.0103]
  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — COMMON: The Chlamydomonas reinhardtii chloroplast envelope has an intermembrane space across which the TOC-TIC import supercomplex spans the outer and inner membranes. [DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2022.10.030]
