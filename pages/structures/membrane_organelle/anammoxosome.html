anammoxosome

GO:0044222 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A ladderane-lipid-bounded intracytoplasmic organelle of anaerobic ammonium-oxidizing Planctomycetota that localizes the hydrazine-producing anammox catabolic pathway and its energy-conserving membrane reactions. [GO:0044222]

Functions

  • anaerobic ammonium oxidation — The anammoxosome compartmentalizes the conversion of ammonium and nitrite through nitric oxide and hydrazine intermediates to dinitrogen gas.
    • DOI:10.1111/1574-6976.12014 Kartal et al. 2013 review anaerobic ammonium oxidation as the energy metabolism that occurs in the anammoxosome.
  • energy conservation — Anammoxosome membrane reactions couple hydrazine oxidation and electron transfer to proton motive force generation across the organelle membrane.
    • DOI:10.1111/1574-6976.12014 Kartal et al. 2013 review the proposed anammoxosome proton motive force generated by pathway electron transport.

Mechanism graphs

Hydrazine metabolism is localized inside a ladderane-bounded organelle (FUNCTION)

The ladderane membrane bounds an anammoxosome lumen enriched for hydrazine synthase and HAO-family cytochromes. Nitrite-derived nitric oxide and ammonium are converted to hydrazine, hydrazine is oxidized to dinitrogen, and electrons recycle through membrane-associated carriers that support energy conservation.
