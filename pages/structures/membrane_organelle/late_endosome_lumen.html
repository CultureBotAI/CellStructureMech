Functions

  • multivesicular body sorting pathway GO:0071985 — In multivesicular bodies, a late-endosome child class, the lumen receives ubiquitinated membrane cargo on intraluminal vesicles generated from the limiting membrane during ESCRT-dependent MVB sorting.
    • GO:0031906 GO:0031906 defines the late endosome lumen as the volume enclosed by the membrane of a late endosome.
    • GO:0005771 GO:0005771 defines multivesicular bodies as late-endosome-class endosomes with internal vesicles formed by invagination of the limiting endosomal membrane.
    • GO:0071985 GO:0071985 denotes the multivesicular body sorting pathway that targets ubiquitylated transmembrane proteins into luminal vesicles.
    • PMID:11511343 Katzmann, Babst and Emr 2001 identified ESCRT-I as a conserved yeast endosomal protein-sorting complex needed for ubiquitin-dependent MVB cargo sorting.
    • PMID:12194857 Babst et al. 2002 characterized yeast ESCRT-III as an endosome-associated complex required for MVB sorting.
