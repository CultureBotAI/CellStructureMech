acidocalcisome

GO:0020022 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

An acidic electron-dense membrane-bounded organelle found in diverse microbes that stores polyphosphate, pyrophosphate, calcium and other cations and uses proton pumps and cation transporters to maintain its ion-rich matrix. [GO:0020022]

Synonyms: metachromatic granule; volutin granule; polyphosphate vacuole

Mechanism graphs

Proton pumps and polyphosphate build an acidic cation store (FUNCTION)

The acidocalcisome membrane bounds a polyphosphate-rich matrix. Proton pumps acidify the lumen, and the anionic polyphosphate matrix chelates calcium and other cations to create an electron-dense storage compartment.
