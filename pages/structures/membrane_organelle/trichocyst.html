trichocyst

GO:0055039 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A crystalline exocytotic organelle composed of small, acidic proteins existing primarily as disulphide-linked dimers. The trichocyst releases long filamentous proteins that capture predators in net-like structures to slow them down when the cell is disturbed. [GO:0055039]
