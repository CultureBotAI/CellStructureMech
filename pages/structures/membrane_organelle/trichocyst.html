trichocyst

GO:0055039 · Membrane organelle · Membrane bounded organelle · Proposed

A crystalline exocytotic organelle composed of small, acidic proteins existing primarily as disulphide-linked dimers. The trichocyst releases long filamentous proteins that capture predators in net-like structures to slow them down when the cell is disturbed. [GO:0055039]

Mechanism graphs

Calcium-triggered matrix proteins discharge as a filament (FUNCTION)

Paramecium trichocyst matrix proteins coassemble into a crystalline trichocyst matrix that is stored in the organelle. After stimulated exocytosis, extracellular Ca2+ triggers matrix recrystallization and extension into the discharged filament.
