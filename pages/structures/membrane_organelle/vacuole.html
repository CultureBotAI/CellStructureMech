vacuole

GO:0005773 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A closed structure, found only in eukaryotic cells, that is completely surrounded by unit membrane and contains liquid material. [GO:0005773]

Has part: GO:0005774 GO:0005775

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding yeast has a lysosome-like vacuole with a bounding vacuolar membrane and acidic lumen. [DOI:10.1016/j.bbamcr.2008.08.003]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Budding-yeast model for a vacuole specialized for lysosome-like degradation, storage, and homeostasis. [DOI:10.1016/j.bbamcr.2008.08.003]

Functions

  • vacuolar storage and degradation — In budding yeast, the vacuole uses an acidic lumen and vacuolar membrane to combine small-molecule storage with macromolecule degradation.
    • GO:0000324 GO:0000324 defines the fungal-type vacuole as a vacuole with lytic and storage functions.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.bbamcr.2008.08.003 Li and Kane 2009 review the yeast vacuole as a lysosome-like organelle at the intersection of storage, degradation, and homeostasis.

Mechanism graphs

Vacuoles pair a membrane with a lumen (FUNCTION)

The vacuole is a membrane-bounded organelle whose broad topology consists of a vacuolar membrane surrounding a vacuolar lumen; the fungal-type vacuole specializes that same membrane/lumen organization in yeast.
