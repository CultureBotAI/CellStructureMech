Woronin body

GO:0140266 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A peroxisome-derived dense-core vesicle of filamentous fungi that seals septal pores upon hyphal wounding. [GO:0140266]

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:147538 Pezizomycotina — VARIABLE: Woronin bodies are specialized peroxisome-derived organelles of filamentous ascomycete fungi; this record does not assert presence across all Ascomycota. [DOI:10.1083/jcb.200705049]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5141 Neurospora crassa — Filamentous ascomycete model in which hex-1 was tied to Woronin body formation and the HEX-1 lattice was structurally linked to septal-pore sealing. [DOI:10.1038/nsb910]

Functions

  • septal pore sealing — Woronin bodies plug septal pores after hyphal damage, restricting cytoplasmic bleeding from neighboring filamentous-fungal compartments.
    • DOI:10.1006/fgbi.2000.1230 Tenney et al. 2000 connected Neurospora crassa hex-1 to Woronin bodies and septal-pore plugging.
