Maurer's cleft

GO:0020036 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A disk-like structure that appears at the periphery of a red blood cell infected by an apicomplexan parasite, characterized by a translucent lumen and an electron-dense coat of variable thickness; often appears to be tethered to the host cell membrane by fibrous connections with the erythrocyte cytoskeleton. [GO:0020036]

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Model Maurer's clefts as parasite-derived structures inside infected erythrocytes rather than as a conserved host red-blood-cell organelle. Maurer's clefts are induced by malaria parasites in the host erythrocyte cytoplasm and are populated by parasite-exported proteins; this record therefore uses Plasmodium falciparum as the canonical taxon and avoids treating the clefts as normal erythrocyte organelles.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact source-neutral families for SBP1, MAHRP1, and other Maurer's cleft resident protein classes. The first record curates SBP1 and MAHRP1 from P. falciparum localization and knockout evidence but does not assign broad domain-family CURIEs to exact Maurer's cleft resident protein classes.
