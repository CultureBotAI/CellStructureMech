phagolysosome

GO:0032010 · Membrane organelle · Membrane bounded organelle · Proposed

A membrane-bounded intracellular vesicle formed by maturation of an early phagosome following the ingestion of particulate material by phagocytosis; during maturation, phagosomes acquire markers of late endosomes and lysosomes. A mature digestive organelle formed by the fusion of a phagosome with lysosomes in phagotrophic microbial eukaryotes such as Dictyostelium discoideum, Entamoeba histolytica, and ciliates. The phagolysosome features a strongly acidified lumen maintained by V-type H+-ATPases and an arsenal of acid hydrolases, proteases, and lysozymes that degrade engulfed bacteria and particulate nutrients. [GO:0032010]

Synonyms: late phagocytic vesicle; late phagosome

Has part: phagolysosome membrane (GO:0061474); phagolysosome vesicle lumen (GO:0106174)
