plasma membrane-derived chromatophore membrane

GO:0042717 · Membrane organelle · Membrane · Proposed

The lipid bilayer associated with a plasma membrane-derived chromatophore; surrounds chromatophores that form complete vesicles. [GO:0042717]

Synonyms: cellular chromatophore membrane; chromatophore membrane

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0042 — Cellular chromatophore membrane

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — RARE: Plasma membrane-derived chromatophore membranes occur in purple photosynthetic bacteria rather than broadly across bacteria. [uniprot.location:SL-0042 — Cellular chromatophore membrane]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:1063 Cereibacter sphaeroides — Purple photosynthetic bacterium, still commonly cited under the synonym Rhodobacter sphaeroides, whose native chromatophore membrane architecture was resolved by atomic force microscopy. [DOI:10.1038/nature02823]

Functions

  • photosynthetic electron transport chain GO:0009767 — Chromatophore membranes organize reaction-center antenna cores, peripheral antenna complexes and quinone-linked cytochrome bc1 so light absorption drives cyclic electron transport and builds a proton motive force.
    • DOI:10.1038/nature02823 Bahatyrova et al. 2004 directly imaged native chromatophore membranes and resolved how LH2 complexes are arranged around RC-LH1-PufX core arrays.
    • DOI:10.1074/jbc.M412088200 Comayras et al. 2005 studied excitation transfer and quinone domains in Rhodobacter sphaeroides chromatophore membranes.
  • photosynthetic phosphorylation — The sealed chromatophore vesicle membrane can maintain a light-driven electrochemical gradient that F0F1-ATP synthase uses to produce ATP.
    • DOI:10.1016/S0006-3495(02)75470-2 Feniouk et al. 2002 assayed ATP synthesis in Rhodobacter capsulatus chromatophore vesicles driven by a transmembrane proton motive force.

Purple-bacterial chromatophores couple light harvesting to ATP synthesis (FUNCTION)

LH2 antenna complexes and RC-LH1-PufX cores organize inside the plasma membrane-derived chromatophore membrane. Cyclic electron transport through quinone and cytochrome bc1 is coupled to F0F1-ATP synthase use of a chromatophore transmembrane proton motive force.
