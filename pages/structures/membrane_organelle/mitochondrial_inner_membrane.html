mitochondrial inner membrane

GO:0005743 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE · PROPOSED

The inner, i.e. lumen-facing, lipid bilayer of the mitochondrial envelope. It is highly folded to form cristae. [GO:0005743]

Synonyms: inner mitochondrial membrane; inner mitochondrion membrane; mitochondrion inner membrane
