inner membrane pellicle complex

An apicomplexan double-membrane pellicle structure formed by flattened vesicles beneath the plasma membrane. [GO:0070258]

Synonyms: inner membrane complex

Canonical examples

Functions

  • pellicle shape support — The IMC and associated subpellicular proteins stabilize parasite morphology as part of the apicomplexan pellicle.
    • DOI:10.3389/fcimb.2020.611801 Ferreira et al. 2021 review the Plasmodium IMC as a compartment that confers cell stability and shape.
  • daughter-cell scaffolding — The IMC provides a scaffold for apicomplexan daughter-cell formation during parasite replication.
    • DOI:10.1093/molbev/mss081 Kono et al. 2012 followed IMC architecture during asexual and sexual Plasmodium development.
    • DOI:10.3389/fcimb.2020.611801 Ferreira et al. 2021 review the IMC as an important scaffold during daughter-cell formation in Plasmodium spp.
  • glideosome anchoring for motility — The apicomplexan IMC anchors gliding-motility machinery used for parasite movement and host-cell invasion.
    • DOI:10.3389/fcimb.2020.611801 Ferreira et al. 2021 review the IMC as the anchor for the actin-myosin glideosome that powers gliding motility and invasion.
