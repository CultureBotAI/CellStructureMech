mitochondrial crista junction

GO:0044284 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

A tubular structure of relatively uniform size that connects a mitochondrial crista to the mitochondrial inner boundary membrane. [GO:0044284]

Part of: GO:0005743

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2759 Eukaryota — VARIABLE: Crista junctions occur in canonical crista-bearing mitochondria, whereas some anaerobic eukaryotic lineages carry reduced mitochondrion-related organelles with simplified or absent crista architecture. [DOI:10.1038/nature12985]
  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — COMMON: Budding-yeast mitochondrial inner membranes form crista junctions whose positioning and abundance are regulated by MICOS subcomplexes. [DOI:10.7554/elife.07739]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:4932 Saccharomyces cerevisiae — Budding-yeast model used for electron tomography and genetic analysis of MICOS-dependent mitochondrial crista-junction architecture. [DOI:10.7554/elife.07739]

Functions

  • cristae formation GO:0042407 — Crista junctions are neck-like inner-membrane regions that connect crista folds to the inner boundary membrane during maintenance of mitochondrial crista topology.
