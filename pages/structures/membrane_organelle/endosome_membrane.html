Functions

  • endosomal membrane cargo sorting — The endosome membrane provides the sorting surface where ubiquitinated transmembrane cargo and ESCRT machinery are brought together for delivery into intraluminal vesicles.
    • PMID:11511343 Katzmann, Babst and Emr 2001 identified ESCRT-I as a conserved yeast endosomal protein-sorting complex needed for ubiquitin-dependent MVB cargo sorting.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.200302136 Katzmann et al. 2003 found that Vps27 recruits ESCRT-I to endosomal membranes during MVB sorting.
  • multivesicular body sorting pathway GO:0071985 — The endosome membrane buds inward during ESCRT-dependent sorting to package endosomal membrane cargo into intraluminal vesicles.
    • GO:0005771 GO:0005771 defines multivesicular bodies as endosomes containing internal vesicles formed by invagination of the limiting endosomal membrane.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.devcel.2011.05.015 Henne, Buchkovich and Emr 2011 reviewed ESCRT-driven intraluminal vesicle formation at endosomes.
