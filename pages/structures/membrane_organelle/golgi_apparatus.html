Golgi apparatus

GO:0005794 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A membrane-bound cytoplasmic organelle of the endomembrane system that further processes the core oligosaccharides (e.g. N-glycans) added to proteins in the endoplasmic reticulum and packages them into membrane-bound vesicles. The Golgi apparatus operates at the intersection of the secretory, lysosomal, and endocytic pathways. [GO:0005794]

Functions

  • glycan processing and secretory sorting — The Golgi apparatus processes glycoprotein oligosaccharides and sorts membrane-bound cargo within the secretory, endocytic and lysosomal trafficking network.
    • GO:0005794 GO:0005794 defines the Golgi apparatus as the endomembrane organelle that processes ER-derived core oligosaccharides and packages cargo into membrane-bound vesicles.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.ceb.2014.04.010 Papanikou and Glick 2014 review Golgi compartment identities and resident enzyme organization.
  • cisternal maturation — In budding yeast, individual Golgi cisternae mature from early to late cisternae while resident markers arrive and depart over time.
    • DOI:10.1038/nature04717 Losev et al. 2006 visualized Golgi maturation dynamics in living Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
    • DOI:10.1038/nature04737 Matsuura-Tokita et al. 2006 used live imaging to follow yeast Golgi cisternal maturation.
