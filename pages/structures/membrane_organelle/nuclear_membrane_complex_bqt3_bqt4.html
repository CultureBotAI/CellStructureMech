nuclear membrane complex Bqt3-Bqt4

GO:1990862 · Membrane organelle · Multiprotein complex · Proposed

A protein complex that resides in the inner nuclear membrane and anchors telomeres to the nuclear envelope. In fission yeast, it is composed of Bqt3 and Bqt4. [GO:1990862]

Part of: nuclear inner membrane (GO:0005637)
