vacuolar membrane

Functions

  • vacuolar acidification GO:0007035 — Vacuolar membranes host proton-pumping V-ATPase complexes that raise the hydrogen-ion concentration in the vacuolar lumen.
    • GO:0007035 GO:0007035 denotes processes that lower vacuolar pH by raising the vacuolar hydrogen-ion concentration.
    • DOI:10.1128/MMBR.70.1.177-191.2006 Kane 2006 reviews yeast V-ATPase proton pumping at organelle membranes.
  • nucleus-vacuole junction formation — Yeast vacuolar membranes supply Vac8p for direct binding to Nvj1p on the nuclear outer membrane.
    • DOI:10.1091/mbc.11.7.2445 Pan et al. 2000 showed that Saccharomyces cerevisiae Nvj1p and Vac8p directly interact to form nucleus-vacuole junctions.
