early phagosome

GO:0032009 · Membrane organelle · Membrane bounded organelle · Proposed

A membrane-bounded intracellular vesicle as initially formed upon the ingestion of particulate material by phagocytosis. A newly formed phagocytic vacuole generated immediately after phagocytic cup closure in phagotrophic protists and amoebae such as Dictyostelium discoideum and Entamoeba histolytica. The early phagosome retains plasma membrane lipids and early endosomal Rab5/Rab7 GTPases while initiating V-ATPase recruitment prior to lysosome fusion. [GO:0032009]

Synonyms: early phagocytic vesicle

Has part: early phagosome membrane (GO:0036186); early phagosome lumen (GO:0062113)
