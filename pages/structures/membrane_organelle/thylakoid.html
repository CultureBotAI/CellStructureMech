Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the whole thylakoid parent distinct from the generic thylakoid membrane. GO:0009579 denotes the whole membranous thylakoid structure across photosynthetic bacteria and plastids, whereas GO:0042651 denotes the pigmented membrane constituent. The whole generic thylakoid also has multiple exact child lumen classes instead of one exact generic lumen term.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent thylakoid as a membranous cellular structure without asserting that it is an organelle. GO:0009579 describes thylakoids as membranous cellular structures and scopes thylakoids away from the organelle class.
