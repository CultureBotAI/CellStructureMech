glyoxysome

GO:0009514 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A specialized form of peroxisome that contains the enzymes of the glyoxylate pathway. The glyoxysome is found in some plant cells, notably the cells of germinating seeds. [GO:0009514]
