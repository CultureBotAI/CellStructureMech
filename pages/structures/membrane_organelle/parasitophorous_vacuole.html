parasitophorous vacuole

cellstructuremech:parasitophorous_vacuole · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A membrane-bounded host-cell vacuole in which an invading apicomplexan parasite resides and develops; its bounding parasitophorous vacuolar membrane is initiated during host-cell invasion and surrounds the intracellular parasite. [uniprot.location:SL-0194]

Has part: cellstructuremech:parasitophorous_vacuole_membrane cellstructuremech:parasitophorous_vacuole_lumen

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0194

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:5794 Apicomplexa — COMMON: UniProt describes the parasitophorous vacuole as the host-cell vacuole in which most apicomplexan parasites reside and develop. [uniprot.location:SL-0194]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5811 Toxoplasma gondii — Apicomplexan parasite whose parasitophorous vacuole and parasite-modified PVM are reviewed as a model host-parasite interface. [DOI:10.1016/j.pt.2017.02.007]
  • NCBITaxon:5833 Plasmodium falciparum — Human malaria parasite whose intraerythrocytic PV/PVM was used in a BioID survey of PVM-associated proteins. [DOI:10.1128/mSphere.00522-17]

Functions

  • intracellular parasite replication niche — The parasitophorous vacuole surrounds the intracellular parasite and supplies the host-cell compartment in which apicomplexan parasites reside and develop.
