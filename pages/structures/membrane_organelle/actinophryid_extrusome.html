actinophryid extrusome

cellstructuremech:actinophryid_extrusome · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A secretory granule beneath the cell or axopodial surface of actinophryid protists, containing amorphous electron-dense material released during prey capture. [DOI:10.1016/j.ejop.2007.11.001]

Discussions

  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve an exact GP40 protein-family grounding and isoform-specific extrusome localization. B2NIF0 and B2NIF1 correspond to gp40-1 and gp40-2 direct submissions, not isoform-specific localization experiments in the 2001 study. Do not infer enzymatic activity from broad glucanase-related domains or add both sequences as experimentally localized protein examples.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Identify the lipid constituents of the extrusome membrane. Membrane continuity is evidenced, but lipid chemistry remains uncharacterized in the cited study.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Do not treat pharmacological targets as localized granule components. Channel-blocker and calmodulin-antagonist effects do not identify granule-resident proteins. Ionophore-mediated granule depletion was proposed but unverified, and Con A could target other surface glycoconjugates.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep actinophryid extrusomes distinct from ciliate extrusomes and context-specific vacuole terms. The source distinguishes amorphous actinophryid contents from organized ciliate extrusomes; toxicyst is not adopted as an exact synonym. GO:0043231 is the broader parent; GO:0030141 additionally assumes Golgi origin, not established here. The prey-vacuole membrane node remains ungrounded because GO:0020020 denotes a parasite vacuole digesting host cytoplasm.
