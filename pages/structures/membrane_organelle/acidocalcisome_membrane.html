  • NCBITaxon:2759 Eukaryota — VARIABLE: Acidocalcisomes occur across diverse unicellular eukaryotes and metazoan cell types, but are not present in every eukaryotic cell. [DOI:10.1128/MMBR.00042-23]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:358 Agrobacterium tumefaciens — Bacterial species whose calcium- and polyphosphate-rich volutin granules were shown to be membrane-bound acidocalcisome-like organelles. [DOI:10.1074/jbc.M304548200]

Functions

  • acidocalcisome lumen acidification — Proton pumps in the acidocalcisome membrane generate an acidic lumen that supports polyphosphate and cation storage.
    • DOI:10.1038/nrmicro1097 Docampo et al. 2005 review proton pumps in the acidocalcisome membrane and the acidic lumen they maintain.
  • acidocalcisome cation transport — Transport systems in the acidocalcisome membrane move calcium and other cations into and out of the organelle.
    • DOI:10.1128/MMBR.00042-23 Docampo and Huang 2024 review calcium and cation transport across acidocalcisome membranes.
