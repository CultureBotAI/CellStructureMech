multivesicular body

GO:0005771 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A type of endosome in which regions of the limiting endosomal membrane invaginate to form internal vesicles; membrane proteins that enter the internal vesicles are sequestered from the cytoplasm. [GO:0005771]

Synonyms: MVB; endosomal carrier vesicle; ECV; corpuscula multivesicularis; multivesicular bodies

Has part: GO:0032585 cellstructuremech:multivesicular_body_lumen

Functions

  • vacuolar cargo delivery — Yeast multivesicular bodies deliver endosomal membrane cargo into the vacuole lumen for degradation.
