trans-Golgi network transport vesicle membrane

GO:0012510 · Membrane organelle · Membrane · Proposed

The lipid bilayer surrounding a vesicle transporting substances between the trans-Golgi network and other parts of the cell. [GO:0012510]

Synonyms: TGN transport vesicle membrane; trans-Golgi network constitutive secretory pathway transport vesicle membrane

Part of: trans-Golgi network transport vesicle (GO:0030140 — trans-Golgi network transport vesicle)
