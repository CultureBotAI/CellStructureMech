Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5691 Trypanosoma brucei — African trypanosome in which flagellar-pocket membrane domains, electron-tomographic architecture and BILBO1-dependent biogenesis have been studied. [DOI:10.1242/jcs.045740]

Functions

  • endocytic and exocytic gateway — The flagellar pocket concentrates endocytosis and exocytosis at the site where the flagellum exits the trypanosome cell body.
    • DOI:10.1038/nrmicro2221 Field and Carrington 2009 reviewed the trypanosome flagellar pocket as the dominant site of endo- and exocytosis.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.pt.2020.11.005 Halliday and Sunter 2021 review how the trypanosomatid flagellar pocket couples pocket structure to trafficking functions.
  • flagellum exit-site organization — The pocket organizes the membrane invagination and neck through which the eukaryotic flagellum emerges from the cell body.
    • DOI:10.1242/jcs.045740 Lacomble et al. 2009 reconstructed the T. brucei flagellar pocket and associated cytoskeleton by electron microscope tomography.

Mechanism graphs

Flagellar pocket boundaries organize surface traffic (FUNCTION)

The flagellar pocket membrane forms a specialized surface invagination whose neck is marked by the ciliary pocket collar, concentrating endocytic and exocytic traffic at the base of the flagellum.
