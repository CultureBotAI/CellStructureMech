magnetosome

GO:0110143 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A membrane-bounded organelle of magnetotactic bacteria enclosing a nanocrystal of a magnetic iron mineral — magnetite (Fe3O4) or greigite (Fe3S4). Magnetosomes are invaginations of the cytoplasmic membrane that are filled with iron, and are arranged into a chain along a cytoskeletal filament so that the cell behaves as a swimming compass needle. [GO:0110143]

Functions

  • magnetoreception — The magnetosome chain gives the cell a permanent magnetic dipole, so it aligns passively with the geomagnetic field while swimming.
    • DOI:10.1038/nrmicro.2016.99 Uebe & Schüler 2016 describe the chain as a compass needle aligning the cell with the geomagnetic field.
