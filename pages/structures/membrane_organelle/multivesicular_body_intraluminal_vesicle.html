multivesicular body intraluminal vesicle

cellstructuremech:multivesicular_body_intraluminal_vesicle · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · MEMBRANE_BOUNDED_ORGANELLE · PROPOSED

A membrane-bounded vesicle inside a multivesicular body that forms when the limiting endosomal membrane buds inward and pinches into the lumen during multivesicular body sorting. [DOI:10.1016/j.devcel.2011.05.015]
