ER to Golgi transport vesicle membrane topology (FUNCTION)

The ER to Golgi transport vesicle membrane is the lipid-bilayer boundary of the COPII-coated ER-to-Golgi transport vesicle formed during endoplasmic-reticulum membrane evagination.
