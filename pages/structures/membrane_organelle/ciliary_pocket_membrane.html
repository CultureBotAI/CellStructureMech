ciliary pocket membrane

GO:0020018 · Membrane organelle · Membrane · Proposed

Synonyms: cilial pocket membrane; cilium pocket membrane; flagellar pocket membrane

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the ciliary pocket membrane distinct from the whole flagellar pocket and from the cytoskeletal pocket collar. GO:0020018 denotes the specialized plasma-membrane domain lining the ciliary or flagellar pocket, GO:0020016 denotes the complete ciliary pocket invagination, and GO:1990900 denotes the collar complex at the pocket neck.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve exact lipid classes and membrane proteins of the T. brucei flagellar pocket membrane. Gadelha et al. 2009 support the flagellar-pocket membrane domain and its cytoskeletal boundaries, but this first child-membrane record does not assign exact source-neutral lipid or protein-family groundings for the pocket membrane.
