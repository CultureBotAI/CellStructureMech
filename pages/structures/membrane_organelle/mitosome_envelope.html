Functions

  • mitosome compartmentalization — The mitosome envelope surrounds the reduced organelle and separates the matrix from the cytosol.
    • uniprot.location:SL-0438 UniProt SL-0438 defines the mitosome envelope as the inner and outer mitosomal membranes plus intermembrane space.
    • DOI:10.1046/j.1365-2958.1999.01414.x Tovar et al. 1999 identified a double-membrane mitochondrion-related mitosome in Entamoeba histolytica.
  • mitosomal protein import boundary — The mitosome envelope is the double-membrane boundary crossed by nuclear-encoded, N-terminally targeted mitosomal proteins.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.0500349102 Dolezal et al. 2005 tested Giardia mitosomal protein targeting and compared the import mode with hydrogenosomes.
    • DOI:10.1038/nature01945 Tovar et al. 2003 localized nuclear-encoded ISC proteins to Giardia mitosomes.
