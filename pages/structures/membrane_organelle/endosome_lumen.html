Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent the endosome membrane as the boundary of the endosome lumen rather than as a lumen component. GO:0031904 denotes the volume enclosed by the endosome membrane, whereas GO:0010008 denotes the surrounding lipid bilayer.
