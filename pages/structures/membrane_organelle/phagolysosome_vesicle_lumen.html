phagolysosome vesicle lumen

GO:0106174 · Membrane organelle · Subcellular region · Proposed — Drafted from literature to fill a coverage gap; not yet reviewed. How records are made

The volume enclosed by the membrane of a phagolysosome. In phagotrophic amoebae (Dictyostelium discoideum, Entamoeba histolytica) and ciliates, the acidic phagolysosome vesicle lumen concentrates cathepsin-like cysteine and aspartic proteases, lysozymes, phospholipases, and reactive oxygen species that kill and digest engulfed microbial cells. [GO:0106174]

Synonyms: phagolysosome lumen

Part of: phagolysosome (GO:0032010)

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Represent the phagocytic vesicle membrane as the boundary of the lumen rather than as a lumen component. GO:0097013 denotes the lumenal volume enclosed by the phagocytic vesicle membrane, not a stable molecular constituent table. The membrane is therefore modeled as a boundary node in the topology graph.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep variable phagocytosed cargo and maturation enzymes out of the generic phagocytic vesicle lumen components. The phagocytic vesicle lumen is the bounded volume inside the vesicle. The ingested particle and soluble hydrolases depend on phagosome cargo and maturation stage, so this generic lumen record does not assert a stable cargo or enzyme component.
