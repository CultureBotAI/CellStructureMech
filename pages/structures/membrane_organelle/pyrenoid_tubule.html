Functions

  • pyrenoid inorganic carbon delivery — Pyrenoid tubules are thylakoid-membrane continuities that traverse the Rubisco-rich pyrenoid matrix and are thought to support delivery of concentrated inorganic carbon to the pyrenoid.
    • DOI:10.1093/plcell/koad157 He, Crans and Jonikas 2023 review the pyrenoid as a eukaryotic CO2-concentrating organelle whose matrix is traversed by membranes in many lineages.
    • DOI:10.1111/nph.70669 Franklin et al. 2026 describe pyrenoid-traversing membranes as membranes thought to supply concentrated CO2 to pyrenoids.
