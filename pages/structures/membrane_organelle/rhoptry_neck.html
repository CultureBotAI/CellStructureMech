Functions

  • rhoptry neck secretion — The rhoptry neck forms the narrow electron-dense apical domain used as rhoptry contents are secreted during host-cell invasion.
    • GO:1990225 GO:1990225 defines the rhoptry neck as the narrow part of the rhoptry that extends through the apical conoid.
    • DOI:10.1038/nrmicro1800 Boothroyd and Dubremetz 2008 review neck and bulb compartmentation of Toxoplasma rhoptries during invasion.
  • moving junction assembly — Rhoptry neck proteins are secreted from the rhoptry neck and help form the moving junction that coordinates apicomplexan host-cell invasion.
    • DOI:10.1371/journal.ppat.0010017 Alexander et al. 2005 showed that RON4 leaves the rhoptry neck and contributes to the moving-junction complex in Toxoplasma tachyzoites.

Mechanism graphs

Rhoptry neck dispatches RON proteins for invasion (FUNCTION)

The rhoptry neck is the narrow apical rhoptry domain through which rhoptry-neck proteins are secreted. In Toxoplasma, secreted RON proteins relocalize to the moving junction used for host-cell invasion.
