apicomplexan dense granule

GO:0020026 · Membrane organelle · Membrane bounded organelle · Proposed

Electron-dense organelle with a granular internal matrix found in a apicomplexan parasite; contains proteins destined to be secreted into the parasitophorous vacuole following parasite invasion of a host cell. [GO:0020026]

Synonyms: dense body

Has part: apicomplexan dense granule membrane (GO:0030668)

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5811 Toxoplasma gondii — Apicomplexan model in which dense-granule GRA proteins, GRA16 export, and GRA17/GRA23-mediated PVM permeability have been experimentally studied. [DOI:10.1016/j.chom.2015.04.003]

Functions

  • dense granule protein secretion — Dense granules secrete GRA cargo proteins into the parasitophorous vacuole after host-cell invasion.
    • PMID:9208224 Carruthers and Sibley 1997 showed that Toxoplasma dense-granule secretion follows microneme and rhoptry secretion during host-cell invasion.
    • DOI:10.1111/jeu.12904 Griffith et al. 2022 review dense-granule secretion in Toxoplasma gondii.
  • parasitophorous vacuole membrane permeability — Dense-granule cargo proteins can localize to the PVM and mediate passive small-molecule movement between the host cell and parasitophorous vacuole.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.chom.2015.04.003 Gold et al. 2015 linked the dense-granule proteins GRA17 and GRA23 to PVM permeability and nutrient access.
  • host cell effector delivery — Some dense-granule proteins cross the PVM and reach host-cell compartments where they modulate host gene expression and signaling.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.chom.2013.03.002 Bougdour et al. 2013 showed that the exported dense-granule protein GRA16 reaches the host nucleus and alters host gene expression.

Dense-granule GRA proteins remodel the parasitophorous vacuole (FUNCTION)

The dense-granule membrane encloses a secretory cargo compartment; after host-cell invasion, dense-granule exocytosis releases GRA proteins, including GRA17 and GRA23 PVM permeability factors, into the PV/PVM.
