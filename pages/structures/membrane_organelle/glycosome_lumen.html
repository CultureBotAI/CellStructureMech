glycosome lumen

GO:0034468 · MEMBRANE_ORGANELLE · SUBCELLULAR_REGION · PROPOSED

The volume enclosed by the glycosome membrane. [GO:0034468]

Synonyms: glycosomal lumen; glycosomal matrix; glycosome matrix

Part of: GO:0020015

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0332

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:5653 Kinetoplastea — COMMON: Glycosome lumina are the matrix compartments of specialized peroxisomes in kinetoplastid protists. [DOI:10.1016/j.bbamcr.2015.09.015]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:5691 Trypanosoma brucei — Kinetoplastid parasite whose glycosome lumen houses glycolytic enzymes required to maintain controlled glucose catabolism. [DOI:10.1073/pnas.0806664105]

Functions

  • glycosome lumen glycolysis — The glycosome lumen is the matrix compartment that contains major glycolytic enzymes and thereby compartmentalizes glycolytic flux in kinetoplastids.
    • DOI:10.1016/0014-5793(77)80476-6 Opperdoes and Borst 1977 localized nine glycolytic enzymes to Trypanosoma brucei glycosomes.
    • DOI:10.1073/pnas.0806664105 Haanstra et al. 2008 showed that mislocalizing glycosomal enzymes disrupts glycolytic control in Trypanosoma brucei.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep the glycosome membrane as the boundary of the glycosome lumen rather than as a lumen component. GO:0034468 denotes the volume enclosed by the glycosome membrane, whereas GO:0046860 denotes the membrane surrounding a glycosome.
