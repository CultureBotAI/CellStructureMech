proteasome storage granule

GO:0034515 · Inclusion · Inclusion body · Proposed

A cytoplasmic, membrane-free accumulation of proteasome core and regulatory particles observed as discrete foci during cellular quiescence. [GO:0034515]

Synonyms: PSG

Part of: GO:0005737 — cytoplasm (no local record)

Functions

  • reversible proteasome storage — Concentrates proteasome material during quiescence for subsequent mobilization. This does not assert protection from autophagic degradation.
    • DOI:10.1083/jcb.200711154 Figures 4-5 test refeeding with water and translation-inhibition controls.

Discussions

  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Exclude the retracted 2018 study and derivative commentary from positive support. Ontology citation chains can retain secondary accounts of withdrawn work.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Reconcile in situ PSG architecture with older extract-based assembly assays. Colocalized subunits and dissociated particles in lysates do not uniquely establish assembly state inside living-cell granules.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Separate experimentally observed residents from indirect genetic modifiers. A deletion phenotype or a different reversible focus is not PSG membership.
  • CURATION_TODO (OPEN): Resolve an exact source-neutral identifier for unconjugated mature ubiquitin. Domains and a family including other ubiquitin-like proteins are broader than the curated component.
  • INTERPRETATION (RESOLVED): Keep PSG distinct from individual proteasomes and other protein inclusions. Shared components or transient proximity do not establish identity.
