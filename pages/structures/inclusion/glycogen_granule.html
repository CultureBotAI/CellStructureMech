glycogen granule

cellstructuremech:glycogen_granule · INCLUSION · INCLUSION_BODY · PROPOSED

A microbial carbohydrate storage inclusion in which glycogen polymer accumulates as cytoplasmic granules that store glucose-derived carbon and energy for later mobilization. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2016.00966]

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: Glycogen storage granules occur in cyanobacteria and other glycogen-producing bacteria but are absent from bacterial lineages that lack bacterial glycogen biosynthesis. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2016.00966]

Canonical examples

Functions
