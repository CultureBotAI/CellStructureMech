glycogen granule

cellstructuremech:glycogen_granule · Inclusion · Inclusion body · Proposed

A microbial carbohydrate storage inclusion in which glycogen polymer accumulates as cytoplasmic granules that store glucose-derived carbon and energy for later mobilization. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2016.00966]

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: Glycogen storage granules occur in cyanobacteria and other glycogen-producing bacteria but are absent from bacterial lineages that lack bacterial glycogen biosynthesis. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2016.00966]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:1148 Synechocystis sp. PCC 6803 — Unicellular cyanobacterium used to analyze glycogen granule formation during macronutrient starvation. [DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2016.00966]

Functions

  • glycogen carbon and energy storage — Glycogen granules sequester glucose-derived carbon and energy as branched glycogen polymer that can be degraded when external nutrients limit growth.
    • DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2016.00966 Damrow, Maldener and Zilliges 2016 study glycogen as a common microbial carbon polymer that accumulates in Synechocystis during macronutrient starvation.
