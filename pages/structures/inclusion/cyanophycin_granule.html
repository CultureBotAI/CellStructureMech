cyanophycin granule

cellstructuremech:cyanophycin_granule · INCLUSION · INCLUSION_BODY · PROPOSED

A bacterial storage inclusion in which insoluble cyanophycin polymer accumulates as cytoplasmic granules that store fixed nitrogen and carbon for later mobilization. [DOI:10.5772/intechopen.77049]

Functions

  • fixed nitrogen and carbon storage — Cyanophycin granules sequester nitrogen-rich cyanophycin polymer as a storage reserve that can be mobilized when cells need nitrogen or carbon.
