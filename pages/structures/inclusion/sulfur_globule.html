sulfur globule

cellstructuremech:sulfur_globule · Inclusion · Inclusion body · Proposed

A bacterial sulfur storage inclusion that accumulates elemental sulfur from reduced sulfur compounds and packages it with sulfur-globule envelope proteins for temporary storage and later oxidation. [DOI:10.1159/000351335]

Synonyms: intracellular sulfur globule

  • NCBITaxon:2 Bacteria — VARIABLE: Intracellular sulfur globules occur in multiple sulfur-oxidizing bacterial groups; the cited review establishes positive occurrences, not a quantitative estimate of absence across all bacteria. [DOI:10.1159/000351335]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:572477 Allochromatium vinosum DSM 180 — Purple sulfur bacterium used for SgpA, SgpB and SgpC sulfur globule protein mutagenesis. [DOI:10.1007/s00203-004-0683-3]

Functions

  • temporary elemental sulfur storage — Sulfur globules sequester elemental sulfur formed during reduced sulfur oxidation and retain it as an intracellular reserve for later oxidation.
    • DOI:10.1159/000351335 Maki 2013 reviews intracellular sulfur globules as temporary sulfur reservoirs in sulfur-oxidizing bacteria.
