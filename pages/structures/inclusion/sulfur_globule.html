sulfur globule

cellstructuremech:sulfur_globule · INCLUSION · INCLUSION_BODY · PROPOSED

A bacterial sulfur storage inclusion that accumulates elemental sulfur from reduced sulfur compounds and packages it with sulfur-globule envelope proteins for temporary storage and later oxidation. [DOI:10.1159/000351335]

Synonyms: intracellular sulfur globule
