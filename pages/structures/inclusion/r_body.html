R-body

cellstructuremech:r_body · Inclusion · Inclusion body · Proposed

A bacterial proteinaceous inclusion body assembled from Reb proteins that forms a coiled ribbon and can extend into a hollow tube. [DOI:10.1021/acssynbio.5b00237]

Synonyms: R body; refractile body; R-body inclusion

UniProt location names: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Reference label and route sources.

Functions

  • pH-triggered R-body extension — Coiled R-body ribbons can rapidly extend into hollow tubes when acidification triggers the conformational transition.
    • DOI:10.1021/acssynbio.5b00237 Polka and Silver 2016 engineered and imaged type 51 R-bodies that extend from coiled ribbons into tubes after acidification.
