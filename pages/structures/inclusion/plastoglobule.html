plastoglobule

GO:0010287 · Inclusion · Inclusion body · Proposed

A lipoprotein particle present in chloroplasts. [GO:0010287]

Synonyms: PG; plastoglobuli

Part of: chloroplast stroma (GO:0009570)

Xrefs: uniprot.location:SL-0217 — Plastoglobule

Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — COMMON: Plastoglobules are present in the Chlamydomonas reinhardtii chloroplast and were resolved by in situ cryo-electron tomography. [DOI:10.7554/eLife.04889]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — Unicellular green alga whose native chloroplast architecture was imaged by cryo-focused ion beam milling and in situ cryo-electron tomography, revealing plastoglobules among thylakoid and pyrenoid subcompartments. [DOI:10.7554/eLife.04889]

Functions

  • thylakoid lipid remodeling — Plastoglobules participate in chloroplast lipid metabolism and thylakoid remodeling. Exchange of lipid metabolites with thylakoids is a proposed model, not a directly established transport mechanism in the cited review or the Chlamydomonas example.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.bbabio.2015.02.002 Rottet, Besagni and Kessler 2015 review evidence that plastoglobules participate in thylakoid lipid remodeling.

Mechanism graphs

Higher-plant plastoglobule architecture and proposed lipid exchange (ASSEMBLY)

The higher-plant model places a lipid core within a monolayer continuous with the thylakoid outer leaflet. This architecture motivates a lipid-exchange hypothesis; it is not evidence for universal continuity or direct transport in algae.
