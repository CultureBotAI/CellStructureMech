Taxonomic distribution

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — COMMON: Plastoglobules are present in the Chlamydomonas reinhardtii chloroplast and were resolved by in situ cryo-electron tomography. [DOI:10.7554/eLife.04889]

Canonical examples

  • NCBITaxon:3055 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii — Unicellular green alga whose native chloroplast architecture was imaged by cryo-focused ion beam milling and in situ cryo-electron tomography, revealing plastoglobules among thylakoid and pyrenoid subcompartments. [DOI:10.7554/eLife.04889]

Functions

  • thylakoid lipid remodeling — Plastoglobules exchange lipid metabolites with adjacent thylakoid membranes and participate in thylakoid lipid remodeling during chloroplast development and stress responses.
    • DOI:10.1016/j.bbabio.2015.02.002 Rottet, Besagni and Kessler 2015 review evidence that plastoglobules participate in thylakoid lipid remodeling.

Plastoglobule monolayer organizes lipid exchange (ASSEMBLY)

A hydrophobic plastoglobule lipid core is surrounded by a lipid monolayer that remains physically continuous with the thylakoid outer leaflet, positioning the plastoglobule to exchange lipid-derived metabolites with thylakoid membranes.
