lipid droplet

GO:0005811 · INCLUSION · INCLUSION_BODY · PROPOSED

An intracellular non-membrane-bounded organelle comprising a matrix of coalesced lipids surrounded by a phospholipid monolayer. May include associated proteins. [GO:0005811]

Synonyms: adiposome; lipid body; lipid particle

Has part: GO:0034430

Functions

  • neutral lipid storage — Lipid droplets sequester hydrophobic triglycerides and sterol esters as neutral-lipid reserves that can be mobilized by lipases and hydrolases.

    • PMID:29397034 Graef 2018 reviews budding-yeast lipid droplets as dedicated stores of triacylglycerols and steryl esters.
