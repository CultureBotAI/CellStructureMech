Protein sequence map (pLM)

2D PACMAP projection of 469 unique protein sequences across 140 microbial cell structures (1169 protein–structure mentions), embedded with facebook/esm2_t33_650M_UR50D (revision 08e4846e537177426273712802403f7ba8261b6c, 1280-d mean-pooled residue representations).

Each point represents a curated UniProtKB protein from a cell structure component or assembly machinery entry. Positions depend only on amino-acid sequence; structure categories, component roles, and extraction channels are layered on as colors and filters. Click a protein to inspect its linked structure records; scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to reset view.
