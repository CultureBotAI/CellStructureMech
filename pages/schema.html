CellStructureMech - Microbial Cell Structure Schema

LinkML schema for microbial cell structures: organelles, envelope layers, appendages, microcompartments, inclusions, cytoskeletal filaments and the multi-protein complexes that build them. One CellStructureRecord per YAML file, grounded in GO cellular component where a term exists and minted under the `cellstructuremech:` prefix otherwise.

Granularity contract: a record is a structure a microbiologist would point to in an electron micrograph or name as a subcellular entity — from the whole organelle (carboxysome, thylakoid) down to a discrete multi-protein complex (ribosome, flagellar motor, ATP synthase). Individual proteins are NOT records: they are `components` of a record and, where a taxon-agnostic family or organism-specific accession exists, they ground to InterPro / UniProtKB and hand off to ProteinTraitsMech. Phenotypes conferred by a structure (motile, flagellated, Gram-negative) are TraitMech records linked via `associated_traits`, not duplicated here.

Follows the dismech / TraitMech / HabitatMech pattern: one YAML per entity, ontology-grounded, evidence-backed, closed-schema validated, curated incrementally through an append-only audit trail.

Generated by just render from the LinkML schema src/cellstructuremech/schema/cellstructuremech.yaml, src/cellstructuremech/schema/mech_shared.yaml. Slots are listed as LinkML resolves them for each class, with imports, attributes and slot usage applied. Descriptions are shown as written in the schema, without links: the identifiers in them are often format examples. Schema licence: BSD-3-Clause.
