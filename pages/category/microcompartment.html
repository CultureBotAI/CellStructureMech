carboxysome

A bacterial microcompartment — a polyhedral, selectively permeable protein shell about 100–200 nm across — that encapsulates RuBisCO and carbonic anhydrase to concentrate CO2 around RuBisCO in cyanobacteria and many chemoautotrophs. Alpha-carboxysomes (cso genes) and beta-carboxysomes (ccm genes) evolved their shells and scaffolds independently.

carboxysome shell

The polyhedral protein coat of alpha- and beta-carboxysomes, built from BMC-H, BMC-T, and BMC-P shell proteins that enclose RuBisCO and carbonic anhydrase while controlling metabolite traffic between the lumen and cytosol.

encapsulin nanocompartment

A proteinaceous nanocompartment, 24 to 42 nm across, built from a single HK97-fold shell protein that self-assembles into an icosahedron. Cargo enzymes are packaged inside by a short targeting peptide that binds the shell interior; the commonest cargoes are ferritin-like iron-mineralising proteins and peroxidases.
