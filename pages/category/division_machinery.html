divisome complex

The membrane-associated protein machine that constricts a dividing bacterial cell. It is organised by the FtsZ ring — treadmilling filaments of the tubulin homologue FtsZ tethered to the inner membrane — which positions and moves the septal peptidoglycan synthases that build the division septum.

elongasome

The sidewall peptidoglycan-synthesis machine of rod-shaped bacteria, also called the Rod complex. A SEDS-family glycosyltransferase (RodA) works with a class B penicillin-binding protein (PBP2) to polymerise and crosslink new wall material into the cylinder, while MreC, MreD and RodZ couple the complex to MreB filaments, which distribute it around the circumference. It is the elongation counterpart of the divisome.
