# MicrO live reassessment

Checked 2026-09-01 as the last remaining candidate in the source queue.

## Source state

OBO Foundry still marks the Ontology of Prokaryotic Phenotypic and Metabolic
Characters (`MICRO`) inactive. The official `carrineblank/MicrO` repository is
not archived, but its latest content commit remains 2018-06-14. Its `License`
file grants CC BY 2.0.

The static ontology remains indexed by OLS. A local copy of the upstream
`MicrO.owl` at commit `73963269d9a681157d710f272f8e65e4f7e8897e` had SHA-256
`ef21dbfab755143a9c390ad274d4e1405f344f6a8dbfbd749de09af0b4b54910`.

## Mapping decision

Adopt MicrO only as a static xref vocabulary. Two current records have exact,
useful identity mappings:

- `GO:0031411` gas vesicle to `MICRO:0000214` gas vacuole. The MicrO term names
  "gas vesicles" as an exact synonym and defines the non-membrane-bound protein
  buoyancy organelle.
- `GO:0110143` magnetosome to `MICRO:0000216` magnetosome.

The live OLS endpoint returned each exact CURIE once, under the `micro`
ontology, with the expected label and `is_obsolete: false`.

Several tempting string matches were rejected. MicrO's flagellar entries are
mostly cell qualities or flagellated-cell classes, not the bacterial flagellum
itself. `MICRO:0000213` is the whole differentiated endospore cell, not this
corpus's endospore external encapsulating structure. Fimbriae are not asserted
equivalent to the broader pilus record.

## Adapter boundary

Because the ontology is inactive and heterogeneous, `scripts/micro_xrefs.py`
contains the two reviewed mappings as a fixed allow-list. It does not search by
label or add plausible matches. Before writing, it verifies exact OLS CURIE,
IRI, ontology, label, and non-obsolete status, as well as the expected record
identifier and label. Dry-run is the default.
