## Licence correction

The live BioCyc download page says EcoCyc data-file and API access is free but
still requires a licence. Its linked data-file click-through licence applies
when using the BioCyc web-services API and defines EcoCyc as an "Open
Database." It permits use, modification, and redistribution worldwide,
royalty-free, for any purpose.

Redistribution is conditional. A distributor must:

- notify SRI that it is making BioCyc databases available;
- link the distribution to `https://www.biocyc.org`;
- include the prescribed BioCyc-under-licence statement and BioCyc logo; and
- for modified copies, identify the source databases, preserve applicable
  copyright notices and author lists, and summarize modifications.

This supersedes the queue's prior reading of the individual subscription
terms. The more specific data-file licence explicitly covers web-service use
and distinguishes open EcoCyc from the non-redistributable limited databases.

## Decision

Mark EcoCyc `BLOCKED`, not rejected. The data are technically accessible and
redistributable, but adoption requires an external notification to SRI and
site-wide compliance with the required notice and logo. No notification was
sent during this reassessment. Keep the source `CURATE_ONLY` until the
repository owner authorizes that communication and the presentation
requirements are implemented.
